# Register results

Editors only — not linked from grammar pages. Findings from translating the register samples ([register-samples](register-samples.md)) for Phase 2b of the expressiveness review (`docs/proposals/expressiveness-review.md`, where the translations live). Each row is a point where the translator had to stop, paraphrase heavily, or settle for an approximation. Rows cite `RS-n.m`.

Progress: RS-1 to RS-5 translated. G-01–G-12, L-01–L-06 and L-10–L-12 are ruled. L-13 (*bark*, *grass*, *heel*) is open. L-07–L-09 (taste words, *sauce*, *lid*) are open.

Fields follow [syntax-test-results](syntax-test-results.md): **Verdict** is **awkward** · **missing** · **by design?** (needs a ruling). **Priority** is **P1** common everyday English · **P2** common in writing · **P3** niche. `G-nn` is a grammar gap and `L-nn` is lexicon only.

| G-01 | 1.2c | *late* against an agreed time (*sorry I'm late*) | Not a gap. *Later than usual* is `thobam zel bral` (comparatives.md § bars), which is the wrong bar here. | G-02 | 1.3b | *the same X as last time* (a previous occasion as the comparee) | Not a gap. | G-04 | 1.5b | *so,* opening a question turn | Ruled: covered. The act word opens the turn and the linker starts the body (`yom xevavel …`); a linker before the act word would keep the previous act. | G-05 | 1.7b | *should have* / *ought to have* (an obligation that was not met, said afterwards) | Ruled: covered by [stance as-of](../grammar/relations.md#stance-as-of), which already dates sakes; undated *back then* is `thuhum brul` (signed offsets allowed on stance as-of only, D-10): `zehodon thuhum brul thegathem vezebel`. The evidential proposal is rejected (WITNESSED cannot report an ought). Recipe row added in say-tense.md § must / should; the non-fulfilment is its own sentence. Original note: The ought is `thegathem` (`the` on the unspecified-sake root, sakes.md § prescription), which the doc defines as **deontic force, not a report that the act is happening**. It has no tense. `thevom` before it does parse (`zehodon thevom vezebel thegathem`), but no page says that a channel dates the ought, and WITNESSED says *I observed it*, which an ought cannot be. So that stack is a guess, not a route. Other routes checked: `thumem` (*required by a person*) has the same problem; `thavom` (NOTIONAL) with `thanathel` says *imagine it served*, not *it was owed*; `vohum` (*wish*) says *I wish you had* but drops the obligation and can't take `thevom` inside without claiming the telling happened. Safe route: two sentences, *you did not say* (`zehodon thevom vezebel vul.`) then *saying ought to happen* (`zehodon thegathem vezebel.`), which loses that the ought belonged to the past. | covered | sakes.md (prescription), knowing.md (channels) | — | P1 |
| G-06 | 1.11b | *by the way* in front of a question | Resolved: new sentence linker `xavazel` *by the way* (🍟 `avaze` *accessory*: a side dish), dependents.md § sentence linkers; `or …` returns to the main line. Original note: An aside must keep the outer sentence's speech act, and it packages a clause as a stance word, so a bare *by the way* with no clause inside cannot open a question. Route: `yol xevavel …`, which gives *so, …* rather than *by the way*. | done | spans.md (asides), say-questions.md | — | P1 |
| G-07 | 2.6b | *ask someone to do X* (a reported request) | Not a gap. | G-08 | 3.2b | *as ADJ as possible* / *as … as you can* | Resolved: the ABIL bar **`thezexal`** (comparatives.md § every bar). Tie `ae` is *as ADJ as possible*; a holder or `/b/` says whose ability. (First resolved as a named *Best-effort* bar, since dropped with the other named bars: design-decisions.md § Bars.) Listed in english.md (*as*). Original note: no taught form ranked against what can be done; the route was *so that* plus a superlative (`zel gamazal`), which ranks against an unstated group. Phrases tried: *as possible*, *as much as*, *best*, *smallest*, *most*, *try*, *ability*. | done | comparatives.md (best effort), english.md | — | P1 |
| G-09 | 3.4b, 3.8b, 3.8c | *if X, do Y* (a condition on a command) | Ruled: an *if* pole on a command or request limits the instruction to that case. Taught with an example in dependents.md § dependent clauses (`yel vowogal thoyem barl zalahen vehahel`, *if Alahen sits, walk*). The parser already accepted it. Original note: *if* was defined only as the speaker's stance on why a claim holds, and no page showed a condition on `yel` / `yem`. Phrases tried: *if*, *when*, *unless*, *in case*. | done | dependents.md (dependent clauses) | — | P1 |
| G-10 | 4.2a, 4.4a, 4.8b | a date with only a month, only a year, or only a day (*in March*, *built in 1962*, *on the 12th*) | A calendar date fills its fields by position: day, month, optional year (numbers-applied.md § Time). The parser rejects an empty leading field (`h_#,3`, `h_#,,1962`), so there is no taught way to name a month or a year alone. Stand-ins used: *in March* is the 3rd month as an ordinal on `umuha` (`huwem bumuham grerel`), copying the weekday rule (`zelagam grewol` *Monday*); *in 1962* is the year unit with a label (`huwem bavawem g_1962`). Day-only `h_#12` (*on the 12th*) parses, but the page does not say a date may stop after the day. Phrases tried: *in March*, *on Tuesday*, *month*, *year*, *1962*. | done | numbers-applied.md (time), say-tense.md | Resolved: a month is an ordinal on `umuha` (`zumuham grerel` *March*, like weekdays on `elaga`), a year is a label on `avawe` (`zavawem g_1962`), each placed with `huwem` + `/b/`; a date may stop after the day (`h_#12`). Taught in numbers-applied.md § Time, listed in say-tense.md § everyday time words. The parser now reads a label after a hosted `/b/` (`bavawem g_1962`) as an adjective on the landmark, not an offset amount; test in constructions.test.ts. | P2 |
| G-11 | 5.2a–5.10 | story past: English sets the story's past once and keeps it (*She stood … A dog was barking … She sat down …*) | A past claim needs a channel (*Simple past: pick how you know*, say-tense.md), and the bare verb reports *this stretch*. For fiction the honest channel is TALE (`thozem`). The translation puts `thozem` on the first sentence only and uses bare verbs after it, reading them as the tale's own now, like an English historical present. No page says a channel carries over to later sentences, so this is a guess; the safe route repeats `thozem` in every narrated sentence. Phrases tried: *story*, *once upon*, *tale*, *narrative*. | done | knowing.md (evidentiality), say-tense.md | Resolved: once a sentence frames a passage as a tale (`thozem`), later sentences in the same turn with no channel go on telling the tale in its own now, until another channel or a new turn; a quote keeps its speaker's own now. Taught in knowing.md § Evidentiality, listed in say-tense.md § one English tense, several jobs. No parser change. | P2 |
| G-12 | 5.2c | a simile whose model is an event (*like a hand through hair*, *the way a cat stretches*) | **`humum`** takes a noun in `/b/` (relations.md § Simile), and the parser rejects **`humum barl`** + a sentence (*a hosted stand-in is `barl` after a listed pole*). Route: say the model event first, then point back at it: `zahadal vuvudel uol behebelx. zewedul vuvudel uol bevedar humum barth.` (*A hand goes through hair. The wind goes through the field like that.*). The parser accepts `humum barth`, but no page teaches `barth` after a relation word. With the noun alone (`humum bahadal`) the *through hair* part is lost. Phrases tried: *like*, *as if*, *the way*, *just as*. | done | relations.md (simile), dependents.md, english.md | Resolved: **`humum barl`** + the next sentence is *the way …* / *like* an event; the model event is a picture, not asserted. Taught in relations.md § Simile, listed in english.md (*as*); **`humum barth`** (*like that*) is in dependents.md § pointing back. Parser now accepts `barl` after `humum` (enforce.ts), with a test in parse.test.ts. | P2 |

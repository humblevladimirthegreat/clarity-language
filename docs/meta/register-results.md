# Register results

Editors only — not linked from grammar pages. Findings from translating the register samples ([register-samples](register-samples.md)) for Phase 2b of the expressiveness review (`docs/proposals/expressiveness-review.md`, where the translations live). Each row is a point where the translator had to stop, paraphrase heavily, or settle for an approximation. Rows cite `RS-n.m`.

Progress: RS-1 to RS-3 translated. G-01–G-07 and L-01–L-05 are ruled. G-08, G-09 and L-06–L-10 (from RS-3) are open.

Fields follow [syntax-test-results](syntax-test-results.md): **Verdict** is **awkward** · **missing** · **by design?** (needs a ruling). **Priority** is **P1** common everyday English · **P2** common in writing · **P3** niche. `G-nn` is a grammar gap and `L-nn` is lexicon only.

## Grammar gaps

| ID | RS | English job | Current route | Verdict | Owning page | Proposal | Priority |
|----|----|-------------|---------------|---------|-------------|----------|----------|
| G-01 | 1.2c | *late* against an agreed time (*sorry I'm late*) | Not a gap. *Later than typical* is `zehon zel bral` (comparatives.md § benchmarks), which is the wrong bar here. An agreed time is a plan, so *late* is *after the planned arrival*: `zamagon thodom vevahal henum barl zahan thamam vevahal`. Both `henum barl` and PLAN are taught. My first pass used `zegan zel bral` (*too late for some sake*) because I missed this. | covered | dependents.md, intention.md | Optional recipe in say-amounts.md (*late* / *early* against a plan). | P1 |
| G-02 | 1.3b | *the same X as last time* (a previous occasion as the comparee) | Not a gap. Count the occasions from the end: the previous one is *2nd from the end* (`gruedul`, numbers.md § from the end). `yol zaxuvudel gugol bozaxuvudel gruedul` is *is the driver the same as the penultimate driver?*, and reads as *last time* whenever this ride is the latest. A dated resume (`-r`) or a `henum` / `habam` clause would also work but says more than the English does. | covered | numbers.md | Optional recipe: *last time* = the penultimate item. | P1 |
| G-03 | 1.4c | *anyway* (drop the digression and return to the point) | Not a gap. A sentence-initial resume hook `or …` is *Anyway, …* (hooks.md § Point back). My first pass used `xevavel` because I missed it. | covered | hooks.md | None; listed in english.md only if a learner-facing entry is wanted. | P2 |
| G-04 | 1.5b | *so,* opening a question turn | Ruled: covered. The act word opens the turn and the linker starts the body (`yom xevavel …`); a linker before the act word would keep the previous act. Now taught with an example in dependents.md § sentence linkers. Original note: A linker cannot precede the act word: `xevavel yom …` does not parse. Route: the act word first, then the linker (`yom xevavel …`). | covered | dependents.md (sentence linkers), speech-moves.md | — | P2 |
| G-05 | 1.7b | *should have* / *ought to have* (an obligation that was not met, said afterwards) | Ruled: covered by [stance as-of](../grammar/relations.md#stance-as-of), which already dates sakes; undated *back then* is `thuhum brul` (signed offsets allowed on stance as-of only, D-10): `zehodon thuhum brul thegathem vezebel`. The evidential proposal is rejected (WITNESSED cannot report an ought). Recipe row added in say-tense.md § must / should; the non-fulfilment is its own sentence. Original note: The ought is `thegathem` (`the` on the unspecified-sake root, sakes.md § prescription), which the doc defines as **deontic force, not a report that the act is happening**. It has no tense. `thevom` before it does parse (`zehodon thevom vezebel thegathem`), but no page says that a channel dates the ought, and WITNESSED says *I observed it*, which an ought cannot be. So that stack is a guess, not a route. Other routes checked: `thumem` (*required by a person*) has the same problem; `thavom` (NOTIONAL) with `thanathel` says *imagine it served*, not *it was owed*; `vohum` (*wish*) says *I wish you had* but drops the obligation and can't take `thevom` inside without claiming the telling happened. Safe route: two sentences, *you did not say* (`zehodon thevom vezebel vul.`) then *saying ought to happen* (`zehodon thegathem vezebel.`), which loses that the ought belonged to the past. | covered | sakes.md (prescription), knowing.md (channels) | — | P1 |
| G-06 | 1.11b | *by the way* in front of a question | Resolved: new sentence linker `xavazel` *by the way* (🍟 `avaze` *accessory*: a side dish), dependents.md § sentence linkers; `or …` returns to the main line. Original note: An aside must keep the outer sentence's speech act, and it packages a clause as a stance word, so a bare *by the way* with no clause inside cannot open a question. Route: `yol xevavel …`, which gives *so, …* rather than *by the way*. | done | spans.md (asides), say-questions.md | — | P1 |
| G-07 | 2.6b | *ask someone to do X* (a reported request) | Not a gap. The to-clause stand-in **`derl`** (dependents.md § *to …*) takes the person told as unhosted `/b/`: `yem zehodon bahaben vezebel derl vaen homal belagam gremol` is *please tell Ahaben to confirm by Thursday*. My first pass used two sentences because I missed it. Phrases tried: *ask her to*, *tell her to*, *request*. | covered | dependents.md | Optional recipe row in say-questions.md (*ask / tell X to Y*). | P1 |
| G-08 | 3.2b | *as ADJ as possible* / *as … as you can* | No taught form ranks against what can be done. Route: *so that* plus a superlative, `yel vanavar hogom barl zozazor zel gamazal` (*cut them so the pieces are the smallest*). The superlative ranks against an unstated group, not against what the cook can manage. `thudul` (*keeps trying*) adds effort but no degree, and the ability `xa` is a verb form, not a bar. Phrases tried: *as possible*, *as much as*, *best*, *smallest*, *most*, *try*, *ability*. | awkward | comparatives.md (superlatives, sake benchmarks) | A named bar for *what can be done*, used like the sake bars (*X ties the possible on ADJ* = *as ADJ as possible*); or a recipe row that teaches the *so that* + superlative route. | P1 |
| G-09 | 3.4b, 3.8b, 3.8c | *if X, do Y* (a condition on a command) | `yel vadahem dubem thoyem barl …` parses, but dependents.md defines *if* as the speaker's stance on why **the claim** holds, and a command makes no claim. Only the time poles are said to work under a command (knowing.md § dated channel). No page shows a condition on `yel` / `yem`. Phrases tried: *if*, *when*, *unless*, *in case*. | by design? | dependents.md (dependent clauses), speech-moves.md | Rule that a `/th/` condition pole on a command limits the instruction to that case (*do Y in case X*), and add one example to dependents.md. | P1 |

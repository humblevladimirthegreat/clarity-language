# Proposal: Hook prefixes (*anti-*, *pro-*, *trans-*)

**Status:** PROPOSED. Nothing here is implemented. It needs the role-letter + vowel + **`x`** + stem shape that role compounds use today, which `role-consonant-compounds.md` moves to role letter + **`w`**. The two land in the same change, so no page ever reads a hook prefix as a role compound.  
**Related:** [hook compounds](../grammar/hooks.md#hook-compounds), [extra-noun hooks](../grammar/hooks.md#extra-noun), [stacked hook vowels](../grammar/hooks.md#extra-noun-intermediate), [compounds](../grammar/compounds.md), [left and right of `x`](../grammar/compounds.md#families-by-shape).

## Problem

English builds many words from a relation plus a thing: *anti-war*, *pro-choice*, *indoor*, *transoceanic*, *homeward*, *water-powered*. Agebon has these relations as extra-noun hooks (**`uel`** *against*, **`el`** *for*, **`al`** *in*, **`uol`** *through*), but it fuses a hook only as a suffix, onto a citation, and the result is a verb ([hook compounds](../grammar/hooks.md#hook-compounds): `owurelul` *leave*, `owureluel` *oppose*). To describe a thing by its relation to something else, a speaker has to write a hook and a `/b/` after the noun (`behaham om buwon` *the village near me*). There is no single word for *anti-fighting*, and so no dictionary of such words.

## The shape

Role letter + hook vowel(s) + **`x`** + stem + ending.

> `zuzozen guexavuvul.`
>
> z-Uzozen | g-against-x-fight
>
> "Uzozen is anti-fighting."

The hook vowels name the relation, mid-word **`x`** joins them to the stem, and the stem is the hook's landmark. The word names something that stands in that relation **to** the stem. **`x`** is the compound seam (**x** ≈ plus), and English writes these words with a hyphen too: *anti-war*, *pro-choice*.

This mirrors the existing hook compound:

| | Hook compound (exists) | Hook prefix (new) |
|-|------------------------|-------------------|
| Order | stem, then hook | hook, then stem |
| Shape | citation + hook: `owurelul` | role letter + hook + **`x`** + stem: `guexavuvul` |
| The stem is | the event | the landmark |
| The word names | the event, done in that relation to a `/d/` | something in that relation to the stem |
| English | *leave*, *oppose*, *enter* | *anti-fighting*, *indoor*, *homeward* |

## The ten prefixes

Each hook in the extra-noun grid gives one prefix. The vowels and cues are the hooks' own.

The literal column uses a stem's concrete sense (**-l**) and a place or physical contact. The metaphorical column uses an abstract sense (**-m**): a state, a policy, a debt.

| Hook | Prefix | Reading | Literal | Metaphorical |
|------|--------|---------|---------|--------------|
| **`uel`** *against* | **`uex`** | opposed to the stem | `guexohalal` *sea-facing* (a seawall) | `guexonam` *anti-policy* |
| **`el`** *for* | **`ex`** | in favor of, meant for | `gexahehal` *for the home*, household | `gexonam` *pro-policy* |
| **`al`** *in* | **`ax`** | inside the stem | `gaxahehal` *indoor* | `gaxobowum` *mid-crisis*, in crisis mode |
| **`aol`** *on* | **`aox`** | on the stem's surface | `gaoxohalal` *seagoing*, on the ocean | `gaoxonam` *on-policy*, following the policy |
| **`ol`** *at* | **`ox`** | located at | `goxahehal` *at-home* | `goxabevam` *at issue* |
| **`oel`** *toward* | **`oex`** | heading to | `goexahehal` *homeward* | `goexuzebum` *habit-forming* |
| **`ul`** *from* | **`ux`** | starting from, sourced at | `guxahehal` *from home*, home-sourced | `guxuzobam` *heritage-derived* |
| **`ual`** *out of* | **`uax`** | coming or made out of | `guaxowulal` *out of the water* | `guaxaram` *debt-free*, out of debt |
| **`uol`** *through* | **`uox`** | across, by way of | `guoxohalal` *transoceanic* | `guoxobowum` *crisis-spanning*, lasting through the crisis |
| **`ael`** *using* | **`aex`** | running on, by means of | `gaexowulal` *water-powered* | `gaexugarom` *tradition-based* |

The metaphorical readings follow the hook's own extended uses: *in* a state, *on* a plan, *at* issue, *toward* a tendency, *out of* a condition. Each one keeps the same picture as the literal reading, so a learner who knows the hook can read a new prefix word without a dictionary.

The stem keeps its own sense choice: **-l** the concrete sense, **-m** the abstract. `guexuduvul` is *anti-dueling*, and `guexuduvum` is *anti-conflict*. A stem may be any root, including a three-letter one, or an **`x`** compound: `guexahehaxowurel` is *anti-house-walking*.

## Role letters

The role letter is the word's slot, as on any content word.

| Role letter | Reading | Example | English |
|-------------|---------|---------|---------|
| `/ɡ/` | a property: in that relation to the stem | `gexegobol` | *pro-body*, body-positive |
| `/z/` `/d/` `/b/` | someone or something in that relation | `zuexavuvul` | *an opponent of fighting* |
| `/h/` | the clause stands in that relation | `hoexahehal` | *homeward* (of the movement) |

Scope works as on any word, after the stem with the **`th`** seam: `zuexavuvuthel` is *a committed opponent of fighting* (the practiced reach), and `zuexavuvuthal` is *against fighting this time*. **-r** resumes the whole word, as usual.

The `/h/` reading says the same as the free hook and its `/b/` on the clause (`oel bahehal` *toward the house*). The fused form is the kind (*homeward*), and the free form names the landmark. Both are allowed.

## Compare with the free hook

`zumon uel buduvul` and `zuexavuvul` overlap. The free phrase puts a landmark in play that later words can point back to (`buduvur`), and it takes adjectives on the landmark. The prefix makes one word that names a kind: it can enter the dictionary, take scope, and sit in any slot. Use the prefix for *an anti-war person* as a category, and the phrase for *someone against that duel*.

## The -m frames

The prefix spells only the hook's vowels, not its ending, so it carries the **-l** hook's reading. The **-m** frames (**`uem`** *contrary to*, **`am`** *amid*, **`om`** *near*, **`um`** *away from*) have no prefix. The **-l** prefix carries the general sense, so **`uex`** covers *contra-* as well as *anti-*. If *inter-* (*amid*) or *para-* (*near*) is needed later, it needs its own spelling.

## Spelling and parsing

- **Left of `x`:** one hook vowel or one hook pair. A full root is at least three letters and alternates consonant and vowel, so the [left and right of `x`](../grammar/compounds.md#families-by-shape) test that tells role compounds from ordinary compounds today tells hook prefixes from them after the move.
- **Right of `x`:** a stem of at least three letters. A single vowel and an ending (role letter + vowel + **`x`** + vowel + ending, today's pointer shape) is not a hook prefix; it stays unclaimed, as `role-consonant-compounds.md` lists it.
- **Vowel pairs that are not hooks** (**`ea`**, **`eo`**, **`eu`**, **`oa`**, **`ou`**, **`au`**, and so on) before **`x`** have no reading. List them in `unassigned-reserved.md`.
- **Roots:** no root contains **`x`**, so the seam is never inside a root.
- **Hosted:** a hook prefix in a `bl-` slot is `bluexavuvul`.
- **Syllables:** **`x`** opens the stem's first syllable (`gue-xa-vu-vul`), as in any **`x`** compound. No consonant closes a syllable mid-word.
- **Same spellings as today's role compounds:** `zaxuduyel` is *someone who teaches* today and would be *in-teaching* here, and `daexarayel` (*a writing tool*) would be *write-powered*. Every role compound on the grammar pages must be respelled to the **`w`** shape in the same change, before any hook prefix is added. A retie pass that misses one leaves a well-formed word with a new meaning, so `npm run build` and the parser checks are the only safety net; check the doc examples by hand.

## Derivation in the dictionary

Like hook compounds, common hook prefixes become dictionary words, listed as citations with no role letter (`uexavuvul`). A dictionary entry may narrow the sense, as long as the hook still reads plainly.

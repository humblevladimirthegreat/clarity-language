# Lesson 6: Allowed, required, agreed {#allowed-required-agreed}

## The gap {#gap}

English *can*, *may*, *should*, and *OK* blur who allowed something, and whether the person it affects agreed or was only assumed to. Agazan has a word for each, and its ending says where the yes or no comes from.

## The forms {#forms}

| Drop-in | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| `thegol` / `thegom` / `thegor` | allowed by a rule / by a person / tolerated |
| `thedel` / `thedem` / `theder` | forbidden by a rule / by a person / frowned on |
| `thumel` / `thumem` / `thumer` | required by a rule / by a person / by custom |
| `thuxegol` / `thuxegom` / `thuxegor` | the affected person agreed in advance / said yes (and can take it back) / is assumed to be OK |

The endings have a new job here: the **source**. Cue: **l ≈ law, m ≈ mouth, r ≈ read the room.** **-l** is a rule, **-m** is a person's word (the default), and **-r** is no one's word at all.

On the consent words, **-r** is allowed but visibly weaker: it puts *I assumed* into the sentence instead of hiding it.

`x` sounds like the *s* in *vision*.

## Examples {#examples}

> I borrowed your car `thuxegor`. I should have asked for `thuxegom`.  
> You can leave early `thegom`.  
> We wear ties `thumer`, not `thumel`.  
> Visitors sign in at the desk `thumel`.  
> No phones at dinner `thedem`.

*We wear ties `thumer`, not `thumel`* says it's custom, not a rule.

## Practice {#practice}

Add the drop-in that fits the note.

**1.** *You can park here.* (the rules allow it)

**2.** *I posted the photo of us.* (you said yes)

::: details Show answer

:::

**3.** *People bring a dish.* (custom expects it)

:::

**4.** *Talking during the film.* (frowned on)

::: details Show answer

:::

::: tip Full Agazan
In a full sentence, you can name who gave the yes.

> `zazawan vezebel thuxegom bahaben.`
>
> z-Azawan | v-tell | [th-CONSENT-given | b-Ahaben]
>
> "Azawan tells, and Ahaben said yes to it."
:::

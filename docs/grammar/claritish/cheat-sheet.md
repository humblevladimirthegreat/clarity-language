# Claritish cheat sheet {#cheat-sheet}

## Vowels and x {#sounds}

| Letter | Like | Letter | Like |
|--------|------|--------|------|
| `u` | *you*, without the *y* | `a` | *spa* |
| `o` | *Cambodia*, no glide | `e` | *bet* |
| `x` | the *si* in *vision* (not English *x*) | | |

Each vowel always sounds the same; two vowels in a row are two beats. All other letters sound like English.

| intuition: a gut sense | `thabol` | `thabom` | `thabor` |

## [Sakes](thanks-and-sorry.md) {#sakes}

| Sake, need | Sake, need | Sake, need |
|------------|------------|------------|
| `una` autonomy | `ure` purpose | `odo` pleasure |
| `uwa` competence | `awo` relatedness | `oba` physical: health, rest, safety |
| `owo` understanding | `ewa` beneficence | `uda` a sake, unspecified |

Sake word: `th` + sake + stance + ending.

| Stance | Says | Example | **-l** | **-m** | **-r** |
|--------|------|---------|--------|--------|--------|
| `tha` met | [thanks](thanks-and-sorry.md) | `thawotham` | it will stay with me | thank you | got me through right now |
| `thu` unmet | [sorry](thanks-and-sorry.md), with `blawan` for *yours* | `thawothum`, `thawothum blawan` | deeply sorry | I'm sorry | my bad |
| `the` prescription | [ought to be done](oughts-and-motives.md) for it | `thawothem` | they invited it | your own offer | worth a try |
| `tho` motive | [being done](oughts-and-motives.md) for it | `thawothom` | over time | unstated | right away |

Thanks for a thing you own: `g` for `th`, after the noun: *my coat* `gobathal`.

## [Feelings](feelings.md) {#feelings}

`thuwathuraer` *can I measure up?* hanging over everything, in waves:

| `thuwa` | `thu` | `r` | `ae` | `r` |
|---------|-------|-----|------|-----|
| competence | unmet | for now | over the room (locus) | surging (motion) |

Motion ending: **-l** still, **-m** flowing, **-r** surging.

| Locus | Meaning | Locus | Meaning |
|-------|---------|-------|---------|
| `a` | mine, held inside | `uo` | pushing back |
| `ae` | the atmosphere of the situation | `o` | felt on someone's behalf |
| `ue` | can't place where it comes from | `u` | moving off: avoiding, hiding |
| `e` | aimed at someone | `ua` | out of fight: giving up |
| `oe` | turning to someone for comfort | `ao` | using yourself to placate |

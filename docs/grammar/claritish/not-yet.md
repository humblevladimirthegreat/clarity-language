# Not yet {#not-yet}

## The gap {#gap}

*I can't do it* and *it didn't work* close the book on something you are in the middle of and expect to change. English has *not yet*, but it sounds like a stall, and it never says whether the change is actually coming or whether you have stopped believing in it. Four Agazan words compare where things stand **now** against a change that is expected: already, still, not yet, and no longer.

## The forms {#forms}

| Drop-in | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| `hoham` | already: the change has come |
| `hagem` | still: the state goes on |
| `huzem` | not yet: the change is expected, and it hasn't come |
| `hewem` | no longer: the state has stopped |

The drop-in goes where an English adverb or tag would go, and it covers the clause it sits in.

The endings are the settled-to-passing scale again: **-l** for a stage that is not expected to turn back, **-m** when it could go either way or you don't know, **-r** for a stage that holds only for now. So `huzel` is *not yet, but it's coming*, `huzer` is *not yet, and maybe never*, `hohal` is *done, and it will stay done*, `hagel` is *still, with no end in sight*, and `hewel` is *never again*.

None of the four is a tense. They say nothing about when anything happened; `huzem` places you next to a change you are still expecting.

Leave `huzem` off when you are not expecting the change. A plain *no* says that, and *not yet* about something you do not want is a promise you did not mean to make.

Trading *I can't* for `huzem` is the whole point of the word: it keeps a goal open while it is still in progress, and it costs you nothing you were entitled to claim.

## Examples {#examples}

> The deposit cleared `huzem`.  
> I'm paying off the car `hagel`.  
> She's taking my calls `hewer`.  
> The draft's in review `hoham`.  
> I'm running the 10k `huzer`.

*The deposit cleared `huzem`* says the money is coming and hasn't landed, with no complaint attached. *She's taking my calls `hewer`* marks the silence as this week's, not forever, which is a different thing to sit with than *she won't talk to me*. *I'm running the 10k `huzer`* is honest in the other direction: still on the list, and you are no longer sure it will happen.

# Decisions and tries {#decisions-and-tries}

## The gap {#gap}

*I've decided* sounds final, and *I'll try* sounds like a hedge. Neither one says how settled you are. In Agazan, the ending on the decision or try word says it.

## The forms {#forms}

| Drop-in | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| `thehul` / `thehum` / `thehur` | decided for good / decided, can still change / decided for now |
| `thudul` / `thudum` / `thudur` | committed to trying / tries / a trial run |

The endings are back on the settled-to-passing scale: **-l** for good, **-m** the default, **-r** right now only.

A trial run (`thudur`) plans for failure: if it doesn't work, finding that out was the point. Use it to lower the stakes of starting something, for yourself or for someone else.

## Examples {#examples}

> I'm going vegetarian `thudur`.  
> We're moving to Denver `thehul`.  
> I'm taking the job `thehum`.  
> I'm learning piano `thudul`.  
> We're skipping the party `thehur`.

*I'm learning piano `thudul`* says you'll keep at it until it works or is ruled out.

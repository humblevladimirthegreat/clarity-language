# Journal editor {#editor}

Write a journal entry here, or paste one in. The editor puts a dotted line under English that a lesson's gap is about: the *I think* that hides a guess, the *I heard* that passes on a rumor, the *thanks* that doesn't say what it did for you. Open a dotted phrase to see the habit in one line, answer a question or two, and add the drop-in that fits. Or leave the English as it is: that is always a fine answer.

It marks cues from every lesson. Turn a lesson off above the editor until you have read it.

To add a drop-in with no English cue, type <kbd>/</kbd> and pick one: <kbd>/sure</kbd>, <kbd>/know</kbd>, <kbd>/label</kbd>, <kbd>/thanks</kbd>, <kbd>/feel</kbd>, <kbd>/tone</kbd>, and the rest. <kbd>/feel</kbd> builds a feeling word part by part: the need, met or unmet, the emotion locus (where the feeling sits), and the emotion motion (how it moves). Hover a drop-in you have written to see what it means; a word that is one letter away from a drop-in gets a grey dashed line and a suggestion. The panel beside the text counts the phrases still in plain English, as a mirror, not a score. *Practice a lesson* loads a lesson's practice sentences to try your drop-ins against the lesson's.

The editor is for writing to yourself. What you write stays in this browser and is never sent anywhere.

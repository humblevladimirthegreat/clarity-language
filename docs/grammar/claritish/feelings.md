# Feelings in three parts {#feelings}

## The gap {#gap}

One English emotion word (*anxious*, *resentful*, *proud*) hides three things: which need is at stake, where the feeling sits or what it points at, and how it moves. A single label can be a judgment in disguise. Ageben names the three parts in one word.

## The forms {#forms}

Start from a thanks or sorry word from Thanks and sorry: `th` + sake + `tha` (met) or `thu` (unmet) + its ending. Keep that whole word, then add a **locus** and a **motion ending**. Put it after the English clause the feeling is about, like any other drop-in. On its own, with no clause, it says how you feel right now.

The word now has two **-l / -m / -r** slots: the ending from Thanks and sorry stays in the middle, and the motion ending goes last.

| `thuwa` | `thu` | `r` | `ao` | `r` |
|---------|-------|-----|------|-----|
| competence | unmet | for now | over the room (locus) | surging (motion) |

Pick a locus from this menu. (This is in a cheat sheet at the end)

| Locus | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `a` | mine, held inside |
| `ae` | the atmosphere of the situation |
| `ue` | I can't place where it comes from |
| `e` | aimed at someone |
| `oe` | turning to someone for comfort |
| `uo` | pushing back |
| `o` | felt on someone's behalf |
| `u` | moving off (avoiding, hiding) |
| `ua` | out of fight: giving up |
| `ao` | using yourself to placate |

| Motion | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **-l** | still, muted, numb |
| **-m** | flowing, steady |
| **-r** | surging, in waves |

The motion endings are the settled-to-passing scale applied to water: still water is lasting (**-l**), a current is the default (**-m**), and a wave is right now (**-r**). Motion is not strength. Strong but steady anger is flowing.

## Examples {#examples}

> We shipped the draft `thuwathamar`.  
> The review is tomorrow `thuwathuraer`.  
> He rewrote my memo `thunathumuom`.  
> My sister got laid off `thunathulom`.  
> `thuwathuruel`, and I can't say why.

| Drop-in | What it says | Instead of | The three parts |
|---------|--------------|------------|-----------------|
| `thuwathamar` | a rush of *I pulled it off*, kept to myself | *proud* | competence met; held inside; surging |
| `thuwathuraer` | *can I measure up?* hanging over everything, in waves, for now | *anxious* | competence at stake for now; over the room; surging |
| `thunathumuom` | a steady push back at losing my say, while it can still change | *objecting* | autonomy unmet; pushing back; flowing |
| `thunathulom` | a steady ache on her behalf: her say was taken for good | *upset for her* | autonomy unmet for good; on her behalf; flowing |
| `thuwathuruel` | a quiet *can I handle this?* from nowhere I can point to | *uneasy* | competence at stake for now; can't place it; still |

# Leaving out who acts {#leaving-out-who-acts}

### Leaving out who acts {#no-subject}

English uses the passive (*The cat was seen*, *Mistakes were made*) to avoid naming who acted. Ageben simply leaves out the `/z/` word. The verb and its object stay the same, and the sentence says nothing about who did it.

> `dazozon vayebel.`
>
> d-Azozon | v-see
>
> "Azozon was seen."

#### English → Ageben {#beginner-english-to-ageben}

**1.** *The window was opened.*

::: details Show answer
`duwohel vohel.`

d-window | v-open

**Also correct:** `vohel duwohel.`
:::

**2.** *Ahelan was paid hastily.*

::: details Show answer
`hadurom varal bahelan.`

h-haste | v-pay | b-Ahelan

**Also correct:** `varal bahelan hadurom.`, `bahelan hadurom varal.`
:::

**3.** *The candy was thrown very hastily.*

::: details Show answer
`dunedal welazem hadurom vobovol.`

d-candy | [w-very | h-haste] | v-throw

**Also correct:** `dunedal vobovol welazem hadurom.`
:::

**4.** *I doubt the jar was found.*

::: details Show answer
`dadal thevemem vumewel.`

d-jar | th-doubt | v-find

**Also correct:** `thevemem dadal vumewel.`
:::

#### Ageben → English {#beginner-ageben-to-english}

**1.** `dazozon vumewel.`

::: details Show answer
d-Azozon | v-find

*Azozon was found.*
:::

**2.** <!-- lint: moved -->`vobovol dadal.`

::: details Show answer
v-throw | d-jar

*A jar was thrown.*
:::

**3.** `zahelan dunedal vobovol.`

::: details Show answer
z-Ahelan | d-candy | v-throw

*Ahelan throws the candy.*
:::

**4.** `varal bazozon gonumul.`

::: details Show answer
v-pay | [b-Azozon | g-angry]

*An angry Azozon was paid.*
:::

#### Pick one {#beginner-pick-one}

**1.** *The candy was found.* `dunedal vumewel.` or `zunedal vumewel.`

::: details Show answer
`dunedal vumewel.`

d-candy | v-find

What was found stays `/d/`. With `/z/`, the candy would be the one finding.
:::

#### Fix it {#beginner-fix-it}

**1.** *Alazun was paid.* <!-- lint: error -->`zalazun varal.`

::: details Show answer
`varal balazun.`

v-pay | b-Alazun

The one paid stays plain `/b/`, as in an active sentence. `zalazun` makes Alazun the one who pays.
:::

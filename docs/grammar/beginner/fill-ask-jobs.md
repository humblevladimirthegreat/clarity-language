# Fill-ask jobs {#what-kind-when-where-whose-how-why}

### What kind? {#what-kind}

To ask what something is **like** (*What color is it?*, *What kind of dog?*), put the blank on an adjective: **`gar`**. Answer with the adjective as a [citation](word-endings.md#citation-forms).

> `yel gar zodozel.`
>
> y-question | g-wh | z-dog
>
> "What is the dog like?"

> `gelazem.`
>
> "Big."

| Agebon | English |
<!-- cheat-sheet: talking -->
|--------|---------|
| **`gar`** | *What is it like?* / *What kind?* |
| **`ger`** | *Like anything in particular?* |
| **`gur`** | *What else is it like?* |

**Compare with:** *like what?* is the similative blank, **`hewem blar`**: it asks for a model to resemble, not a property.

### When? {#when}

To ask *when?* / *in what case?*, put the blank **`har`** in the `/h/` slot (or **`war`** on `/w/`).

> `yel har zahelan vowurol.`
>
> y-question | h-when | z-Ahelan | v-walk
>
> "When does Ahelan walk?"

| Agebon | English (statement, bare) | English (under question, bare) |
|--------|---------------------------|--------------------------------|
| **`har`** / **`war`** | *sometimes* | *When?* / *In what case?* |

### Where? {#where}

To ask *where?*, write an [extra-noun hook](hooks.md#extra-noun) (a short word like *at*, *in*, or *from* that brings in a `/b/` noun), then the blank **`bar`** in the `/b/` slot after it. The hook picks which *where* you mean.

> `yel el bar zahelan vowurol.`
>
> y-question | [at | b-wh] | z-Ahelan | v-walk
>
> "Where does Ahelan walk?"

> `yel al bar zodozel vezuvel.`
>
> y-question | [in | b-wh] | z-dog | v-sleep
>
> "What does the dog sleep in?"

| Agebon | English |
<!-- cheat-sheet: talking -->
|--------|---------|
| **`el bar`** | *Where (at)?* |
| **`al bar`** | *In what?* |
| **`ul bar`** | *Where from?* |
| **`ol bar`** | *For whom?* |
| **`el ber`** | *Anywhere?* |
| **`el bur`** | *Where else?* |

Answer with the hook and the landmark, as a [citation](word-endings.md#citation-forms): `el bohehal.` ("At the house.").

**Compare with:** *when?* is **`har`** on `/h/`. Occasions are times or cases, so a place never goes there.

### Whose? {#whose}

To ask *whose?*, write the hook **`om`** (*used by*) after the thing, then the blank **`bar`**: the same shape as *where?*. Because **`om`** says the `/b/` noun uses the thing, the question asks *who uses it?*

> `yel zodozel om bar vowurol.`
>
> y-question | [z-dog | [used-by | b-wh]] | v-walk
>
> "Whose dog walks?"

Answer with the hook and the user, as a [citation](word-endings.md#citation-forms): `om bazozon.` ("Azozon's.").

### How? {#how}

To ask *how?*, use the same shape as *where?*: a word that says how the answer relates to the event, then the blank **`blar`**. For manner (*in what way?*), that word is [**`hewem`**](relations.md) *like*, so the question asks *like what?*

> `yel hewem blar zazozon vowurol.`
>
> y-question | [h-like | bl-wh] | z-Azozon | v-walk
>
> "How does Azozon walk?" (*like what?*)

To ask about means (*with what?*), use the *using* hook with the blank: **`aol bar`**.

### Why? {#why}

To ask *why?*, write a [condition word](causation.md) (*because*, *so that*, *in case*), then the blank **`blar`**. Pick the word for the kind of reason you want.

> `yel thahum blar zazozon vowurol.`
>
> y-question | [th-because | bl-wh] | z-Azozon | v-walk
>
> "Why does Azozon walk?" (*because of what?*)

> `yel hovam blar zazozon vowurol.`
>
> y-question | [h-so-that | bl-wh] | z-Azozon | v-walk
>
> "What does Azozon walk for?"

| Agebon | English |
<!-- cheat-sheet: talking -->
|--------|---------|
| **`thahum blar`** | *Why?* (*because of what?*) |
| **`hovam blar`** | *What for?* (*for what purpose?*) |
| **`thohem blar`** | *Under what condition?* / *In what case?* |

# Fill-ask jobs {#what-kind-when-where-whose-how-why}

To ask *what kind*, *when*, *where*, *whose*, *how*, or *why*, you put the blank in the role that fits the question. For *what kind* the blank is an adjective (`gar`), and for *when* it sits in the role that names an occasion (`har`). For *where* and *whose* it is a noun after a short word: *at* or *in* for a place, *used by* for whose. For *how* and *why* it follows a word that names the kind of answer, *like* for manner or a condition word such as *because* for a reason, and that word picks which answer you mean.

### What kind? {#what-kind}

To ask what something is **like** (*What color is it?*, *What kind of dog?*), put the blank on an adjective: **`gar`**. Answer with the adjective alone. It keeps its letter: only a [one-noun answer](questions.md#fill-ask) drops it.

> `yel gar zodozel.`
>
> y-question | g-wh | z-dog
>
> "What is the dog like?"

> `gelazem.`
>
> "Big."

<!-- cheat-sheet: talking -->
| Ageben | English |
|--------|---------|
| **`gar`** | *What is it like?* / *What kind?* |
| **`ger`** | *Like anything in particular?* |
| **`gur`** | *What else is it like?* |

**Compare with:** *like what?* is the likeness blank, `hewem blar`: it asks for a model to resemble, not a property.

### When? {#when}

To ask *when?* or *in what case?*, put the blank `har` on `/h/`, the role that names an occasion. The answer names the time or the case. To ask when one adjective holds, put `war` right before that adjective instead, as an [`/w/` restrictor](restrictors.md#on-the-following-adjective-w) does.

> `yel har zahelan vowurol.`
>
> y-question | h-when | z-Ahelan | v-walk
>
> "When does Ahelan walk?"

| Ageben | English (statement, bare) | English (under question, bare) |
|--------|---------------------------|--------------------------------|
| **`har`** / **`war`** | *sometimes* | *When?* / *In what case?* |

### Where? {#where}

To ask *where?*, write an [extra-noun hook](hooks.md#extra-noun) (a short word like *at*, *in*, or *from* that brings in a `/b/` noun), then the blank `bar` on `/b/` after it. The hook picks which *where* you mean.

> `yel el bar zahelan vowurol.`
>
> y-question | [at | b-wh] | z-Ahelan | v-walk
>
> "Where does Ahelan walk?"

> `yel al bar zodozel vezuvel.`
>
> y-question | [in | b-wh] | z-dog | v-sleep
>
> "What does the dog sleep in?"

<!-- cheat-sheet: talking -->
| Ageben | English |
|--------|---------|
| **`el bar`** | *Where at?* |
| **`al bar`** | *In what?* |
| **`ul bar`** | *Where from?* |
| **`ol bar`** | *For whom?* |
| **`el ber`** | *Anywhere?* |
| **`el bur`** | *Where else?* |

Answer with the hook and the landmark, keeping the `/b/` letter: `el bohehal.` ("At the house.").

**Compare with:** *when?* is `har` on `/h/`. Occasions are times or cases, so a place never goes there.

### Whose? {#whose}

To ask *whose?*, write the hook `om` (*used by*) after the thing, then the blank `bar`: the same shape as *where?*. Because `om` says the `/b/` noun uses the thing, the question asks *who uses it?*

> `yel zodozel om bar vowurol.`
>
> y-question | [z-dog | [used-by | b-wh]] | v-walk
>
> "Whose dog walks?"

Answer with the hook and the user, keeping the `/b/` letter: `om bazozon.` ("Azozon's.").

### How? {#how}

To ask *how?*, use the same shape as *where?*: a word that says how the answer relates to the event, then the blank `blar`. For manner (*in what way?*), that word is [`hewem`](relations.md) *like*, so the question asks *like what?*

> `yel hewem blar zazozon vowurol.`
>
> y-question | [h-like | bl-wh] | z-Azozon | v-walk
>
> "How does Azozon walk?" (*like what?*)

To ask about means (*with what?*), use the *using* hook with the blank: `aol bar`.

### Why? {#why}

To ask *why?*, write a [condition word](causation.md) (*because*, *so that*, *in case*), then the blank `blar`. Pick the word for the kind of reason you want.

> `yel thahum blar zazozon vowurol.`
>
> y-question | [th-because | bl-wh] | z-Azozon | v-walk
>
> "Why does Azozon walk?" (*because of what?*)

*What for?* asks about an aim, and in a question the aim is the listener's. So ask the one who acts:

> `yel thovam blar zawan vowurol.`
>
> y-question | [th-so-that | bl-wh] | z-listener | v-walk
>
> "What do you walk for?"

<!-- cheat-sheet: talking -->
| Ageben | English |
|--------|---------|
| **`thahum blar`** | *Why?* (*because of what?*) |
| **`thovam blar`** | *What for?* (*what is your aim?*) |
| **`thohem blar`** | *Under what condition?* / *In what case?* |

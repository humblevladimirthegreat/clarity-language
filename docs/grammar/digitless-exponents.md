# Digitless and exponents {#digitless-exponents}

English *how many*, *thousands*, *27 million*, and *infinitely many* name an amount without listing every unit, or they name how big a number is. Everyday *three cats* and *the second book* stay in [numbers](numbers.md).

## Intermediate {#intermediate}

### Digitless {#digitless}
<a id="digitless-numbers"></a>
<a id="bare-marker"></a>

Beginner already used **`gral`** for *more than one*. Any marker may drop every digit group: **role letter + marker + ending** only. The marker still says what kind of number this is. With **-l** / **-m** / **-n**, the amount, rank, or label is **unspecified**. With **-r**, the word is a blank, like a standalone join **-r**: *some number* in a statement, *which number?* in a [fill-ask](questions.md#fill-ask-r).

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->
| Agebon | Use | English | Cue |
|--------|---------|---------|-----|
| **`ra`** (`…+`) | unspecified **positive scalar**, specialized to **plural count / amount `>1`** | complements `…+0` (zero), `…+1` (one), `…+N` (exact N≥2) | **`a`** ≈ add |
| **`ru`** (`…-`) | unspecified **negative scalar** | *some negative amount*; exact negatives keep digits (`…-N`) | **`u`** ≈ undo |
| **`re`** (`…#`) | unspecified **rank from the start** | *some nth* / *some place*; exact ranks keep digits (`…#N`) | **`e`** ≈ order |
| **`ro`** (`…_`) | unspecified **digit-string / label** | *some code*; *a label*; exact labels keep digits (`…_…`) | **`o`** ≈ one |

**Compare with:** noun [**-x**](plurality.md) names a **group of things**. Digitless **`ra`** names a **count or amount above one** without saying how many. Both may sit on the same noun (`zagozolx gral`).

On a [hosted](clause.md#extra-nouns) offset right after a channel, digitless **`blral`** is *later* and **`blrul`** is *earlier*, with no amount claimed. A hosted number keeps its `bl` in writing too (`bl+` for `blral`), and its `l` is a [beat of its own](phonology.md#syllabic-l).

<a id="a-few"></a>

Open **`gram`** is a soft count: *a few* / *some*. It says there is more than one without claiming a number. Closed **`gral`** still means *more than one*.

> `zagozolx gram vuhohol.`
> [z-cat-x | g-more-than-one.about] | v-sit
>
> "A few cats sit."

<a id="mass-some"></a>

A mass noun such as *tea* is not counted, so *more than one* makes no sense for it. On a mass noun, **`gral`** is *some*: an amount that is not zero.

> `zududal gral vuhohol.`
> [z-tea | g-more-than-one] | v-sit
>
> "Some tea sits there."

*Many*, *few*, *enough*, and *too much* compare the amount against a baseline, so they are comparatives, not number words.

Words such as **`zar`** / **`zor`** stand for an unnamed **member of a set**. Digitless number **-r** stands for an unnamed **value** the same way: *some amount*, with no claim that it is more than one. Digitless number **-l** (and **-m** / **-n**) brings in an unnamed amount of that kind, and **`gral`** still says *more than one*.

> `zuzozen drar vawebol.`
> z-Uzozen | d-some-amount | v-see
>
> "Uzozen sees some number of them."

In a question, the same word is the blank: *how many?*.

> `yol drar zuzozen vawebol.`
> y-question | d-how-many | z-Uzozen | v-see
>
> "How many does Uzozen see?"

To repeat a number already given, keep at least one digit (`drager`), as in [number endings](numbers.md#number-endings).

### How many? {#how-many}

To ask for a **number**, write a [number word](numbers.md) with no digits and the **-r** ending (written **`=`** after the role letter). That empty number is the blank, the same way `zar` is the blank for *who?* ([fill-ask](questions.md#fill-ask-r)). The marker says which kind of number you want. (cue: **-r** names this member; the marker vowel says count, place, or code)

> `yol dagozolx grar zuzozen vawebol.`
> y-question | [d-cat-x | g-how-many] | z-Uzozen | v-see
>
> "How many cats does Uzozen see?"

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->
| Agebon | Use | English | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|-----|
| `g=+` (`grar`) | fill a count or amount | *How many?* / *How much?* | **a** ≈ add |
| `g=#` (`grer`) | fill a place in order | *Which place?* (*which floor, which chapter*) | **e** ≈ order |
| `g=_` (`gror`) | fill a code or label | *What number?* (*phone, room*) | **o** ≈ one |

> `yol ol brer zolazun vuhohol.`
> y-question | [at | b-which-place] | z-Olazun | v-sit
>
> "Which place does Olazun sit in?" (first, second, and so on)

Answer with the number as a [citation](word-endings.md#citation-forms) or a full sentence.

**Compare with:** outside a question, the same word is *some number* (`zuzozen drar vawebol.` *Uzozen sees some number of them*). To repeat a number already given, keep a digit: `drager` ([above](#digitless)).

### How big? How fast? {#how-big}

To ask **how much** of a quality, put the same blank on `/w/` immediately before the adjective or adverb: **`wrar`**.

> `yol wrar gelazom zudozol.`
> y-question | [w-how-many | g-big] | z-dog
>
> "How big is the dog?"

> `yol wrar hedurem zudozol varuvel.`
> y-question | [w-how-many | h-haste] | z-dog | v-run
>
> "How fast does the dog run?"

Other role letters take the same blank: the marker with no digits and **-r** (on `/h/`, *how many times?*). Do not confuse that blank with **`har`** (*sometimes*), which is a [restrictor](restrictors.md), not a number.

### Exponents {#exponents}

To write a large or tiny amount in one word (*27 million*, *5.2e-4*, *25%*), put an **exponent** (the power of ten) in the digit group, before the ordinary digits (the **mantissa**).

Speech uses **`wa`** / **`wu`** for that exponent, then **`ya`** if mantissa digits follow. Preferred writing is **`e`** for **`wa`** and **`e-`** for **`wu`**. You never need a separate *times ten to the …* phrase: the size is part of the number word.

> `g+27e12`
>
> 🔊 `grawabadoyadorel`
>
> g-27e12
>
> "27 × 10¹²."

| Marker | Use | English | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|-----|
| **`wa`** | positive exponent, then its digits | *e12* | **`a`** ≈ add |
| **`wu`** | negative exponent, then its digits | *e-6* | **`u`** ≈ undo |
| **`ya`** | after exponent digits when a mantissa follows | (not a digit) | |
| **`ye`** | decimal point | *5.2* | |
| **`yo`** | percent closer | *25%* | |
| **`yu`** | percentage-point closer | *+2 pp* | |

- The exponent comes **before** that group’s mantissa digits.
- If a mantissa follows, say **`ya`** after the last exponent digit, then the mantissa. Example: `27e12` → `wa` + `ba` + `do` + `ya` + `do` + `re`.
- Exponent only, no mantissa: say `wa` / `wu` and the exponent digits, with no **`ya`**. Example: *e9* → `wa` + `na`.
- Ordinary amounts usually omit exponent **`0`**: write `gragel`.
- By default, write the **order of magnitude** (the power of ten) in **engineering notation**: exponents are usually multiples of **3** (e.g. `27e6`).
- **Decimal point:** say **`ye`** after the digit(s) left of the point; digits after **`ye`** are the fractional part.

> `g+5.2`
>
> 🔊 `grazayedol`
>
> g-5.2
>
> "5.2."

### Bare OoM {#bare-oom}
<a id="bare-oom-bands"></a>

English *hundreds* or *thousands* names a range of sizes without a count. To say that, write a **bare order of magnitude** (OoM): exponent digits with **no mantissa**. The word then covers every amount from 10ᴷ up to, but not including, 10ᴷ⁺¹, so `g+e3` is 1000–9999, not every larger amount.

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->
| Form | Band | Gloss |
|------|------|--------|
| **`g+e0`** | `[10⁰, 10¹)` = **1–9** | *ones* (some amount in the ones place) |
| **`g+e1`** | `[10¹, 10²)` = **10–99** | *tens* |
| **`g+e2`** | `[10², 10³)` = **100–999** | *hundreds* |
| **`g+e3`** | `[10³, 10⁴)` = **1000–9999** | *thousands* (`e4` / `e5` are tens/hundreds of thousands) |
| **`g+e6`** | `[10⁶, 10⁷)` | *millions* band only |
| **`g-e3`** | `(−10⁴, −10³]` polarity with **`ru`** | negative thousands band |

These work the same way on `/z/`, `/d/`, and `/b/`. Soft **`grawagem`** ≈ *about in the thousands band*. Exact count inside a band uses a mantissa (`g+4e3` = 4000), not bare OoM.

### Group separator {#group-separator}

When a number has more than one digit group, you say each comma. The spoken comma is **`the`** on every marker. Written text keeps the comma.

> `g+5e3,860`
>
> 🔊 `grawageyazatheluvomol`
>
> g-5e3,860
>
> "5860."

Syllable by syllable: `g` + `ra` + wa ge ya za + **the** + lu vo mo + `l`.

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->
| Agebon | Use | English |
|--------|-----|---------|
| `g+5e3,860` | count | *5860* |
| `g-1e9,265e3,4` | negative count | *−1,000,265,004* |
| `g#2,3` | rank | *2nd within the 3rd* |
| `d_555,123,4567` | code | *555-123-4567* |

The **e** of **`the`** is the vowel of order: the next group comes in order. Say a separator exactly where a comma is written, and nowhere else: `g+860` has none; `g+5e3,860` has one. The sign is said once, at the start of the word.

<a id="nested-rank"></a>

**Nested rank.** On an ordinal, each comma opens a place *within* the place before it, innermost first, as in dates: `g#2,3` is *seat 2 in row 3*; `g#16,4` is *verse 16 of chapter 4*. A large ordinal is one group of plain digits: `g#1005` (*the 1005th*).

> `zozohol g#2,3.`
>
> 🔊 `zozohol gredothegel.`
>
> z-seat | g-#-2,3
>
> "There is seat 2 in row 3."

## Advanced {#advanced}

To say *infinitely many*, *last place*, *a gazillion*, or *absolutely zero*, write the exponent mark **`wa`** or **`wu`** with **no** exponent digits. What the word means depends on what comes before the empty exponent: nothing, nonzero mantissa digits, or the mantissa **`0`** (table). A word has only one such group, and that group takes no percent closer.

| Shape | Reading |
|-------|---------|
| **`wa`/`wu` alone** (no mantissa) | special value: +∞ / −∞ / last place / start place / … (tables below) |
| **Mantissa `≠0` + `wa`/`wu`∅** | hyperbole (*N gazillion* / *umpteenth*) |
| **Mantissa `0` + `wa`/`wu`∅** | zero × digitless exp |

**Writing:** **`e`** = speech **`wa`**; **`e-`** = speech **`wu`**. Digitless means no power digits after that mark. Bare / assert **`e0`** writes power **`0`** (not digitless): [bare OoM](#bare-oom).

The marker still says amount or rank. A code has no magnitude, so it takes no exponent.

### Special values {#special-values}
<a id="extremum-digitless"></a>
<a id="infinity"></a>
<a id="special-numeric-values"></a>

> `zagozolx grawal.`
> z-cat-x | g-plus-infinity
>
> "There are infinitely many cats."

#### Referential (`/ɡ/` `/z/` `/d/` `/b/`) {#special-referential}

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->
| Form | Anatomy | Reading |
|------|---------|---------|
| **`g+e`** (etc.) | **`ra`** + **`wa`∅** + **-l** | **+∞** |
| **`g~+e`** | **`ra`** + **`wa`∅** + **-m** | **arbitrarily large but finite** |
| **`g+e-`** | **`ra`** + **`wu`∅** + **-l** | **arbitrarily small but finite** |
| **`g-e`** | **`ru`** + **`wa`∅** + **-l** | **−∞** |
| **`g~-e`** | **`ru`** + **`wa`∅** + **-m** | **arbitrarily large but finite** (negative / deficit) |
| **`g#e`** | **`re`** + **`wa`∅** + **-l** | **last place** / final-place landmark |
| **`g~#e`** | **`re`** + **`wa`∅** + **-m** | **near last place** |
| **`g#e-`** | **`re`** + **`wu`∅** + **-l** | **start / beginning place** / origin landmark: first with emphasis on **onset** (*the starting one*, *at the beginning*). Numbered 1st is `g#1`. Ultimate-ancestor pole |
| **`g~#e-`** | **`re`** + **`wu`∅** + **-m** | **near first place** |
| **`g-e-`** | **`ru`** + **`wu`∅** + **-l** | **just short:** arbitrarily small but finite shortfall (*a hair under*); against a target, *almost* |
| **`g~-e-`** | **`ru`** + **`wu`∅** + **-m** | **nearly**: a small shortfall, not claimed to be tiny |

Same under `/z/` `/d/` `/b/` (`zrawal` = +∞ as subject; `drewul` = start-place as object; `brawul` = *of an arbitrarily small but finite amount*; `zruwul` = a hair-short amount as subject). Contrast **`grebal`** = numbered *first* / *1st*; **`grewal`** = last-place landmark; **`grewul`** = beginning/onset landmark.

<a id="just-short"></a>

**Just short (`gruwul`):** negative marker (**`ru`**) + digitless negative exponent (**`wu`∅**), no mantissa. It mirrors **`grawul`** (a hair over zero): **`gruwul`** is a hair **under**: the smallest shortfall. Against a target it reads *almost*.

**Compare with:** the same form on each role letter: **`gruwul`** the amount, **`xruwul`** *just before that:*, **`thruwul`** *virtually*, **`vruwul`** *nudge down*, **`hruwul`** *almost*, **`yruwul`** *so close!*. Pretend *as if* is a different thing: the mood **`eyu`** ([Notional](notional-standing.md#notional), e.g. `theyum`).

**Endings** (no-mantissa specials):

| Ending | Sense |
|--------|-------|
| **-l** | Exact special value (default): +∞ / −∞ / last place / start place / arbitrarily small but finite / just short as in the form table |
| **-m** on **`wa`∅** (`…em`) | **Arbitrarily large but finite** on scalars (`g~+e` / `g~-e`); on ordinals (`g~#e`) = **near last place** |
| **-m** on **`wu`∅** (`…e-m`) | Soft / approximate small on scalars (`g~+e-`); *nearly* on **`ru`+`wu`∅** (`g~-e-`); on ordinals (`g~#e-`) = **near first place** |
| **-n** | Conventional designation of that special (named ∞ symbol, official *last place* / *starting* title, …) |
| **-r** | Resume a prior special value of that marker+exp polarity (`g=+e` = *that +∞ again*; `g=+e-` = *that arbitrarily small amount again*; `g=-e-` = *that same hair-short amount again*) |

#### Number as verb and adverb {#special-number-as-verb-and-adverb}

On `/v/` and `/h/`, these special values (∞, last, start, a tiny amount) become actions and adverbs. Soft **-m** softens them the same way it does on `/ɡ/` (*a lot but finite*, *near last*, …).

**Scalars (`ra` / `ru`):**

| Form | Reading |
|------|---------|
| **`v+e`** | **increase without bound** |
| **`v~+e`** | *increase a lot but finite* |
| **`v-e`** | **decrease without bound** |
| **`v~-e`** | *decrease a lot but finite* |
| **`v+e-`** | **nudge up**: increase by an arbitrarily small but finite amount |
| **`v-e-`** | **nudge down**: decrease by an arbitrarily small but finite amount |
| **`v~-e-`** | *ease down a little* |
| **`h+e`** | **unbounded multiplicity** (× without bound / unboundedly many times) |
| **`h~+e`** | *many times but finite* |
| **`h+e-`** | **barely**: the action clears one full time by a hair (`zuzozen h+e- vuhohol.` *Uzozen barely sits*) |
| **`h~+e-`** | *scraped by*: it happened, but only with strain |
| **`h-e-`** | **almost**: the action falls a hair short of one full time (`zuzozen h-e- vuhohol.` *Uzozen almost sits*) |
| **`h~-e-`** | *came close*: it fell short despite a real effort |

**Margin vs effort:** **-l** (`hrawul` / `hruwul`) measures the margin: over or under by a hair. **`~`** (**-m**) reports the felt effort instead: `zuzozen hrawum vuhohol.` is *Uzozen managed to sit, just*; `zuzozen hruwum vuhohol.` is *Uzozen tried hard to sit and didn't quite*. Division into parts keeps its counted form `h-N`.

**On a quality:** the same pair sits on `/w/` before an adjective or adverb. **`wrawul`** = *barely*; **`wruwul`** = *almost*.

> `zudozol wruwul gunemul vowurel.`
> [z-dog | [w---e- | g-angry]] | v-walk
>
> "An almost angry dog walks."

**Stance:** a whole claim that holds all but a hair is stance, not manner, so it sits on `/th/`: **`thruwul`** = *virtually* / *practically*; **`thruwum`** = *more or less*. **`hruwul`** says the event fell short; **`thruwul`** says your claim is a hair short of exact.

**Ordinals (`re`), start/last landmarks:**

| Form | Reading |
|------|---------|
| **`v#e`** | *take / come in last place* |
| **`v~#e`** | *take / come in near last place* |
| **`v#e-`** | *take / come in starting place* |
| **`v~#e-`** | *take / come in near first place* |
| **`h#e`** | *for the last time* |
| **`h~#e`** | *for a near-last time* |
| **`h#e-`** | *for the first time* (onset framing). Numbered **`h#1`** is a counted first. |
| **`h~#e-`** | *for a near-first time* |

Soft, named, and resume endings (**`~`** / **`@`** / **`=`**) work as on the other special values.

#### Number as interjection {#special-number-as-interjection}

On `/y/`, the special values are cheers:

| Form | Reading |
|------|---------|
| **`y+e`** | *To infinity!* |
| **`y~+e`** | *To a huge (but finite) amount!* |
| **`y#e`** | *Finally!* (place/coda cheer). Discourse **`x#e`** is *Finally:* |
| **`y~#e`** | *Almost finally!* / soft coda cheer |
| **`y#e-`** | *(finally, we're) starting!* (onset cheer). Discourse **`x#e-`** is *Starting with:* |
| **`y~#e-`** | soft onset cheer |
| **`y-e-`** | *So close!* / *Almost!* (near-miss cheer) |
| **`y~-e-`** | soft *nearly!* |

#### Number as discourse {#special-number-as-discourse}

**Last point / *Finally*:** **`xrewal`** = *Finally:* / *last point:* (committed coda item). Soft **`xrewam`** = near-final; named **`xrewan`** = ritual / titled *Finally*; **`xrewar`** resumes that last-point marker. Extra items use discourse hook **`al`** (*additionally*: [hooks.md](hooks.md#discourse-hooks)).

**Starting point / *Starting with*:** **`xrewul`** = *Starting with:* / *to begin:* (committed opening item). Soft **`xrewum`** = near-start; named **`xrewun`** = ritual / titled *Starting with*; **`xrewur`** resumes that start-point marker. Numbered *firstly* is **`xrebal`**.

Under `/x/`, a digitful exponent on **`#`** is **outline depth** relative to the focused agenda item: **`x#3e2`** = *3rd sub-sub-point under the focus*; **`xrewubal`** = *parent-layer item(s)*; **`xrewamol`** = *current-layer item(s)*. A flat list uses **`x#N`** (and *Finally* / *Starting with* as above).

These sit beside the numbered points of [Number as discourse marker](numbers.md#number-as-discourse-marker-by-marker). End-relative discourse points use **`x#-N`** ([from the end](numbers.md#from-the-end)). **Just-before discourse:** **`xruwul`** = *just before that:* (step back a moment in the telling). Soft **`xruwum`**; named **`xruwun`**; resume **`xruwur`**. Corroborating is **`xral`**; independent is **`xrul`**.

### Zero × exponent {#zero-exponent}
<a id="zero-exponential"></a>
<a id="annihilate"></a>

To say *absolutely nothing*, *empty at the seed*, or *a residue that will not go away*, pair the **mantissa `0`** with an exponent: a digitless one, or the engineering **`e-1`**. Soft, named, and resume endings (**`~`** / **`@`** / **`=`**) work as on the other special values.

#### Referential (`/ɡ/` `/z/` `/d/` `/b/`) {#zero-exponent-referential}

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->
| Form | Anatomy | Reading |
|------|---------|---------|
| **`g+0e`** | mantissa **`0`** + digitless **`wa`** | **absolute zero** / totalized null quantity; stronger than plain **`g+0`** |
| **`g+0e-`** | mantissa **`0`** + digitless **`wu`** | **sterile / null-at-seed** amount: emptiness at grain scale |
| **`g-0e`** | **`ru`** + mantissa **`0`** + digitless **`wa`** | **absolute residue** quantity: amount that will not totally wipe |
| **`g-0e-`** | **`ru`** + mantissa **`0`** + digitless **`wu`** | **micro-residue** amount |
| **`g+0e-1`** | mantissa **`0`** + digitful **`wu`** + exp **`1`** | **engineering null at OoM −1:** amount wiped at scale 10⁻¹ (deci-null). Sterile digitless is **`g+0e-`**. Bare OoM is **`g+e-1`**. |
| **`g-0e-1`** | **`ru`** + mantissa **`0`** + digitful **`wu`** + exp **`1`** | **engineering residue at OoM −1**: irreducible leftover at that scale |

Same under `/z/` `/d/` `/b/` (`zramowal` = absolute-zero as subject; `z+0e-1` = deci-null as subject).

**Emptiness chain (amounts):** **`gruwal`** (−∞ deficit), **`gramol`** (plain zero), **`gramowal`** (absolute zero), **`gramowul`** (sterile micro-null), **`g+0e-1`** (engineering null at 10⁻¹). Bare **`grawamol`** is the [ones decade band](#bare-oom-bands) (1–9).

**Only amounts take `±0e-1`:** the engineering null uses **`ra`** / **`ru`**.

#### Number as discourse {#zero-exponent-number-as-discourse}

#### Ordinal rank annihilated (`#0e`)
<a id="rank-annihilated"></a>

Mantissa **`0`** + digitless **`wa`** under forward ordinal **`re` / `#`**. Numbered zeroth is **`#0`** (still a rung). Origin landmark is **`#e-`**. Current generation is **`#e0`**. Scalar absolute zero is **`+0e`**.

| Form | Reading |
|------|---------|
| **`g#0e`** | **rank annihilated** / **disqualified** / place wiped; unplaceable in the series |
| **`z#0e`** / **`d#0e`** / **`b#0e`** | same as subject / object / modifier |
| **`v#0e`** | *disqualify* / take annihilated place |
| **`h#0e`** | *for a disqualified / struck turn* |
| **`y#0e`** | *Disqualified!* / *Rank wiped!* |

Soft **-m** hedges (*near-disqualified*, …).

#### Free vs derived

Free **`grawamol`** / **`g+Ne0`** are bare OoM / OoM assert on free numbers, not kind derivation morphs. Engineering OoM-null is **`±0e-1`**.

### Hyperbole {#hyperbole}
<a id="gazillion"></a>
<a id="hyperbole-gazillion"></a>

For a joking exaggeration (*a gazillion*, *the umpteenth*), write nonzero **mantissa digits** and then a digitless **`wa`** or **`wu`**. On an amount this is comically huge (**`wa`**) or tiny (**`wu`**); on a rank it is a comically late or early place. Mantissa **`0`** + digitless is [zero × exponent](#zero-exponent). Real powers of ten keep digitful exponents (`g+1e9`). End-relative place is marker **`#-`** ([from the end](numbers.md#from-the-end)).

#### Scalar (`ra` / `ru`)

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->
| Form | Reading |
|------|---------|
| **`g+1e`** | *one gazillion* (committed hyperbolic huge) |
| **`g+3e`** | *three gazillion* |
| **`g+1e-`** | *one gazillionth* / vanishingly small hyperbole |
| **`g-1e`** | *minus one gazillion* (hyperbolic deficit) |

Amounts work the same way on `/z/`, `/d/`, and `/b/`.

#### Ordinal umpteenth / umpteenth-first (`re` + **`wa`∅** / **`wu`∅**)

The mantissa counts how many joke-units **late** (**`wa`**) or **early** (**`wu`**) the place is. The place is still **finite**; this is only exaggeration. Landmarks are **`grewal`** / **`grewul`**.

| Form | Reading |
|------|---------|
| **`g#1e`** | *the gazillionth* / *umpteenth* (comic late place, toward last place) |
| **`g#3e`** | *the three-gazillionth* |
| **`g~#1e`** | *about the gazillionth* / soft umpteenth |
| **`g#1e-`** | *the gazillionth-first* / comic early place (toward onset) |
| **`g#3e-`** | *the three-gazillionth-first* |
| **`g~#1e-`** | *about the gazillionth-first* |

`grewal` is last place; `grewul` is the start landmark; `grebal` is numbered *1st*.

**Endings** on hyperbolic forms:

| Ending | Sense | Example |
|--------|-------|---------|
| **-l** | Committed hyperbole: speaker stands behind the joke magnitude | `g+1e` *one gazillion*; `g#1e` *the gazillionth*; `g#1e-` *the gazillionth-first* |
| **-m** | Soft / hedged hyperbole: *about a gazillion*, *umpteen* | `g~+1e` *roughly a gazillion*; `g~#1e` *about the gazillionth* |
| **-n** | **Proper noun / proper designation**: the hyperbolic numeral as a name (a building called *the Gazillion*, a title *One Gazillion*, *the Gazillionth*, …) | `g@+1e`; `g@#1e` |
| **-r** | Resume that prior hyperbolic amount or place; keep the mantissa (`g=+1e`, `g=#1e`), since `g=+e` / `g=#e` resume +∞ / last place | |

On `/v/`, `/h/`, `/y/`, and `/x/`, hyperbole works the same way:

| Form | Reading |
|------|---------|
| **`h+1e`** | *a gazillion times* |
| **`h#1e`** | *for the gazillionth time* |
| **`h#1e-`** | *for the gazillionth-first time* |
| **`v+1e`** | *add a gazillion* |
| **`v#1e`** | *place / come in gazillionth* / *rank as umpteenth* |
| **`v#1e-`** | *place / come in gazillionth-first* |
| **`y+1e`** | *a gazillion more!* |
| **`y#1e`** | *Gazillionth!* (comic late place cheer) |
| **`y#1e-`** | *Gazillionth-first!* (comic early place cheer) |
| **`x#1e`** | *umpteenth point:* / joke late agenda item |
| **`x#1e-`** | *umpteenth-first point:* / joke early agenda item |

Digitless **`e`/`e-`** sits in its own group (no **`ye`** / **`yo`** / **`yu`** stacked in that group).

### Number as stance {#special-number-as-stance}

Digitless specials, zero × exponent, and hyperbole keep their meaning under [stance](numbers.md#number-as-stance-by-marker) `/th/`:

| Form | Reading |
|------|---------|
| **`th+e`** | **certain**: not an estimate; no exceptions allowed |
| **`th+0e`** | **no chance at all**: ruled out, not just 0% |
| **`th+1e`** | *a gazillion percent sure* (hyperbole) |
| **`th-e-`** | **virtually** / *practically*: the claim holds all but a hair |
| **`th~-e-`** | *more or less* |

`th+100` and `thramol` are still estimates (*I'd put it at 100%*). `thrawal` and `thramowal` step outside the estimate.

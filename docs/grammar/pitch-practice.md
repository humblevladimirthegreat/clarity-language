# Pitch Practice

Each vowel has its own [pitch](/phonology#vowel-pitch), counted up from your home note: `a` low, `o` mid-low, `e` mid-high, `u` high. Practice here whenever you like. Both drills share your home note, which stays saved on this device.

## Controls {#controls}

Pick a pattern and a tempo, then press **Play**. A voice sings each vowel and the vowel lights up: say it on the same note. The corner shows the note's name while it sounds. With a microphone, the drill can also listen:

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Find my home note** | Listens while you hold a long `a` on a comfortable low note, and picks the nearest home note. |
| **Check me** | Leaves a pause after each note for you to say it back, then marks how you did. |
| **Strict** | Makes **Check me** stricter. Each level below says how. |
| **From memory** | Skips the notes, so you say each vowel unprompted. |
| Click a note | Checks just that vowel against its note. |

All listening happens in your browser. Nothing you say is recorded or sent anywhere, and your home note is saved only on this device.

## Beginner {#beginner}

Scales and pairs of vowels. **Check me** marks whether each vowel went up or down from the one you said before it. Only the direction counts, so a home note that is a little off does no harm. Switch on **Strict** to match each note itself, within about a semitone (one piano key).

## Intermediate {#intermediate}

Stacks, and the [letter names](/letter-names#letter-names) sung through in digit and alphabet order. **Check me** matches each note itself, within about a semitone; switch on **Strict** to narrow that to about half a semitone.

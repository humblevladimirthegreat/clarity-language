# Why and allowed cheat sheet {#why-allowed-cheat-sheet}

A quick look at [causation](../causation.md), [sakes](../sakes.md), and [feelings and permission](../feelings-permission.md) on one page. Nothing here is new: each row links to the section that teaches it. Stage: **B** Beginner, **I** Intermediate, **A** Advanced.

## Because, if, only if {#poles}

Write the outcome first, then the pole, then the condition as the pole's `/b/`. The pole is on `/th/` after a verb and on `/ɡ/` after a noun (*there is X if …*).

<!-- cheat-sheet: why-allowed -->
| After a verb / a noun | Link | Condition | English | Stage |
|-----------------------|------|-----------|---------|-------|
| [`thohom` / `gohom`](../causation.md#if) | enough | not asserted | *if* | B |
| [`thebom` / `gebom`](../causation.md#only-if) | needed | not asserted | *only if* / *needs* | B |
| [`thahum` / `gahum`](../iff-fault.md#only-because) | enough | asserted as the reason | *because* | I |
| [`thebom thahum` / `gebom gahum`](../iff-fault.md#only-because) | needed | asserted | *only because* / *depends on* | I |
| [`thehem` / `gehem`](../iff-fault.md#only-because) | both ways | not asserted | *if and only if* | I |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [If, after a verb](../causation.md#if) | `zuzozen vowurel thohom blerulal.` | *Uzozen walks if it rains.* | B |
| [If, after a noun](../causation.md#if) | `zerulal gohom blugalal.` | *There is rain if there are clouds.* | B |
| [Only if](../causation.md#only-if) | `zuzozen vowurel thebom blerulal.` | *Uzozen walks only if it rains.* | B |
| [Condition is a whole sentence](../iff-fault.md#dependent): `blarl`, then the sentence | `zuzozen vowurel thohom blarl zolazun vezuvol.` | *Uzozen walks if Olazun is asleep.* | I |
| [Because](../iff-fault.md#only-because) | `zuzozen vowurel thahum blarl zolazun vezuvol.` | *Uzozen walks because Olazun is asleep.* | I |
| [If and only if](../iff-fault.md#only-because) | `zuzozen vowurel thehem blarl zolazun vezuvol.` | *Uzozen walks if and only if Olazun is asleep.* | I |
| [Only because](../iff-fault.md#only-because) | `zuzozen vowurel thebom thahum blerulal.` | *Uzozen walks only because of the rain.* | I |
| [Unless](../iff-fault.md#only-because): `blurl` for `blarl` | `zuzozen vowurel thohom blurl zolazun vezuvol.` | *Uzozen walks unless Olazun is asleep.* | I |
| [Even if](../iff-fault.md#only-because) | `zuzozen vowurel hezom thohom blarl zolazun vezuvol.` | *Uzozen walks even if Olazun is asleep.* | I |

A noun after a because pole names an act or a thing, never a person. For *because of Olazun*, give what Olazun did as a sentence after `blarl`.

### Fault and share {#fault}

The ending on the because pole says whether anyone did wrong.

<!-- cheat-sheet: why-allowed -->
| Agebon | Use | English | Stage |
|--------|-----|---------|-------|
| [`thahul`](../iff-fault.md#fault) | the reason broke a norm | *it's their fault* / *to blame* | I |
| [`thahum`](../iff-fault.md#fault) | the reason made it happen; no fault claimed | *because* | I |
| [`thahur`](../iff-fault.md#fault) | one share among others | *partly because* | I |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Fault, on an act](../iff-fault.md#fault) | `zuzozen velurul thahul blarl zolazun vezuyel.` | *Uzozen left because Olazun spoke, and Olazun was wrong to.* | I |
| [A share](../iff-fault.md#fault) | `zuzozen velurul thahur blerulal.` | *The rain is part of why Uzozen left.* | I |

### CAUSE: the mechanism {#cause}

`thewem` before a pole says the condition is how the result is produced.

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Mechanism](../iff-fault.md#cause) | `zuzozen vowurel thewem thohom blerulal.` | *Uzozen walks if it rains.* (rain as the mechanism) | I |
| [Make someone do it](../iff-fault.md#make): causer in `/b/`, no pole | `zolazun vezuyel thewem bluzozen.` | *Uzozen makes Olazun speak.* | I |
| [One adjective only](../iff-fault.md#cause): `wewem` | `zuzozen wewem galunom vowurel.` | *Uzozen walks, straining.* (something is causing the strain) | I |

## The sakes {#sakes}

A sake is a kind of payoff. A sake word is a sake root, mid-word `th`, a stance vowel, and an ending. It takes only **-l**, **-m**, **-r**, never **-n**. Put it on `/ɡ/` after a noun that is yours, or on `/th/` for the event.

## Feelings {#feelings}

An [emotion](../feelings-permission.md#emotion-compose) is built on one word: the sake word, a **locus**, then a **motion ending**. Example: `wuwathur` + `ao` + **-r** is `wuwathuraor`.

| [`ao`](../feelings-permission.md#emotion-compose) | *over* | the atmosphere | *hangs over the room* | I |
| [`uo`](../feelings-permission.md#emotion-compose) | *through* | unplaced | *I can't place it* | I |
| [`o`](../feelings-permission.md#emotion-compose) | *at* | aimed | *annoyed at*, *grateful to* | I |
| [`oe`](../feelings-permission.md#emotion-compose) | *toward* | seeking | *turning to someone for comfort* | I |
| [`ue`](../feelings-permission.md#emotion-compose) | *against* | resisting | *objecting to* | I |
| [`e`](../feelings-permission.md#emotion-compose) | *for* | on behalf | *upset for* | I |
| [`u`](../feelings-permission.md#emotion-compose) | *from* | away | *avoiding*, *fleeing* | I |
| [`ua`](../feelings-permission.md#emotion-compose) | *out of* | collapse | *giving up* | I |

### Motion: how it moves {#motion}

| Ending | Use | English | Stage |
|--------|-----|---------|-------|
| [**-l**](../feelings-permission.md#emotion-compose) | still | *held, frozen, numb* | I |

## Allowed, required, agreed {#deontic}

A [say word](../feelings-permission.md#say-words) on `/h/`: **`h`** **`w`**, a scope vowel, whose say, the answer, and an ending. Whose say: **`v`** someone over the act (the speaker, or the `bl-` word after), **`z`** the doer, **`d`** the one it's done to, **`b`** the recipient. Answer: **`a`** allow, **`u`** forbid, **`e`** require, **`o`** their call. Ending: **-l** a rule or binding agreement, **-m** a person said so, **-r** nobody said. When unsure, use **-m**. All Intermediate.

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Permission, granted](../feelings-permission.md#say-words) | `zuzozen vowurel hwavam.` | *Uzozen may walk; I'm allowing it.* | I |
| [Permission, by someone](../feelings-permission.md#permission) | `zuzozen vowurel hwavam blolazun.` | *Olazun lets Uzozen walk.* | I |
| [Permission, by rule](../feelings-permission.md#permission) | `zahelan vuhohol hwaval.` | *The rules allow Ahelan to sit.* | I |
| [Requirement, by custom](../feelings-permission.md#permission) | `zolazun vezuvol hwaver.` | *Olazun is supposed to sleep.* | I |
| [Ask permission](../feelings-permission.md#permission) | `yom zuwon vuhohol hwavam.` | *May I sit?* | I |
| [Your own *have to*](../feelings-permission.md#permission): say who requires it | `zuwon vowurel hwavel.` / `zuwon vowurel hwazem.` | *I have to walk: the rules / I'm making myself* | I |
| [Consent, by the recipient](../feelings-permission.md#consent) | `zuzozen vezuyel bahelan hwabam.` | *Uzozen tells Ahelan, and Ahelan said yes.* | I |
| [Consent, by the one it's done to](../feelings-permission.md#consent) | `zuzozen dolazun vawebol hwadam.` | *Uzozen looks at Olazun, and Olazun agreed.* | I |
| [Assumed consent, several people](../feelings-permission.md#consent) | `zuzozen vezuyel bahelan bolazun bal hwabar.` | *assuming Ahelan and Olazun are OK* | I |
| [Letting yourself](../feelings-permission.md#consent) | `zolazun vezuvol hwazam.` | *Olazun lets themself sleep.* | I |
| [A promise](../feelings-permission.md#consent) | `zuwon vowurel hwazal.` | *I promise to walk.* (to you) | I |
| [Promise to someone else](../feelings-permission.md#say-scope): pair scope **`o`** | `zuwon vowurel hwozal blahelan.` | *I promise Ahelan I'll walk.* | I |
| [Check in](../feelings-permission.md#consent) | `yom zuwon vezuyel bawan hwabam.` | *Are you still OK with me telling you?* | I |

## Don't mix up {#dont-mix-up}

| This | vs | That |
|------|----|------|
| `thohom` *if* ([enough, not claimed](../causation.md#if)) | | `thahum` *because* ([enough, and a fact](../iff-fault.md#only-because)) |
| `thohom` *if* ([other routes remain](../causation.md#only-if)) | | `thebom` *only if* ([the outcome needs it](../causation.md#only-if)) |
| `thahum` *because* ([a cause, no blame](../iff-fault.md#fault)) | | `thahul` *fault* ([someone did wrong](../iff-fault.md#fault)) |
| `thahum` *because* ([the condition](../iff-fault.md#cause)) | | `thewem` *mechanism* ([how it is produced](../iff-fault.md#cause)) |
| `hwavam` *may* ([someone over the act allows it](../feelings-permission.md#permission)) | | `hwadam` *they agreed* ([the yes of the one it's done to](../feelings-permission.md#consent)) |
| `hwavam` *may* ([the act is allowed](../feelings-permission.md#permission)) | | `hwavem` *has to* ([the act is demanded](../feelings-permission.md#permission)) |
| `hwavel` *the rules require it* ([an outside demand](../feelings-permission.md#permission)) | | `hwazer` *I feel I have to* ([an unspoken should](../feelings-permission.md#consent)) |
| `thawothem` *ought to, for relatedness* ([advice for a sake](../prescription.md#sake-force)) | | `thawothom` *for relatedness* ([why it is done](../prescription.md#sake-preference)) |
| `thawotham` *thank you* ([my sake is met](../feelings-permission.md#thanks-sorry)) | | `thawothum blawan` *I'm sorry* ([your sake is hurt](../feelings-permission.md#thanks-sorry)) |

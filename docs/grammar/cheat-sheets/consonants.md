# Consonants cheat sheet {#consonants-cheat-sheet}

How consonants are reused. Sounds, names, and digit syllables are on [Sounds and spelling](/cheat-sheets/sounds-spelling). Nothing here is new: each row links to the section that teaches it. Stage: **B** Beginner, **I** Intermediate, **A** Advanced.

## Role letters {#role-letters}

From the [role letters](/clause#role-letters) table. `/y/` and `/x/` start a turn or keep one going.

| Letter | Job | Stage |
|--------|-----|-------|
| [`/z/`](/clause#role-letters) | subject: who acts | B |
| `/d/` | direct object: who is acted on | B |
| `/b/` | extra noun: recipient, or the piece after a relation word | B |
| `/v/` | verb: the action | B |
| `/ɡ/` | adjective: a property of the noun | B |
| `/w/` | extra detail on the next `/ɡ/`, `/h/`, or `/th/` word | B |
| `/h/` | adverb: how, when, where | B |
| `/th/` | stance: how sure, how known, why, as if | B |
| [`/y/`](/speech-moves#turn-y) | turn: call, reaction, or speech act | B |
| [`/x/`](/dependents#continue-x) | continue: glue this sentence to the last one | B |

The same letters name a part in an earlier event on [role pointers](/pronouns#role-pointers) and [role compounds](/roles#role-compounds): **z** the doer, **d** the one it happens to, **b** the extra party.

## Endings {#endings}

Shown on one root, `uzoze`. From [word endings](/word-endings#concrete-l) and [resume](/pronouns#resume-r).

| Ending | Means | Example | English | Stage |
|--------|-------|---------|---------|-------|
| **-l** | [first mention, everyday kind](/word-endings#concrete-l) | `uzozel` | *swan* | B |
| **-m** | [first mention, published abstract](/word-endings#abstract-m) | `uzozem` | *grace* | B |
| **-n** | [a name](/word-endings#named-n-beginner) | `uzozen` | *Uzozen* | B |
| **-r** | [the same one again](/pronouns#resume-r) | `zuzozer` | *Uzozen* (again) | B |
| **-x** after any of these | [associates, or a group](/plurality#associative) | `zuzozenx` | *Uzozen and associates* | B |

On a join, the same endings are [closed / open / unnamed member](/joins#join-type-vowel-series): **-l** the listed items only, **-m** *(and possibly more)*, **-r** *someone* / *something*.

## Root-tail kinds {#root-kinds}

A root's last consonant hints at the kind of thing. From [root tails](/root-tails#root-tails). Memory aid only: grammar does not read it.

| Letter | Kind | Example | Why this letter | Stage |
|--------|------|---------|-----------------|-------|
| `z` | creatures | `agozo` *cat* | [`/z/`](/clause#role-letters) who acts | A |
| `y` | people | `eboye` *baby* | [`/y/` + name](/speech-moves#vocative) calls someone | A |
| `m` | mind and feeling | `evemu` *fear* | [**-m**](/word-endings#abstract-m) abstract | A |
| `b` | body and what goes on it | `aheba` *hand* | [`/b/`](/clause#role-letters) the extra piece | A |
| `v` | doing, playing, moving | `aruve` *run* | [`/v/`](/clause#role-letters) the action | A |
| `h` | places and fixtures | `aheha` *house* | [`/h/`](/clause#role-letters) where | A |
| `l` | nature and raw stuff | `edale` *tree* | [**-l**](/word-endings#concrete-l) concrete | A |
| `d` | food, drink, kitchen | `oledo` *apple* | [`/d/`](/clause#role-letters) what is done to | A |
| `w` | tools | `anewu` *knife* | [`/w/`](/clause#role-letters) *with* | A |
| `g` | signs and marks | `awugu` *warning* | [`/ɡ/`](/clause#role-letters) a mark on something | A |
| `r` | counting, time, money | `egore` *clock* | `r` after a role letter starts a [number](/numbers#counts) | A |
| `n` | titles, paper, named sports | `unona` *newspaper*, `azuna` *soccer* | [**-n**](/proper-names#proper-name--n) the name ending | A |

| Letter | Where | Job | Stage |

| [`l`](/left-bound-adjectives#left-bound-adjectives) after `/ɡ/` | `gl-` | adjective before the noun | B |

# Numbers cheat sheet {#numbers-cheat-sheet}

A quick look at [numbers](../numbers.md) and [numbers in use](../numbers-applied.md) on one page. Nothing here is new: each row links to the section that teaches it. Stage: **B** Beginner, **I** Intermediate, **A** Advanced. Written shorthand is used from two digits up; zero, one digit, or none stays spelled.

## Building a number word {#build}

A number word is one word: role letter, marker, one syllable per digit, ending. `grarel` is *three*.

| Digit | Syllable | Digit | Syllable |
|-------|----------|-------|----------|
| [1](../numbers.md#counts) | `wo` | [6](../numbers.md#counts) | `gu` |
| [2](../numbers.md#counts) | `du` | [7](../numbers.md#counts) | `le` |
| [3](../numbers.md#counts) | `re` | [8](../numbers.md#counts) | `ha` |
| [4](../numbers.md#counts) | `mo` | [9](../numbers.md#counts) | `na` |
| [5](../numbers.md#counts) | `va` | [0](../numbers.md#counts) | `zo` |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Count](../numbers.md#counts) | `zagadulx grarel.` | *Three cats.* | B |
| [Count, many digits](../numbers.md#counts): one word | `graduzol` | *twenty* | B |
| [Ordinal](../numbers.md#ordinals): `re` for `ra` | `zugul gredul.` | *The second book.* | B |
| [More than one](../numbers.md#more-than-one) | `zagadulx gral.` | *Cats, more than one.* | B |

### The ending {#ending}

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->
| Written | Spoken | Use | Stage |
|---------|--------|-----|-------|
| [*(none)*](../numbers.md#number-endings): `g+3`, `g+` | **-l** | exact, newly stated | B |
| [**`~`**](../numbers.md#number-endings): `g~+3`, `g~+`, [`g~+27e6`, `g~#2`](../numbers.md#ending-marks-second-slot-after-pos) | **-m** | approximate: *about N* | I |
| [**`@`**](../numbers.md#number-endings): `g@#2`, [`g@_12`](../numbers.md#ending-marks-second-slot-after-pos) | **-n** | conventional title or label | I |
| [**`=`**](../numbers.md#number-endings): `g=+3`, `g=#2`, `d=_12` | **-r** | resume a number already given (needs a digit) | I |

Digitless **-r** is not a resume: `grar` is *some number* in a statement and *which number?* in a question.

## Blanks and bands {#blanks}

Drop every digit and the word says what kind of number, not its value. With **-r** it is the blank: *some number* in a statement, *which number?* in a question.

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->
| Marker | Writing | Unspecified | Stage |
|--------|---------|-------------|-------|
| [**`ra`**](../numbers.md#digitless) | `…+` | positive amount, specialized to *more than one* | I |
| [**`ru`**](../numbers.md#digitless) | `…-` | a negative amount | I |
| [**`re`**](../numbers.md#digitless) | `…#` | a rank from the start | I |
| [**`ro`**](../numbers.md#digitless) | `…_` | a digit-string or label | I |

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->
| Agazan | Use | English | Stage |
|--------|-----|---------|-------|
| [`g=+` (`grar`)](../numbers.md#how-many) | fill a count or amount | *How many?* / *How much?* | I |
| [`g=#` (`grer`)](../numbers.md#how-many) | fill a place in order | *Which place?* | I |
| [`g=_` (`gror`)](../numbers.md#how-many) | fill a code or label | *What number?* | I |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [A few](../numbers.md#digitless): open `gram` | `zagadulx gram vehahel.` | *A few cats sit.* | I |
| [Some, on a mass noun](../numbers.md#digitless) | `zedehel gral vehahel.` | *Some tea sits there.* | I |
| [Some amount](../numbers.md#digitless) | `zazawan drar vahahal.` | *Azawan sees some number of them.* | I |
| [How many?](../numbers.md#digitless) | `yol drar zazawan vahahal.` | *How many does Azawan see?* | I |
| [How many cats?](../numbers.md#how-many) | `yol dagadulx grar zazawan vahahal.` | *How many cats does Azawan see?* | I |
| [Which place?](../numbers.md#how-many) | `yol ol brer zalahen vehahel.` | *Which place does Alahen sit in?* | I |
| [How big?](../numbers.md#how-big): blank on `/w/` | `yol wrar gelavam zodogal.` | *How big is the dog?* | I |
| [How fast?](../numbers.md#how-big) | `yol wrar hadehum zodogal varahal.` | *How fast does the dog run?* | I |

*Many*, *few*, *enough*, and *too much* are [comparatives](../comparatives.md#vague-amounts), not number words. Do not confuse the blank on `/h/` with `har` *sometimes*.

## Big and small {#big-small}

An **exponent** is a power of ten before the mantissa digits: **`ba`** positive, **`bu`** negative, **`ya`** after exponent digits when a mantissa follows. Use engineering notation (multiples of 3). A bare exponent, with no mantissa, names a band.

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->
| Form | Band | Reading | Stage |
|------|------|---------|-------|
| [`g+e0`](../numbers.md#bare-oom) | 1–9 | *ones* | I |
| [`g+e1`](../numbers.md#bare-oom) | 10–99 | *tens* | I |
| [`g+e2`](../numbers.md#bare-oom) | 100–999 | *hundreds* | I |
| [`g+e3`](../numbers.md#bare-oom) | 1000–9999 | *thousands* | I |
| [`g+e6`](../numbers.md#bare-oom) | 10⁶ up | *millions* band only | I |
| [`g-e3`](../numbers.md#bare-oom) | negative | negative thousands band | I |

The next bands, `e4` and `e5`, are tens and hundreds of thousands. Soft `grabarem` is *about in the thousands band*. An exact count inside a band takes a mantissa (`g+4e3` is 4000).

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Exponent](../numbers.md#exponents) | `g+27e12` | *27 × 10¹²* | I |
| [Decimal](../numbers.md#exponents) | `g+5.2` | *5.2* | I |
| [Several groups](../numbers.md#group-separator) | `g+5e3,860` | *5860* | I |
| [Seat in a row](../numbers.md#group-separator) | `zezedal g#2,3.` | *Seat 2 in row 3.* | I |

Each comma is spoken as the separator, which matches the marker.

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->
| Marker | Separator | Example | Stage |
|--------|-----------|---------|-------|
| [**`ra`** (`+`)](../numbers.md#group-separator) | **`tha`** | `g+5e3,860` | I |
| [**`ru`** (`-`)](../numbers.md#group-separator) | **`thu`** | `g-1e9,265e3,4` | I |
| [**`re`** (`#`)](../numbers.md#group-separator) | **`the`** | `g#2,3` | I |
| [**`ro`** (`_`)](../numbers.md#group-separator) | **`tho`** | `d_555,123,4567` | I |

On a rank, each comma opens a place within the one before. A large ordinal is one group (`g#1005`).

In casual talk a stretch may set an ambient order of magnitude, so bare numbers are heard at that size until cancelled. Write `Ne0` to insist on ones ([ambient](../numbers.md#ambient-magnitude)).

## The number in another slot {#slots}

The role letter picks the use; the marker still says amount, rank, or code. Only the markers and writing are listed here, because every row is the same shape: `v`, `h`, `y`, `x`, `w`, `th` + the number.

| Slot | `ra` `+` | `ru` `-` | `re` `#` | `ro` `_` | Stage |
|------|----------|----------|----------|----------|-------|
| [`/v/` verb](../numbers.md#number-as-verb-by-marker) | add N | remove N | take place N | enter / dial the code | I |
| [`/h/` adverb](../numbers.md#number-as-adverb-by-marker) | *N times* | *÷N*, *every Nth* | *for the Nth time* | clock time (`h_15,30`) | I |
| [`/y/` shout](../numbers.md#number-as-interjection-by-marker) | *N more!* | *N short!* | *First!* | *Three!* (a score) | I |
| [`/x/` list point](../numbers.md#number-as-discourse-marker-by-marker) | corroborating N | independent N | point N | cite label N | I |
| [`/th/` stance](../numbers.md#number-as-stance-by-marker) | N% likely | unlikely | N-th hand | per source N | I |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Three times](../numbers.md#number-as-adverb-by-marker) | `zalahen vowogal hrarel.` | *Alahen walks three times.* | I |
| [For the third time](../numbers.md#number-as-adverb-by-marker) | `hrerel` | *for the third time* | I |
| [Three more!](../numbers.md#number-as-interjection-by-marker) | `yrarel` | *Three more!* | I |
| [Three!](../numbers.md#number-as-interjection-by-marker) | `y_3` | *Three! (a score)* | I |
| [First!](../numbers.md#number-as-interjection-by-marker) | `yrewol` | *First!* | I |
| [Point 2](../numbers.md#number-as-discourse-marker-by-marker) | `xredul` | *point 2:* | I |
| [Corroborating 2](../numbers.md#number-as-discourse-marker-by-marker) | `xradul` | *corroborating 2:* | I |
| [Independent 2](../numbers.md#number-as-discourse-marker-by-marker) | `xrudul` | *independent 2:* | I |
| [Likelihood](../numbers.md#number-as-stance-by-marker): the digits are already a percent | `zalahen vowogal th+70.` | *Alahen is probably walking (I'd put it at 70%).* | I |
| [Place on a scale](../numbers.md#number-as-degree): `w#N` from 2; [see comparatives](../comparatives.md#place-on-a-scale) | `zazawan zel wredul gelavam.` | *Azawan is the second biggest.* | I |

`th+` / `th-` is *likely* / *unlikely* with no figure; `th_N` is *per source N*; `th#N` is *N-th hand* from 2. A factor goes on `/h/`, never `/w/`. Likelihood **-m** hedges (`th~+70`), **-n** names a level (`th@+95`), **-r** repeats it (`th=+70`).

## Numbers in use {#applied}

### Codes and labels {#codes}

The digit-string marker `_` holds a phone, room, channel, or serial number. Group digits in threes, no exponent. **-x** on a label names a run (`z_90x`, the ’90s).

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [A code as object](../numbers-applied.md#digit-strings) | `zazawan d_555,123,4567 vahahal.` | *Azawan sees 555-123-4567.* | I |
| [Plural label](../numbers-applied.md#plural-labels) | `zazawan vowogal huwem b_90x.` | *Azawan walks in the ’90s.* | I |

### Time and date {#time}

A digit string under `/h/` with no relation word before it. Clock is `h_…`; a date is `h_#…`, read day, month, optional year.

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->
| Job | How | Stage |
|-----|-----|-------|
| [Clock](../numbers-applied.md#time) | digit-string `ro` as bare `/h/`, 24-hour, fields left to right (`h_15,30`, `h_15,30,00`) | I |
| [Date](../numbers-applied.md#time) | `_` with `oe` (`h_#22,7`, `h_#22,7,2026`; modifier `g_#22,7`) | I |
| [Day of the week](../numbers-applied.md#time) | ordinal on `elaga`, from Monday (`zelagam grewol` *Monday*) | I |
| [Month or year](../numbers-applied.md#time) | ordinal on `umuha`; label on `avawe` (`zavawem g_1962`) | I |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [At a time](../numbers-applied.md#time) | `h_15,30 zazawan vowogal.` | *At 15:30 Azawan walks.* | I |
| [In March](../numbers-applied.md#time) | `zazawan vowogal huwem bumuham grerel.` | *Azawan walks in March.* | I |
| [In 1962](../numbers-applied.md#time) | `zazawan vowogal huwem bavawem g_1962.` | *Azawan walks in 1962.* | I |
| [From … to](../numbers-applied.md#time) | `zazawan ul b_9 vowogal oel b_17.` | *Azawan walks from 9:00 to 17:00.* | I |

Digitless `hrol` is *some clock or date*; `hror` asks *when?* *Three times* (`hrarel`) is not a clock time.

### Ranges {#ranges}

Write both ends with the hook `al`: `A al B`. It reads as *through* when both ends are the same kind of number with digits. `ul` excludes the end after it.

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->
| Agazan | Use | English | Stage |
|--------|-----|---------|-------|
| [`zrarel al zraval`](../numbers-applied.md#ranges) | both ends in | *3 to 5* | I |
| [`zrarel ul zraval`](../numbers-applied.md#ranges) | 5 left out | *3 up to but not including 5* | I |
| [`zraval ul zrarel`](../numbers-applied.md#ranges) | 3 left out | *more than 3, up to 5* | I |
| [`zrarel am zraval`](../numbers-applied.md#ranges) | soft ends | *roughly 3 to 5* | I |
| [`zrarel um zraval`](../numbers-applied.md#ranges) | soft exclusion | *3 up to about 5, not quite 5* | I |
| [`zrarel ual zraval`](../numbers-applied.md#ranges) | both ends out | *strictly between 3 and 5* | I |
| [`zrarel uel zraval`](../numbers-applied.md#ranges) | outside the band | *below 3 or above 5* | I |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [3 to 5](../numbers-applied.md#ranges) | `zrarel al zraval.` | *From 3 to 5.* | I |
| [Of a noun](../numbers-applied.md#ranges) | `zodogal grarel al graval vowogal.` | *Three to five dogs walk.* | I |
| [Hours](../numbers-applied.md#ranges) | `zagazem grarel al graval.` | *Three to five hours.* | I |

To limit to one end, make the other end infinity:

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->
| Agazan | Use | English | Stage |
|--------|-----|---------|-------|
| [`zraval al zrabal`](../numbers-applied.md#rays) | 5 up to +∞ | *5 or more* | A |
| [`zrabal ul zraval`](../numbers-applied.md#rays) | 5 left out | *more than 5* | A |
| [`zrubal al zraval`](../numbers-applied.md#rays) | −∞ up to 5 | *5 or less* | A |
| [`zrubal ul zraval`](../numbers-applied.md#rays) | 5 left out | *less than 5* | A |
| [`zraval am zrabal`](../numbers-applied.md#rays) | soft | *about 5 or more* | A |

Ranks take `grebal` (last) and `grebul` (first) as ends: `zredul al zrebal` is *2nd through last*. A named band uses **-n**, and **-r** stands for a value in it.

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->

|--------|-----|---------|-------|

| [`zrarel ar zraval`](../numbers-applied.md#named-ranges) | some value in the band | *some value from 3 to 5* | A |
| [`zrarel ur zraval`](../numbers-applied.md#named-ranges) | some value, 5 left out | *some value from 3 up to 5* | A |

### Percent, parts, and kin {#parts}

End a count with **`yo`** for a percent, **`yu`** for percentage points. A minus number right after a plain noun is a fraction.

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->
| Agazan | Use | English | Stage |
|--------|-----|---------|-------|
| [**`yo`**](../numbers-applied.md#percent-and-percentage-points) | percent of a whole | `25` in `25%`; those digits ÷ 100 | I |
| [**`yu`**](../numbers-applied.md#percent-and-percentage-points) | percentage points | `2` in `+2%*`; those digits ÷ 100 | I |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [A percent](../numbers-applied.md#percent-and-percentage-points) | `zagadulx g+25%.` | *25% of the cats.* | I |
| [Half of](../numbers-applied.md#fractions) | `zagadulx grudul vehahel.` | *Half of the cats sit.* | I |
| [A third of](../numbers-applied.md#fractions) | `zedehel grurel vehahel.` | *A third of the tea sits there.* | I |
| [Two thirds](../numbers-applied.md#fractions): the count, then the fraction | `zagadulx gradul grurel vehahel.` | *Two thirds of the cats sit.* | I |
| [A third of an hour](../numbers-applied.md#fractions) | `zazawan vowogal bagazem grawol grurel.` | *Azawan walks for a third of an hour.* | I |

After a measure unit a lone minus number is a negative amount (`bezezem grudul` *minus 2 meters*).

## Special values {#special}

A digitless exponent (**`e`** speech `ba`, **`e-`** speech `bu`, no digits after it) makes a special value. What comes before it decides which kind.

<!-- cheat-sheet: numbers -->
| Form | Reading | Stage |
|------|---------|-------|
| [`g+e`](../numbers.md#special-referential) | +∞ | A |
| [`g~+e`](../numbers.md#special-referential) | arbitrarily large but finite | A |
| [`g+e-`](../numbers.md#special-referential) | arbitrarily small but finite | A |
| [`g-e`](../numbers.md#special-referential) | −∞ | A |

### Zero and hyperbole {#zero-hyperbole}

Mantissa **`0`** with a digitless exponent is a strong zero. A nonzero mantissa with a digitless exponent is a joke size.

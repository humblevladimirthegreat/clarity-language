# People and pointing cheat sheet {#people-pointing-cheat-sheet}

A quick look at [pronouns](/pronouns), [topic](/topic), and [plurality](/plurality) on one page. Nothing here is new: each row links to the section that teaches it. Stage: **B** Beginner, **I** Intermediate, **A** Advanced.

## Pointing back {#pointing-back}

### Which pointer to use {#which-pointer}

| You want | Use | Example | English | Stage |
|----------|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [The same one again, by name](/pronouns#resume-r) | role letter + whole stem + **-r** | `zuzozer` | *Uzozen* (again) | B |
| [The same kind again](/pronouns#resume-r) | the same, on a kind | `zudozor` | *the dog* | B |
| [By the part they played](/pronouns#role-pointers) | role letter + `wu` + part consonant + pointer vowel + **-r** | `zwuzer` | *the latest doer* | B |
| [A new one of that kind](/pronouns#a-new-one) | the same pointer with **-l** | `dwudel` | *another one* (as object) | B |
| [Their part, not them](/pronouns#share) | the same pointer with **-m** | `zwuzem` | *what the doer did* | B |
| [Pin one sense](/tag-pronouns#resume-sense) | **-rl** concrete, **-rm** abstract | `dudozorl` | *the dog* (not the loyalty) | I |
| [A label you chose](/tag-pronouns#tag-pronouns) | role letter + `w` + tag vowel | `zwar` | *A* | I |
| [The talk's topic](/topic#topic-pronoun) | `alo` + **-n** | `zalon` | *the topic* | I |

A resume uses the **whole stem**: `dobahohar` is *the bedroom*, `dobahor` is *the bed*. It points at the **most recent** word with that stem.

### Role pointers {#role-pointers}

Role letter for the slot now (see [role pointers](/pronouns#role-pointers)), **`wu`**, a **part consonant** for the part (the role letter it had then), a **pointer vowel** for which event, then the ending.

| Part consonant | Part in the earlier event | Where it sat | Example | Stage |
|----------------|---------------------------|--------------|---------|-------|
| **z** | the doer | `/z/` | `zwuzer` | B |
| **d** | the one it happens to | `/d/` | `dwuder` | B |
| **b** | the extra party | `/b/` | `zwuber` | B |

| Pointer vowel | Picks | Example | English | Stage |
|---------------|-------|---------|---------|-------|
| **e** | [the latest sentence with someone in that part](/pronouns#role-pointers) | `zwuzer` | *they* | B |
| **a** | [the nearest one other than the last](/pronouns#the-other-one) | `zwuzar` | *the other one* | B |
| **o** | [this sentence's own event](/pronouns#themself) | `dwuzor` | *themself* | B |
| **u** | [the latest sentence that left the part unsaid](/pronouns#whoever-it-was) | `zwuzur` | *whoever did it* | B |

Endings on a pointer: **-r** the same one, **-l** [a new one](/pronouns#a-new-one) (not with **u**), **-m** [their part](/pronouns#share) (not with **o**). A pointer never names its own slot. A topic change [ends its reach](/topic#topic-resets).

### Other ways to point {#other-pointing}

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Same action again](/tag-pronouns#cross-role-recast): change only the role letter | `zolazun vuzozer.` | *Olazun does the same with Uzozen.* | I |
| [Each other](/tag-pronouns#reciprocal) | `zuzozen zolazun zal vawebol hehem.` | *Uzozen and Olazun see each other.* | I |

### Tags {#tags}

A [tag](/tag-pronouns#tag-pronouns) is a label you choose: role letter, `w`, a tag vowel (A **a**, E **e**, O **o**, U **u**), an ending. It stays on its referent. Tags fill `/z/`, `/d/`, `/b/` only.

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| Assign: tag **-l** after a phrase | `zudozol zwal` | *a dog, call it A* | I |
| A new referent with no name | `zwal` | *someone A* | I |
| Recall | `zwar` / `dwar` / `bwar` | *A* | I |
| Recall a tag assigned to a name | `zwan` / `dwan` / `bwan` | *A* (a named one) | I |
| A's part in the latest event | `zwam` / `dwam` / `bwam` | *what A did* | I |
| A and associates | `zwarx` / `zwanx` | *A and associates* | I |
| Two tags (six stacks: `zwaer` `zwaor` `zwoer` `zwuar` `zwuor` `zwuer`) | `zwaer` | *A and E* | I |
| Two tags, their parts | `zwaem` | *what A and E did* | I |
| Two tags and associates | `zwaerx` | *A, E, and associates* | I |

## The topic {#topic}

Only an `/x/` word at the start of a sentence sets the [topic](/topic#topic). Being first, being the subject, and *as for* do not.

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| Introduce a person | `xuzozen` | *now, about Uzozen* | I |
| Introduce a kind | `xudozol` | *now, about a dog* | I |
| [Return to an earlier topic](/topic#topic-return) | `xuzozer` | *back to Uzozen* | I |
| [Return to the kind from before](/topic#topic-return) | `xudozor` | *back to the dog* | I |
| [Clear the topic](/topic#topic) | `xuvewem` / `xuvedam` | *next* / *by the way* | I |
| [The topic itself, in any slot](/topic#topic-pronoun) | `zalon` / `dalon` / `balon` | *the topic* | I |

The topic pronoun is never a topic word or a resume (`xalon`, `xalor`, `zalor` are not sentences). A dropped subject is never the topic: write `zalon`.

| [What a topic change resets](/topic#topic-resets) | Resets? | Stage |
|----------------------------|---------|-------|
| [Role pointer anchors](/topic#topic-resets) | yes | I |
| Bare-number magnitude | yes | I |
| Whole-stem **-r** | no | I |
| Tags | no | I |

## People in the conversation {#people}

### Special pronouns {#special-pronouns}

Role letter + the root + **-n** ([special pronouns](/pronouns#special-pronouns)). Prefer a proper name when you have one.

| Word | Means | In a slot | English | Same root as | Stage |
|------|-------|-----------|---------|--------------|-------|
| [`uwon`](/pronouns#special-pronouns) | the speaker | `zuwon` | *I* | `uwol` *microphone* | B |
| `awan` | the one listening | `zawan` / `dawan` | *you* | `awal` *headphones* | B |
| `ayan` | speaker and addressees together | `zayan` | *we* (you and I) | `ayal` *handshake* | B |
| `umon` | a nonspecific individual | `zumon` | *someone* | `umol` *neutral* | B |
| [`eyon`](/pronouns#generic-pronoun) | any person, as a rule | `zeyon` | *one*, generic *you* | `eyol` *person* | B |

**Notes:** As an object, *we* is `dayan`. `awan` is always the person actually listening. `eyon` takes no **-x**.

### Groups with **-x** {#groups}

| Form | Means | Example | English | Stage |
|------|-------|---------|---------|-------|
| [name **-nx**](/plurality#associative) | anchor and associates | `zuzozenx` | *Uzozen and associates* | B |
| [**-lx** / **-mx**](/plurality#associative) | an indefinite group | `zagozolx` | *some cats* | B |
| [**-lnx**](/proper-names#name-instance--ln) | some of a name | `duzozelnx` | *some Uzozens* | I |
| [**-rx**](/plurality#associative) | someone mentioned, and associates | `zuzozerx` | *they* | B |
| [speaker **-x**](/plurality#person-role-x) | the speaker and their people, you not included | `uwonx` (`zuwonx`, `duwonx`) | *I and my people* | B |
| [listener **-x**](/plurality#person-role-x) | the address set, speaker not included | `awanx` (`zawanx`, `dawanx`) | *you all* | B |
| [interlocutors **-x**](/plurality#person-role-x) | everyone here, and their people | `ayanx` | *we all* | B |

Who counts as an associate is [open unless you say](/collectives#associate-resolution): a join or `ul` names it; otherwise a group already named, then the obvious group in the scene. Nonspecific `umon` takes no **-x**; *some people* is `zeyolx`.

### One act, one unit {#collective}

**-x** on a verb or adjective says the group acts, or the property holds, as one.

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Group, act left open](/collectives#verbs-v) | `zuzozenx vezuyol` | *Uzozen and associates sing* | I |
| [One shared act](/collectives#verbs-v) | `zuzozenx vezuyolx` | *they sing as one performance* | I |
| [Joint doing, others unnamed](/collectives#verbs-v) | `zuzozen vezuyolx` | *Uzozen sings jointly* | I |
| [The group as one unit](/collectives#verbs-v) | `zavayal vowurelx` | *the family walks as one unit* | I |
| [Property of each member](/collectives#adjectives-g) | `zavonal zuwal zal garolom` | *the file-box and the toolbox are each heavy* | I |
| [Property of the whole](/collectives#adjectives-g) | `zavonal zuwal zal garolomx` | *heavy together* | I |
| [Group-level size](/collectives#adjectives-g) | `zavayal gogalam` | *the family is vast* | I |
| [Members, plain](/collectives#adjectives-g) | `zavonalx garolom` | *the file-boxes are heavy* | I |
| [Members, collectively](/collectives#adjectives-g) | `zavonalx garolomx` | *the file-boxes are heavy collectively* | I |
| [Call a group](/collectives#vocatives-y) | `yawanx` | *hey, you all* | I |

## Don't mix up {#dont-mix-up}

| This | vs | That |
|------|----|------|
| `zuzozer` *Uzozen, again* ([the stem, resumed](/pronouns#resume-r)) | | `zwuzer` *the latest doer* ([by the part they played](/pronouns#role-pointers)) |
| `dwuder` *the same one* ([-r](/pronouns#a-new-one)) | | `dwudel` *another one* ([-l](/pronouns#a-new-one)) |
| `zwuzer` *the person* ([-r](/pronouns#share)) | | `zwuzem` *what they did* ([-m](/pronouns#share)) |
| `zayan` *you and I* ([`aya`](/pronouns#special-pronouns)) | | `zuwonx` *I and my people*, not you ([speaker -x](/plurality#person-role-x)) |
| `zawan` *you* ([one listener](/pronouns#special-pronouns)) | | `zawanx` *you all* ([the address set](/plurality#person-role-x)) |
| `zumon` *someone* ([tied to no event](/pronouns#special-pronouns)) | | `zwuzur` *whoever did that* ([tied to one known event](/pronouns#whoever-it-was)) |
| `zeyon` *one*, people at large ([generic](/pronouns#generic-pronoun)) | | `zawan` *you*, the listener ([just them](/pronouns#special-pronouns)) |
| `xuzozen` *now, about Uzozen* ([sets the topic](/topic#topic)) | | `zalon` *the topic* ([refers to it](/topic#topic-pronoun)) |

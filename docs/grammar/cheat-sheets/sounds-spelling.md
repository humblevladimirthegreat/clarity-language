# Sounds and spelling cheat sheet {#sounds-spelling-cheat-sheet}

A quick look at [phonology](/phonology) on one page. Nothing here is new: each row links to the section that teaches it. Stage: **B** Beginner, **I** Intermediate, **A** Advanced.

## Letters {#letters}

Each letter has one sound, and native text is lowercase. Two vowels in a row are two syllables. `th` is one letter written with two characters, and there is no `t`. `y` is always a consonant.

### Vowels {#vowels}

| Letter | Say | Like | Pitch | Stage |
|--------|-----|------|-------|-------|
| [`u`](/phonology#vowels) | /ʉ/ | *you* (without the *y*) | high | B |
| [`a`](/phonology#vowels) | /ä/ | *spa* | low | B |
| [`o`](/phonology#vowels) | /o̞/ | *Cambodia* (no glide) | mid-low | B |
| [`e`](/phonology#vowels) | /e̞/ | *bet* | mid-high | B |

Vowel pitch is optional and adds no meaning; when sound and pitch disagree, the sound wins. [Stress](/singability#rhythm-and-stress) is loudness, never pitch. Exact pitches: `a` 0, `o` +3, `e` +5, `u` +7 semitones ([vowel pitch values](/singability#vowel-pitch-values), A).

### Consonants and their names {#consonants}

The name is what you say when you [spell a word aloud](/phonotactics#letter-names). The unvoiced sound is fine for style; prefer the voiced one so you can hold a note.

| Letter | Say | Like | Unvoiced | Name | Stage |
|--------|-----|------|----------|------|-------|
| [`b`](/phonology#consonants) | /b/ | *be* | /p/ | `bu` | B |
| `d` | /d/ | *do* | /t/ | `de` | B |
| `g` | /ɡ/ | *go* | /k/ | `go` | B |
| `h` | /ɦ/ | *ahead* | /h/ | `ha` | B |
| `z` | /z/ | *zoo* | /s/ | `zu` | B |
| `v` | /v/ | *vie* | /f/ | `ve` | B |
| `r` | /ɹ/ | *red* | | `ro` | B |
| `l` | /l/ | *lie* | | `la` | B |
| `n` | /n/ | *no* | | `nu` | B |
| `m` | /m/ | *me* | | `me` | B |
| `th` | /ð/ | *this* | /θ/ | `tho` | B |
| `w` | /w/ | *we* | | `wa` | B |
| `y` | /j/ | *yes* | | `yu` | B |
| `x` | /ʒ/ | *vision* (not English *x*) | /ʃ/ | `xe` | B |

Rows are in alphabetical order: the ten digit letters first (1 to 9, then 0), then the other consonants. The vowels come last and name themselves (`u`, `a`, `o`, `e`). Pause between names: `agozo` is `a` `go` `o` `zu` `o`. A letter you talk about in a clause is a [mention](/span-mention#mention), read by its name.

## Word shape {#word-shape}

### Edges and syllables {#edges}

| Rule | Example | Stage |
|------|---------|-------|
| [A content word ends in **-l**, **-m**, **-n**, or **-r**](/phonology#word-edges), the audible end | `uzozen` = *u-zo-zen* | B |
| [Inside a word, a consonant starts a syllable](/phonology#word-edges) and never closes one | `udozol` = *u-do-zol* | B |
| [Roots are vowel-first](/phonotactics#phonotactics): **V(CV)+** | `zubulel` = `z` + `ubule` + **-l** | I |
| [Place-name root](/singability#place-name-roots) with no cluster: seven letters, ends `n` + vowel | `azabanan` *Japan* | A |
| [Place-name root](/singability#place-name-roots) that keeps a cluster | `avranzan` *France* | A |
| [A root's last two letters hint at its kind and job](/root-tails#root-tails) | `agozo` *cat*: `z` a creature, `o` one of its kind | A |

### Parts of a word, in order {#parts-in-order}

| Part | What it is | Stage |
|------|------------|-------|
| [Role letter](/phonotactics#phonotactics) | first letter: the word's job in the clause | I |
| Optional `l` after `/ɡ/` | [adjective before the noun](/left-bound-adjectives#left-bound-adjectives) | B |
| Optional `l` after `/b/` | [`bl-`](/clause#extra-nouns): completes the word right before it | B |
| Root(s) | vowel-first; a [dictionary compound](/phonotactics#phonotactics) is one long root (`obahoha` *bedroom*); a root after a root is joined by mid-word `x` ([compounds](/compounds)) | I |
| Mid-word `th` | the stance seam: [scope](/label-seams#label-scope), [sakes](/sakes), [viewpoint](/viewpoint#viewpoint-laterals) | I |
| **-l** / **-m** / **-n** / **-r** | the [word ending](/word-endings) | B |
| **-ln** | [one of a name](/proper-names#name-instance--ln) | I |
| **-x** | [plural / associates](/plurality), after the ending | B |
| **-rl** / **-rm** | [stand-in or pinned resume](/dependents#dependent-clauses) | I |
| **-rth** | [pointing back](/sentence-linkers#stand-in-back): the only word-final `th` | I |

Word-initial `x` is the [continue](/dependents#continue-x) prefix, not a compound seam.

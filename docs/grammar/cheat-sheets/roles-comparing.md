# Roles and comparing cheat sheet {#roles-comparing-cheat-sheet}

A quick look at [role compounds](../roles.md), [mid-word `x`](../x-compounds.md), and [comparatives](../comparatives.md) on one page. Nothing here is new: each row links to the section that teaches it. Stage: **B** Beginner, **I** Intermediate, **A** Advanced.

## Naming a part of an event {#roles}

Write a thing letter (`/z/` `/d/` `/b/`), the role vowel, mid-word **`x`**, the event root, then the ending. The vowel picks the part. **-l** / **-m** / **-n** mean anyone in that part; **-r** means this instance, from the latest matching event.

| Vowel | Names | Example | English | Stage |
|-------|-------|---------|---------|-------|
| [**`a`**](../roles.md#role-compounds) | the doer | `zaxedehol` | *someone who teaches* | B |
| [**`e`**](../roles.md#the-place-e) | the scene (place or time) | `dexezebal` | *a place where someone sleeps* | B |
| [**`u`**](../roles.md#the-undergoer-u) | the undergoer | `zuxahahar` | *the one seen* | B |
| [**`o`**](../roles.md#the-extra-b-party-o) | the extra `/b/` party | `zoxezeber` | *the one told* | B |
| [**`ae`**](../roles.md#instrument) | the tool | `daexaradal` | *a writing tool* | I |
| [**`oe`**](../roles.md#goal-source-path) | the goal | `doexuvudel` | *a destination* | I |
| [**`ua`**](../roles.md#goal-source-path) | the source | `duaxobohor` | *where it was poured from* | I |
| [**`uo`**](../roles.md#goal-source-path) | the path | `duoxowogal` | *a walkway* | I |
| [**`ao`**](../roles.md#result) | the result, newly made | `daoxagozal` | *a building* | I |
| [**`ue`**](../roles.md#bearer) | the one who pays | `zuexaragum` | *a theft victim* | I |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [The one, from an earlier event](../roles.md#this-instance-r): **-r** | `zazawan vehahel. zaxehaher vowogal.` | *Azawan sits. The one sitting walks.* | B |
| [A noun in the stem](../roles.md#role-compounds): another **`x`** | `zaxodogaxowogal varahal.` | *Someone who walks dogs runs.* | B |
| [Is one who](../roles.md#saying-someone-is-a-teacher): on `/ɡ/` | `zazawan gaxedehol.` | *Azawan is one who teaches.* | B |
| [The usual role](../roles.md#usual-role): **`the`** before the ending | `zaxedehothel varahal.` | *A teacher runs.* | I |
| [Is a teacher](../roles.md#usual-role) | `zazawan gaxedehothel.` | *Azawan is a teacher.* | I |
| [The event itself](../roles.md#the-event-itself): no vowel | `davadal` | *a fight* | B |

A role compound has one role vowel, left of the first **`x`**. Its stem is always an event: `zaxamun` is not a word.

### Without the stem {#pointers}

A role pointer drops the stem. The pointer vowel picks the event by position: **`a`** the latest, **`e`** this sentence's own, **`o`** the other, **`u`** the one that left the part unsaid. **-r** is the participant, **-l** a new one of its kind, **-m** its part.

## Reading mid-word `x` {#x}

After the role letter, look at both sides of the **first** **`x`**. A normal root is longer than one vowel, so a lone vowel left of **`x`** means a role.

<!-- cheat-sheet: roles-comparing -->
| Left of **`x`** | Right of **`x`** | Reading | Example | Stage |
|-----------------|------------------|---------|---------|-------|
| [full root](../x-compounds.md#families-by-shape) | full root | one ordinary compound | `zebeyaxabodel` *peanut butter*; `zohuxaluden` (a multipart name) | B |
| [role vowel](../x-compounds.md#families-by-shape) | **`a`** / **`e`** / **`o`** / **`u`**, then **-r** / **-l** / **-m** | role pointer | `zaxar` *they*; `zaxal` *another one*; `zaxam` *what they did* | B |
| [role vowel](../x-compounds.md#families-by-shape) | a longer root | role compound | `zaxedehol` *someone who teaches* | B |
| [name](../x-compounds.md#conversation-length) | a single vowel, then **-n** | conversation length | *Azawan, I have time* | I |
| [longer root](../x-compounds.md#families-by-shape) | a single vowel | [ability](../intention.md#ability) | *can't sing right now* | I |

Mid-word **`th`** is another family: a sake root and a vowel (`gulothal`), any other root and a vowel ([label scope](../predication.md#label-scope)), or a direction root and a facing person (`gewezathazawan`). After a sake root, root-shaped letters are always the emotion tail.

<!-- cheat-sheet: roles-comparing -->
| Agazan | Use | English | Stage |

### Conversation length {#conversation-length}

Name + **`x`** + one vowel + **-n**, on a greeting or a call. At arrival it says how long you expect to talk; at departure, how soon you leave. The lesser bid sets the shared limit.

## More, as much, less {#comparing}

Write the names, then the rank join, then the shared adjective on `/ɡ/`. **`e`** ranks the first higher; **`ue`** the first lower; **`oe`** ties. **-l** is firm, **-m** open.

<!-- cheat-sheet: roles-comparing -->
| Agazan | Use | English | Stage |
|--------|-----|---------|-------|
| [Rank **`e`** (`zel`)](../comparatives.md#comparatives) | rank on a shared quality | *more … than* / *the …-est* | B |
| [Equality **`oe`** (`zoel` / `zoem`)](../comparatives.md#equatives) | same height on the quality | *as … as* / *about as … as* | B |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [More than](../comparatives.md#comparatives) | `zazawan zalahen zel gamadam.` | *Azawan is more challenging than Alahen.* | B |
| [The most](../comparatives.md#superlatives): one name | `zazawan zel gamadam.` | *Azawan is the most challenging.* | B |
| [Much more](../comparatives.md#degree): `/w/` before the adjective | `zazawan zalahen zel wohahal gamadam.` | *Azawan is much more challenging than Alahen.* | B |
| [As … as](../comparatives.md#equatives) | `zazawan zalahen zoel gamadam.` | *Azawan is as challenging as Alahen.* | B |
| [About as … as](../comparatives.md#equatives) | `zazawan zalahen zoem gabawal.` | *Azawan is about as bright as Alahen.* | B |
| [Both are](../comparatives.md#equatives): an *and*-list, no ranking | `zazawan zalahen zal gamadam` | *Azawan and Alahen are both challenging.* | B |
| [Not as … as](../comparatives.md#comparative-arity): `zuel` | `zalahen zazawan zuel gamadam` | *Alahen is not as challenging as Azawan.* | I |
| [No more … than](../comparatives.md#comparative-arity): deny with `zul` | `zalahen zazawan zel gamadam zul.` | *Alahen is no more challenging than Azawan.* | I |
| [Second biggest](../comparatives.md#place-on-a-scale): ordinal in `/w/` | `zazawan zel wredul gelavam.` | *Azawan is the second biggest.* | I |

### Other scales {#scales}

Keep the rank join; change what the shared word is.

| Scale | Example | English | Stage |
|-------|---------|---------|-------|
| [Manner](../comparatives.md#manner-scale): `/h/` right after the join | `zalahen zazawan zel hahegem vowogal.` | *Alahen walks more intensely than Azawan.* | I |
| [Amount](../comparatives.md#amount-scale): `gral` | `zagadulx zodogalx zel gral.` | *There are more cats than dogs.* | I |
| [Amount, fewer](../comparatives.md#amount-scale) | `zagadulx zodogalx zuel gral.` | *There are fewer cats than dogs.* | I |
| [Amount, as many](../comparatives.md#amount-scale) | `zagadulx zodogalx zoel gral.` | *There are as many cats as dogs.* | I |
| [Amount, by impression](../comparatives.md#amount-scale): `gram` | `zagadulx zodogalx zel gram.` | *It looks like there are more cats than dogs.* | I |
| [How many more](../comparatives.md#amount-scale): difference in `/b/` | `zagadulx zodogalx zel gral brarel.` | *There are three more cats than dogs.* | I |
| [Frequency](../comparatives.md#frequency-scale): `hral` | `zazawan zalahen zel hral vowogal.` | *Azawan walks more often than Alahen.* | I |
| [Time, later](../comparatives.md#time-scale): `bral` | `zazawan zalahen zel bral vevahal.` | *Azawan arrives later than Alahen.* | I |
| [Time, earlier](../comparatives.md#time-scale) | `zazawan zalahen zuel bral vevahal.` | *Azawan arrives earlier than Alahen.* | I |
| [The more …, the more …](../comparatives.md#covarying) | `zazawan hral vowogal xoel zalahen hral vezebal.` | *The more Azawan walks, the more Alahen sleeps.* | I |
| [The more …, the less …](../comparatives.md#covarying) | `zazawan hral vowogal xuel zalahen hral vezebal.` | *The more Azawan walks, the less Alahen sleeps.* | I |
| [Twice as](../comparatives.md#factor): factor after the adjective | `zazawan zalahen zoel gelavam hradul.` | *Azawan is twice as big as Alahen.* | I |
| [Half as](../comparatives.md#factor): inverse `hrudul` | `zalahen zazawan zoel gelavam hrudul.` | *Alahen is half as big as Azawan.* | I |

*Three times bigger* is `zoel` with `hrarel`, not `zel`. The covarying joins state only that the two go together, not which causes the other.

| [NOTIONAL `thavom`](../comparatives.md#stance-bars) | the imagined case | *than imagined* / *than in theory* | A |

| [WANT `thohul` / `thohum` / `thohur`](../comparatives.md#stance-bars) | the level wanted | *than wanted* / *than I wanted* | A |
| [ABIL `thezexal`](../comparatives.md#stance-bars) | what can be done | *as … as possible* | A |
| [REQUIRE `thumel` / `thumem` / `thumer`](../comparatives.md#stance-bars) | the rule, the demand, or custom | *up to code* / *than demanded* / *than customary* | A |
| [PERMIT `thegol` / `thegom` / `thegor`](../comparatives.md#stance-bars) | the rule, the grant, or what is tolerated | *over the limit* / *than they allow* | A |
| [CONSENT `thuxegom`](../comparatives.md#stance-bars) | the agreed terms | *more than agreed* | A |
| [speaker attitude `thevegem`, `thewedam`](../comparatives.md#stance-bars) | the case you hoped for or feared | *than hoped* / *than feared* | A |

The bar's ending keeps its own job: on a channel it is evidence strength (`thobal`, `thobar`). The bar's `/b/` is the source (REPORTED), the holder (WANT, REQUIRE, PERMIT, CONSENT), or whose cases set the pattern (PATTERN). Only stances that set a value are bars: a pole, MAY, MIRATIVE, DECISION, and ATTEMPT are not.

### Vague amounts {#vague}

English *many* and *often* rank against an unstated baseline. Agazan names the bar.

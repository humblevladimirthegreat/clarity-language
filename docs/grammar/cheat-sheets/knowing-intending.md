# Knowing and intending cheat sheet {#knowing-intending-cheat-sheet}

[Knowing](/knowing), [notional and standing](/residue-and-former), and [intention](/intention). Stage: **B** Beginner, **I** Intermediate, **A** Advanced. Ending: **-l** firm, **-m** default, **-r** for now.

## How you know {#how-you-know}

### Channels {#channels}

Pick one channel. LIVE and MEMORY place the event in time; the others do not.

<!-- cheat-sheet: knowing-intending -->
| Channel | Kind | Ageben | English | Stage |
|---------|------|--------|---------|-------|
| LIVE | direct | [`thugem`](/knowing#live-vs-memory) | *live* / *from the scene* | B |
| MEMORY | direct | [`thevum`](/knowing#live-vs-memory) | *from memory* | B |
| PATTERN | reasoned | [`thubom`](/evidentiality#evidentiality) | *from the pattern* | I |
| CLUES | reasoned | [`theyom`](/evidentiality#evidentiality) | *from the clues* / *apparently* | I |
| DEDUCED | reasoned | [`thanam`](/evidentiality#evidentiality) | *it follows* / *by deduction* | I |
| REPORTED | received | [`thebam`](/evidentiality#evidentiality) | *so they say* | I |
| INTUITION | internal | [`thabem`](/evidentiality#evidentiality) | *my gut says* | I |

### Evidence strength {#strength}

The ending grades evidence, not how sure you feel or how likely the event is: **-l** show, **-m** describe, **-r** gesture.

<!-- cheat-sheet: knowing-intending -->
| Channel | Graded on | **-l** show | **-m** describe | **-r** gesture | Stage |
|---------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|----------------|-------|
| [LIVE](/evidentiality#evidence-strength) | clarity of view | `thugel` a clear view | `thugem` | `thuger` a glimpse | I |
| [MEMORY](/evidentiality#evidence-strength) | vividness | `thevul` a vivid memory | `thevum` | `thevur` a hazy memory | I |
| [PATTERN](/evidentiality#evidence-strength) | cases, consistency | `thubol` well established | `thubom` | `thubor` a few cases | I |
| [CLUES](/evidentiality#evidence-strength) | strength of clues | `theyol` strong clues | `theyom` | `theyor` a weak clue | I |
| [DEDUCED](/evidentiality#evidence-strength) | how far you checked the steps | `thanal` every step shown | `thanam` | `thanar` seems to follow | I |
| [REPORTED](/evidentiality#evidence-strength) | reliability of source | `thebal` a reliable source | `thebam` | `thebar` a rumor | I |
| [INTUITION](/evidentiality#evidence-strength) | how well you can state it | `thabel` a strong sense | `thabem` | `thaber` a faint hunch | I |

### Adding to a channel {#channel-add}

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Name the source](/evidentiality#source): a noun in a `bl-` word right after | `zalazun thebam blazozon vudurul.` | *Alazun left, according to Azozon.* | I |
| [A device as the source](/evidentiality#source) | `zalazun thugem blugewel vezuvel.` | *Alazun is asleep, going by the camera.* | I |
| [A source you don't name](/evidentiality#source): `thebam bleyel blar`, then say whether you know who | `zalazun thebam bleyel blar vudurul. zuwen vanam derl zar.` | *Alazun left, according to someone. I know who.* | I |
| [How many hands](/evidentiality#hand-depth): `th#N` after a channel | `zalazun thebam throgel vudurul.` | *Alazun left, so they say, third-hand.* | I |
| [A forecast](/evidentiality#forecast): channel + `blral` | `thubom blral verulal.` | *It will rain, going by the pattern.* | I |
| [A hunch about later](/evidentiality#forecast) | `zalazun thaber blral vudurul.` | *Alazun will leave, a faint hunch.* | I |
| [A dated claim](/dated-channel#dated-channel): signed time measure | `zazozon thevum blagorum grugel vowurol.` | *I saw Azozon walk three hours ago.* | I |
| [Later, dated](/dated-channel#dated-channel) | `zalazun thebam blagorum gragel vudurul.` | *I hear Alazun leaves in three hours.* | I |
| [Give the grounds](/evidentiality#evidence-clause): `blarl`, grounds in the next sentence | `theyom thahum blarl` | *inferred, and it was caused* | I |

| Offset | Sign | Stage |
|--------|------|-------|
| [`thevum` MEMORY](/dated-channel#dated-channel) | **`-`** only | I |
| [`thugem` LIVE](/dated-channel#dated-channel) | no offset | I |
| [`thubom` PATTERN](/dated-channel#dated-channel) | **`-`** or **`+`** | I |

After a channel, a `bl-` person or thing is the [source](/evidentiality#source); a time measure is the [offset](/dated-channel#dated-channel). `blarl` takes only CLUES, PATTERN, and DEDUCED.

<!-- cheat-sheet: knowing-intending -->
| You say | Clause after `blarl` is | Form | Stage |
|---------|------------------------|------|-------|
| what made it happen | the cause | [`thahum blarl`](/evidentiality#evidence-clause) | I |
| what must hold for it | the condition | [`thohem blarl`](/evidentiality#evidence-clause) / `thebem blarl` / `thehom blarl` | I |
| how you know it happened | your grounds | [`theyom blarl`](/evidentiality#evidence-clause) / `thubom blarl` / `thanam blarl` | I |

### Deduction {#deduction}

What sits before `blarl` is what you assert: the channel alone asserts the premises; a hinge between them asserts only the link. Join premises with `xam`.

<!-- cheat-sheet: knowing-intending -->
| You claim | Form | You assert | Stage |
|-----------|------|------------|-------|
| *P, so Q* | Q [`thanam blarl`](/evidentiality#deduction) P | P and Q | I |
| *Q follows from P* | Q [`thanam thohem blarl`](/evidentiality#deduction) P | only the link | I |
| *P and Q come to the same thing* | Q [`thanam thehom blarl`](/evidentiality#deduction) P | only the link, both ways | I |
| *P rules out Q* | Q denied with `vul`, then [`thanam thohem blarl`](/evidentiality#deduction) P | only that P and Q cannot both hold | I |

### Whose view {#holder}

Join the name to a channel, MAY, or NOTIONAL: keep the word's last letter and write the name straight after it. The name keeps its own **-n**, which is never the seam.

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Holder on a channel](/holder#holder) | `zalazun theyomazozon vudurul.` | *I gather Azozon thinks Alazun left.* | I |
| [Holder on REPORTED](/holder#holder) | `zalazun thebamazozon vudurul.` | *I hear Azozon thinks Alazun left.* | I |
| [Holder on MAY](/holder#holder) | `zalazun thomamazozon vudurul.` | *Maybe Azozon thinks Alazun left.* | I |
| [Holder on NOTIONAL](/notional#notional) | `zalazun theyumazozon vudurul.` | *Imagine it as Azozon sees it.* | I |
| [Strength before the name](/holder#holder) | `zalazun theyolazozon vudurul.` | *Strong clues say Azozon thinks Alazun left.* | I |
| [Shared history](/holder#holder): MEMORY with the interlocutors | `zawan vebal hal thevumayan.` | *From what we both remember, you've never listened.* | I |

### Keeping a channel {#carry}

A channel you name is your frame, and later claims of yours keep it. See [carrying a channel forward](/evidentiality#carry-forward).

| It keeps the frame | It ends the frame |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| [your later statements](/evidentiality#carry-forward) | [a new channel or a holder](/evidentiality#carry-forward) · [NOTIONAL](/notional#notional) |
| [a new topic, sentence linkers, asides](/evidentiality#carry-forward) | [`yal` written out](/evidentiality#carry-forward) |
| [MAY](/evidentiality#carry-forward) · [MIRATIVE](/mirative#mirative) | [`xavedam` *by the way*, or a goodbye](/evidentiality#carry-forward) |

A question, command, request, or prohibition does not take the frame, and does not end it.

## Could be, imagined, surprising, still in effect {#stance}

MAY, NOTIONAL, and MIRATIVE are `/th/` words; RESIDUE and FORMER are `/h/` words that cover the whole clause. MAY is *could be* with nothing behind it; NOTIONAL is an imagined scene; MIRATIVE says the news ran against what you expected; RESIDUE says an outcome still counts; FORMER says a usual pattern is not claimed for now.

<!-- generated: overlays pos=th|h kind=may|notional|mirative|residue|former_climate -->
| Ageben | English | Taught in | Stage |
|--------|---------|-----------|-------|
| `haram` | residue mood (outcome still on the current tally) | [Residue and former climate](/residue-and-former#residue-and-former-climate) | I |
| `helam` | former-climate mood (not the climate claimed now) | [Residue and former climate](/residue-and-former#residue-and-former-climate) | I |
| `themel` | mirative mood overturning a firm expectation | [Mirative (themem)](/mirative#mirative) | I |
| `themem` | mirative mood (newly found out and not expected) | [Mirative (themem)](/mirative#mirative) | I |
| `themer` | mirative mood against a loose expectation | [Mirative (themem)](/mirative#mirative) | I |
| `theyul` | notional mood rehearsal follow-up (practicing a possible scene to prepare for action) | [Follow-up endings (NOTIONAL)](/notional#follow-up-endings-notional) | I |
| `theyum` | notional mood default follow-up (imagining a scene without a further purpose) | [Notional (as-if framing)](/notional#notional-as-if-framing) | I |
| `theyur` | notional mood suppose follow-up (toying with a possibility or assumption for now) | [Follow-up endings (NOTIONAL)](/notional#follow-up-endings-notional) | I |
| `thomal` | may mood find-out follow-up (I'll / let's find out) | [Follow-up endings (MAY)](/knowing#follow-up-endings-may) | B |
| `thomam` | may mood (could be; default) | [MAY](/knowing#may) | B |
| `thomar` | may mood who-knows follow-up (leave unsettled; may never know) | [Follow-up endings (MAY)](/knowing#follow-up-endings-may) | B |
<!-- /generated -->

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [MAY](/knowing#may) | `zalazun thomam dahelan vudurul.` | *Alazun may be leaving Ahelan.* | B |
| [MAY, ask to find out](/knowing#follow-up-endings-may) | `yel zazozon vabuvul thomal dalazun.` | *Might Azozon be punching Alazun? let's find out.* | B |
| [RESIDUE](/residue-and-former#residue-and-former-climate) | `zalazun haram vudurul.` | *Alazun has left and is still gone.* | I |
| [RESIDUE with a channel](/residue-and-former#residue-and-former-climate) | `zalazun thevum haram vudurul.` | *I remember Alazun leaving, and Alazun is still gone.* | I |
| [FORMER after the verb](/residue-and-former#residue-and-former-climate) | `zazozon hual vozuyel helam.` | *Azozon always used to sing.* | I |
| [A channel as the bar](/bars#channel-bars) | `thugem` before `zol` | *than it looks* | I |

Neither MAY nor MIRATIVE can be a [comparison bar](/bars#channel-bars).

### Already, still, not yet, no longer {#phasal}

Each compares what is true now with a change. Not a tense. **-l** is settled, **-m** could change back, **-r** only for now. *Not yet* is a `/th/` stance, since it waits on an expectation; its ending says how firmly you expect the change.

<!-- cheat-sheet: knowing-intending -->
| Meaning | **-l** | **-m** | **-r** | Stage |
|---------|--------|--------|--------|-------|
| *already* | [`havel`](/already-still-yet#phasal) *for good* | `havem` | `haver` *for now* | I |
| *still* | [`hawul`](/already-still-yet#phasal) *and will keep on* | `hawum` | `hawur` *about to end* | I |
| *not yet* | [`thelol`](/already-still-yet#phasal) *but it will* | `thelom` | `thelor` *maybe never* | I |
| *no longer* | [`huvul`](/already-still-yet#phasal) *never again* | `huvum` | `huvur` *paused* | I |

For *has ever*, use `havem` with `har`; *has never* is `hal` ([ever / never](/already-still-yet#ever-never)).

## Intending {#intending}

A want, a plan, and a decision are three steps, and they stack in that order. A want, a plan, and a decision are all `/th/` words (yours unless a holder names someone else), and none says the act happens.

| `howam` | attempt mood (commitment unstated) | [Try](/try#try) | I |
| `howar` | attempt mood trial run (low-stakes probe; failing is useful data) | [Try](/try#try) | I |
| `thagol` | plan mood atlas map-resolution (includes backups; fully prepared) | [Plan](/intention#plan) | B |

| [Someone else's plan](/holder#holder): a holder | `zazozon thebamazozon thagom vowurol.` | *I hear Azozon plans to walk.* | I |

### Ability {#ability}

After the root, write `x`, a vowel, and the ending the word would have anyway. Only a verb or adjective takes it; with no single verb, use `oda`.

<!-- cheat-sheet: knowing-intending -->
| Ageben | Use | English | Stage |
|--------|-----|---------|-------|
| [`xa`](/can-and-cant#can) | they can | *can* | B |
| [`xo`](/can-and-cant#ability-stance) | can't, just for now | *can't right now* | B |
| [`xe`](/can-and-cant#ability-stance) | can't yet | *can't yet* | B |
| [`xu`](/can-and-cant#ability-stance) | can never | *can never* | B |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Can](/can-and-cant#can) | `zazozon vozuyexal.` | *Azozon can sing.* | B |
| [So can I](/can-and-cant#can) | `zazozon vowuroxal. zalazun vowuroxar.` | *Azozon can walk, and so can Alazun.* | B |
| [Can't right now](/can-and-cant#ability-stance) | `zalazun vozuyexol.` | *Alazun can't sing right now.* | B |
| [Can't yet](/can-and-cant#ability-stance) | `zahelan vozuyexel.` | *Ahelan can't sing yet.* | B |

## Don't mix up {#dont-mix-up}

| This | vs | That |
|------|----|------|
| `thomam` *could be* ([a guess, no reasons](/knowing#may)) | | `theyom` *from the clues* ([reasons you worked out](/evidentiality#evidentiality)) |
| `thebam` *so they say* ([someone said it](/evidentiality#evidentiality)) | | `theyum` *imagine* ([a scene you make up](/notional#notional-as-if-framing)) |
| `theyol` *strong clues* ([how good your evidence is](/evidentiality#evidence-strength)) | | `throgel` *third-hand* ([how many hands](/evidentiality#hand-depth)) |
| `thubom blral` *will, by the pattern* ([a forecast](/evidentiality#forecast)) | | `thagom` *I plan to* ([an intention](/intention#plan-predict)) |
| `haram` *still in effect* ([the outcome stays](/residue-and-former#residue-and-former-climate)) | | `hawum` *still* ([the state goes on](/already-still-yet#phasal)) |
| `thogem` *I've decided* ([how open the choice is, no time](/want-and-decide#decision)) | | `thagom` *I plan* ([how worked out, still ahead](/intention#plan-predict)) |
| `howam` *tries* ([acts, outcome open](/try#try)) | | `vozuyexal` *can sing* ([a claim about ability](/can-and-cant#can)) |
| `thalom` *I want* ([your own wanting](/want-and-decide#want)) | | `thebamazozon thagom` *I hear Azozon plans* ([someone else's plan](/holder#holder)) |

# Knowing and intending cheat sheet {#knowing-intending-cheat-sheet}

A quick look at [knowing](../knowing.md) and [intention](../intention.md) on one page. Nothing here is new: each row links to the section that teaches it. Stage: **B** Beginner, **I** Intermediate, **A** Advanced.

Verbs have no tense. A `/th/` word says how you know, how you hold the claim, or what someone means to do, and the ending says how firm: **-l** firm, **-m** default, **-r** for now. When unsure, use **-m**.

## How you know {#how-you-know}

### Channels {#channels}

Pick one channel for how you know. LIVE and MEMORY place the event in time; the others do not.

<!-- cheat-sheet: knowing-intending -->
| Channel | Kind | Agazan | English | Stage |
|---------|------|--------|---------|-------|
| LIVE | direct | [`thodom`](../knowing.md#evidentiality) | *live* / *from the scene* | I |
| MEMORY | direct | [`thevom`](../knowing.md#evidentiality) | *from memory* | I |
| PATTERN | reasoned | [`thobam`](../knowing.md#evidentiality) | *from the pattern* | I |
| CLUES | reasoned | [`thunem`](../knowing.md#evidentiality) | *from the clues* / *apparently* | I |
| REPORTED | received | [`thewam`](../knowing.md#evidentiality) | *so they say* | I |
| INTUITION | internal | [`thahom`](../knowing.md#evidentiality) | *my gut says* | I |

### Evidence strength {#strength}

The ending grades your evidence: **-l** you could show it, **-m** you could describe it, **-r** you could only gesture at it. It is not how sure you feel or how likely the event is.

<!-- cheat-sheet: knowing-intending -->
| Channel | Graded on | **-l** show | **-m** describe | **-r** gesture | Stage |
|---------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|----------------|-------|
| [LIVE](../knowing.md#evidence-strength) | clarity of view | `thodol` a clear view | `thodom` | `thodor` a glimpse | I |
| [MEMORY](../knowing.md#evidence-strength) | vividness | `thevol` a vivid memory | `thevom` | `thevor` a hazy memory | I |
| [PATTERN](../knowing.md#evidence-strength) | cases, consistency | `thobal` well established | `thobam` | `thobar` a few cases | I |
| [CLUES](../knowing.md#evidence-strength) | strength of clues | `thunel` strong clues | `thunem` | `thuner` a weak clue | I |
| [REPORTED](../knowing.md#evidence-strength) | reliability of source | `thewal` a reliable source | `thewam` | `thewar` a rumor | I |
| [INTUITION](../knowing.md#evidence-strength) | how well you can state it | `thahol` a strong sense | `thahom` | `thahor` a faint hunch | I |

### Adding to a channel {#channel-add}

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Name the source](../knowing.md#source): a noun in `/b/` right after | `zalahen thewam bazawan vedabal.` | *Alahen left, according to Azawan.* | I |
| [A device as the source](../knowing.md#source) | `zalahen thodom bagahol vezebal.` | *Alahen is asleep, going by the camera.* | I |
| [How many hands](../knowing.md#hand-depth): `th#N` after a channel | `zalahen thewam threrel vedabal.` | *Alahen left, so they say, third-hand.* | I |
| [A forecast](../knowing.md#forecast): channel + `bral` | `thobam bral verehel.` | *It will rain, going by the pattern.* | I |
| [A hunch about later](../knowing.md#forecast) | `zalahen thahor bral vedabal.` | *Alahen will leave, a faint hunch.* | I |
| [A dated claim](../knowing.md#dated-channel): signed time measure | `zazawan thevom bagazem grurel vowogal.` | *I saw Azawan walk three hours ago.* | A |
| [Later, dated](../knowing.md#dated-channel) | `zalahen thewam bagazem grarel vedabal.` | *I hear Alahen leaves in three hours.* | A |
| [Give the grounds](../knowing.md#evidence-clause): `barl`, grounds in the next sentence | `thunem thevem barl` | *inferred, and it was caused* | I |

| Offset | Sign | Stage |
|--------|------|-------|
| [`thevom` MEMORY](../knowing.md#dated-channel) | **`-`** only | A |
| [`thodom` LIVE](../knowing.md#dated-channel) | no offset | A |
| [`thobam` PATTERN](../knowing.md#dated-channel) | **`-`** or **`+`** | A |

What a `/b/` after a channel does: a person or thing is the [source](../knowing.md#source); a time measure is the [offset](../knowing.md#dated-channel); `barl` takes only CLUES and PATTERN. A cause or condition word may not come before the channel.

<!-- cheat-sheet: knowing-intending -->
| You say | Clause after `barl` is | Form | Stage |
|---------|------------------------|------|-------|
| what made it happen | the cause | [`thevem barl`](../knowing.md#evidence-clause) | I |
| what must hold for it | the condition | [`thoyem barl`](../knowing.md#evidence-clause) / `tholum barl` / `thedam barl` | I |
| how you know it happened | your grounds | [`thunem barl`](../knowing.md#evidence-clause) / `thobam barl` | I |

### Whose view {#holder}

Join the name to a channel, MAY, or NOTIONAL: keep the word's last letter and write the name straight after it. The name keeps its own **-n**, which is never the seam.

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Holder on a channel](../knowing.md#holder) | `zalahen thunemazawan vedabal.` | *I gather Azawan thinks Alahen left.* | A |
| [Holder on REPORTED](../knowing.md#holder) | `zalahen thewamazawan vedabal.` | *I hear Azawan thinks Alahen left.* | A |
| [Holder on MAY](../knowing.md#holder) | `zalahen thovumazawan vedabal.` | *Maybe Azawan thinks Alahen left.* | A |
| [Holder on NOTIONAL](../knowing.md#holder) | `zalahen thavomazawan vedabal.` | *Imagine it as Azawan sees it.* | A |
| [Strength before the name](../knowing.md#holder) | `zalahen thunelazawan vedabal.` | *Strong clues say Azawan thinks Alahen left.* | A |

### Keeping a channel {#carry}

A channel you name is your frame, and later claims of yours keep it. See [carrying a channel forward](../knowing.md#carry-forward).

| It keeps the frame | It ends the frame |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| [your later statements](../knowing.md#carry-forward) | [a new channel, NOTIONAL, or a holder](../knowing.md#carry-forward) |
| [a new topic, sentence linkers, asides](../knowing.md#carry-forward) | [`yal` written out](../knowing.md#carry-forward) |
| [MIRATIVE or MAY](../knowing.md#carry-forward) | [`xavazem` *by the way*, or a goodbye](../knowing.md#carry-forward) |

A question, command, request, or prohibition does not take the frame, and does not end it.

## Could be, imagined, surprising, still in effect {#stance}

Each of these is a `/th/` word with the same ending scale. MAY is *could be* with nothing behind it; NOTIONAL is an imagined scene; MIRATIVE says the news ran against what you expected; RESIDUE says an outcome still counts; FORMER says a usual pattern is not claimed for now.

<!-- generated: overlays pos=th kind=may|notional|mirative|residue|former_climate -->
| Agazan | English | Taught in | Stage |
|--------|---------|-----------|-------|
| `thamom` | residue mood (outcome still on the current tally) | [Residue and former climate](../knowing.md#residue-and-former-climate) | I |
| `thavol` | notional mood rehearsal hold (practicing a possible scene to prepare for action) | [Hold endings (NOTIONAL)](../knowing.md#hold-endings-notional) | I |
| `thavom` | notional mood default hold (imagining a scene without a further purpose) | [Notional (as-if framing)](../knowing.md#notional-as-if-framing) | I |
| `thavor` | notional mood suppose hold (toying with a possibility or assumption for now) | [Hold endings (NOTIONAL)](../knowing.md#hold-endings-notional) | I |
| `thenom` | former-climate mood (not the climate claimed now) | [Residue and former climate](../knowing.md#residue-and-former-climate) | I |
| `thezul` | mirative mood overturning a firm expectation | [Mirative (thezum)](../knowing.md#mirative) | I |
| `thezum` | mirative mood (newly found out and not expected) | [Mirative (thezum)](../knowing.md#mirative) | I |
| `thezur` | mirative mood against a loose expectation | [Mirative (thezum)](../knowing.md#mirative) | I |
| `thovul` | may mood find-out hold (I'll / let's find out) | [Hold endings (MAY)](../knowing.md#hold-endings-may) | B |
| `thovum` | may mood (could be; default) | [MAY](../knowing.md#may) | B |
| `thovur` | may mood who-knows hold (leave unsettled; may never know) | [Hold endings (MAY)](../knowing.md#hold-endings-may) | B |
<!-- /generated -->

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [MAY](../knowing.md#may) | `zalahen thovum vedabal dahaben.` | *Alahen may be leaving Ahaben.* | B |
| [MAY, ask to find out](../knowing.md#may-hold) | `yol zazawan thovul vabahel dalahen.` | *Might Azawan be punching Alahen? let's find out.* | B |
| [RESIDUE](../knowing.md#residue-and-former-climate) | `zalahen thamom vedabal.` | *Alahen has left (and is still gone).* | I |
| [RESIDUE with a channel](../knowing.md#residue-and-former-climate) | `zalahen thevom thamom vedabal.` | *I remember Alahen leaving, and Alahen is still gone.* | I |
| [FORMER after the verb](../knowing.md#residue-and-former-climate) | `zazawan hual vezebel thenom.` | *Azawan always tells, a former pattern.* | I |
| [A channel as the bar](../knowing.md#channel-bars) | `thodom` before `zel` | *than it looks* | I |

Neither MAY nor MIRATIVE can be a [comparison bar](../knowing.md#channel-bars).

### Already, still, not yet, no longer {#phasal}

Each compares what is true now with a change. Not a tense. **-l** is settled, **-m** could change back, **-r** only for now.

<!-- cheat-sheet: knowing-intending -->
| Meaning | **-l** | **-m** | **-r** | Stage |
|---------|--------|--------|--------|-------|
| *already* | [`hohal`](../knowing.md#phasal) *for good* | `hoham` | `hohar` *for now* | I |
| *still* | [`hagel`](../knowing.md#phasal) *and will keep on* | `hagem` | `hager` *about to end* | I |
| *not yet* | [`huzel`](../knowing.md#phasal) *but it will* | `huzem` | `huzer` *maybe never* | I |
| *no longer* | [`hewel`](../knowing.md#phasal) *never again* | `hewem` | `hewer` *paused* | I |

For *has ever*, use `hoham` with `har`; *has never* is `hal` ([ever / never](../knowing.md#ever-never)).

## Intending {#intending}

A want, a plan, and a decision are three steps, and they stack in that order. All of them are `/th/` words before an ordinary verb, and none says the act happens.

| `thamam` | plan mood itinerary map-resolution (steps on the map) | [Plan](../intention.md#plan) | B |
| `thamar` | plan mood sketch map-resolution (direction-only intention) | [Plan](../intention.md#plan) | B |
| `thehul` | decision mood irreversible changeability (settled pick) | [Decision (revisability)](../intention.md#decision-revisability) | I |
| `thehum` | decision mood modifiable changeability (open to revision) | [Decision (revisability)](../intention.md#decision-revisability) | I |
| `thehur` | decision mood temporary changeability (pick for now) | [Decision (revisability)](../intention.md#decision-revisability) | I |
| `thohul` | want mood lasting (a wish that keeps returning) | [Want](../intention.md#want) | I |

| [Committed](../intention.md#try) | `zalahen thudul vowogal.` | *Alahen keeps trying to walk.* | I |
| [Want](../intention.md#want) | `zazawan thohum vowogal.` | *Azawan wants to walk.* | I |

The `/b/` right after PLAN holds whose plan it is or when the act comes, not both.

### Ability {#ability}

After the root, write `x`, a vowel, and the ending the word would have anyway. Only a verb or adjective takes it; with no single verb, use `eze`.

<!-- cheat-sheet: knowing-intending -->
| Agazan | Use | English | Stage |
|--------|-----|---------|-------|
| [`xa`](../intention.md#can) | they can | *can* | I |
| [`xe`](../intention.md#ability-stance) | can't, just for now | *can't right now* | I |
| [`xo`](../intention.md#ability-stance) | can't yet | *can't yet* | I |
| [`xu`](../intention.md#ability-stance) | can never | *can never* | I |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Can](../intention.md#can) | `zazawan vezehexal.` | *Azawan can sing.* | I |
| [Could, a past skill](../intention.md#can) | `zazawan vezehexal thenom.` | *Azawan could sing.* | I |
| [So can I](../intention.md#can) | `zazawan vowogaxal. zalahen vowogaxar.` | *Azawan can walk, and so can Alahen.* | I |
| [Can't right now](../intention.md#ability-stance) | `zalahen vezehexel.` | *Alahen can't sing right now.* | I |
| [Can't yet](../intention.md#ability-stance) | `zahaben vezehexol.` | *Ahaben can't sing yet.* | I |
| [Can never](../intention.md#ability-stance) | `zazawan varahaxul.` | *Azawan can never run.* | I |
| [No single verb](../intention.md#ability-fallback) | `zazawan thezexel.` | *Azawan can't right now.* | I |

## Don't mix up {#dont-mix-up}

| This | vs | That |
|------|----|------|
| `thovum` *could be* ([a guess, no reasons](../knowing.md#may)) | | `thunem` *from the clues* ([reasons you worked out](../knowing.md#evidentiality)) |
| `thewam` *so they say* ([someone said it](../knowing.md#evidentiality)) | | `thavom` *imagine* ([a scene you make up](../knowing.md#notional-as-if-framing)) |
| `thunel` *strong clues* ([how good your evidence is](../knowing.md#evidence-strength)) | | `threrel` *third-hand* ([how many hands](../knowing.md#hand-depth)) |
| `thobam bral` *will, by the pattern* ([a forecast](../knowing.md#forecast)) | | `thamam` *plans to* ([an intention](../intention.md#plan-predict)) |
| `thamom` *still in effect* ([the outcome stays](../knowing.md#residue-and-former-climate)) | | `hagem` *still* ([the state goes on](../knowing.md#phasal)) |
| `thehum` *has decided* ([how open the choice is](../intention.md#decision)) | | `thamam` *plans* ([how worked out](../intention.md#plan-predict)) |
| `thudum` *tries* ([acts, outcome open](../intention.md#try)) | | `vezehexal` *can sing* ([a claim about ability](../intention.md#can)) |
| `thohum` *wants* ([the subject's own wanting](../intention.md#want)) | | `thehum balahen` *Alahen decides* ([nothing asked of the subject](../intention.md#whose-intention)) |

# Word shape and clause cheat sheet {#word-shape-clause-cheat-sheet}

A quick look at [word endings](../word-endings.md), [the clause](../clause.md), and [predication](../predication.md) on one page. Nothing here is new: each row links to the section that teaches it. Stage: **B** Beginner, **I** Intermediate, **A** Advanced.

## Word shape {#word-shape}

A word in a sentence is **role letter + root + ending**. Outside a sentence (a list, a heading) you write a [citation](../word-endings.md#citation-forms): root + ending, no role letter.

### Endings {#endings}

Shown on one root, `uzazo`.

| Ending | Means | Example | English | Stage |
|--------|-------|---------|---------|-------|
| **-l** | [first mention, everyday kind](../word-endings.md#concrete-l) | `uzazol` | *swan* | B |
| **-m** | [first mention, published abstract](../word-endings.md#abstract-m) | `uzazom` | *grace* | B |
| **-n** | [a name](../word-endings.md#named-n-beginner) | `uzazon` | *Uzazon* | B |
| **-r** | [the same one again](../pronouns.md#resume-r) | `zuzazor` | *Uzazon* (again) | B |
| **-ln** | [one of a name](../word-endings.md#name-instance--ln) | `duzazoln` | *an Uzazon* | I |
| **-lnx** | [some of a name](../word-endings.md#name-instance--ln) | `duzazolnx` | *some Azawans* | I |
| **-x** after any of these | [associates, or a group](../plurality.md#associative) | `zuzazonx` | *Uzazon and associates* | B |

A greeting is [your own name and a period](../word-endings.md#greeting): `SELFn.`

### What **-n** names, by role {#n-by-role}

The role letter says the job ([role map](../word-endings.md#proper-name--n)); **-n** says the thing bears that name, not the kind the root describes.

| Role | **-n** there | Stage |
|------|--------------|-------|
| `/z/` `/d/` `/b/` | [a named person, place, work, or brand](../word-endings.md#proper-name--n) | I |
| `/v/` | a named event or rite | I |
| `/ɡ/` | a named style or brand | I |
| `/w/` | a named scale or grade | I |
| `/h/` | a named standard or frame | I |
| `/y/` | [call this named person](../word-endings.md#proper-name--n) | I |

### Names of more than one word {#long-names}

| Shape | Example | English | Stage |
|-------|---------|---------|-------|
| [Full name](../word-endings.md#multipart-names): given name, then family name on `/ɡ/` + **-n** | `zohun gulowon voworel.` | *Ohun Ulowon walks.* | I |
| [Full name](../word-endings.md#multipart-names) as a citation, and its resume | `ohun gulowon`; `zohun gulowon`; `zohur` | *Ohun Ulowon*; *Ohun* (the same person) | I |
| [Hyphenated name](../word-endings.md#multipart-names): roots joined by mid-word `x` | `zohuxulowon voworel.` | *Ohuxulowon walks.* | I |
| [One of a full name](../word-endings.md#name-instance--ln) | `zohuln gulowon` | *someone named Ohun Ulowon* | I |
| [Titled phrase](../word-endings.md#titled-phrases): **-n** on the hook, join, or span that packages it | `dedadal on dogadal` | *Coffee-Instead-of-Tea* (a cafe) | I |
| [Country and tradition](../word-endings.md#countries-traditions) on `ohuna` | `ohunan` / `gohunam` / `dohunal` | *Japan* / *Japanese* / *a Japanese flag* | I |

## The clause {#clause}

### Role letters {#role-letters}

From the [role letters](../clause.md#role-letters) table.

| Letter | Job | Stage |
|--------|-----|-------|
| [`/z/`](../clause.md#role-letters) | subject: who acts | B |
| `/d/` | direct object: who is acted on | B |
| `/b/` | extra noun: recipient, or the piece after a relation word | B |
| `/v/` | verb: the action | B |
| `/ɡ/` | adjective: a property of the noun before it | B |
| `/w/` | extra detail on the next `/ɡ/`, `/h/`, or `/th/` word | B |
| `/h/` | adverb: how, when, where | B |
| `/th/` | stance: how sure, how known, why, as if | B |

Calls and sentence glue use [`/y/`](../speech-moves.md) and [`/x/`](../dependents.md).

### Default order and moving words {#order}

| Rule | Example | English | Stage |
|------|---------|---------|-------|
| [Subject, verb](../clause.md#who-acts-and-the-action) | `zodozol voworel.` | *A dog walks.* | B |
| [Subject, object, verb](../clause.md#direct-object-d) | `zodozol degazol vanobol.` | *A dog sees a cat.* | B |
| [`/h/` and `/th/` go anywhere](../clause.md#adverbs-h) | `zodozol hedarem voworel.` | *A dog walks hastily.* | B |
| [Leave out the subject](../clause.md#no-subject): nobody is named as the doer | `duzazon vanobol.` | *Uzazon was seen.* | I |
| [Moving words changes nothing](../clause.md#direct-object-d) | `degazol zodozol vanobol.` | *A dog sees a cat.* | B |
| [Usual order by speech act](../speech-moves.md#act-word-order): question verb second, command verb first, prohibition verb late | `yol zuzazon vabavul dabagal.` · `yel vabavul dabagal.` · `yul dabagal vabavul.` | *Does Uzazon punch the box?* · *Punch the box!* · *Don't punch the box.* | B |
| [Fill-ask: blank first](../questions.md#fill-ask) | `yol dar zuzazon vanobol.` | *What does Uzazon see?* | B |
| [Contrast one word with `&`](../clause.md#english-emphasis) | `&zuzazon degazol vanobol.` · `zuzazon &degazol vanobol.` · `zuzazon degazol &vanobol.` | *It was Uzazon who saw the cat.* · *It's a cat that Uzazon sees.* (not something else) · *Uzazon sees the cat.* (rather than doing something else to it) | A |
| [Cleft with no contrast: plain sentence](../clause.md#english-emphasis) | `zuzazon degazol vanobol.` | *It's a cat that Uzazon sees.* (answering *what?*) | A |

### The extra noun `/b/` {#extra-noun}

Tell the two jobs apart by the word right before the `/b/` word.

| Right before `/b/` | Job | Example | English | Stage |
|--------------------|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| a `/ɡ/`, `/h/`, or `/th/` word | [completes that word](../clause.md#extra-nouns) (the host) | `humum buzazon` | *like Uzazon* | B |
| anything else | [who receives](../clause.md#extra-nouns) (at most one) | `zuzazon vezeyel bolozun.` | *Uzazon tells Olozun.* | B |
| a host, with `/w/` first | [`/w/` stays before the host](../clause.md#extra-nouns) | `weluzom humum buzazon` | *very like Uzazon* | B |
| a host's `/b/`, then `/ɡ/` | [describes the extra noun](../clause.md#complex-chaining) | `zayoyul gugol buzazon gozumol` | *a guard who is the same person as sleepy Uzazon* | I |
| two relations on one noun | [put one before with `gl-`](../clause.md#complex-chaining) | `glugol buzazon zayoyul gugol bebayel` | *a guard who is both Uzazon and the police officer* | I |

A [hook](../clause.md#complex-chaining) followed by `/b/` means *in* / *at* / *for*, not the person who receives.

## Predication {#predication}

Egabon has no word for *is*. The kind of claim comes from the pattern.

### Saying what someone is {#is}

| Job | Pattern | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|---------|-------|
| [Quality](../predication.md#classification-property) | noun + `/ɡ/` | `zuzazon gumahom.` | *Uzazon is challenging.* | B |
| [Belonging](../predication.md#classification-property) | noun + `/ɡ/` on **-m** | `zuzazon gohunam.` | *Uzazon is Japanese.* | B |
| [Kind or role](../predication.md#classification) | noun + kind on `/ɡ/` | `zuzazon godozol.` | *Uzazon is a dog.* | B |
| [In the middle of an event](../predication.md#in-progress) | noun + event root on `/ɡ/` | `zuzazon goworel.` | *Uzazon is walking.* | B |
| [Something exists](../predication.md#existence) | a lone new noun | `zodozol.` | *There is a dog.* | B |
| | a new noun + `/ɡ/` | `zodozol geluzom.` | *There is a big dog.* | B |
| [Same person](../predication.md#identity) | `gugol` + `/b/`, side by side | `zolozun gugol buzazon.` | *Olozun is Uzazon.* (one person, two names) | B |
| [Same one again](../predication.md#identity) | `gugol`, no `/b/` | `zolozun dugul gugol vuruyel.` | *Olozun is writing the same book.* (the book already mentioned) | B |
| [Basically the same](../predication.md#same-endings) | **-m** | `zolozun gugom buzazon` | *Olozun is basically Uzazon.* | I |
| [Not the same](../predication.md#same-endings) | `gul` after the pair | `zolozun gugol buzazon gul` | *Olozun is not Uzazon.* | I |
| [The one who is Uzazon](../predication.md#same-endings) | inside a noun phrase | `zobel gugol buzazon` | *the person who is Uzazon* | I |

With a name or a resumed noun, the pair is a [property](../predication.md#existence), not a new thing: `zodozor geluzom.` is *The dog is big.*

| Job | Example | English | Stage |

### How far a label reaches {#label-scope}

Root, mid-word `th`, a scope vowel, then the ending. It goes on `/ɡ/`, `/z/`, `/d/`, `/b/`, `/v/`, and `/h/`, and on a [role compound](../predication.md#scope-role).

| Vowel | Reach | Example | English | Stage |
|-------|-------|---------|---------|-------|
| [`tha`](../predication.md#scope-occasion) | this episode | `zuzazon vulamuthal.` | *Uzazon lied this once.* | I |
| [`the`](../predication.md#scope-occupation) | a practiced role | `zolozun gaxodeyethel.` | *Olozun is a teacher.* | I |
| [`tho`](../predication.md#scope-relative) | this pair (`/b/` follows) | `zuzazon geleyothom bolozun.` | *Uzazon is a stranger to Olozun.* | I |
| [`thu`](../predication.md#scope-type) | a type across scenes | `zolozun ganumuthul.` | *Olozun is an anxious type.* | I |

[Stacked scope](../predication.md#scope-stacks): pairs that start with **u** set the second vowel's reach aside.

| Vowel | Reach | English | Stage |
|-------|-------|---------|-------|
| `thao` | this episode, on this pair | *teacher this time, to Olozun* | I |
| `thae` | using the role as a means | *using teacher (as a tool)* | I |
| `thoe` | toward the role | *becoming a teacher* | I |
| `thua` | type, except this episode | *a teacher type, except this time* | I |
| `thuo` | type, except this pair | *a teacher type, except with Olozun* | I |
| `thue` | not in that capacity | *not as a teacher* | I |

## Don't mix up {#dont-mix-up}

| This | vs | That |
|------|----|------|
| `zuzazon godozol.` *Uzazon is a dog* ([kind](../predication.md#classification)) | | `zolozun gugol buzazon.` *Olozun is Uzazon* ([one person, two labels](../predication.md#identity)) |
| `humum buzazon` *like Uzazon* ([`/b/` completes a host](../clause.md#extra-nouns)) | | `zuzazon vezeyel bolozun.` *Uzazon tells Olozun* ([`/b/` receives](../clause.md#extra-nouns)) |
| `zodozol geluzom.` *There is a big dog* ([new noun](../predication.md#existence)) | | `zodozor geluzom.` *The dog is big* ([resumed noun](../predication.md#existence)) |
| `zuzazon voworel.` *Uzazon walks* ([the verb](../clause.md#who-acts-and-the-action)) | | `zuzazon goworel.` *Uzazon is walking* ([under way](../predication.md#in-progress)) |
| `duzazon vanobol.` *Uzazon was seen* ([no subject named](../clause.md#no-subject)) | | `zunan` *someone did it* ([an unnamed someone](../pronouns.md#special-pronouns)) |
| `duzazol` *a swan* ([-l](../word-endings.md#proper-name--n)) | | `duzazoln` *an Uzazon* ([-ln: one of the name](../word-endings.md#name-instance--ln)) |

# Word shape and clause cheat sheet {#word-shape-clause-cheat-sheet}

A quick look at [word endings](../word-endings.md), [the clause](../clause.md), and [predication](../predication.md) on one page. Nothing here is new: each row links to the section that teaches it. Stage: **B** Beginner, **I** Intermediate, **A** Advanced.

## Word shape {#word-shape}

A word in a sentence is **role letter + root + ending**. Outside a sentence (a list, a heading) you write a [citation](../word-endings.md#citation-forms): root + ending, no role letter.

### Endings {#endings}

Shown on one root, `azawa`.

| Ending | Means | Example | English | Stage |
|--------|-------|---------|---------|-------|
| **-l** | [first mention, everyday kind](../word-endings.md#concrete-l) | `azawal` | *swan* | B |
| **-m** | [first mention, published abstract](../word-endings.md#abstract-m) | `azawam` | *grace* | B |
| **-n** | [a name](../word-endings.md#named-n-beginner) | `azawan` | *Azawan* | B |
| **-r** | [the same one again](../pronouns.md#resume-r) | `zazawar` | *Azawan* (again) | B |
| **-ln** | [one of a name](../word-endings.md#name-instance--ln) | `dazawaln` | *an Azawan* | I |
| **-lnx** | [some of a name](../word-endings.md#name-instance--ln) | `dazawalnx` | *some Azawans* | I |
| **-x** after any of these | [associates, or a group](../plurality.md#associative) | `zazawanx` | *Azawan and associates* | B |

A greeting is [your own name and a period](../word-endings.md#greeting): `SELFn.`

### What **-n** names, by role {#n-by-role}

The role letter says the job ([role map](../word-endings.md#proper-name--n)); **-n** says the thing bears that name, not the kind the root describes.

| Role | **-n** there | Stage |
|------|--------------|-------|
| `/z/` `/d/` `/b/` | [a named person, place, work, or brand](../word-endings.md#proper-name--n) | I |
| `/v/` | a named event or rite | I |
| `/ɡ/` | a named style or brand | I |
| `/w/` | a named scale or grade | I |
| `/h/` | a named standard or frame | I |
| `/y/` | [call this named person](../word-endings.md#proper-name--n) | I |

### Names of more than one word {#long-names}

| Shape | Example | English | Stage |
|-------|---------|---------|-------|
| [Full name](../word-endings.md#multipart-names): given name, then family name on `/ɡ/` + **-n** | `zohun galuden vowogal.` | *Ohun Aluden walks.* | I |
| [Full name](../word-endings.md#multipart-names) as a citation, and its resume | `ohun galuden`; `zohun galuden`; `zohur` | *Ohun Aluden*; *Ohun* (the same person) | I |
| [Hyphenated name](../word-endings.md#multipart-names): roots joined by mid-word `x` | `zohuxaluden vowogal.` | *Ohuxaluden walks.* | I |
| [One of a full name](../word-endings.md#name-instance--ln) | `zohuln galuden` | *someone named Ohun Aluden* | I |
| [Titled phrase](../word-endings.md#titled-phrases): **-n** on the hook, join, or span that packages it | `dedehel on dagavel` | *Coffee-Instead-of-Tea* (a cafe) | I |
| [Country and tradition](../word-endings.md#countries-traditions) on `aheba` | `aheban` / `gahebam` / `dahebal` | *Japan* / *Japanese* / *a Japanese flag* | I |

## The clause {#clause}

### Role letters {#role-letters}

From the [role letters](../clause.md#role-letters) table.

| Letter | Job | Stage |
|--------|-----|-------|
| [`/z/`](../clause.md#role-letters) | subject: who acts | B |
| `/d/` | direct object: who is acted on | B |
| `/b/` | extra noun: recipient, or the piece after a relation word | B |
| `/v/` | verb: the action | B |
| `/ɡ/` | adjective: a property of the noun before it | B |
| `/w/` | extra detail on the next `/ɡ/`, `/h/`, or `/th/` word | B |
| `/h/` | adverb: how, when, where | B |
| `/th/` | stance: how sure, how known, why, as if | B |

Calls and sentence glue use [`/y/`](../speech-moves.md) and [`/x/`](../dependents.md).

### Default order and moving words {#order}

| Rule | Example | English | Stage |
|------|---------|---------|-------|
| [Subject, verb](../clause.md#who-acts-and-the-action) | `zodogal vowogal.` | *A dog walks.* | B |
| [Subject, object, verb](../clause.md#direct-object-d) | `zodogal dagadul vahahal.` | *A dog sees a cat.* | B |
| [`/h/` and `/th/` go anywhere](../clause.md#adverbs-h) | `zodogal hadehum vowogal.` | *A dog walks hastily.* | B |
| [Leave out the subject](../clause.md#no-subject): nobody is named as the doer | `dazawan vahahal.` | *Azawan was seen.* | I |
| [First word is highlighted](../clause.md#word-order-emphasis) | `dagadul zazawan vahahal.` | *It's a cat that Azawan sees.* | A |
| [Subject contrasted](../clause.md#word-order-emphasis) with `&` | `&zazawan dagadul vahahal.` | *It was Azawan who saw the cat.* | A |
| [Verb first](../clause.md#word-order-emphasis) | `vahahal zazawan dagadul.` | *What Azawan does is see a cat.* | A |
| [Adverb first](../clause.md#word-order-emphasis) | `hadehum zazawan vowogal.` | *Hastily, Azawan walks.* | A |

### Describing words {#describing}

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Adjective after the noun](../clause.md#adjectives-ɡ) | `zodogal gubuhel vowogal.` | *A blue dog walks.* | B |
| [Adjective before the noun](../clause.md#left-bound-adjectives): `l` after `/ɡ/` | `glubuhel zodogal` | *a blue dog* | I |
| [Detail on an adjective](../clause.md#adjective-detail-w) | `zodogal welavam gubuhel vowogal.` | *A very blue dog walks.* | B |
| [Stance on the whole claim](../clause.md#stance-th) | `zodogal thevegam vowogal.` | *I doubt a dog is walking.* | B |

### Degree words {#degree}

| Slot | Words | English | Stage |
|------|-------|---------|-------|
| [`/w/`](../clause.md#degree-w), before an adjective or adverb | `welavam` | *very* | B |
| | `wohahal` | *extremely* | I |

| | `wahahom` | *completely* | I |

### The extra noun `/b/` {#extra-noun}

Tell the two jobs apart by the word right before the `/b/` word.

| Right before `/b/` | Job | Example | English | Stage |
|--------------------|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| a `/ɡ/`, `/h/`, or `/th/` word | [completes that word](../clause.md#extra-nouns) (the host) | `humum bazawan` | *like Azawan* | B |
| anything else | [who receives](../clause.md#extra-nouns) (at most one) | `zazawan vezebel balahen.` | *Azawan tells Alahen.* | B |
| a host, with `/w/` first | [`/w/` stays before the host](../clause.md#extra-nouns) | `welavam humum bazawan` | *very like Azawan* | B |
| a host's `/b/`, then `/ɡ/` | [describes the extra noun](../clause.md#complex-chaining) | `zodogal gugol bazawan gubuhel` | *a dog the same as blue Azawan* | I |
| two relations on one noun | [put one before with `gl-`](../clause.md#complex-chaining) | `glugol bazawan zodogal gugol balahen` | *a dog the same as Azawan, the same as Alahen* | I |

A [hook](../clause.md#complex-chaining) followed by `/b/` means *in* / *at* / *for*, not the person who receives.

## Predication {#predication}

Agazan has no word for *is*. The kind of claim comes from the pattern.

### Saying what someone is {#is}

| Job | Pattern | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|---------|-------|
| [Quality](../predication.md#classification-property) | noun + `/ɡ/` | `zazawan gamadam.` | *Azawan is challenging.* | B |
| [Belonging](../predication.md#classification-property) | noun + `/ɡ/` on **-m** | `zazawan gahebam.` | *Azawan is Japanese.* | B |
| [Kind or role](../predication.md#classification) | noun + kind on `/ɡ/` | `zazawan godogal.` | *Azawan is a dog.* | B |
| [In the middle of an event](../predication.md#in-progress) | noun + event root on `/ɡ/` | `zazawan gowogal.` | *Azawan is walking.* | B |
| [Something exists](../predication.md#existence) | a lone new noun | `zodogal.` | *There is a dog.* | B |
| | a new noun + `/ɡ/` | `zodogal gelavam.` | *There is a big dog.* | B |
| [Same person](../predication.md#identity) | `gugol` + `/b/`, side by side | `zalahen gugol bazawan.` | *Alahen is Azawan.* | B |
| [Same one again](../predication.md#identity) | `gugol`, no `/b/` | `zalahen dubugal gugol varadal.` | *Alahen writes the same book.* | B |
| [Basically the same](../predication.md#same-endings) | **-m** | `zalahen gugom bazawan` | *Alahen is basically Azawan.* | I |
| [Not the same](../predication.md#same-endings) | `gul` after the pair | `zalahen gugol bazawan gul` | *Alahen is not Azawan.* | I |
| [The one who is Azawan](../predication.md#same-endings) | inside a noun phrase | `zobel gugol bazawan` | *the person who is Azawan* | I |

With a name or a resumed noun, the pair is a [property](../predication.md#existence), not a new thing: `zodogar gelavam.` is *The dog is big.*

### Hedging, denying, commanding {#packaging}

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Not](../predication.md#classification-packaging) | `zazawan godogal gul.` | *Azawan is not a dog.* | I |
| [Always](../predication.md#classification-packaging) | `zazawan godogal hual` | *Azawan is always a dog.* | I |
| [Sort of](../predication.md#classification-packaging) | `zazawan wabedem godogal.` | *Azawan is somewhat of a dog.* | I |
| [Be ADJ!](../predication.md#command-property) | `yel geyayem.` | *Be careful!* | I |
| [Don't be ADJ](../predication.md#command-property) | `yul` + `/ɡ/` | | I |

### How far a label reaches {#label-scope}

Root, mid-word `th`, a scope vowel, then the ending. It goes on `/ɡ/`, `/z/`, `/d/`, `/b/`, `/v/`, and `/h/`, and on a [role compound](../predication.md#scope-role).

| Vowel | Reach | Example | English | Stage |
|-------|-------|---------|---------|-------|
| [`tha`](../predication.md#scope-occasion) | this episode | `zazawan valahathal.` | *Azawan lied this once.* | I |
| [`the`](../predication.md#scope-occupation) | a practiced role | `zalahen gaxedehothel.` | *Alahen is a teacher.* | I |
| [`tho`](../predication.md#scope-relative) | this pair (`/b/` follows) | `zazawan gelehothom balahen.` | *Azawan is a stranger to Alahen.* | I |
| [`thu`](../predication.md#scope-type) | a type across scenes | `zalahen ganagothul.` | *Alahen is an anxious type.* | I |

[Stacked scope](../predication.md#scope-stacks): pairs that start with **u** set the second vowel's reach aside.

| Vowel | Reach | English | Stage |
|-------|-------|---------|-------|
| `thao` | this episode, on this pair | *teacher this time, to Alahen* | I |
| `thae` | using the role as a means | *using teacher (as a tool)* | I |
| `thoe` | toward the role | *becoming a teacher* | I |
| `thua` | type, except this episode | *a teacher type, except this time* | I |
| `thuo` | type, except this pair | *a teacher type, except with Alahen* | I |
| `thue` | not in that capacity | *not as a teacher* | I |

## Don't mix up {#dont-mix-up}

| This | vs | That |
|------|----|------|
| `zazawan godogal.` *Azawan is a dog* ([kind](../predication.md#classification)) | | `zalahen gugol bazawan.` *Alahen is Azawan* ([one person, two labels](../predication.md#identity)) |
| `humum bazawan` *like Azawan* ([`/b/` completes a host](../clause.md#extra-nouns)) | | `zazawan vezebel balahen.` *Azawan tells Alahen* ([`/b/` receives](../clause.md#extra-nouns)) |
| `zodogal gelavam.` *There is a big dog* ([new noun](../predication.md#existence)) | | `zodogar gelavam.` *The dog is big* ([resumed noun](../predication.md#existence)) |
| `zazawan vowogal.` *Azawan walks* ([the verb](../clause.md#who-acts-and-the-action)) | | `zazawan gowogal.` *Azawan is walking* ([under way](../predication.md#in-progress)) |
| `dazawan vahahal.` *Azawan was seen* ([no subject named](../clause.md#no-subject)) | | `zunan` *someone did it* ([an unnamed someone](../pronouns.md#special-pronouns)) |
| `dazawal` *a swan* ([-l](../word-endings.md#proper-name--n)) | | `dazawaln` *an Azawan* ([-ln: one of the name](../word-endings.md#name-instance--ln)) |

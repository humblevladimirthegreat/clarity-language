# Vowels cheat sheet {#vowels-cheat-sheet}

How the four vowels are reused. Sounds are on [Sounds and spelling](/cheat-sheets/sounds-spelling). Nothing here is new: each row links to the section that teaches it. Stage: **B** Beginner, **I** Intermediate, **A** Advanced.

The usual map is **a** add, **o** one, **e** order, **u** undo. Jobs that share a mapping sit in one table. Pointer vowels, tags, size, and pitch use the same letters for other jobs.

## Act words {#acts}

Stand-ins and say-word answers use the same vowels as the [act words](/speech-moves#speech-act-beginner): **a** hold, **o** pick, **e** instruct, **u** take back.

| Job | **a** | **o** | **e** | **u** | Stage |
|-----|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| [Speech act](/speech-moves#speech-act-beginner) | statement (`yal`) | question (`yol`) | command (`yel`) | prohibition (`yul`) | B |
| [Stand-in](/sentence-linkers#stand-in) | `darl` *that …* | `dorl` *whether …* | `derl` *to …* | `durl` *lest / not to …* | B–I |
| [Say-word answer](/feelings-permission#say-words) | allow | their call | require | forbid | I |

### Polar answers {#polar}

Polar answers stack two of these vowels. Beginner *yes* / *no* / *sure*: [polar stance](/questions#polar-stance). The rest: [polar inventory](/polar-inventory).

| Vowels | Polar | Stage |
|--------|-------|-------|
| **ae** | `yael` *yes* / *will do* | B |
| **ue** | `yuel` *no* / *I won't* | B |
| **ao** | `yaol` *sure* | B |
| **uo** | `yuol` *not that* | I |
| **ua** | `yual` *doesn't apply* | I |
| **oe** | `yoel` *I won't answer* | I |

## Joins {#series}

[Joins](/joins#join-type-vowel-series), [same-role hooks](/hooks#including), and [discourse glue](/hooks#discourse-hooks) share **a** add, **o** one, **e** order, **u** undo.

| Job | **a** | **o** | **e** | **u** | Stage |
|-----|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| [Join](/joins#join-type-vowel-series) | *and* | pick one | rank: first matters most | *not* / *none of* | B |
| [Same-role hook](/hooks#including) | [*including*](/hooks#including) | [*instead*](/hooks#instead) | [*or rather*](/hooks#rather) | [*except*](/hooks#except) | B |
| [Discourse glue](/hooks#discourse-hooks) | `al` *Additionally* | `ol` *Instead* | `el` *In other words* | `ul` *Except* | B |
| [Hook resume, front](/discourse-hooks#hook-resume) | `ar` *Going back to that* | `or` *Anyway* | `er` *As I said* | `ur` *Never mind that* | I |
| [Number marker](/number-markers#marker-vowel-referential-identity) | `+` count | `_` digit string | `#` ordinal | `-` negative | I |
| [Restrictor](/restrictors) | `hal` *only when* / *never* | `hor` *anytime* | | `hur` *some other time*; `hual` *always* | B |
| [Conversation length](/compound-families#conversation-length) | `SELFxan` open-ended | `SELFxon` one slot | `SELFxen` a few minutes | `SELFxun` passing | I |
| [Comparative](/comparatives#comparatives) / [equative](/comparatives#equatives) | | `zoel` *as … as* | `zel` *more … than* | | B |
| [Sake stance](/sakes#word-shape) | `tha` met / serves | | | `thu` unmet / detracts | B |
| [Root job](/root-tails#root-tails) | holds or gathers | one whole thing | order, path, sequence | takes away | A |

## Root job and size {#root-vowels}

From [root tails](/root-tails#root-tails). Memory aids only: grammar does not read them.

| Last vowel | Job | Examples | Stage |
|------------|-----|----------|-------|
| `a` | holds or gathers | `abowa` *basket*, `oruda` *rice*, `uneza` *ant* | A |
| `o` | one whole thing | `agozo` *cat*, `oledo` *apple* | A |
| `e` | order, path, sequence | `orahe` *road*, `egore` *clock*, `aruve` *run*, `urule` *rainbow* | A |
| `u` | takes away | `anewu` *knife*, `ezozu` *snake*, `evemu` *fear* | A |

| [Middle vowel](/root-tails#root-tails) | Size | Stage |
|----------------------------------------|------|-------|
| `a` | about your size or bigger | A |
| `o` | between your hand and you | A |
| `e` | no bigger than your hand | A |
| `u` | nothing you could measure | A |

Feelings have no size: a feeling's middle vowel carries no meaning.

## Other jobs {#other-jobs}

These use the same letters, not the add / one / order / undo map.

| Job | **a** | **o** | **e** | **u** | Stage |
|-----|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| [Pointer](/pronouns#role-pointers) | latest with that part (`zwazar`) | [the other one](/pronouns#the-other-one) (`zwazor`) | [this sentence](/pronouns#themself) (`dwazer`) | [left unsaid](/pronouns#whoever-it-was) (`zwazur`) | B |
| [Tag](/tag-pronouns#tag-pronouns) | tag A (`zwar`) | tag O | tag E | tag U | I |
| [Pitch](/phonology#vowel-pitch) | low | mid-low | mid-high | high | B |

Pitch adds no meaning. Pointer vowels are taught as *again* / *other* / *echo* / *unsaid*, not as the series.

# Vowels cheat sheet {#vowels-cheat-sheet}

How the four vowels are reused. Sounds are on [Sounds and spelling](/cheat-sheets/sounds-spelling). Nothing here is new: each row links to the section that teaches it. Stage: **B** Beginner, **I** Intermediate, **A** Advanced.

The letter order **a o e u** has two sibling **scales**: size is amount (biggest to smallest), order is sequence (first to fourth). Each **series** reuses one scale’s leftover-logic. A **hang cue** says why.

## Scales and series {#scales}

| Series | Parent | Hang cue | **a** | **o** | **e** | **u** | Stage |
|--------|--------|----------|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| [Size](/root-tails#root-tails) (scale) | — | how much is there | big | medium | small | none | A |
| [Order](/numbers#counts) (scale) | — | which slot in a count of four | first | second | third | fourth | B |
| [Join](/joins#join-type-vowel-series) | size | how much list is left | *and* (grows) | rank (stays) | choice (one) | *not* (gone) | B |
| [Act](/speech-moves#speech-act-beginner) | join | what you do to the discourse list | statement | command | question | prohibition | B |
| [Hook](/hooks#including) | join | how much of A is still in the slot | *including* | *rather* | *instead* | *except* | B |
| [Extra-noun](/hooks#extra-noun) | hook (same spelling) | landmark as the hook job | *in* | *for* | *at* | *from* | B |
| [Scope](/label-seams#label-scope) | size | how far the label reaches | type | practiced role | this pair | this episode | I |
| [Ability](/can-and-cant#ability) | size | how much can-do is in | can | can't right now | can't yet | never | B |
| [Pointer](/pronouns#role-pointers) | size | how far the pointing reaches | the other one | this sentence | latest | unsaid | B |
| [Sake](/sakes#word-shape) | size | how much of the sake is in | met | motive | prescription | unmet | B |
| [Tag](/tag-pronouns#tag-pronouns) | order | same assignment queue as digits | first (A) | second (O) | third (E) | last (U) | I |
| [Digit](/numbers#counts) | order | next unused vowel | `ba` `za` `na` | `do` `vo` `mo` | `ge` `re` | `hu` `lu` | B |
| [Finger code](/finger-code#counting) (vowel hand) | order | pinky-first count | 1 (pinky) | 2 | 3 | 4 | B |

### How the scales hang {#vowel-tree}

```text
four vowels
├── size — how much stuff
│     ├── middle vowel — how big the thing is
│     ├── capability — how much can-do is in
│     ├── pointer vowel — how far the pointing reaches
│     ├── conversation length — how much talk is left
│     ├── sake — how much of the sake is in
│     ├── scope — how far the label reaches
│     │     ├── label scope
│     │     ├── role compound
│     │     ├── say-word scope
│     │     ├── landmark facing
│     │     ├── pointer scope slot
│     │     └── stacked scope (writes scope, reads as join / hook)
│     └── join — how much list is left
│           ├── stacked joins
│           ├── unspecified member
│           ├── restrictors / more occasions
│           ├── comparatives / equative
│           ├── number markers
│           ├── join-act / join-relation
│           ├── root job — what the root does to stuff / tail suffixes
│           ├── hook — how much of A is still in the slot
│           │     ├── same-role
│           │     ├── discourse glue
│           │     ├── hook resume
│           │     ├── hook prefix / hooked join
│           │     ├── ranges
│           │     └── place hook (same spelling; landmark as hook job)
│           │           ├── stacked place hook (writes hook, reads as join stacks)
│           │           └── feeling loci
│           └── act — what you do to the discourse list
│                 ├── speech act
│                 ├── stand-in
│                 ├── say-word answer
│                 ├── polar / polar inventory
│                 └── rhetorical / quiz stacks
└── sequence — which slot in a count of four
      ├── digit names — next unused vowel
      │     └── letter names — opposite, so naming is not counting
      ├── tags — same assignment queue: A first, U last
      ├── vowel pitch — the count climbs: low to high
      └── finger-code vowel hand — pinky-first count; consonant hand does not
```

A say word is scope then act (`hwuvom`: this-act **u**, require **o**). A role pointer is scope then pointer (`zwuzer`: episode **u**, latest **e**).

| `o` | between your hand and you | A |
| `e` | no bigger than your hand | A |

## Join {#series}

[Joins](/joins#join-type-vowel-series) use leftover-list: **a** grows, **o** ranks, **e** chooses one, **u** takes away. [Same-role hooks](/hooks#including) hang on join: how much of A is still in the slot.

| Job | **a** | **o** | **e** | **u** | Stage |
|-----|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| [Join](/joins#join-type-vowel-series) | *and* | rank: first matters most | pick one | *not* / *none of* | B |
| [Same-role hook](/hooks#including) | [*including*](/hooks#including) | [*or rather*](/hooks#rather) | [*instead*](/hooks#instead) | [*except*](/hooks#except) | B |
| [Discourse glue](/hooks#discourse-hooks) | `al` *Additionally* | `ol` *In other words* | `el` *Instead* | `ul` *Except* | B |
| [Hook resume, front](/discourse-hooks#hook-resume) | `ar` *Going back to that* | `or` *As I said* | `er` *Anyway* | `ur` *Never mind that* | I |
| [Number marker](/number-markers#marker-vowel-referential-identity) | `+` count | `#` ordinal | `_` digit string | `-` negative | I |
| [Restrictor](/restrictors) | `hal` *only when* / *never* | | `her` *anytime* | `hur` *some other time*; `hual` *always* | B |
| [Comparative](/comparatives#comparatives) / [equative](/comparatives#equatives) | | `zol` *more … than* | | | B |
| [Root job](/root-tails#root-tails) | holds or gathers | order, path, sequence | one whole thing | takes away | A |

Equative **`oe`** `zoel` *as … as* is a join stack ([rank joins](/rank-joins#rank-joins)).

## Extra nouns {#place}

[Extra-noun hooks](/hooks#extra-noun) are the hook spelling after `/b/`. [Feeling loci](/feelings-permission#emotion-compose) reuse them: **a** *in*, **o** *for*, **e** *at*, **u** *from*.

| Job | **a** | **o** | **e** | **u** | Stage |
|-----|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| [Extra-noun](/hooks#extra-noun) | *in* | *for* | *at* | *from* | B |
| [Feeling locus](/feelings-permission#emotion-compose) | *in* (inside) | *for* (on behalf) | *at* (aimed) | *from* (away) | I |

Full extra-noun grid: [extra nouns](/extra-noun-hooks#extra-noun-intermediate). Feeling loci reuse the same stacks. English stays; letters follow join stacks.

## Root job {#root-vowels}

From [root tails](/root-tails#root-tails). Last vowel hangs on join. Memory aids only: grammar does not read them.

| Last vowel | Job | Examples | Stage |
|------------|-----|----------|-------|
| `a` | holds or gathers | `abowa` *basket*, `aruda` *rice*, `eneza` *ant* | A |
| `o` | order, path, sequence | `oraho` *road*, `ugoro` *clock*, `eruvo` *run*, `urulo` *rainbow* | A |
| `e` | one whole thing | `ogoze` *cat*, `alede` *apple* | A |
| `u` | takes away | `anewu` *knife*, `ezozu` *snake*, `evemu` *fear* | A |

## Order {#order}

| Job | **a** | **o** | **e** | **u** | Stage |
|-----|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| [Digit](/numbers#counts) | `ba` `za` `na` | `do` `vo` `mo` | `ge` `re` | `hu` `lu` | B |
| [Tag](/tag-pronouns#tag-pronouns) | tag A (`zwar`) | tag O | tag E | tag U | I |
| [Pitch](/phonology#vowel-pitch) | low | mid-low | mid-high | high | B |
| [Finger code](/finger-code#counting) vowel hand | 1 (pinky) | 2 | 3 | 4 | B |

Pitch adds no meaning. Letter names are the opposite of the digit (`bu` `de` `go` `ha` …), so naming a letter never sounds like counting.

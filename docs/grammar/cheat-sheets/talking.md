# Talking cheat sheet {#talking-cheat-sheet}

A quick look at [speech moves](../speech-moves.md) and [questions](../questions.md) on one page. Nothing here is new: each row links to the section that teaches it. Stage: **B** Beginner, **I** Intermediate, **A** Advanced.

## Starting a turn {#turns}

A `/y/` word starts a new turn and goes before the body of the clause. A `/y/` word after a finished body starts the next turn.

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Call someone](../speech-moves.md#vocative): `/y/` + name (**-n**) | `yalahen.` | *Alahen!* | B |
| [Call a kind](../speech-moves.md#vocative): `/y/` + kind with **-n** | `yagavon` | *Guard!* (come here) | B |
| [Reaction](../speech-moves.md#interjections): `/y/` + root with **-l** / **-m** | `yezul.` / `yezum.` | *Surprise!* / *Amazing!* | B |
| [Reaction, not a call](../speech-moves.md#interjections): **-l** vs **-n** | `yagavol` | *Guard!* (an alarm) | B |
| [Call a group](../plurality.md#vocatives-y) | `yehodonx` | *hey, you all* | I |

## Speech acts {#acts}

The vowel picks the act: **a** add, **o** one, **e** order, **u** undo. A period already marks a statement, so `yal` is usually left out.

| Act | Firm **-l** | Soft **-m** | For now **-r** | Stage |
|-----|-------------|-------------|----------------|-------|
| statement | [`yal`](../speech-moves.md#speech-act-beginner) *(often omitted)* | [`yam`](../speech-moves.md#speech-act) *for what it's worth* | [`yar`](../speech-moves.md#act-r) *as things stand* | B / I |
| question | [`yol`](../speech-moves.md#speech-act-beginner) | [`yom`](../speech-moves.md#speech-act) *I wonder* | [`yor`](../speech-moves.md#act-r) *just checking* | B / I |
| command | [`yel`](../speech-moves.md#speech-act-beginner) | [`yem`](../speech-moves.md#speech-act) *please* | [`yer`](../speech-moves.md#act-r) *for now* | B / I |
| prohibition | [`yul`](../speech-moves.md#speech-act-beginner) | [`yum`](../speech-moves.md#speech-act) *please don't* | [`yur`](../speech-moves.md#act-r) *hold off* | B / I |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Command](../speech-moves.md#speech-act-beginner) | `yel vowogal.` | *Walk!* | B |
| [Request with *let's*](../speech-moves.md#speech-act) | `yem zahan vowogal.` | *Let's walk.* | I |
| [Soft prohibition with *let's not*](../speech-moves.md#speech-act) | `yum zahan vezebal.` | *Let's not sleep.* | I |
| [Provisional statement](../speech-moves.md#act-r) | `yar zazawan vowogal.` | *As things stand, Azawan walks.* | I |
| [Emphatic prohibition](../speech-moves.md#emphatic-prohibition): repeat only the opening `yul` | `yul yul vezevul.` | *Don't sneak. I mean it.* | I |

## Tone marks {#tone-marks}

A [tone mark](../speech-moves.md#tone-marks) changes your voice only, never the speech act. Double a mark for more; stack different marks to mix (no spaces, any order, each at most twice).

<!-- cheat-sheet: talking -->
| Mark | Feeling | Cue | Stage |
|------|---------|-----|-------|
| `!` | strong feeling | English *!* | I |
| `?` | unsure | English *(?)* | I |
| `%` | joking, teasing | a wink | I |
| `&` | contrast: this one, not another | *and* not the other | I |
| `;` | warm, affectionate | a soft pause | I |

| Where you write it | Colors | Example | Stage |
|--------------------|--------|---------|-------|
| [Attached to a word](../speech-moves.md#tone-marks) | that word | `zazawan vahahal ?dodogal.` | I |
| [Attached to a span](../speech-moves.md#tone-marks) | the span | `!d[ … ]` | I |
| [Alone, then a space](../speech-moves.md#tone-marks) | the rest, to the period | `! zazawan vowogal.` | I |
| [Doubled](../speech-moves.md#tone-marks) | stronger | `zazawan !!vowogal.` | I |
| [Stacked](../speech-moves.md#tone-marks) | mixed | `?! zalahen vahahal dodogal.` | I |

`yol` asks; `?` only sounds unsure. A third copy (`!!!`) is not a mark.

## Speech manner {#manner}

A stance word says in words how you are speaking, where a tone mark only changes your voice.

| Agazan | English | Stage |
|--------|---------|-------|
| [`thagazam`](../speech-moves.md#speech-manner) | *to be clear* / *to put it plainly* | I |
| [`thaveham`](../speech-moves.md#speech-manner) | *frankly* / *honestly* | I |

## Asking {#asking}

### Yes/no and fill-in {#ask}

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Yes/no question](../questions.md#question) | `yol zazawan vowogal.` | *Does Azawan walk?* | B |
| [Gentler](../questions.md#question) | `yom zalahen vezebal.` | *Is Alahen sleeping, I wonder?* | B |
| [No body: ask about what was just said](../questions.md#question) | `yol.` | *Huh?* | B |
| [Fill one blank](../questions.md#fill-ask-r): join **-r** in the slot | `yol zar vowogal.` | *Who walks?* | B |
| [Fill every blank](../questions.md#fill-all) | `yol zar vahahal dar.` | *Who sees what?* | B |
| [One blank, one known](../questions.md#fill-all) | `yol zar vahahal dodogal.` | *Who sees a dog?* | B |
| [Rise on the blank](../questions.md#question-tone) | `yol ?zar vowogal.` | *Who walks?* | B |
| [Rise on the whole question](../questions.md#question-tone) | `yol ? zazawan vowogal.` | *Does Azawan walk?* | B |
| [Check one named item](../questions.md#yes-no-single-item-standalone) | `yol zazawan zal.` | *Just Azawan?* | I |
| [Offer one named item](../questions.md#yes-no-single-item-standalone) | `yol zahaben zam.` | *How about Ahaben?* | I |

The reply to a fill-ask is the fill as a [citation](../word-endings.md#citation-forms), or a full sentence with role letters. The sentence still ends in a period.

|----------|-------|---------|-------|

| [When?](../questions.md#when) | `yol zahaben vowogal har.` | *When does Ahaben walk?* | I |
| [Where?](../questions.md#where) | `yol zahaben vowogal ol bar.` | *Where does Ahaben walk?* | I |
| [Whose?](../questions.md#whose) | `yol zodogal em bar vowogal.` | *Whose dog walks?* | I |
| [How?](../questions.md#how) | `yol zazawan vowogal humum bar.` | *How does Azawan walk?* | I |
| [Why?](../questions.md#why) | `yol zazawan vowogal thevem bar.` | *Why does Azawan walk?* | I |
| [What for?](../questions.md#why) | `yol zazawan vowogal hogom bar.` | *What does Azawan walk for?* | I |

### Fill-ask by vowel and count {#arity}

The blank **-r** takes a vowel; the count is how many items you listed before it ([fill-ask arity](../questions.md#fill-ask-arity)).

<!-- cheat-sheet: talking -->
| Items listed | **…ar** | **…or** | **…er** | **…ur** | Stage |
|--------------|---------|---------|---------|---------|-------|
| **0** | *who/what?* | *anything?* | *what's the priority?* | *what else?* | I |
| **1** | *which X?* | *any X?* | *which X is first?* | *what else besides X?* | I |
| **2+** | *which of these?* | *any of these?* | *which ranks highest?* | *other than these?* | I |

### Questions inside other clauses {#embedded}

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Report a question](../questions.md#embedded-whether) | `zazawan vahahal dorl zalahen vowogal.` | *Azawan sees whether Alahen walks.* | B |
| [Blank belongs to *whether*](../questions.md#blank-in-dependent) | `yol zehodon vubugam dorl zar vowogal.` | *Do you know who walks?* | I |
| [Blank belongs to the outer question](../questions.md#blank-in-dependent) | `yol zehodon vevegal darl zar vowogal.` | *Who do you think walks?* | I |
| [Question you don't want answered](../questions.md#rhetorical): `yal` before `yol` | `yal yol zar vegehel.` | *Who cares?* (nobody) | I |
| [Musing](../questions.md#rhetorical) | `yam yol zar vegehel.` | *Who really cares, though?* | I |

## Answering {#answering}

Write `y`, two vowels, then an ending. A turn holds one polar word; an answer followed by a question is two turns.

<!-- cheat-sheet: talking -->
| Stance | **-l** firm | **-m** soft | **-r** for now | Stage |
|--------|-------------|-------------|----------------|-------|
| [**`ae`**](../questions.md#polar-endings) accept, match | `yael` *yes* / *will do* | `yaem` *I think so* | `yaer` *true, for now* | B / I |
| [**`ue`**](../questions.md#polar-endings) refuse, mismatch | `yuel` *no* / *I won't* | `yuem` *I don't think so* | `yuer` *not so, for now* | B / I |
| [**`ao`**](../questions.md#polar-endings) take up an option | `yaol` *sure* | `yaom` *okay* | `yaor` *fine, for now* | B / I |
| [**`uo`**](../questions.md#polar-endings) reject this option | `yuol` *not that* | `yuom` *nah* | `yuor` *not that, for now* | I |
| [**`ua`**](../questions.md#polar-endings) reject the premise | `yual` *the question doesn't apply* | `yuam` *it's more complicated* | `yuar` *it doesn't apply, for now* | I |
| [**`oe`**](../questions.md#polar-endings) decline to answer | `yoel` *I won't answer* | `yoem` *I'd rather not say* | `yoer` *pass, for now* | I |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Answer with a sentence after](../questions.md#polar-stance): no `yal` | `yael` + body | *yes, …* | B |
| [Back up your own claim](../questions.md#polar-stance) | `zazawan vowogal. yael.` | *Azawan walks. Yes.* | B |
| [Answer a command or request](../questions.md#answer-command) | `yael` / `yuel` | *will do* / *I won't* | I |

### Tags: asking for an answer {#tags}

After your sentence, write `yol` and a polar word as a turn of its own. It asks for that answer, so pick the one you want to make easy.

<!-- cheat-sheet: talking -->
| Agazan | Use | English | Stage |
|--------|-----|---------|-------|
| [`yol yael.`](../questions.md#tags) | confirm a claim | *…, right?* | B |
| [`yol yuel.`](../questions.md#tags) | invite a correction | *…, or am I wrong?* | I |
| [`yol yaol.`](../questions.md#tags) | take up a request or offer | *…, okay?* | I |
| [`yol yuol.`](../questions.md#tags) | offer the refusal | *…, or would you rather not?* | I |
| [`yol yaer.`](../questions.md#tags) | accept for now | *…, can we go with that for now?* | I |
| [`yol yoem.`](../questions.md#tags) | after a question, offer a pass | *…, or would you rather not say?* | I |

## Don't mix up {#dont-mix-up}

| This | vs | That |
|------|----|------|
| `yalahen` *Alahen!* ([a call, **-n**](../speech-moves.md#vocative)) | | `zazawan vowogal` *Azawan walks* ([a subject](../speech-moves.md#vocative)) |
| `yagavon` *Guard!* ([call the guard](../speech-moves.md#interjections)) | | `yagavol` *Guard!* ([an alarm](../speech-moves.md#interjections)) |
| `yol` *question* ([asks](../questions.md#question)) | | `?` *unsure voice* ([only sounds unsure](../speech-moves.md#tone-marks)) |
| `yul` *don't* ([prohibition](../speech-moves.md#speech-act-beginner)) | | `yuel` *no, false* ([answers a claim](../questions.md#polar-stance)) |
| `yol yael.` *…, right?* ([a tag asks](../questions.md#tags)) | | `yael.` *yes* ([an answer gives](../questions.md#polar-stance)) |
| `thevem bar` *why?* ([the cause](../questions.md#why)) | | `humum bar` *like what?* ([a model to resemble](../questions.md#how)) |

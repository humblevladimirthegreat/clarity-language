# Linking clauses cheat sheet {#linking-clauses-cheat-sheet}

A quick look at [dependents](../dependents.md) and [relations](../relations.md) on one page. Nothing here is new: each row links to the section that teaches it. Stage: **B** Beginner, **I** Intermediate, **A** Advanced.

## A sentence that fills a slot {#dependents}

Agazan has no *who / that / which*. For a noun, write [two sentences](../dependents.md#which-noun). For a whole clause in a role, end the main sentence on a **stand-in** and write the clause right after it. Do not open that clause with `/y/`; it names its own `/z/`.

### Stand-ins {#stand-ins}

The vowel is the kind of content, the same four vowels as the act words. **-rl** is the whole content, **-rm** leaves it open.

<!-- cheat-sheet: linking-clauses -->
| Stand-in | Content | **-rl** locked | **-rm** open | Stage |
|----------|---------|----------------|--------------|-------|
| [**`a`**](../dependents.md#stand-in) | a statement | `darl` *that …* | `darm` *that …* (gist) | B |
| [**`o`**](../dependents.md#stand-in) | a question | `dorl` *whether …* | `dorm` *whether …* (open) | B |
| [**`e`**](../dependents.md#stand-in) | an instruction | `derl` *to …* | `derm` *to …* (open) | I |
| [**`u`**](../dependents.md#stand-in) | something to avoid | `durl` *that not / lest …* | `durm` *lest …* (open) | I |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [That-clause](../dependents.md#dependent-clauses) | `zazawan vezebel balahen darl zodogal varahal.` | *Azawan tells Alahen that the dog runs.* | B |
| [Whether](../dependents.md#dependent-clauses) | `zazawan vahahal dorl zalahen vowogal.` | *Azawan sees whether Alahen walks.* | B |
| [Told to](../dependents.md#stand-in) | `zazawan vezebel balahen derl vehahel.` | *Azawan tells Alahen to sit.* | I |
| [Told not to](../dependents.md#stand-in) | `zazawan vezebel balahen durl vehahel.` | *Azawan tells Alahen not to sit.* | I |
| [Own subject, no one told](../dependents.md#stand-in) | `zazawan vohum derl zazawan vehahel.` | *Azawan wishes to sit.* | I |
| [Join inside the clause](../dependents.md#stand-in): `xol` | `zazawan vahahal dorl zalahen vowogal xol zahaben varahal.` | *Azawan sees whether Alahen walks or Ahaben runs.* | I |

The vowels match the act words: `yal` statement, `yol` question, `yul` avoid.

Drop the inner `/z/` only after `derl` / `derm` or `durl` / `durm` filling `/d/`, when the outer sentence has one unhosted `/b/` (the person told). Everywhere else, write it. The clause runs to the end of the written sentence.

### Pointing back {#back}

**-rth** points back at content already said, so nothing follows it. The vowel picks the kind, and the word stands for the most recent content of that kind.

<!-- cheat-sheet: linking-clauses -->
| Stand-in | Points back to | English | Stage |
|----------|----------------|---------|-------|
| [**`darth`**](../dependents.md#stand-in-back) | the last statement | *so* / *that* | I |
| [**`dorth`**](../dependents.md#stand-in-back) | the last question | *that question* | I |
| [**`derth`**](../dependents.md#stand-in-back) | the last instruction | *that (instruction)* | I |
| [**`durth`**](../dependents.md#stand-in-back) | the last *not* / *lest* content | *not* (*I hope not*) | I |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Say so](../dependents.md#stand-in-back) | `zalahen vehahel. zazawan vezebel darth.` | *Alahen sits. Azawan says so.* | I |

Other role letters work the same way: `barth` after a pole (*because of that*), `zarth` as the subject. There is no stand-in on `/ɡ/`, `/h/`, `/w/`, or `/th/`.

### Verbs and subjects that take a sentence {#stand-in-roles}

**-rn** (or **-n** after two vowels) makes a fixed word for the kind of content. On `/v/` these verbs take the next sentence as their content.

<!-- cheat-sheet: linking-clauses -->
| Agazan | Takes | English | Stage |
|--------|-------|---------|-------|
| [**`varn`**](../dependents.md#stand-in-roles) | a statement | *state…* | A |
| [**`vorn`**](../dependents.md#stand-in-roles) | a question | *question…* | A |
| [**`vern`**](../dependents.md#stand-in-roles) | an instruction | *command…* | A |
| [**`vurn`**](../dependents.md#stand-in-roles) | something to avoid | *prohibit…* | A |
| [**`vaen`**](../dependents.md#stand-in-roles) | accepts a statement | *confirm that…* | A |
| [**`vuen`**](../dependents.md#stand-in-roles) | rejects a statement | *deny that…* | A |
| [**`vaon`**](../dependents.md#stand-in-roles) | accepts a proposed action | *agree to…* | A |
| [**`vuon`**](../dependents.md#stand-in-roles) | rejects a proposed action | *decline to…* | A |
| [**`vuan`**](../dependents.md#stand-in-roles) | rejects the premise | *dispute the premise that…* | A |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Confirm](../dependents.md#stand-in-roles) | `zazawan vaen zalahen vehahel.` | *Azawan confirms that Alahen sits.* | A |
| [Decline](../dependents.md#stand-in-roles) | `zazawan vuon zazawan vehahel.` | *Azawan declines to sit.* | A |
| [It is … that](../dependents.md#stand-in-roles) | `gamadam zarl zazawan vowogal.` | *It is hard for Azawan to walk.* | A |
| [Nested](../dependents.md#nested-dependents) | `zazawan gezebul thevem barl zalahen vowogal thevem barl zahaben vezebal.` | *Azawan is sleepy because Alahen walks because Ahaben sleeps.* | A |

## Because, if, although {#poles}

A **pole** names the link and takes the stand-in `barl` as its `/b/`. Keep the pair last, then the dependent sentence. Poles use the abstract **-m**. *Because* and *if* are stance, so `/th/`; the rest describe the event, so `/h/`. A noun in `/b/` in place of `barl` works too (*despite Alahen*); on a noun, swap the letter to `/ɡ/`.

<!-- cheat-sheet: linking-clauses -->
| Agazan | English | Stage |
|--------|---------|-------|
| [**`thevem barl`**](../dependents.md#poles) | *because* | B |
| [**`thoyem barl`**](../dependents.md#poles) | *if* | B |
| [**`tholum barl`**](../dependents.md#poles) | *only if* | B |
| [**`thedam barl`**](../dependents.md#poles) | *if and only if* | B |
| [**`hezom barl`**](../dependents.md#poles) | *although* / *even though* | B |
| [**`huwem barl`**](../dependents.md#poles) | *while* | B |
| [**`homam barl`**](../dependents.md#poles) | *until* | B |
| [**`heveham barl`**](../dependents.md#poles) | *by the time* | B |
| [**`habam barl`**](../dependents.md#poles) | *before* | B |
| [**`henum barl`**](../dependents.md#poles) | *after* | B |
| [**`hogom barl`**](../dependents.md#poles) | *so that* / *in order to* | B |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Because](../dependents.md#poles) | `zazawan vezehel thevem barl zalahen vowogal.` | *Azawan sings because Alahen walks.* | B |
| [If, on a command](../dependents.md#poles) | `yel vowogal thoyem barl zalahen vehahel.` | *If Alahen sits, walk.* | B |
| [Despite, with a noun](../dependents.md#poles) | `zazawan vowogal hezom balahen.` | *Azawan walks despite Alahen.* | B |
| [So that](../dependents.md#so-that) | `zazawan vowogal hogom barl zalahen vehahel.` | *Azawan walks so that Alahen sits.* | B |
| [In order to](../dependents.md#so-that): name the doer again | `zazawan vowogal hogom barl zazawan vehahel.` | *Azawan walks in order to sit.* | B |
| [So as not to](../dependents.md#stand-in): `burl` for `barl` | `zazawan vowogal hogom burl zazawan vehahel.` | *Azawan walks so as not to sit.* | I |
| [Unless](../dependents.md#stand-in): `burl` for `barl` | `zazawan vowogal thoyem burl zalahen vezebal.` | *Azawan walks unless Alahen sleeps.* | I |
| [So … that](../dependents.md#result-pole): the result happened | `zazawan welavam gadadal hodum barl zazawar vezebal.` | *Azawan is so tired that they sleep.* | I |
| [As soon as](../dependents.md#time-poles) | `zazawan vowogal wadehum henum barl zalahen vehahel.` | *Azawan walks as soon as Alahen sits.* | B |

### Times with a noun {#time-nouns}

Put an event or period in `/b/` right after the time pole. *Until* says this event lasts up to the endpoint; *by* says it is done at the endpoint or sooner. On a noun, swap `/h/` for `/ɡ/`.

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [During](../dependents.md#time-poles) | `zazawan vowogal huwem bavodel.` | *Azawan walks during the thunderstorm.* | B |
| [After](../dependents.md#time-poles) | `zazawan vehahel henum bavodel.` | *Azawan sits after the thunderstorm.* | B |
| [By](../dependents.md#by-deadline) | `zazawan vowogal heveham bavodel.` | *Azawan walks by the thunderstorm.* | B |
| [A noun in time](../dependents.md#time-pole-on-noun) | `zalahen dowogal genum bavodel vahahal.` | *Alahen sees the walk after the thunderstorm.* | I |

On a noun the poles are `guwem` *during*, `gabam` *before*, `genum` *after*, `gomam` *until*, and `geveham` *by*. *Since* is [*from* on a time](../hooks.md#since).

## Keep the same speech move going {#continue}

A **linker** is an `/x/` word that opens the next sentence under the same speech act, with no act word of its own. Linkers use the abstract **-m**. Any other `/x/` word sets the [topic](../pronouns.md#topic). A linker and a topic word do not share a sentence.

<!-- cheat-sheet: linking-clauses -->
| English | Agazan | Stage |
|---------|--------|-------|
| [*therefore*](../dependents.md#continue-x) | **`xodum`** | B |
| [*however*](../dependents.md#sentence-linkers) | **`xezom`** | I |
| [*meanwhile*](../dependents.md#sentence-linkers) | **`xagagam`** | I |
| [*next*](../dependents.md#sentence-linkers): clears the topic | **`xevavem`** | I |
| [*but*](../dependents.md#sentence-linkers): a harder push-back than *however* | **`xagezam`** | I |
| [*by the way*](../dependents.md#sentence-linkers): clears the topic | **`xavazem`** | I |

Three take **-l** for a firm link.

<!-- cheat-sheet: linking-clauses -->
| Agazan | Use | English | Stage |
|--------|-----|---------|-------|
| [**`xodul`**](../dependents.md#sentence-linkers) | the next claim follows necessarily | *it follows that* | I |
| [**`xezol`**](../dependents.md#sentence-linkers) | the contrast stands | *nevertheless* / *even so* | I |
| [**`xagezal`**](../dependents.md#sentence-linkers) | the last claim is blocked and replaced | *on the contrary* | I |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Therefore](../dependents.md#continue-x) | `zodogal vowogal. xodum zagadul varahal.` | *A dog walks. Therefore a cat runs.* | B |
| [On the contrary](../dependents.md#sentence-linkers) | `zalahen vezebal vul. xagezal zalahen varahal.` | *Alahen isn't sleeping. On the contrary, Alahen is running.* | I |
| [Topic, then linker](../dependents.md#sentence-linkers) | `xazawan. xezom zalahen varahal.` | *Now, about Azawan. However, Alahen runs.* | I |
| [Linker in a new turn](../dependents.md#sentence-linkers) | `yom xevavem zehon vowogal.` | *So, do you want to walk?* | I |

Linkers come only at the start of a sentence. They never follow a [clause join](../joins.md#clause-joins) and never open the sentence after a stand-in. Writing and speech rhythm for all of these is [one table](../dependents.md#orthography-and-prosody-periods).

## Hosted relations {#relations}

The relation word takes the other noun as its `/b/` right after it. Use `/h/` on an event and `/ɡ/` on a noun, and an optional `/w/` degree word right before the relation word. With no `/b/` after a joined subject, the other members are the target (*alike*).

### Like, for, on behalf of {#like-for}

<!-- cheat-sheet: linking-clauses -->
| Event / noun | Use | English | Stage |
|--------------|-----|---------|-------|
| [**`humum` / `gumum`**](../relations.md#like-resembles) | resemblance to a model | *like* | B |
| [**`humul` / `gumul`**](../relations.md#exactly-like) | an exact match | *exactly like* | I |
| [**`hehem` / `gehem`**](../relations.md#exchange) | the other side of a swap | *in exchange for* | B |
| [**`hadem` / `gadem`**](../relations.md#proxy) | authorized agency | *on behalf of* | B |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Like, on an event](../relations.md#like-resembles) | `zazawan humum badagul vowogal.` | *Azawan walks like a duck.* | B |
| [Like, on a noun](../relations.md#like-resembles) | `zahazal gumum bazawan.` | *A house like Azawan's.* | B |
| [Like an event](../relations.md#like-resembles): `barl` | `zazawan vowogal humum barl zadagul vezewel.` | *Azawan walks the way a duck swims.* | B |
| [Alike](../relations.md#like-resembles) | `zazawan zalahen zal gumum.` | *Azawan and Alahen are alike.* | B |
| [Exactly like](../relations.md#exactly-like) | `zazawan humul badagul vowogal.` | *Azawan walks exactly like a duck.* | I |
| [In exchange](../relations.md#exchange) | `zazawan dugul hehem bahavol vehem.` | *Azawan trades a book for a hammer.* | B |
| [On behalf of](../relations.md#proxy) | `zazawan hadem balahen vezebel.` | *Azawan tells on behalf of Alahen.* | B |

### Where, around, between {#locative}

Plain *in*, *on*, *at*, *from*, *toward* are [hooks](../hooks.md#extra-noun-intermediate); write the source before the goal. *Between*, *across*, and *around* need a root.

<!-- cheat-sheet: linking-clauses -->
| Event / noun | Use | English | Stage |
|--------------|-----|---------|-------|
| [**`hazam` / `gazam`**](../relations.md#locative-relations) | between two landmarks (one joined `/b/`) | *between* | I |
| [**`hebum` / `gebum`**](../relations.md#locative-relations) | from one edge to the far edge | *across* | I |
| [**`hugem` / `gugem`**](../relations.md#locative-relations) | on all sides of one landmark | *around* | I |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [Between](../relations.md#locative-relations) | `zalahen vehahel hazam bedehal bezedel bal.` | *Alahen sits between a train and a station.* | I |
| [Across](../relations.md#locative-relations) | `zazawan vowogal hebum bezedel.` | *Azawan walks across the station.* | I |
| [Around](../relations.md#locative-relations) | `zagadulx vehahel hugem bedehal.` | *Cats sit around a train.* | I |
| [From, toward](../relations.md#locative-relations) | `zazawan ul bezedel vowogal eol bedehal.` | *Azawan walks from a station toward a train.* | I |
| [Out of, in](../relations.md#locative-relations) | `zalahen ual bahazal varahal al bedehal.` | *Alahen runs out of a house into a train.* | I |

### As of {#as-of}

Whose-now is speech-now unless you move it. `huhum` claims the moment as real; `huram` only holds a place. `/h/` covers the clause, `/ɡ/` one noun, `/w/` the adjective right after, `/th/` the stances. A clause takes one pair; to keep the same whose-now, repeat the word with **-r** and no `/b/`.

<!-- cheat-sheet: linking-clauses -->
| Agazan | Use | English | Stage |
|--------|-----|---------|-------|
| [**`huhum` / `guhum` / `wuhum`**](../relations.md#as-of) | a real now | *as of* (dated ledger) | A |
| [**`huram` / `guram` / `wuram`**](../relations.md#as-of) | a placeholder now | *as of* (placeholder now) | A |
| [**`huhur` / `hurar`**](../relations.md#as-of) | resume that whose-now | *same books* | A |

<!-- cheat-sheet: linking-clauses -->
| Agazan | Use | English | Stage |
|--------|-----|---------|-------|
| [**`thuhum`**](../relations.md#stance-as-of) | stances as of a real now | *I thought / I felt (then)* | A |
| [**`thuram`**](../relations.md#stance-as-of) | stances as of a now you are not claiming | *I would think / I'd feel* | A |
| [**`thuhur` / `thurar`**](../relations.md#stance-as-of) | resume that stance-now | *still as I saw it then* | A |

| Job | Example | English | Stage |
|-----|---------|---------|-------|
| [As of a date](../relations.md#as-of) | `zalahen thamom huhum b_#22,7 vedabal.` | *As of 22 July, Alahen had still left.* | A |
| [Resume the same now](../relations.md#as-of) | `zalahen thamom huhum b_#22,7 vedabal. xazawan thamom huhur vowogal.` | *…Azawan had still walked, same books.* | A |
| [Stance then](../relations.md#stance-as-of) | `zalahen thuhum b_#22,7 thovum vedabal.` | *On 22 July, I thought Alahen might leave.* | A |
| [Back then](../relations.md#stance-as-of) | `zalahen thuhum brul thovum vedabal.` | *Back then, I thought Alahen might leave.* | A |

## Don't mix up {#dont-mix-up}

| This | vs | That |
|------|----|------|

# Compound families {#compound-families}

### Left and right of `x` {#families-by-shape}

Beginner already used two roots glued with **`x`** and dictionary words with no **`x`**. After the role letter, look at both sides of the **first** **`x`**. That pair picks the family. A [role compound](/roles#role-compounds) or [role pointer](/pronouns#role-pointers) is not one of these families: it opens with **`w`**, a scope vowel, and a part consonant (`zwazuduyel`, `zwazar`), and only a noun inside its stem takes **`x`**.

<!-- cheat-sheet: roles-comparing -->
| Agebon | Use | English | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|-----|
| name **`x`** **`a`** / **`o`** / **`e`** / **`u`** + **-n** (conversation length) | conversation length | *Uzozen, I have time* | vowel on the name |
| longer root **`x`** **`a`** / **`e`** / **`o`** / **`u`** | ability | *can’t sing right now* | extra vowel after the root |
| full roots on **both** sides, maybe more **`x`** + root | ordinary compound | `zubedaxuyudal`; `zalexalowen` ([hyphenated names](proper-names.md#multipart-names)) | **x** ≈ plus |
| sake root **`th`** **`a`** / **`e`** / **`o`** / **`u`** | [sakes](/sakes) | `guwathal` | **th** ≈ *think* (your view of the sake) |
| other root **`th`** **`a`** / **`e`** / **`o`** / **`u`** (or a stack) | [label scope](/label-seams#label-scope) | *angry this time*; *a teacher* | **th** ≈ *think* (what kind of claim the label is) |
| direction root **`th`** facing person | [viewpoint lateral](viewpoint.md#viewpoint-laterals) | `gowuhethuzozen` *Uzozen’s left* | **th** ≈ *think* (whose point of view) |

A root is at least three letters, so one or two vowels left of **`x`** are never a root.

### Adding another piece {#ordinary-compound-order}

To narrow a compound further, or to build a hyphenated name from several labels, add another root with another **`x`**. Keep the English order in **one** word (*crush* then *love*; a shop named after three drinks). The **last** root is the kind, and each earlier root narrows it. The ending stays at the end of the whole word. In a name (a person or a shop), the roots are simply labels in order, and none of them is the kind.

> `gagamaxolamal.`
>
> g-crush-x-love
>
> "in love" (the crush kind)

> `zugudaxududaxowulan.`
>
> z-Uguda-x-Ududa-x-Owulan
>
> "Coffee-Tea-Water" (one named shop)

[Hyphenated names](proper-names.md#multipart-names) use the same order (*Mary-Kate*: first piece first). A family name is not part of this stack; it is a separate `/ɡ/` word after the given name. [Body left / right](viewpoint.md#viewpoint-laterals) is not this stack: it joins the direction and whose facing counts with **`th`** (`gowuhethuzozen`), because the second root is a point of view, not a kind.

A [role compound](/roles#role-compounds) can take such a stack as its stem (`zwazudozoxowurel` *someone who walks dogs*), but is never one piece of a stack.

**Compare with:** a short office **handle** is one root plus [**-n**](proper-names.md#proper-name--n), not this stack. A dictionary compound is one listed word, not a live list you extend.

### Which family is this?
<a id="decision-order"></a>

Use the table above. If the word has a mid-word **`th`**, it is a [sake](/sakes) word (a [sake root](/sakes#sake-inventory), then a vowel, with an optional [emotion tail](feelings-permission.md#emotion-compose)), a [label scope](/label-seams#label-scope) word (any other root, then a vowel), or a [viewpoint lateral](viewpoint.md#viewpoint-laterals) (a root follows **`th`**). The root before **`th`** decides: after a sake root, letters that look like a root are always the emotion tail (`guwathamel`), never a lateral. Otherwise read both sides of the first **`x`** in this order:

- A name (one root or a [hyphenated name](proper-names.md#multipart-names)), **`x`**, a single vowel **`a`** / **`o`** / **`e`** / **`u`**, named **-n**, as a conversation-length bid → conversation length.
- A longer root, then **`x`**, then a single vowel **`a`** / **`e`** / **`o`** / **`u`** → **ability**.
- Full roots on **both** sides of the first **`x`** (and maybe more **`x`** + root) → an ordinary compound (sense or name).

### Conversation length (name **`x`** vowel) {#conversation-length}

English *I can stay a while* or *I only have a minute* tells the other person how long you can talk. In Agebon, add **`x`** and one vowel before **-n** on a [greeting](/word-endings#greeting) (your own name with a period, `SELFn.`) or a [call](/speech-moves#vocative) (their name under **`/y/`**, `yolazun.`). At arrival, the vowel sets how long you expect the conversation to last. At departure, the same form says how soon you are leaving. The vowel is about the conversation, not a grade of the person.

> `SELFxan.`
>
> SELF-presence
>
> "I have time; we can stay."

> `yolazuxen.`
>
> y-Olazun-minutes
>
> "Olazun, I have a few minutes."

<!-- cheat-sheet: roles-comparing -->
| Form | At arrival | At departure | Cue |
|------|------------|--------------|-----|
| `SELFxan` | open-ended: *we can stay* | none | **a** ≈ add (time is in) |
| `SELFxen` | a few minutes: *I have a little time* | leaving soon | **e** ≈ order (the slot has an end) |
| `SELFxon` | one slot: *one ask or comment* | about to leave | **o** ≈ one (one last slot) |
| `SELFxun` | passing: *just acknowledging; not opening a talk* | leaving right now | **u** ≈ undo (lift the talk) |

On a [full name](proper-names.md#multipart-names), the vowel goes on the given name (`alexen galowen.`); a family name takes no bid, since **`x`** + vowel on a `/ɡ/` word is ability. Only one of these vowels per name. These forms use the same scale throughout a conversation: a smaller level narrows the remaining time. **-n** is still the [named](/word-endings#named-n-beginner) ending.

At arrival, the other person can answer with their own bid. The **lesser** bid sets the shared limit: **a** (open-ended) is more than **e** (a few minutes), **e** is more than **o** (one slot), and **o** is more than **u** (passing). If you offer open-ended time and Olazun offers one slot, you have one slot.

> `SELFxan.`
>
> SELF-presence
>
> "I have time; we can stay."

> `olazuxon.`
>
> Olazun-ask
>
> "Olazun here. I only have time for one thing."

**Compare with:** the same **`x`** + vowel on a verb or adjective is ability (*can’t sing right now*). A [sake](/sakes) word takes **`th`** + vowel instead (`guwathal`). Conversation-length bids sit on a **named** citation or a **`/y/`** call, with **-n**. In a clause body, a name with **`x`**, a vowel, and **-n** is not a word.

**Compare with:** a plain named citation with a period is the basic hello (`SELFn.`). The bid says how much conversation the person is open to.

At departure, use your own name with the reduced level:

> `SELFxen.`
>
> SELF-minutes
>
> "I’m leaving soon."

> `SELFxon.`
>
> SELF-ask
>
> "I’m about to leave."

> `SELFxun.`
>
> SELF-passing
>
> "I’m leaving now."

# Of-relations and social ties {#of-relations-and-social-ties}

### Exactly like (`hewol` / `gewol`) {#exactly-like}

Beginner used **`hewom`** / **`gewom`** for *like*: the event or thing resembles the model in `/b/`. When the match is exact (*walks exactly like a duck*, *a house just like Uzozen's*), end the same relation word in **-l** instead: **`hewol`** on the event, **`gewol`** on a noun. The model sits in a `bl-` word right after it, as before, and the event or thing is still real. (cue: **-l** ≈ locked: the reflection matches the model line for line)

> `zuzozen hewol bludozal vowurel.`
>
> z-Uzozen | [h-exactly-like | bl-duck] | v-walk
>
> "Uzozen walks exactly like a duck."

> `zahehal gewol bluzozen.`
>
> z-house | [g-exactly-like | bl-Uzozen]
>
> "A house just like Uzozen's."

**Related form:** a degree word before **`hewom`** grades the match instead (`wamezom hewom bludozal` *a bit like a duck*).

### Locative relations {#locative-relations}

Everyday *in* / *on* / *at* / *from* / *toward* / *through* / *out of* are [extra-noun hooks](/extra-noun-hooks#extra-noun-intermediate). To say where someone moves from and to, use one hook for each landmark (*from* one, *toward* another), and write the **source** before the **goal**.

> `zuzozen ul buyahel vowurel oel bedavel.`
>
> z-Uzozen | [from | b-station] | v-walk | [toward | b-train]
>
> "Uzozen walks from a station toward a train."

> `zuzozen ul bahehal vowurel ol buyahel.`
>
> z-Uzozen | [from | b-house] | v-walk | [at | b-station]
>
> "Uzozen walks from a house to a station."

> `zolazun ual bahehal varuvel al bedavel.`
>
> z-Olazun | [out-of | b-house] | v-run | [in | b-train]
>
> "Olazun runs out of a house into a train."

*The guard in a bank* uses the same hook on a noun, not a [*who*-clause](/dependents#which-noun):

> `zagayul al babohal vuhohol.`
>
> z-guard | [in | b-bank] | v-sit
>
> "A guard in a bank sits."

<a id="spatial-path"></a>

*Between* needs a **root**, not a hook: the position depends on **two** landmarks at once. Use hosted **`hedam`** (or **`gedam`** on a noun) and put both landmarks in `/b/` as a [join](/joins). (cue: 🥪 *sandwich*: the filling sits between two sides)

> `zolazun vuhohol hedam bledavel bluyahel bal.`
>
> z-Olazun | v-sit | [h-between | [bl-train | bl-station | b-and]]
>
> "Olazun sits between a train and a station."

The `bl-` word after the relation word is **one slot**. A join (`bal`) or a [plural](/plurality) fills it whole: each item takes `bl-`, the join word needs none (`bl-` on it is also correct), and the landmarks do not each need their own relation word. A join with no items before it is the whole slot, so it takes `bl-` itself (`hewom blar` *like what?*, `hewom blal` *like nothing*). The same holds on a noun:

> `zuzozen gedam bledavel bluyahel bal vuhohol.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | [g-between | [bl-train | bl-station | b-and]]] | v-sit
>
> "Uzozen, between a train and a station, sits."

As a plain noun, `zedal` is *a sandwich*.

<a id="across"></a>

*Across* also needs a root. The hook **`uol`** *through* passes through the inside of the landmark (a crowd, a tunnel). **`hohem`** goes from one edge to the far edge (a street, a river). Put the landmark in a `bl-` word right after it. (cue: 🌉 *bridge*: a bridge spans the gap)

> `zuzozen vowurel hohem bluyahel.`
>
> z-Uzozen | v-walk | [h-across | bl-station]
>
> "Uzozen walks across the station."

On a noun it is **`gohem`**: *Uzozen, across the station, sits.* As a plain noun, `zohel` is *a bridge*.

*Around* needs a root too: the person or thing is on **all sides** of one landmark, like a ring. Use hosted **`hevam`** (or **`gevam`** on a noun) with the landmark in `/b/`. (cue: 🎠 *carousel*: the horses ride on all sides of the center pole)

> `zagozolx vuhohol hevam bledavel.`
>
> z-cat-x | v-sit | [h-around | bl-train]
>
> "Cats sit around a train."

As a plain noun, `zeval` is *a carousel*. **`om`** *near* says only that the person or thing is close, not that it surrounds the landmark.

<!-- cheat-sheet: linking-clauses -->
| Agebon | Use | English | Same root as | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|--------------|-----|
| **`hedam` / `gedam`** | position between two landmarks | *between* | `edal` *sandwich* | 🥪: the filling sits between two sides |
| **`hohem` / `gohem`** | path from one edge of the landmark to the far edge | *across* | `ohel` *bridge* | 🌉: a bridge spans the gap |
| **`hevam` / `gevam`** | on all sides of one landmark | *around* | `eval` *carousel* | 🎠: the horses ride on all sides of the pole |

**Compare with:** a `/b/` word with no relation word before it is the recipient. *Like* is [simile](/relations#similative). *For a hammer* as a swap is [exchange](/relations#exchange). *On behalf of Olazun* is [proxy](/relations#proxy).

### Of relations (*part of*, *cup of*, *made of*, *from*) {#of-relations}
<a id="noun-relations"></a>

English *of* also names how one noun sits toward another (*a hand of Olazun*, *a teapot of tea*, *a house of wood*, *Olazun from the city*). Use the same hosted pair as *like*: the relation word on `/ɡ/` (or `/h/` on the event), and the extra noun in a `bl-` word right after it. The `/b/` word is what comes after *of* in English (the whole, the filling, the material, or the origin), not a recipient.

> `zuzozen dahebal gobem blolazun vawebol.`
>
> z-Uzozen | [d-hand | [g-part-of | bl-Olazun]] | v-see
>
> "Uzozen sees one of Olazun's hands."

> `zuzozen dedodal gadam blududal vawebol.`
>
> z-Uzozen | [d-teapot | [g-contents | bl-tea]] | v-see
>
> "Uzozen sees a teapot full of tea."

> `zuzozen dahehal gulem blulel vawebol.`
>
> z-Uzozen | [d-house | [g-material | bl-wood]] | v-see
>
> "Uzozen sees a house made of wood."

> `zolazun gaham blezahel vowurel.`
>
> [z-Olazun | [g-origin | bl-skyline]] | v-walk
>
> "Olazun, who is from the city, walks."

On an event, the same roots take `/h/` (`zuzozen vuguyol hulem blulel` *Uzozen crafts out of wood*; `zolazun vowurel haham blezahel` *Olazun walks, originating from the city*). As plain nouns, the same roots name ordinary things (`zobel` *a bone*; `zadal` *a jar*; `zulel` *wood*; `zahal` *a country*).

For *a piece of* a mass, put the substance first and `gozowum` right after it. With no `/b/`, the whole it was cut from is left open.

> `zuzozen debodol gozowum vawebol.`
>
> z-Uzozen | [d-bread | g-division] | v-see
>
> "Uzozen sees a piece of bread."

Do not flip the pair: *tea of the teapot* (tea that sits in that pot) is the *in* hook (`al`), not `gadam`.

After a join, `gobem` and `gaham` bound the set the join covers, as SHARED words ([every and none, by set](rank-joins.md#kind-domains)). `gobem` gives membership, and `gaham` gives origin.

> `zual gobem blavayal vezuvol.`
>
> [z-everything | [g-part-of | bl-family]] | v-sleep
>
> "Everyone in the family sleeps."

`zual gaham blahaham` is *everyone from the town*. A hook after the join would be a clause extra and place the act instead.

<!-- cheat-sheet: linking-clauses -->
| Agebon | Use | English | Same root as | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|--------------|-----|
| **`hobem` / `gobem`** | constitutive piece | *part of* | `obel` *bone* | 🦴: the piece that holds the body’s shape |
| **`hadam` / `gadam`** | filling of a vessel | *contents of* | `adal` *jar* | 🫙: the vessel named by what it stores |
| **`hulem` / `gulem`** | constitution | *made of* | `ulel` *wood* | 🪵: the stuff the thing is made of |
| **`haham` / `gaham`** | identity source / provenance | *from* (origin) | `ahal` *country* | 🗾: a map of one country as where it comes from |
| **`hozowum` / `gozowum`** | portion cut from a whole | *a piece of* / *a slice of* | `ozowul` *saw* | 🪚: the saw cuts a piece off |

**Compare with:**

- Plain *Uzozen's*, with no particular tie, is the hook [`em`](/extra-noun-hooks#genitive) (`zudozol em buzozen`). Ownership is `gogunum` + `/b/` ([joins](rank-joins.md#scope-fence-p-join)). A need from [sakes](/sakes) on `/ɡ/` also says the thing is the speaker's.
- Place *in* is the hook `al`; path *from* is the hook `ul` ([locative relations](#locative-relations)).
- *A house like Uzozen’s* is [simile](/relations#similative) (`gewom`), not *made of*. *A book for a hammer* as a swap is [exchange](/relations#exchange) (`geham`).
- `gobem` is a part that belongs to the thing's structure (a bone of the body); `gozowum` is a piece taken off it (a slice of bread).
- *From* as the origin of a whole kind (`#e-xROOT`) is a numeric prefix, not this *from*, which is about one particular thing.

# In a crisis, out of debt {#in-a-crisis-out-of-debt}

### Abstract landmarks (*in a crisis*, *out of debt*) {#abstract-landmarks}

English uses the same prepositions for states, plans, and conditions as for places: *in a crisis*, *on a policy*, *out of debt*. Ageben does the same. Put a noun in its abstract `-m` sense right after an extra-noun hook, and the hook keeps the meaning it has with a place: a state is something you are *in*, a plan is something you stand *on*, a tendency is something you head *toward*, a condition is something you get *out of*. So you need no new word for *in a crisis* or *out of debt*: read each hook as you would with a place.

> `zalazun vavobol ual baram.`
>
> z-Alazun | v-go | [out-of | b-debt]
>
> "Alazun gets out of debt."

> `zazozon varuyol uol bonam.`
>
> z-Azozon | v-write | [against | b-policy]
>
> "Azozon writes against the policy."

| Hook | Abstract landmark | Example | English |
|------|-------------------|---------|---------|
| `al` *in* | a state or condition you are inside | `al babowum` | *in a crisis* |
| `ael` *on* | a plan, rule, or footing you stand on | `ael bonam` | *on policy* |
| `el` *at* | a point of concern or a status | `el bobevam` | *at issue* |
| `oel` *toward* | a tendency you head toward | `oel buzebum` | *toward a habit* |
| `ul` *from* | what something comes from or owes to | `ul buzobam` | *from heritage* |
| `ual` *out of* | a condition you leave | `ual baram` | *out of debt* |
| `uel` *through* | something you last through | `uel babowum` | *through the crisis* |
| `ol` *for* | what you favor | `ol bonam` | *for the policy* |
| `aol` *using* | what you go by | `aol bogarem` | *by tradition* |
| `uol` *against* | what you oppose | `uol bonam` | *against the policy* |

The zones read the same way, with the landmark as the general setting: `am babowum` *amid the crisis*, `uom bonam` *contrary to the policy*. If the meaning you want does not match what the hook means with a place, the hook cannot carry it; say it with its own word.

**Compare with:** the landmark's `-m` picks its abstract sense (`babowum` *catastrophe*, where `babowul` is *bomb*); the hook's own `-m` (`am`, `uom`) makes the landmark the setting. The two choices are separate, and a word can take both.

#### Pick one {#intermediate-pick-one}

**1.** *Azozon writes in a catastrophe.* `zazozon varuyol al babowum.` or `zazozon varuyol al babowul.`

::: details Show answer
`zazozon varuyol al babowum.`

z-Azozon | v-write | [in | b-catastrophe]

The landmark's **-m** picks the abstract sense (*catastrophe*); **-l** is the concrete *bomb*.
:::

**2.** *Alazun writes against the policy.* `zalazun varuyol uol bonam.` or `zalazun varuyol ul bonam.`

::: details Show answer
`zalazun varuyol uol bonam.`

z-Alazun | v-write | [against | b-policy]

**`uol`** is *against* what you oppose; **`ul`** is *from* what something comes from.
:::

#### Pick one {#intermediate-pick-one}

**1.** *Who laughs?* (nobody does; no reply is owed) `yal yel zar valamal.` or `yel yal ? zar valamal.`

::: details Show answer
`yal yel zar valamal.`

y-statement | y-question | z-wh | v-laugh

A rhetorical question puts the statement word first; **`yel yal`** is a quiz and wants the answer said.
:::

**2.** *Must you laugh?* (it forbids; no answer is owed) `yul yel zawan valamal.` or `yel yul ? zawan valamal.`

::: details Show answer
`yul yel zawan valamal.`

y-prohibition | y-question | z-listener | v-laugh

The prohibition comes first, so it forbids and only sounds like a question; **`yel yul`** really asks and hopes for *no*.
:::

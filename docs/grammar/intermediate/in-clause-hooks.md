# Hooks inside the clause {#hooks-inside-the-clause}

Inside a clause, a hook ties B to A, or names a landmark. Beginner already used closed **-l** and open **-m** for how complete B is. Next: say more than one thing about A, add detail to the hook itself, and point back to a landmark with **-r**.

### In-clause {#in-clause}

<!-- cheat-sheet: joins-hooks -->
| | **-l** | **-m** |
|--|--------|--------|
| **a** (*including*) | `al`: including B | `am`: including B, and maybe more |
| **o** (*rather*) | `ol`: or rather B | `om`: or rather B, and maybe more |
| **e** (*instead*) | `el`: instead (B in place of A) | `em`: B instead of A, B being one of several possible replacements |
| **u** (*except*) | `ul`: except B | `um`: except B, and maybe more |

To make a whole `A HOOK B` phrase into a name (a store, a title, a handle), give the hook **-n** ([titled phrases](proper-names.md#titled-phrases)). Otherwise use **-l** or **-m**. The two sides keep their usual endings (kinds **-l**, abstracts **-m**, a name **-n**).

> `dedudal en dogudal.`
>
> NAME[d-tea | instead | d-coffee]
>
> "Coffee-Instead-of-Tea" (a cafe name)

**Compare with:** *no dog* / *not X* uses a prefixed [join](/joins#join-series-ending-shared) (`zodozel zul`). The hook **`ul`**, with no role letter, always takes a B out of A.

> `zodozel zul.`
>
> [z-dog | z-not]
>
> "No dog."

### Parallel chains {#parallel-chains}

To say more than one thing about the **same** A (*including B but except C*, or two *including*s), keep adding hooks after B: `A HOOK₁ B HOOK₂ C`. Each hook applies to A, not to the B before it, and each has its own vowel and ending. So C is never tucked under B the way English *including (B except C)* can be.

> `zuvayal am zazozon ul zalazun.`
>
> z-family | including.open | z-Azozon | except | z-Alazun
>
> "The family, including Azozon and maybe more, except Alazun."

| Chain | English |
|-------|---------|
| `A am B am C` | A including B and including C, and maybe more |
| `A al B al C` | A including B and including C |
| `A am B ul C` | A including B, and maybe more, except C |
| `A ul B ul C` | A except B and except C |

**Compare with:** a [list join](/joins) after the hook is finished first, and the whole list becomes B: `zodozel el zogozel zebezel zam` is *a cat and a bird (and possibly more) instead of a dog*.

### Detail on the hook (`/w/`) {#hook-w}

To add detail to the hook itself (*hesitantly excepting*, *never excepting*), put a `/w/` word right before the hook. A `/w/` word always describes the word **right after** it, and a hook takes one the same way a `/ɡ/` or `/h/` word does. A [restrictor](/restrictors) works there too. The hook's right-hand piece follows as usual.

On a same-role hook, the detail describes the *except* (or *including*, *rather*, *instead*). An `/h/` word in the clause still describes the verb.

> `zuvayal wuzugom ul zazozon.`
>
> z-family | [w-hesitation | except] | z-Azozon
>
> "The family, hesitantly excepting Azozon."

> `zuvayal wal ul zazozon.`
>
> z-family | [w-never | except] | z-Azozon
>
> "The family, never excepting Azozon."

A surprise word on the *including* hook gives English *even*: **`wemem al`** adds B and says openly that B is the one nobody expected.

> `zalazun wemem al zazozon vowurol.`
>
> z-Alazun | [w-amazement | including] | z-Azozon | v-walk
>
> "Alazun walks, and even Azozon does."

**Compare with:** `zuvayal ul zazozon hadurom vuguyol` puts haste on the **cooking**, not on the except.

On an extra-noun hook, `/w/` goes before the hook and `/b/` still comes right after it. A `/ɡ/` word after the landmark still describes the landmark.

> `zodozel vezuvel wal al bohehal.`
>
> z-dog | v-sleep | [[w-never | in] | b-house]
>
> "A dog sleeps, never in a house."

A discourse hook takes `/w/` the same way: `welazem al zazozon vowurol` is *Additionally, very much so, Azozon walks.* A `/b/` right after the hook still makes it an extra-noun hook, not a discourse hook.

### Point back (`er` / `ar` / `ur` / `or`) {#hook-resume}

English *there*, *from there*, and *for it* point back to a place or thing already named. In Ageben, give the extra-noun hook **-r** and leave out the `/b/` word: after the verb, the hook points back to the landmark already in play, the way [resume **-r**](/pronouns#resume-r) does on a noun. A hook with **-r** has no noun on its right, and no phrase on its left to pair with (it is not a same-role hook).

<!-- cheat-sheet: joins-hooks -->
| Ageben | Points back to | English |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| `ar` | inside the place already named | *in there* / *in it* |
| `er` | the place already named | *there* |
| `ur` | the place already named, as a source | *from there* |
| `or` | the one already named, as a goal | *for that* / *for it* |

> `zazozon vehohel el bohehal. zalazun vehohel er.`
>
> z-Azozon | v-sit | [at | b-house] . z-Alazun | v-sit | there
>
> "Azozon sits at the house. Alazun sits there too."

> `zodozel vezuvel al bohehal. zodozer veruvol ur.`
>
> z-dog | v-sleep | [in | b-house] . z-←dog | v-run | from.there
>
> "The dog sleeps in the house. It runs from there."

Stacked extra-noun vowels point back the same way: the vowels keep the extra they named (`ael` *on*, `aol` *using*, `uel` *through*), and **-r** drops the landmark.

<!-- cheat-sheet: joins-hooks -->
| Ageben | Points back to | English |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| `aer` | the face already named | *on it* |
| `aor` | the means already named | *with it* |
| `uer` | the path already named | *through there* |

> `zazozon vehohel ael behohel. zalazun vehohel aer.`
>
> z-Azozon | v-sit | [on | b-chair] . z-Alazun | v-sit | on.it
>
> "Azozon sits on a chair. Alazun sits on it too."

> `zazozon danal aol bebewol varuyol. zalazun varuyol aor.`
>
> z-Azozon | d-book | [using | b-pen] | v-write . z-Alazun | v-write | with.it
>
> "Azozon writes a book with a pen. Alazun writes with it too."

> `zazozon vowurol uel bohehal. zalazun vowurol uer.`
>
> z-Azozon | v-walk | [through | b-house] . z-Alazun | v-walk | through.there
>
> "Azozon walks through the house. Alazun walks through there too."

#### English → Ageben {#intermediate-english-to-ageben}

**1.** *The family, even Azozon, writes.*

::: details Show answer
`zuvayal wemem al zazozon varuyol.`

z-family | [w-amazement | including] | z-Azozon | v-write
:::

**2.** *The family, except Azozon, writes.*

::: details Show answer
`zuvayal ul zazozon varuyol.`

z-family | except | z-Azozon | v-write
:::

**3.** *Ahelan sees the newspaper, or rather the policy.*

::: details Show answer
`zahelan dunonal ol donam vayebel.`

z-Ahelan | d-newspaper | rather | d-policy | v-see
:::

**4.** *The family, including Azozon and maybe more, except Alazun, sits.*

::: details Show answer
`zuvayal am zazozon ul zalazun vehohel.`

z-family | including.open | z-Azozon | except | z-Alazun | v-sit
:::

**5.** *Azozon writes on the newspaper. Ahelan writes on it too.*

::: details Show answer
`zazozon varuyol ael bunonal. zahelan varuyol aer.`

z-Azozon | v-write | [on | b-newspaper] . z-Ahelan | v-write | on.it
:::

#### Ageben → English {#intermediate-ageben-to-english}

**1.** `zahelan dunonal em darowel vayebel.`

::: details Show answer
z-Ahelan | d-newspaper | instead.open | d-radio | v-see

*Ahelan sees the radio instead of the newspaper.* (one possible replacement)
:::

**2.** `zuvayal am zazozon vayebel.`

::: details Show answer
z-family | including.open | z-Azozon | v-see

*The family sees, Azozon included, and maybe more besides.*
:::

**3.** `zalazun vehohel er.`

::: details Show answer
z-Alazun | v-sit | there

*Alazun sits there.*
:::

#### Pick one {#intermediate-pick-one}

**1.** *The family writes, except Azozon and maybe others.* `zuvayal um zazozon varuyol.` or `zuvayal ul zazozon varuyol.`

::: details Show answer
`zuvayal um zazozon varuyol.`

z-family | except.open | z-Azozon | v-write

Open **-m** leaves room for more exceptions; **`ul`** excepts Azozon alone.
:::

#### What changes {#intermediate-what-changes}

**1.** `zuvayal varuyol al bunonal. zalazun varuyol ar.` / `zuvayal varuyol al bunonal. zalazun varuyol ur.`

::: details Show answer
z-family | v-write | [in | b-newspaper] . z-Alazun | v-write | in.there

z-family | v-write | [in | b-newspaper] . z-Alazun | v-write | from.there

**`ar`** points back into the newspaper: Alazun writes in it too. **`ur`** points back to it as a source: Alazun writes from it.
:::

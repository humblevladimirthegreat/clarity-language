# Frankly and to be clear {#frankly-and-to-be-clear}

### Speech manner (*frankly* / *to be clear*) {#speech-manner}

English *frankly* and *to be clear* say in words how you are speaking. A tone mark cannot do that, because it changes only your voice. Instead, use a stance word (`/th/`, [clause](/clause#stance-th)) built on an ordinary root. Like any stance word, it may sit anywhere in the sentence; the start is usual.

<!-- cheat-sheet: talking -->
| Ageben | English | Root |
|--------|---------|------|
| **`thagebem`** | *to be clear* / *to put it plainly* | clarity |
| **`thavewom`** | *frankly* / *honestly* / *to tell the truth* | revelation |

> `thagebem zazozon vowurol.`
>
> th-clarity | z-Azozon | v-walk
>
> "To be clear, Azozon walks."

> `thavewom zalazun vezuvel.`
>
> th-revelation | z-Alazun | v-sleep
>
> "Honestly, Alazun is sleeping."

**Compare with:** the stance word describes your way of saying the claim, not the action. `/h/` on the same root (`hagebem`) would describe how the action itself is done.

#### English → Ageben {#intermediate-english-to-ageben}

**1.** *To be clear, Azozon writes a page.*

::: details Show answer
`thagebem zazozon dobonol varuyol.`

th-clarity | z-Azozon | d-page | v-write
:::

**2.** *Frankly, Alazun runs.*

::: details Show answer
`thavewom zalazun veruvol.`

th-revelation | z-Alazun | v-run
:::

**3.** *Honestly, Azozon sees the trumpet.*

::: details Show answer
`thavewom zazozon dadorol vayebel.`

th-revelation | z-Azozon | d-trumpet | v-see
:::

#### Ageben → English {#intermediate-ageben-to-english}

**1.** `yem thavewom zalazun vayebel dadorol.`

::: details Show answer
y-soft-question | th-revelation | z-Alazun | v-see | d-trumpet

*Honestly, does Alazun see the trumpet, I wonder?*
:::

**2.** `thagebem zalazun dobonol vayebel.`

::: details Show answer
th-clarity | z-Alazun | d-page | v-see

*To put it plainly, Alazun sees the page.*
:::

**3.** `thagebem zazozon vezohul.`

::: details Show answer
th-clarity | z-Azozon | v-stop

*To be clear, Azozon stops.*
:::

#### Pick one {#intermediate-pick-one}

**1.** *Frankly, Alazun writes.* (how you are saying it) `thavewom zalazun varuyol.` or `zalazun varuyol havewom.`

::: details Show answer
`thavewom zalazun varuyol.`

th-revelation | z-Alazun | v-write

The stance word describes your way of saying the claim. On `/h/`, `havewom` would describe how Alazun writes.
:::

**2.** *To be clear, Azozon runs.* (how you are saying it) `thagebem zazozon veruvol.` or `zazozon veruvol hagebem.`

::: details Show answer
`thagebem zazozon veruvol.`

th-clarity | z-Azozon | v-run

On `/th/`, **`thagebem`** is about how you say the claim. On `/h/`, `hagebem` would say Azozon runs clearly.
:::

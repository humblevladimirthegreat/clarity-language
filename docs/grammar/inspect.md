# Inspect

Paste Agalan and click or highlight a word for a floating inspect card with part of speech, reference ending, lexicon or overlay gloss, and morph chips. Failed words stay in the stream with an error card. Highlight a join, span fence, or `^` to inspect the construction.

**IPA:** below the input transcribes the spoken forms (phonology letter table, with syllable dots). Writing shorthand and span brackets expand to their spoken forms first; foreign `<>` interiors and compact loan words are skipped (not Agalan phonology).

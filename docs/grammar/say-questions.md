# Asking and answering

Recipes for English question shapes beyond plain *yes/no* and *who / what*. Every form here is taught on a grammar page; each recipe links what it needs.

## Echo and reported questions {#echo-reported}

### Echo questions {#echo}

**Needs:** [Ask](questions.md#question) · [tone marks](speech-moves.md#tone-marks)

An echo question repeats what someone just said, because you doubt it or did not catch it (*You saw WHAT?*). Repeat the sentence under **`yol`**. Put the doubting [tone mark](speech-moves.md#tone-marks) **`?!`** on the blank or on the word you doubt.

> `yol ?!dar zawan vawebol.`
>
> y-question | ?!d-wh | z-listener | v-see
>
> "You saw WHAT?"

> `yol ?!zuzozen vowurel.`
>
> y-question | ?!z-Uzozen | v-walk
>
> "UZOZEN walked?"

### *Really?*, *Is that so?* {#really}

**Needs:** [Polar stance](questions.md#polar-stance) · [tone marks](speech-moves.md#tone-marks)

English *Really?* asks the speaker to confirm what they just said. Write the confirm tag as your own turn: **`yol yael.`** (*Is that so?*). Put the doubting mark **`?!`** on it for disbelief.

> `zuzozen vowurel. yol ?!yael.`
>
> z-Uzozen | v-walk . y-question | ?!y-yes
>
> "Uzozen walks." "Really?!"

**Compare with:** **`yol.`** (*Huh?*) asks the speaker to say it again. **`yaer.`** (*I see*) takes the news in for now without asking anything.

### Reported questions {#reported-questions}

**Needs:** [Fill-ask](questions.md#fill-ask-r) · [whether](questions.md#embedded-whether)

To report a *who* / *what* question inside a claim (*Uzozen asks who walks*), put the same blank you would use in a direct question inside [**`dorl`**](questions.md#embedded-whether). To quote the question as wording, keep `yol` inside a [cite](spans.md#quote-acts). To ask someone else whether they know such an answer (*Do you know who walks?*), see [a blank inside a dependent](questions.md#blank-in-dependent).

> `zuzozen vezuyel dorl zar vowurel.`
>
> z-Uzozen | v-tell | d-whether-clause | z-something | v-walk
>
> "Uzozen asks who walks."

## Listening and hedged replies {#listening}

### *Mm-hm* while someone talks {#continuer}

**Needs:** [Three endings](questions.md#polar-endings)

While someone tells you something, English keeps them going with *mm-hm* or *I see*, without agreeing or disagreeing. Reply **`yaer.`** after each point: it takes the point on board for now, without committing to it.

> `zuzozen vowurel. yaer.`
>
> z-Uzozen | v-walk . y-yes-for-now
>
> "Uzozen walked." "Mm-hm."

**Compare with:** **`yom.`** (*Hm?*) asks about what was just said, so the speaker stops to explain. **`yaem.`** (*yeah*) agrees with the claim.

### *Well…* before an unwelcome answer {#well}

**Needs:** [Three endings](questions.md#polar-endings)

English *well…* warns that the answer is not the plain *yes* the question hoped for. Start the reply with **`yuam`** (*it's more complicated than that*), then give the answer.

> `yol zuzozen vowurel. yuam zuzozen vuhohol.`
>
> y-question | z-Uzozen | v-walk . y-reject-frame-soft | z-Uzozen | v-sit
>
> "Did Uzozen walk?" "Well… Uzozen sat."

**Compare with:** **`yuel`** says the claim is false. **`yuam`** says the question does not quite fit, and the body says what happened instead.

### *It sounds like…*, *I hear that you…* {#reflecting}

**Needs:** [Whose view](knowing.md#holder) · [Evidentiality](knowing.md#evidentiality) · [Feeling with no object](sakes.md#feeling-no-object) · [Emotion compose](sakes.md#emotion-compose)

English *it sounds like you're anxious* and *I hear that you're upset* give the speaker's feeling back to them. You cannot state someone else's feeling outright, so name the listener as the holder on CLUES (**`theyem`**): you worked it out from what they said.

> `thuwathuruor theyemawan.`
>
> th-competence-unmet-passing-UNPLACED-SURGING | th-CLUES-listener
>
> "It sounds like you're anxious."

To say the feeling **makes sense**, put your own stance in the main sentence and theirs in a dependent, with what the feeling is about:

> `wowotham goyem zarl zezuyel wawothumam goyem theyemawan.`
>
> [w-understanding-met-any-term | g-stimulus] | z-that-clause | [z-speech | [w-relatedness-unmet-modifiable-INTERNAL-FLOWING | g-stimulus]] | th-CLUES-listener
>
> "It makes sense to me that the talk weighs on you."

**Compare with:** REPORTED (**`thebamawan`**) says they told you so in as many words. *I hear that* in a reflection is your reading of them, not hearsay.

### *Admittedly*, *granted* before a *but* {#admittedly}

**Needs:** [Polar stance](questions.md#polar-stance) · [Sentence linkers](dependents.md#sentence-linkers)

English *admittedly*, *granted* and *that's true, but* concede a point before pushing back. Open with **`yael`** (*true*): before a body it concedes that body, and alone it concedes what was just said. Then push back with **`xegohum`** (*but*).

> `yael zuzozen vowurel. xegohum zolazun vuhohol.`
>
> y-yes | z-Uzozen | v-walk . x-but | z-Olazun | v-sit
>
> "Admittedly, Uzozen walks. But Olazun sits."

> `zuzozen vowurel. yael. xegohum zolazun vuhohol.`
>
> z-Uzozen | v-walk . y-yes . x-but | z-Olazun | v-sit
>
> "Uzozen walks." "That's true, but Olazun sits."

**Compare with:** *even so* after the concession is *despite that*, **`hezom blarth`** ([pointing back](dependents.md#stand-in-back)). **`yaem`** (*I think so*) agrees only softly.

## Exclamations and side remarks {#reactions}

### *What a big dog!* {#exclamations}

**Needs:** [tone marks](speech-moves.md#tone-marks) · [extra detail on an adjective](clause.md#adjective-detail-w) · [interjections](speech-moves.md#interjections)

English *What a big dog!* and *How big the dog is!* say the dog is very big and that you feel strongly about it. Say the degree with *very* **`welazom`** and the feeling with the tone mark **`!`** before the sentence. Use **`!!`** for more.

> `! zudozol welazom gelazom.`
>
> ! | z-dog | [w-very | g-big]
>
> "What a big dog!"

For a bare *So big!*, give the reaction as an interjection with **`!`**:

> `!yelazom.`
>
> !y-big
>
> "So big!"

### *By the way* {#by-the-way}

**Needs:** [asides](spans.md#asides)

English *by the way* adds a side remark, then goes back to the main point. Put an aside **`th(`** … **`)`** at the start of the sentence. The closing fence is the return to the main point.

> `th(zolazun vezuvol) zuzozen vowurel.`
>
> th-ASIDE[z-Olazun | v-sleep] | z-Uzozen | v-walk
>
> "By the way, Olazun is asleep. Anyway, Uzozen walks."

## Offers and confirmations with joins {#join-questions}

### Single-item and standalone inventory
<a id="single-item-standalone-inventory"></a>

**Needs:** [Ask](questions.md#question) · [Fill-ask](questions.md#fill-ask-r) · [How many items](joins.md#standalone-phrase)

Under question, the rest of the single-item and standalone join map is confirm / offer (and fill-ask **-r**). On a list of two or more (no **-r**): **-l** picks from that closed menu; **-m** allows another name (and for **o**, *or none*); **u** is *none of* the listed; **ua** / **uo** is the complement of the listed; **ue** reverse-ranks among the listed. Join **-n** here is stock/usual.

> `yol zuzozen zel.`
>
> y-question | [z-Uzozen | z-rank/more]
>
> "Is Uzozen the only priority?"

**Not the same job as:** proper-name **-n** ([word ending](word-endings.md#proper-name--n)).

**Related form:** fill-ask length uses [join **-r**](questions.md#fill-ask-arity).

## Speech acts in everyday talk {#speech-acts}

### Ask, interview, conversation {#ask-words}

**Needs:** [Ask](questions.md#question) · [Reported questions](#reported-questions) · [Stand-in vowels](dependents.md#stand-in) · [Speech act: firm and soft](speech-moves.md#speech-act) · [Stacking two act words](speech-moves.md#stacked-acts) · [Quiz](questions.md#quiz) · [Conversation length](compounds.md#conversation-length) · [Call someone](speech-moves.md#vocative)

English *ask* is four jobs. You can put a question to someone, ask them for something, ask them to do something, or report that a question was asked. A question is the act word **`yol`**. A request is **`yem`**. A reported question hangs on the telling ([reported questions](#reported-questions)). *Ask* or *tell someone to do* something is a telling with the to-clause stand-in **`derl`**, and the person told is plain `/b/`; *not to* is **`durl`**. English picks *ask* or *tell* for politeness, which Agebon carries on the act word, not the verb. A conversation has no verb of its own: it is *speech*, with the abstract sense for the social practice.

| English | Agebon | Reading |
|---------|--------|---------|
| *ask* (a question) | `yol zuzozen vowurel.` | question |
| *ask* (for something, *could you*) | `yem vowurel zawan.` | request |
| *could you…?* (a request phrased as a question) | `yem yol vowurel zawan.` | soft request that sounds like a question |
| *would you maybe…?* (not really optional) | `yel yom vowurel zawan.` | command that sounds like a gentle ask |
| *ask* (a question you know the answer to, as a test) | `yol yal zar vowurel.` | quiz |
| *ask* (report) | `zuzozen vezuyel dorl zar vowurel.` | telling + whether-clause |
| *ask* / *tell someone to* | `zuzozen vezuyel bolazun derl vuhohol.` | telling + to-clause |
| *ask* / *tell someone not to* | `zuzozen vezuyel bolazun durl varuvel.` | telling + lest-clause |
| *please ask her to* | `yem vezuyel bolazun zawan derl vuhohol.` | request: tell Olazun to sit |
| *ask* (one question, as a bid for time) | `olazuxon.` | one slot |
| *interview* | `yolazun.` then a run of `yol` questions | call, then ask |
| *conversation* / *chat* / *dialog* | `zezuyem` | *discourse* |

> `yem vezuyel bolazun zawan derl vuhohol.`
>
> y-request | v-tell | b-Olazun | z-listener | d-to-clause | v-sit
>
> "Please ask Olazun to sit."

### Offer, suggest, recommend, invite {#offer-words}

**Needs:** [Speech act: firm and soft](speech-moves.md#speech-act) · [Tags](questions.md#tags) · [Prescription](sakes.md#sake-force) · [Permission](sakes.md#permission) · [Which noun](dependents.md#which-noun)

English *offer*, *suggest*, *propose*, *recommend* and *advise* all put something forward that the other person may take or leave. Agebon picks how firmly. A soft act word (**-m**) leaves the choice open. A soft request with *we* proposes a joint act, and a tag after it asks whether they take it up. A prescription **`the`** on a sake says the act ought to serve it, and its ending says whether the person invited it (**-l**) or you are offering it unasked (**-m**).

| English | Agebon | Reading |
|---------|--------|---------|
| *offer* (a thing) | `yom` + a giving | soft question: *would you like…?* |
| *suggest* / *propose* (a joint act) | `yem vowurel zayan.` | soft request with *we* |
| *…, okay?* / *…, deal?* (after a suggestion) | `yol yaol.` | tag: asks them to take it up |
| *…, or would you rather not?* | `yol yuol.` | tag: offers them the refusal |
| *suggest* (a view) | `yam` | soft statement: *for what it's worth* |
| *recommend* / *advise* (unasked) | `thawothem` | prescription, offered |
| *recommend* (you asked for it) | `thawothel` | prescription, invited |
| *advice* / *suggestion* / *proposal* | the suggestion as a sentence, then point back | [which noun](dependents.md#which-noun) |
| *invite* / *welcome* (someone) | `vunodum` | *hospitality* |
| *welcome* (you may) | `hwavam` | permission granted |

> `yem vowurel zayan.`
>
> y-request | v-walk | z-interlocutors
>
> "Let's walk." (a suggestion)

> `zawan vowurel thawothem blawan.`
>
> z-listener | v-walk | [th-relatedness-ought-offered | bl-listener]
>
> "You should walk; it would help you feel connected." (unasked advice)

> `zuzozen vunodum dolazun.`
>
> z-Uzozen | v-hospitality | d-Olazun
>
> "Uzozen invites Olazun."

### Hello, sir, yeah, quote, mention {#reply-words}

**Needs:** [Greeting](word-endings.md#greeting) · [Polar stance](questions.md#polar-stance) · [Cite](spans.md#writing) · [Mention](spans.md#mention) · [Asides](spans.md#asides)

| English | Agebon | Reading |
|---------|--------|---------|
| *hello* / *hi* | `SELFn.` | your own name, said with a period |
| *sir* / *madam* | `yolazun.` | call them by name |
| *yeah* / *yep* | `yaem.` / `yael.` | yes, soft / firm |
| *nope* / *nah* | `yuel.` / `yuem.` | no, firm / soft |
| *yeah, sure* | `yaol.` | take up the offer |
| *quote* (the exact words) | `d[zolazun vezuyel]` | cite |
| *quote* (the gist) | `d~[zolazun vezuyel]` | paraphrase cite |
| *mention* (a word) | `glegel z[udozo]` | mention |
| *mention* (in passing) | `th(…)` | aside |
| *mention* (acknowledge someone) | `vewuyal` | *acknowledgment* |

> `zuzozen d[zolazun vezuyel] vezuyel.`
>
> z-Uzozen | d-CITE[z-Olazun | v-tell] | v-tell
>
> "Uzozen said, “Olazun talks.”"

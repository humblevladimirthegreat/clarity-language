# Beginner review {#beginner-review}

Each level ends with one review. It mixes every page of that level, one item per page, so you meet each decision again after the pages that followed it. Take the review once you have finished every page at that level. Each answer ends with a **Rule:** link to the section it tests: when you miss an item, reread that section.

Every word in a review was already met in an earlier practice, so there are no new words.

The Beginner review covers every Beginner section, from word endings to plans.

#### English → Agebon {#beginner-english-to-agebon}

::: details Show answer

#### Pick one {#beginner-pick-one}

**1.** *education* `uzahal` or `uzaham`

::: details Show answer
`uzaham`

education

*Education* is the abstract sense of the root, so it takes **-m**; `uzahal` is a *school*.

**Rule:** [First mention, abstract](word-endings.md#abstract-m)
:::

**2.** *Uzozen sees a mango or a watermelon: exactly one of the two.* `zuzozen demedol dowodol dol vawebol.` or `zuzozen demedol dowodol dal vawebol.`

::: details Show answer
`zuzozen demedol dowodol dol vawebol.`

z-Uzozen | [d-mango | d-watermelon | d-or-exactly-one] | v-see

**`dol`** picks one of the items; **`dal`** would be *a mango and a watermelon*.

**Rule:** [Choice (o)](joins.md#choice-o)
:::

**3.** *What does Olazun write?* `yol ?dar zolazun varayel.` or `yol ?dur zolazun varayel.`

::: details Show answer
`yol ?dar zolazun varayel.`

y-question | ?d-wh | z-Olazun | v-write

**`dar`** asks *what*; **`dur`** asks *what else*.

**Rule:** [Fill-ask](questions.md#fill-ask)
:::

**4.** *Ahelan swims only at night.* `zahelan vazuvel hanurul hal.` or `zahelan vazuvel hanurul ham.`

::: details Show answer
`zahelan vazuvel hanurul hal.`

z-Ahelan | v-swim | h-night | h-only-when

Closed **`hal`** says night is the only time; open **`ham`** leaves room for other times.

**Rule:** [Only when / never](restrictors.md#only-when-never-hal)
:::

**5.** *Uzozen is more intense than Olazun.* `zuzozen zolazun zel guhunem.` or `zolazun zuzozen zel guhunem.`

::: details Show answer
`zuzozen zolazun zel guhunem.`

[z-Uzozen | z-Olazun | z-rank/more | g-intensity]

The first name before **`zel`** ranks higher.

**Rule:** [Comparatives](comparatives.md#comparatives-e)
:::

**6.** *My helmet serves the physical sake in the long term.* `zehobul gobathal.` or `zehobul gobathul.`

::: details Show answer
`zehobul gobathal.`

z-helmet | g-physical-met-lasting

**`tha`** serves the sake; **`thu`** would make the helmet a lasting harm to it.

**Rule:** [Met](sakes.md#met)
:::

**7.** *Uzozen sees someone who was punched.* `zuzozen dwadanuvul vawebol.` or `zuzozen dwazanuvul vawebol.`

::: details Show answer
`zuzozen dwadanuvul vawebol.`

z-Uzozen | d-episode-patient-punch | v-see

Part **`d`** names who the punch happens to; **`z`** is who punches.

**Rule:** [The undergoer](roles.md#the-undergoer-d)
:::

**8.** *a wood basket* `zulexabowal.` or `zabowaxulel.`

::: details Show answer
`zulexabowal.`

z-wood-x-basket

A wood basket is a kind of basket, so *basket* comes last; `zabowaxulel` would be a kind of wood.

**Rule:** [Two roots, one word](compounds.md#two-roots-one-word)
:::

#### Fix it {#beginner-fix-it}

**1.** *Ahelan, don’t run!* <!-- lint: error -->`yahelan yel varuvel.`

::: details Show answer
`yahelan yul varuvel.`

y-Ahelan | y-prohibition | v-run

*Don’t* is **`yul`**; **`yel`** tells Ahelan to run.

**Rule:** [Speech act](speech-moves.md#speech-act-statement-question-command)
:::

**2.** *Olazun is a police officer.* <!-- lint: error -->`zolazun gorol bobayel.`

::: details Show answer
`zolazun gobayel.`

z-Olazun | g-police

**`gorol`** says Olazun and the police are one and the same; to put Olazun in a kind, the kind word goes on `/ɡ/`.

**Rule:** [Kind / role](predication.md#classification)
:::

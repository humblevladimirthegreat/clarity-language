# Level reviews

Each level ends with one review. It mixes every page of that level, one item per page, so you meet each decision again after the pages that followed it. Take the review once you have finished every page at that level. Each answer ends with a **Rule:** link to the section it tests: when you miss an item, reread that section.

Every word in a review was already met in an earlier practice, so there are no new words.

#### Pick one {#advanced-pick-one}

**1.** *Olazun cooks “Tomato and Watermelon.”* (the familiar dish) `zolazun dudedol dowodol dan vuguyel.` or `zolazun dudedol dowodol dal vuguyel.`

::: details Show answer
`zolazun dudedol dowodol dan vuguyel.`

z-Olazun | NAME[d-tomato | d-watermelon | d-and] | v-cook

**-n** on the join names the list as one set phrase; **`dal`** is just *a tomato and a watermelon*.

**Rule:** [Named phrase](joins.md#named-list)
:::

**2.** *Olazun is sleepier than Ahelan said.* `zolazun thebam blahelan zel guzemol.` or `zolazun zel guzemol thebam blahelan.`

::: details Show answer
`zolazun thebam blahelan zel guzemol.`

[z-Olazun | [th-REPORTED | bl-Ahelan] | z-rank/more | g-sleepy]

Before the rank join, REPORTED is the bar: what Ahelan said sets the level. After the fence it is a stance on the whole superlative: *Olazun is the sleepiest, Ahelan says*.

**Rule:** [Every bar](comparatives.md#stance-bars)
:::

**3.** *Uzozen sees a huge but finite number of baguettes.* `zuzozen debodelx grawam vawebol.` or `zuzozen debodelx grawal vawebol.`

`zuzozen debodelx grawam vawebol.`

z-Uzozen | [d-baguette-x | g-plus-infinity.about] | v-see

Open **-m** keeps the amount finite, just very large; **`grawal`** is infinitely many.

**Rule:** [Special values](digitless-exponents.md#special-values)

**4.** *Uzozen, whom I remember as sleepy, is sleeping.* `zuzozen wevum guzemol vezuvol.` or `zuzozen guzemol vezuvol thevum.`

::: details Show answer
`zuzozen wevum guzemol vezuvol.`

[z-Uzozen | [w-MEMORY | g-sleepy]] | v-sleep

On `/w/` right before the adjective, MEMORY covers only *sleepy*; **`thevum`** on `/th/` covers the whole sentence: *I remember sleepy Uzozen sleeping*.

**Rule:** [Mood on one adjective](knowing.md#mood-on-adjective)
:::

**5.** *Uzozen commands that Olazun cook.* `zuzozen vern zolazun vuguyel.` or `zuzozen varn zolazun vuguyel.`

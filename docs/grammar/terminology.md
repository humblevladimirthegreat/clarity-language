# Terminology

English names these grammar pages use. Each entry is a short gloss plus a link to the teaching section. The teaching page is the source of truth for how the form works.

This is not a dictionary of roots. For word senses, use the [Lexicon](lexicon.md).

## A–Z

### Ability / incapability

Hosted *can* / *can’t* (`xa` / `xe` / `xo` / `xu`) and hostless **ABIL**.

[Can and can't](/can-and-cant#ability)

### Act word

The written `/y/` word: **`yal` / `yol` / `yel` / `yul`** and soft **-m**.

[Speech act: firm and soft](/firm-soft#speech-act)

**Compare with:** [speech act](#speech-act) is the setting (statement / question / command / prohibition). Polar [stance](#polar-stance) particles are not act words.

### Address set

Listener special plus **-x**: the current addressees (no speaker).

[Plurality](/plurality#person-role-x)

### Adjective extra (`/w/`)

Detail on the following adjective, not the whole clause.

[Clause](/clause)

**Compare with:** a [restrictor](#restrictor) says when the host applies. Default `/w/` on sakes is [sakes](#sakes).

### Adverb

Ordinary manner `/h/`.

[Clause](/clause#adverbs-h)

**Compare with:** [mood tags](#mood-tags) are closed psychological [stance](#stance) `/th/`, not `/h/`.

### Ambient magnitude

Casual claim that an omitted exponent inherits a discourse order of magnitude; **`Ne0`** asserts OoM 0.

[End-relative and stacked markers](/stacked-markers#ambient-magnitude)

### Antecedent

The word a **-r** form points back to.

[Pronouns](/pronouns)

### Arity (list / single-item / standalone)

How many items sit before a join: **list** (2+), **single-item** (1), **standalone** (0).

[Joins](/joins#single-item-phrase)

### As-for

Complex `/h/` + `/b/` (**`hahugem`**) *as for X*: a frame for one sentence.

[People, things and places](say-people-places.md#as-for)

**Compare with:** sake [met](#met-unmet-motive-prescription-sakes) / unmet on `/ɡ/` is how a sake stands toward a noun, not *as for*. *Now, about X* sets the [topic](#topic).

### Aside

A parenthetical comment span (`th(…)` on the sentence; `g(…)`, `w(…)`, or `h(…)` on a noun, a word, or the action).

[Spans](/spans#asides)

### Associative

**-x** = named **anchor plus associates**. On a kind word (**-lx**) or a join **-r** (`zarx`), an indefinite group.

[Plurality](/plurality#associative)

### Bare OoM

Digitless exponent naming a magnitude band (`g+e0`, `grawagel`).

[Digitless and exponents](/digitless-exponents#bare-oom-bands)

### Bar

A `/th/` stance word in the comparee slot of a rank join: the value you rank against (*than planned*, *enough*, *than usual*).

[Comparative arity and scales](/comparative-arity#bars)

### Binder

Inside a scope island: scope-taking `/h/` or `/th/` and/or a join.

[Mention and scope islands](/span-mention#scope-islands)

### Body (utterance)

Clause content after the left-edge `/y/` cluster.

[Speech moves](/speech-moves#turn-y)

### Break / rest

Silence counted in beats at a sentence edge with no word: a **break** puts the next beat half a beat late (around a scope island), a **rest** is one silent beat (a period).

[Singability and speech edges](/singability#speech-edges)

### Capacity

A slot-part role compound on `/h/` (`hwezuduyel`): the role the act is done in (*as a teacher*).

[Viewpoint and landmark](/viewpoint#capacity)

**Compare with:** resemblance is the [similative](/relations#similative) (`hewom blwezuduyel` *like a teacher*). [Classification](#classification) on `/ɡ/` says someone **is** that role (`gwezuduyel`).

### Invited / offered / trial
<a id="asked-typical-history"></a>
<a id="wording-general-history"></a>
<a id="bound-endorse-invite"></a>

Prescription endings on sake **`the`**: why a move is welcome. **-l** invited, **-m** offered to serve the sake, **-r** a trial, worth trying to see whether it serves the sake.

[Ought for a sake](/ought-for-a-sake#sake-force)

### Changeability

Endings on unmet **`thu`**, **DECISION**, and ability *can’t*.

[Sakes](/sakes#sake-changeability), [Decision and try](/ability#decision)

### Citation / prefix-less citation

Root + ending with no role letter, outside a sentence.

[Word endings](/word-endings#citation-forms)

### Classification

Kind / role as predicative `/ɡ/` (no *to-be* verb).

[Predication](/predication#classification)

**Compare with:** [identity](#identity) uses **SAME**. [Equative](#equative) is *as ADJ as*, not kind-ascription.

### Clause pole / NP pole

Condition vs outcome as NPs vs full sentences after **`blarl`**. **Clause pole** is the causation reading. Concession **`hezom`** uses the same NP vs clause split (*despite Olazun* vs *although Olazun walks*). Intended outcome **`hovam`** uses it too (*for a money-bag* vs *so that Olazun sits*).

[Causation](/causation), [dependents](/dependents#dependent-clauses)

### Clusivity

Who counts as *we* / *you* when names are unavailable.

[Plurality](/plurality#clusivity)

### Collective

**-x** on `/v/` (one shared act) vs on `/ɡ/` (property of the group as a unit).

[Collectives](/collectives#verbs-v), [plurality](/collectives#adjectives-g)

### Command / request / prohibition

Speech act **`yel` / `yem` / `yul` / `yum`**.

[Speech act: firm and soft](/firm-soft#speech-act)

### Comparative / superlative / equative

Rank join + SHARED **scale** (`/ɡ/` quality or `/h/` manner); single-item = superlative; **`oe`** = equative.

[Comparatives](/comparatives)

### Complex adjective / complex adverb

`/ɡ/` or `/h/` plus immediately following `/b/`.

[Clause](/clause#extra-nouns)

### Confirming a negative

Polar patterns that confirm a denied body.

[Polar inventory and rhetorical](/polar-inventory#confirming-a-negative)

### Content of the event

A role word whose part consonant names a quality of the event (`dwahowurel` *a way of walking*, `dwahar` *the way it was done*), not a person in it.

[Viewpoint and landmark](/viewpoint#content-of-the-event)

### Continue (`/x/`)

Same-speech-act forward motion.

[Dependents](/dependents#continue-x)

**Compare with:** a [sentence linker](#sentence-linker) is the *therefore / however* subclass of continue, and a [topic](#topic) word is the *now, about X* subclass.

### Conventional (number **-n**)

Number ending **-n**.

[Word shape, markers, and endings](/number-markers#number-endings)

**Compare with:** content **-n** is [named](#concrete-abstract-named-resume). Phrase join **-n** is a [named list](#named-list). Clause join **-n** is a [sequence](#sequence).

### Cross-role recast

Resume with a different PoS letter than the antecedent.

[Pronouns](/pronouns)

### Dependent clause

A full sentence that fills a [stand-in](#stand-in) slot (`darl` / `barl` / `dorl` …). Last in the host slot; the dependent follows immediately.

[Dependents](/dependents#dependent-clauses)

**Compare with:** English *the dog that runs* (which dog) is [which person or thing](/dependents#which-noun), not a stand-in. Intended outcome *so that* is [**`hovam`**](/dependents#so-that) plus **`blarl`** or a noun `/b/`.

### Digitless

Number word with marker and ending but no digit groups.

[Digitless and exponents](/digitless-exponents#digitless)

### Digitless exponent

Advanced free-number specials (`+e`, `-e-`, `+0e`, …). **Just short** is free `-e-`.

[Special values, hyperbole, number as stance](/special-values#special-values), [just short](/special-values#just-short)

**Compare with:** [quasi](#quasi) is the numeric prefix `-e-xROOT`. **NOTIONAL** is the as-if [mood](#mood-tags), not a number.

### Distributive

Set **`a`** + singular SHARED = *both/each are ADJ*.

[Amounts, sizes and time](say-amounts.md#distributive-both)

### Kind reference

`zuan` plus a SHARED kind: the kind itself as one individual, not its members (*the dodo is extinct*).

[Universals, domains, and generics](/universals-domains#kind-reference)

### Domain / generic / *every K*

SHARED kind plus invert / standalone joins; a SHARED word after the join can bound the set (`zual gwodanam blayan` *everyone we know*).

[Universals, domains, and generics](/universals-domains#universals-domains-generics)

Closed **-l** allows no exceptions; open **-m** leaves the exceptions open (*as far as I know*, *as a rule*). A channel on the clause says how you know the general claim.

### Empty-allowed

Plain **o** + **-m**: *or none*.

[Joins](/joins#empty-allowed-om)

### Ending / word ending

Final **-l / -m / -n / -r** (plus optional **-x**). **Word endings** names the content-word table.

[Word endings](/word-endings)

### Epistemic *because*

Reason for the *could be* (**MAY**), not a *because* about the world (`thahum`).

[Reasons, knowledge and plans](say-reasons.md#epistemic-because)

### Equative

**`oe`** SHARED scale = *as ADJ as*.

[Comparatives](/comparatives#equatives)

### Evidence strength

Endings on an evidential channel: **-l** strong evidence, **-m** default, **-r** weak evidence. How much the claim rests on, not how likely it is.

[Evidentiality](/evidentiality#evidence-strength)

### Evidentiality

Closed `/th/` how-you-know roots: seven channels, two direct (LIVE, MEMORY), three reasoned (PATTERN, CLUES, DEDUCED), one received (REPORTED), one internal (INTUITION).

[Evidentiality](/evidentiality#evidentiality)

### Frame (carried channel)

A channel, NOTIONAL, or holder that keeps covering your later claims with no channel, until a new frame, a written statement word, *by the way*, or a goodbye.

[Evidentiality](/evidentiality#carry-forward)

### Already / still / not yet / no longer

Four `/h/` words for how a change stands now, not when it happened.

[Already, still, not yet, no longer](/already-still-yet#phasal)

### Residue / former climate

Closed `/th/` episode standing: leftover on the tally, or usual weather you are not claiming now. Not past tense.

[Notional and standing](/notional-standing#residue)

### Fault

Endings on *because*: **-l** the reason broke a rule, **-m** plain cause, **-r** one share among others. The act sits in `/b/`, not the person.

[Fault](/fault#fault)

### Extra noun (`/b/`)

Hosted: noun bolted onto a complex `/ɡ/`, `/h/`, or `/th/`. Unhosted: recipient or addressee of the verb.

[Clause](/clause#extra-nouns)

### Label scope

Mid-word **`th`** plus a vowel on a content root, or the vowel right after **`w`** on a role word: this episode (**`tha`**), practiced role (**`the`**), this pair (**`tho`**), type across scenes (**`thu`**), or a stacked vowel.

[How far a label reaches](/label-seams#label-scope)

**Compare with:** [restrictor](#restrictor) names the occasions, not the kind of claim. [Usual role](#usual-role) is practiced-role **`e`** on a role compound.

### Locative relation

Place how (*in*, *at*, *on*, *from*, *toward*, *through*, *out of*; frame **-m** *amid* / *near* / *over* / *away from* / *in the direction of* / *out from among* / *by way of*) as extra-noun [hooks](/hooks#extra-noun) plus landmark `/b/`. *Between* is hosted `/h/` or `/ɡ/` plus a `/b/` join. A *from A to B* path is two hook extras (source, then goal).

[Of-relations and social ties](/of-relations#locative-relations)

### Means

Extra-noun [hook](/hooks#extra-noun) **`ael`** plus implement `/b/` (*using / with* a tool). Not join-relation *with*. To name a kind of tool as its own noun, use a compound with a head root ([places, tools, and times](/viewpoint#places-tools-times)).

[Extra nouns and whose](/extra-noun-hooks#extra-noun-intermediate)

### Simile (*like*)

Hosted **`hewom`** / **`gewom`** plus model `/b/` (*like* a duck / *like* Uzozen’s); with **-l**, **`hewol`** / **`gewol`** is *exactly like*. Not **`SAME`**, not equative *as … as*, not NOTIONAL *as if*, not join-relation *on a par with*.

[Relations](/relations#similative), [Of-relations and social ties](/of-relations#exactly-like)

### Exchange (*for*)

Hosted **`heham`** / **`geham`** plus consideration `/b/` (*in exchange for* a hammer). Not recipient `/b/`, not sakes motive **`tho`**, not intended-outcome [**`hovam`**](/dependents#so-that).

[Relations](/relations#exchange)

### Proxy (*on behalf of*)

Hosted **`hanom`** / **`ganom`** plus principal `/b/` (*on behalf of* Olazun). Not hook *instead*, not recipient `/b/`.

[Relations](/relations#proxy)

### Of relation

Hosted `/h/` or `/ɡ/` plus `/b/` for English *of* that is not ownership or a place: *part of*, *contents of*, *made of*, origin *from*.

[Of-relations and social ties](/of-relations#of-relations)

### Factivity

Whether *if* vs *because* treats the dependent as asserted.

[Reasons, knowledge and plans](say-reasons.md#factivity)

### Fill-ask / fill-all

Under question, join **-r** is a blank; several **-r** = fill all.

[Questions](/questions#fill-ask-r)

### Firm / soft

Speech-act ending **-l** vs **-m**.

[Speech act: firm and soft](/firm-soft#speech-act)

### Forecast
<a id="forecast-source"></a>

A claim that something **will** happen: an evidential channel plus the later offset **`blral`**. There is no forecast word, so a forecast always names how you know it.

[Evidentiality](/evidentiality#forecast)

### Greeting / conversation length

A named citation is a hello (`uzozen.`). Add mid-word **`x`** plus **`a`** / **`e`** / **`o`** / **`u`** to say how long the conversation is expected to last: open-ended, a few minutes, one slot, or passing. At departure, **`xen`** means leaving soon, **`xon`** about to leave, and **`xun`** leaving now. When people set different lengths at arrival, the lesser bid holds.

[Word endings](/word-endings#greeting) · [Compound families](/compound-families#conversation-length)

### Hold

**MAY** find-out / who-knows endings / **NOTIONAL** play endings.

[Knowing](/knowing#may-hold)

### Holder

Whose view a clause reports. The name is fused onto an evidential, **MAY**, or **NOTIONAL** after that word's own ending letter (`theyemuzozen`). The fused word is the speaker's warrant; every other stance in the clause is the holder's.

[Whose view](/holder#holder)

**Compare with:** a person in hosted `/b/` after an evidential is the source you heard it from, not the holder. The ON-BEHALF locus is your own feeling about someone else's stake.

### Host

Content root a sake `th`-stance or an ability `x`-stance attaches to.

[Can and can't](/can-and-cant#ability), [sakes](/sakes)

**Compare with:** a measure [unit](#measure-phrase-unit) is not a host. Classification names the classified noun, not a host.

### Identity

Closed **SAME** + `/b/`.

[Predication](/predication#identity)

### In progress

[Predication](/predication#in-progress)

**Compare with:** a [role compound](#role-compound--agent--place--patient--recipient) names a participant, with no claim that the act is under way.

### Met / unmet / motive / prescription (sakes)

Sake stances **`tha` / `thu`** (Beginner); **`the`** (Beginner ought); **`tho`** (Intermediate motive). Prescription **`the`** on the clause is deontic on the host act (*ought to … for this sake*), not a performance report with a separate norm tag. The ending marks whether the move is [invited, offered, or protective](#asked-typical-history), not how firmly you address them.

[Sakes](/sakes)

**Compare with:** [as-for](#as-for) is **`hahugem`**, not a sake stance.

**Compare with:** clause *so that* is [**`hovam`**](/dependents#so-that), not the goal noun **`oval`**.

### Numeric prefix

Role letter + number body + **`x`** (or **`mx`** for an approximate number) + root: a kind of the root, named by a number idea (`zrawaxolamal` *essence of love*, `zragexezuyel` *a three-part dialogue*). Like English *quasi-*, *proto-*, *multi-*, *tri-*.

[Numeric prefixes](/numeric-derivation)

### Occasion

Listed pieces before a restrictor (when the host applies).

[Restrictors](/restrictors)

### Offer (question)

### Generic pronoun

**`eyon`**: any person, as a rule. English *one*, generic *you*.

[Pronouns](/pronouns#generic-pronoun)

**Compare with:** `awan` is always the person actually listening; `umon` is one unidentified individual.

### One of a name

### Overlay / sense-form

[Lexicon](lexicon.md)

**Compare with:** a number under `/v/` `/h/` `/th/` `/y/` `/x/` is [Word shape, markers, and endings](/number-markers#number-as-roles), not an overlay. Lexicon abstract **-m** stays abstract.

### Quasi

[Numeric prefixes](/numeric-derivation#quasi)

### Range / ray

Numeric from–to with a hook between the ends (`al` / `ul`); a ray has ±∞ as one end. The same idea on any ordered line is the [range hook](#range-hook).

### Relative clause (English *who / that / which*)

Identify a noun with two sentences (then [resume](#resume)), a hosted `/ɡ/` + `/b/` relation, or a [role compound](#role-compound--agent--place--patient--recipient) on `/ɡ/`.

[Dependents](/dependents#which-noun)

### Restrictor

Join-shaped `/h/` `/w/` *when the host applies*.

[Restrictors](/restrictors)

**Compare with:** English *who / that / which* on a noun is [which person or thing](/dependents#which-noun), not a restrictor.

### Resume

Content / number **-r** pointing back. A content resume spells the earlier word’s whole stem.

[Pronouns](/pronouns), [Word shape, markers, and endings](/number-markers#number-endings)

**Compare with:** join / restrictor **-r** is an [unspecified member](#unspecified-member). English *the dog that…* is two sentences, then this resume ([which person or thing](/dependents#which-noun)).

### Hook

Prefix-less *including / rather / instead / except*, discourse glue, extra-noun **in** / **on** / **at** / **toward** / **from** / **out of** / **through** / **for** / **using** / **against** (frame **-m** *amid* / *over* / *near* / *in the direction of* / *away from* / *out from among* / *by way of* / *used by* (whose) / *by* / *contrary to*; placement decides the job; **`uem`** before a `/th/` stance is *contrary to* that stance), and fused [Hook compounds](/hook-compounds#hook-compounds) (citation plus extra-noun hook). Optional `/w/` immediately before the free hook grades that hook (including restrictors); `/b/` sits immediately after the free hook. Stacked discourse, *somewhere* / *here* / *there*, resume **-r**, parallel chains, and extra placements are [discourse hooks](/discourse-hooks).

[Hooks](/hooks)

[Discourse hooks](/discourse-hooks)

### Hook prefix

Role letter + extra-noun hook vowels + **`x`** (or **`mx`** for the hook's **-m** frame) + stem: something in that hook's relation to the stem (`guexavuvul` *anti-fighting*, `gamxohalal` *mid-ocean*). Like English *anti-*, *in-*, *trans-*, *-ward*.

[Hook compounds](/hook-compounds#hook-prefixes)

### Hooked join

Role letter + extra-noun hook vowels (+ **`m`** for the frame or **`r`** to point back) + **`x`** + a join, with the hook's landmark as the `bl-` word after it: the join's set, bounded by that relation (`zoxual blahehal` *everyone at the house*, `dexar bluwon` *something for me*). On `/h/` it bounds which times a restrictor covers (`huxal blenulul` *never since the thunderstorm*).

[Hooked joins](/hooked-joins#hooked-joins)

### Role compound / agent / patient / recipient
<a id="role-compound--agent--place--patient--recipient"></a>

Role letter + **`w`** + scope vowel + part consonant + stem (`zwazuduyel`). The part consonant is the role letter the participant had in the event: **`z`** the doer (agent), **`d`** what it acts on or makes (patient), **`b`** the extra party (recipient, or the other party on a relation stem). On `/h/` a slot part is [capacity](#capacity). A [content](#content-of-the-event) part names a quality of the event.

[Role compounds](/roles#role-compounds)

**Compare with:** *a guard who sits* is this stem on `/ɡ/` with a non-resume ending, and *teacher* takes scope **`e`** for the usual role ([which noun, with resume and kinds](say-people-places.md#which-noun-intermediate)). Resume **-r** is *the one teaching* (this instance), not the usual role.

### Role pointer

Role letter + **`w`** + scope vowel + part consonant + pointer vowel + ending (`zwazar`): a participant of an earlier event (**`a`** the latest one with that role, **`o`** the other one, **`u`** the one the event left unsaid) or of this sentence (**`e`**, a reflexive). **-r** names the participant, **-l** a new one of the same kind (`zwazal`), and **-m** the participant’s part in the event (`zwazam`).

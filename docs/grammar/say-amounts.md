# Amounts, sizes and time

Recipes for English comparisons, percentages, units and dates. Every form here is taught on a grammar page; each recipe links what it needs.

## Comparing {#comparing}

### Sorted lists (`ae`) {#sequence-scale}

**Needs:** [Sequence `ao`](/rank-joins#sequence-ao) · [Comparatives](/comparatives#comparatives)

When English would say *from least to most ADJ* (*easiest to hardest*, a syllabus order), you are not naming a winner. You are listing items along the scale from its low end. Use [sequence](/rank-joins#sequence-ao) **`ae`** before the shared adjective: the first name is the **start** of the scale, the last is the far end.

> `zuzozen zolazun zahelan zaol gamahum.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | z-Olazun | z-Ahelan | z-in-order | g-challenge]
>
> "Uzozen, Olazun, Ahelan, from least to most challenging."

**Compare with:** `zuzozen zolazun zahelan zol gamahum` puts Uzozen at the **top** (*most* challenging first). Rank **`e`** lists best-first; sequence **`ae`** lists start-first.

### Distributive *both are ADJ*
<a id="distributive-both"></a>

**Needs:** [And-lists](/joins#and-lists-a) · [Equatives](/comparatives#equatives-oe-shared-scale)

English *both are challenging* (or *Uzozen and Olazun are challenging*) says **each** has the quality. It does not rank them and does not say they match in height. Write the names, then set join **`a`** (`zal`), then a shared singular adjective (`/ɡ/`). Each name gets that adjective.

> `zuzozen zolazun zal gamahum.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | z-Olazun | z-and | g-challenge]
>
> "Uzozen and Olazun are both challenging."

**Compare with:** *as challenging as* uses equality **`oe`** (`zoel`). *More challenging than* uses rank **`e`**.

**Related form:** *ADJ as a unit* uses shared `/ɡ/`…**-x** under **`a`**: [plurality](/collectives#adjectives-g). Shared comparative and equative adjectives are singular.

Equality **`oe`** with a shared scale needs **two or more** names. **`oe`** with no shared scale is a tie among the names (equal rank).

| Agebon | Reading |
|--------|---------|
| `zuzozen zolazun zahelan zoel gamahum` | *Uzozen, Olazun, and Ahelan are equally challenging* |
| `zudozol zagozol zoel gamahum` | *the dog is as challenging as the cat* |

### Measured differentials {#measured-differentials}
<a id="measure-gap"></a>

**Needs:** [Comparatives](/comparatives#comparatives) · [Measure phrases](/numbers-applied#measure-phrases)

English *two meters taller* still ranks two people on one quality, and it also names **how large the gap is**. Keep the rank join and the shared scale adjective. After that adjective, write a **measure noun** (`/b/`): that noun is the **unit** (published abstract = SI name), and `/ɡ/` on the unit is the **amount** ([measure phrases](/numbers-applied#measure-phrases)).

> `zuzozen zolazun zol gadahem blezerem gradol.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | z-Olazun | z-rank/more | [g-height | [bl-meter | g-two]]]
>
> "Uzozen is two meters taller than Olazun."

**Related form:** vague *much* / *slightly* is shared `/w/` immediately before the scale adjective (`wohalal`), not a counted `/b/`.

### Snapshot on a shared scale
<a id="as-of-scale"></a>

**Needs:** [Comparatives](/comparatives#comparatives) · [*as-of*](/as-of#as-of)

To score the shared adjective as of a dated now, write [*as-of*](/as-of#as-of) on `/w/` immediately before that `/ɡ/` word, with `/b/` between them. That snapshot does not retarget the verb; the clause still needs `/h/` *as-of* if the event also scores against that now.

> `zuzozen zolazun zol wenam b_#22,7 gamahum.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | z-Olazun | z-rank/more | [[w-as-of.ledger | b-_22,7] | g-challenge]]
>
> "As of 22 July, Uzozen is more challenging than Olazun."

## Amounts and percents {#amounts}

### Scale the amount
<a id="no-metric-prefixes"></a>
<a id="metric-prefixes"></a>

**Needs:** [Measure phrases](/numbers-applied#measure-phrases) · [Exponents](/digitless-exponents#exponents)

Keep the **base** unit. Put the power of ten on the **amount** (`e3` / `e-3` / an exact count): `bezerem g+5400` or `bezerem g+5.4e3`; `beberom g+70e3`.

**For *kilometers* / *milligrams*, use:** that scaled amount on the base unit. There is no *kilo-* / *milli-* / *mega-* stem.

For *two meters taller*, see [measured differentials](#measured-differentials). Vague degree uses `/w/` on the scale (`zol wohalal gadahem …`) with no unit. Duration and other clause measures use the same unit+amount habit in whatever slot the relation needs.

### Denominator (portion “of what”)
<a id="percent-denominators"></a>
<a id="percent-of-what"></a>

**Needs:** [Percent](/numbers-applied#percent-and-percentage-points) · [Associative `-x`](/plurality#associative) · [Resume](/pronouns#resume-r)

| Agebon | Use | English |
|--------|-----|---------|
| `zagozolx g+25%` | percent grades the modified noun | *25% of the cats* |
| `dowalx g+95%` | same, object slot | *95% of the tests* |
| `beyolx g+5%` under a host relation | same, `/b/` | *5% of the people* |

For a count out of a group already named (*three of them*), resume the group and give the count.

> `zagozolx vuhohol. zagozorx gragel varuvel.`
> z-cat-x | v-sit . [z-←cat-x-x | g-three] | v-run
>
> "The cats sit. Three of them run."

The noun names the reference class; **`yo`** grades how much of that class. Same endings on the percent word (**-m** ≈ *about 25% of …*).

**Related form:** `25%` (`…yo`) and `0.25` (plain scalar) name the same magnitude; **`yo`** chooses the percent-scale reading. **`yu`** likewise names a ÷100 magnitude, framed as **points**.

### *Most* {#most}

**Needs:** [Percent](/numbers-applied#percent-and-percentage-points) · [Ranges](/numbers-applied#ranges)

*Most of the cats* means *more than half*: the band from all of them down to half, with half left out. Write **`g+100%`**, then **`ul`**, then **`g+50%`**. The hook excludes the endpoint after it.

> `zagozolx g+100% ul g+50% vuhohol.`
>
> 🔊 `zagozolx grabamomoyol ul grazamoyol vuhohol.`
>
> [z-cat-x | g-100yo] | through-excluding | g-50yo | v-sit
>
> "Most of the cats sit."

### *One in three* {#one-in-n}

**Needs:** [Fractions](/numbers-applied#fractions)

*One in three cats* is the same share as *a third of the cats*. Write the [fraction](/numbers-applied#fractions) **`g-N`** right after the noun.

> `zagozolx grugel vuhohol.`
>
> [z-cat-x | g-third-of] | v-sit
>
> "One in three cats sits."

**Compare with:** `/h/` **`h-3`** is *every third time*: it counts repeats of the event, not members of a group.

### *Per hour* and *3 to 1* {#per-unit}

**Needs:** [Number as adverb](/number-markers#number-as-adverb-by-marker) · [Measure phrases](/numbers-applied#measure-phrases) · [Factor](/comparative-arity#factor)

*Three times an hour* is a count per unit. Write the count, then the divider **`hrubal`** (*÷1*), then the unit as a `bl-` word right after it. The divider hosts the unit, so the `bl-` word is structural, not a recipient.

| English | Agebon |
|---------|--------|
| *per* (*per hour*, *three times an hour*) | the count, then **`hrubal`**, then the unit in `/b/` |

> `zuzozen vowurel hragel hrubal blagorum.`
>
> z-Uzozen | v-walk | h-three | [h-divided-by-one | bl-hour]
>
> "Uzozen walks three times an hour."

A ratio (*three cats to one dog*) is a [factor](/comparative-arity#factor) on the equative amount scale: `zagozolx zudozolx zoel gral hragel` is *three times as many cats as dogs*.

### Percent points, factors, and other wholes

**Needs:** [Percent](/numbers-applied#percent-and-percentage-points) · [Number as adverb](/number-markers#number-as-adverb-by-marker) · [Of relations](/of-relations#of-relations)

**`yu`** is a point-scale amount or delta (`+2` percentage points; often `/v/` **`ra`** / **`ru`**: increase or decrease by that point amount). `from A% to B%` uses [ranges](/numbers-applied#ranges).

**For *+50% relative to baseline*, use:** a multiplicative `/h/` factor (**`h+1.5`**, …) or ordinary *relative-to* wording.

When the whole is not the modified head, use a complex `/ɡ/` or `/h/` [of relation](/of-relations#of-relations) (`gobem` *part of*) + `/b/` whole, with the percent elsewhere in the clause as needed. Numbers take no `/w/` and no open arguments.

**`…yu`** point amounts, factor change (`/h/` **`h+…`**), *top / bottom N%* rank bands, and *N% done* take their “whole” from context or a separately named class.

A bare `/z/` (or other freestanding) **`…yo`** percent with no named whole is grammatical; prefer an explicit whole (*percent of what*). Bare **`…yu`** point amounts are fine when the percent-scale quantity being moved is already clear. Rates outside everyday percent talk use a plain scalar (`ye` / `e-N`).

### Unspecified value in the range (**-r**)
<a id="unspecified-value-in-the-range--r"></a>

**Needs:** [Ranges](/numbers-applied#ranges) · [Named and unknown ranges](/initials#named-ranges)

A range hook with **-r** names **one value in the band** without saying which.

| Agebon | Use | English |
|--------|-----|---------|
| `z+3 ar z+5` | some value in the inclusive band | *some value from 3 to 5* |
| `z+3 ur z+5` | some value in the band, 5 left out | *some value from 3 up to 5* |
| `zrazal ar zrawal` | some value on the ray | *some value of 5 or more* |
| `z+3 uor z+5` | some value outside the band | *some value below 3 or above 5* |

Under [question](/questions#fill-ask-r) force, these are fill-asks (*which value from 3 to 5?*). The same shape works under `/d/` `/b/` `/ɡ/` as the slot needs (`d+10 ur d+20` = object *some value from 10 up to 20*).

**Not the same job as:** the resume hooks `ar` / `ur` with nothing on the right (*in there* / *from there*), and discrete *something among two listed values* (`zragel zrazal zar`).

## Degree, manner and frequency words {#degree-manner}

### How much: *very*, *slightly*, *barely* {#degree-words}

**Needs:** [Degree](/left-bound#degree-w) · [Number as verb and adverb](/special-values#special-number-as-verb-and-adverb) · [Number as stance](/special-values#special-number-as-stance)

English degree words (*really*, *quite*, *barely*) say how far a quality or an action goes. Agebon has stock degree words for the adjective or adverb that follows them, and a pair for *barely* and *almost*. A claim you are sure of takes the stance number `th+e`.

| English | Agebon |
|---------|--------|
| *very* / *really* / *highly* / *truly* | `welazom` before the adjective or adverb |
| *extremely* / *greatly* / *dramatically* / *significantly* / *considerably* | `wohalal` |
| *slightly* / *somewhat* | `wamezom` |
| *completely* / *totally* / *entirely* / *fully* / *altogether* | `wuhuram` |
| *quite* / *fairly* / *pretty* / *reasonably* / *relatively* | `wadowom` |
| *huge* | `wohalal gelazom` (*extremely big*) |
| *barely* / *hardly* | `hrawul` on the verb, `wrawul` before an adjective |
| *almost* | `hruwul` on the verb, `wruwul` before an adjective |
| *definitely* / *absolutely* / *certainly* | stance number `th+e` |

> `zudozol wadowom gelazom vowurel.`
>
> [z-dog | [w-quite | g-big]] | v-walk
>
> "A fairly big dog walks."

> `zuzozen hrawul vuhohol.`
>
> z-Uzozen | h-+-e- | v-sit
>
> "Uzozen barely sits."

### How: *carefully*, *properly*, *gently*, *hard to* {#manner-words}

**Needs:** [Adverbs `/h/`](/clause#adverbs-h) · [Manner scale](/comparative-arity#manner-scale) · [Every bar](/stance-bars#stance-bars) · [Can](/can-and-cant#can)

English *-ly* adverbs say how an action is done. Write `/h/`, the root, and the abstract ending **-m**, as in `hedurem` *hastily*. When the quality is the root's everyday sense and the abstract sense is something else, use **-l**: `ogugo` is *correct* and its abstract sense is *inclusion*, so *properly* is `hogugol`.

| English | Agebon |
|---------|--------|
| *quickly* | `havugem` |
| *carefully* | `hayulem` |
| *gently* | `hegemam` |
| *properly* / *correctly* | `hogugol` |
| *properly* (*up to standard*) | the doer, then `hwuvol zoel hogugol` (as correctly as the rules require) |
| *precisely* / *accurately* | `hubugom` |
| *neatly* / *in an orderly way* | `henodam` |
| *seriously* | `hozedom` |
| *surprisingly* / *suddenly* | `hemom` |
| *surprisingly* + adjective (*surprisingly blue*) | `wemom` right before the `/ɡ/` word |
| *pleasantly* / *usefully* / *frustratingly* + adjective (*pleasantly hot*) | a `/w/` sake word right before the `/ɡ/` word: `wodotham gohamul` ([personal possession](/sakes#personal-possession)) |
| *successfully* | `hemebem` |
| *strongly* | `hobobom` |
| *heavily* | `harolom` |
| *perfectly* | `huhuram` |
| *significantly* (*matters a lot*) | `hagugem` |
| *gradually* / *slowly* | `huzezam` |
| *widely* / *broadly* | `horahem` |
| *deeply* | `hebowum` |
| *easily* (*with less effort than usual*) | `thubem zuol huzubam` before the verb |
| *hard to* / *easy to* + a verb (*hard to ignore*) | the doer, then `thubem zol huzubam` / `thubem zuol huzubam`, and the verb with *can* (`-xal`) |

> `zuzozen hayulem vowurel.`
>
> z-Uzozen | h-caution | v-walk
>
> "Uzozen walks carefully."

The easy / hard pair on a quality ranks effort against a bar ([vague amounts](/vague-amounts#vague-amounts)): `zanal thubem zuol guzubam` is *the book is easy* (less effort than usual), and `zol` in place of `zuol` is *hard*.

*Hard to* + a verb puts the same effort scale on whoever would do it: they can, but only with more effort than usual. Write the doer as the ranked item, the bar, the rank word and **`huzubam`**, then the verb with [*can*](/can-and-cant#can) **`xa`**.

> `zuzozen thubem zol huzubam danal varayexal.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | th-PATTERN | z-rank/more | h-effort] | d-book | v-write-able
>
> "The book is hard for Uzozen to write."

*Properly* in the sense of *up to standard* names the standard as a bar ([every bar](/stance-bars#stance-bars)): REQUIRE **`hwuvol`** is the rule, and a tie meets it. Use **`hwuvor`** for what is customary.

> `zuzozen hwuvol zoel hogugol varayel.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | h-episode-event-REQUIRE-rule | z-equal-rank | h-correct] | v-write
>
> "Uzozen writes properly." (as correctly as the rules require)

### How often {#frequency-words}

**Needs:** [Always](/restrictors#always-hual) · [Sometimes](/restrictors#sometimes--anytime--some-other-time) · [Frequency scale](/comparative-arity#frequency-scale) · [Whose view](/holder#holder)

English *always*, *usually*, *often* and *sometimes* sit on a scale from every time to some time. Agebon uses the restrictor for the ends and the frequency scale for *often*. For *every day*, hang a unit on `herem` *while* with `bual` (*everything*).

| English | Agebon |
|---------|--------|
| *always* / *constantly* | `hual` |
| *usually* / *normally* / *generally* / *regularly* / *typically* (*as a rule*) | `huam` |
| *often* / *frequently* | `thubem zol hral` before the verb |
| *frequent* / *regular* / *usual* (*a regular visitor*, *the usual route*) | put the frequency on the verb: `zuzozen vavobel huam.` (Uzozen usually comes) |
| *sometimes* / *occasionally* | `har` |
| *never, as far as I know* / *as a rule, never* | `ham` |
| *never* | `hal` |
| *never, in all the times we both know* | `hal thevumayan` |
| *daily* | `herem blual guzurem` |
| *weekly* | `herem blual gagorem` |
| *monthly* | `herem blual gumalem` |
| *annually* | `herem blual gavuvam` |

> `zuzozen herem blual guzurem vowurel.`
>
> z-Uzozen | [h-while | [bl-everything | g-day]] | v-walk
>
> "Uzozen walks daily."

| English | Agebon |
|---------|--------|
| *numerous* / *considerable* (*many cats*) | `zagozolx thubem zol gral` |
| *enough* / *sufficient* / *adequate* / *full* (as much as is needed) | `zagozolx thudatham zoel gral` |
| *too many* / *excess* / *extra* / *spare* (more than needed) | `zagozolx thudatham zol gral` |

| *single* (*just one*) | the plain number `g+1` |
| *secondary* / *second* | the rank `g#2` |

| *exactly* / *precisely* / *specific* (*exactly five*) | the plain number `g+5`; `gram` is *about five* |

| *twice* / *double* (two times) | `h+2` before the verb |
| *ratio* (*three cats to one dog*) | `zagozolx zudozolx zoel gral hragel` ([factor](/comparative-arity#factor)) |
| *at least 5* / *at most 5* (*minimum* / *maximum*) | `zrazal al zrawal` / `zruwal al zrazal` |
| *decade* / *century* | `bavuvam g+10` / `bavuvam g+100` |
| *unit* | the measure phrase: `bezerem g+2` |

### Expensive, cheap, weak, remarkable {#bar-words}

**Needs:** [Vague amounts](/vague-amounts#vague-amounts) · [Every bar](/stance-bars#stance-bars) · [Adjectives](/clause#adjectives-ɡ) · [Important](say-reasons.md#sake-words)

English *expensive*, *cheap*, *weak* and *remarkable* compare a quality with an unstated usual. Agebon always states the bar. Write the PATTERN bar **`thubem`** (*than usual*), then the rank word (**`zol`** for *more than*, **`zuol`** for *less than*), then the quality as an adjective. The quality is the root that names what is measured, in its abstract sense (**-m**): cost, strength, effort.

With no `/b/`, **`thubem`** is the item's own usual level or the usual case in the situation, so `zuzozen thubem zuol gobobom` is *Uzozen is weaker than usual* (today, compared with themself). When English means a trait, compared with other people (*a rich family*, *a weak swimmer*), put the people you compare against in the bar's `/b/`: **`thubem bleyolx`** is *than most people*.

| English | Agebon |
|---------|--------|
| *expensive* / *costly* / *pricey* | `thubem zol goderom` (costs more than usual) |
| *cheap* / *inexpensive* | `thubem zuol goderom` |
| *rich* / *wealthy* (than most people) | `thubem bleyolx zol gegomam` |
| *poor* (*little money*, than most people) | `thubem bleyolx zuol gegomam` |
| *strong* / *powerful* (than most people) | `thubem bleyolx zol gobobom` |
| *weak* / *feeble* / *frail* (than most people) | `thubem bleyolx zuol gobobom` |
| *weak today* / *not as strong as usual* | `thubem zuol gobobom` |
| *not strong enough* (for what you need) | `thudatham zuol gobobom` ([sake bar](/prescription#sake-bars)) |
| *busy* / *hard-working* | `thubem zol guzubam` (more effort than usual) |
| *far* / *far away* / *distant* / *remote* | `thubem zol gazavem` (farther than usual) |
| *wide* / *broad* | `thubem zol gorahem` |
| *narrow* | `thubem zuol gorahem` |
| *deep* | `thubem zol gebowum` |
| *shallow* | `thubem zuol gebowum` |
| *thick* | `gunedol gul` (not flat) |
| *remarkable* / *special* (unusual) / *outstanding* | `thubem zol` + the quality that stands out: `thubem zol gelazom` |
| *picky* / *choosy* / *particular* (fussy) | `thubem zol guhugom` (more selective than usual) |
| *major* / *significant* (matters more than usual) | `thubem zol gurethal` ([important](say-reasons.md#sake-words)) |

> `zanal thubem zol goderom.`
>
> [z-book | th-PATTERN | z-rank/more | g-cost]
>
> "The book is expensive."

> `zuzozen thubem bleyolx zuol gobobom.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | [th-PATTERN | bl-person-x] | z-rank/less | g-strength]
>
> "Uzozen is weak." (weaker than most people)

> `zuzozen thubem zuol gobobom.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | th-PATTERN | z-rank/less | g-strength]
>
> "Uzozen is weaker than usual."

> `zuzozen thudatham zuol gobobom.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | th-sake-met-any-term | z-rank/less | g-strength]
>
> "Uzozen is not strong enough."

**Compare with:** any stance that sets a value can be the bar ([every bar](/stance-bars#stance-bars)). *More expensive than I expected* is INTUITION, `thabom zol goderom`, and *too expensive* is a sake bar, `thudatham zol goderom`. Bare *good* and *bad* have no root ([wrong, ugly](say-reasons.md#wrong-ugly)).

### Just, only, also, especially {#focus-words}

**Needs:** [One item or none](/joins#standalone-phrase) · [Join vowels and endings](/joins#join-type-vowel-series) · [Stacked discourse hooks](/discourse-hooks#stacked-discourse) · [Dated channel](/universality#now)

English *just*, *only*, *especially* and *also* point at one item and say how it stands among the others. Agebon does that with a [join](/joins) that has one item in front of it. The vowel says what the item is worth among the rest: **`zal`** (the closed ending) says it is the whole story, **`zom`** (the open ending) says it comes first and others may follow, and **`zol`** says it is the only one that matters. For *also*, there is no word to hang on the item: open the next sentence with the discourse hook **`al`**, which adds a separate point.

| English | Agebon |
|---------|--------|
| *just Uzozen* / *only* / *merely* / *simply* / *alone* / *a mere* / *this particular one* | `zuzozen zal`, the join after the one item |
| *especially Uzozen* / *particularly* / *specifically* / *mainly* / *primarily* / *largely* / *in particular* / *main* (*the main one*) | `zuzozen zom` |
| *only Uzozen matters* | `zuzozen zol` |
| *also* / *too* / *as well* / *besides* / *in addition* | `al …` at the start of the next sentence |
| *just now* (*a moment ago*) | `blruwul` on a channel |
| *just about to* | `blrawul` on a channel |

> `zuzozen zal vowurel.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | z-and] | v-walk
>
> "Only Uzozen walks."

> `zuzozen zom vowurel.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | z-rank/more.open] | v-walk
>
> "Uzozen in particular walks."

> `zuzozen vowurel. al zolazun vowurel.`
>
> z-Uzozen | v-walk . additionally | z-Olazun | v-walk
>
> "Uzozen walks. Also, Olazun walks."

**Compare with:** *just* meaning *only a moment ago* is the hair before now (**`blruwul`**, [recently](say-tense.md#time-words)), not a join. *Only when* is the restrictor **`hal`** ([restrictors](/restrictors)), and *only if* is **`thebom`** ([only if](/causation#only-if)).

## Units and dates {#units-dates}

### Group, crowd, bunch, pair, list, range, member {#group-words}

**Needs:** [Associative group](/plurality#associative) · [Vague amounts](/vague-amounts#vague-amounts) · [Unspecified member](/joins#unspecified-member-r) · [Ranges](/numbers-applied#ranges) · [Of relations](/of-relations#of-relations)

English has many nouns for a set of things (*group*, *crowd*, *bunch*, *pair*). Agebon has no noun for the set itself. It marks the kind as a **group in this situation** with **-x** after the ending (**-lx** for everyday things), and a number or a vague amount says how big. A set of people, flowers or cats is the kind plus **-x**, not a separate word.

| English | Agebon | Reading |
|---------|--------|---------|
| *group* / *bunch* | `zudozolx` | some dogs, as a set here |
| *crowd* | `zeyolx thubem zol gral` | people, more than usual |
| *pair* (two of a kind) | `zudozolx g+2` | two dogs |
| *couple* (two people who are together) | `zagayal` | the everyday sense of `agaya` |
| *titled group* (the group named Ahunan) | `zahunanx` | named, **-nx** |
| *member* (of a group) | `gobem` + `/b/` group | part of the group |
| *member* (unspecified, of a menu or list) | `zer` | anything among the listed |
| *list* (*a list of cats and dogs*) | `zagozol zudozol zal` | an and-list |
| *list* (written) | `zegowal` | *clipboard* |
| *range* (of numbers) | `zragel al zrazal` | the band from 3 to 5 |
| *range* (of things: how far it reaches) | `zaham` | *scope*, the abstract sense of `aha` |

> `zudozolx vowurel.`
>
> z-dog-x | v-walk
>
> "A group of dogs walks."

> `zeyolx thubem zol gral.`
>
> [z-person-x | th-PATTERN | z-rank/more | g-amount]
>
> "There is a crowd." (more people than usual)

> `zuzozen gobem blahunanx vowurel.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | [g-part-of | bl-Ahunan-x]] | v-walk
>
> "Uzozen, a member of the group Ahunan, walks."

**Compare with:** a named group needs its own name (**-nx**). *A few* or *several* is an approximate number ([how many](#quantity-words)).

### Named standards and derived units
<a id="unit-short-form"></a>

**Needs:** [Measure phrases](/numbers-applied#measure-phrases) · [Proper name `-n`](/proper-names#proper-name--n)

Everyday measure uses the **unit metaphor** on the stock roots above (`bezerem gradol`). When you cite the **conventional SI name** as a titled standard (*the meter*, *the pascal*), use **-n** on that unit root ([named handles](/word-endings#named-n-beginner)). Resume a prior unit phrase with **-r** on the same root (`bezerer gradol` after `bezerem gradol` is on the table).

**Common derived (optional)**: unit metaphor on a dedicated root; related **quantity** stays on the original row:

| English unit | Unit root (metaphor) | Example | Quantity (unchanged) |
|--------------|----------------------|---------|----------------------|
| newton | `avava` *newton* | `bavavam g+10` | `anuvu` *force* |
| pascal | `odowa` *pascal* | `bodowam g+101325` | `agewa` *pressure* |
| joule | `uzale` *joule* | `buzalem g+500` | `abewa` *energy* |
| watt | `emave` *watt* | `bemavem g+60` | `ubewo` *power* |
| hertz | `azula` *hertz* | `bazulam g+50` | `udora` *rhythm* |
| ohm | `obewo` *ohm* | `bobewom g+100` | `egohu` *blockage* |
| coulomb | `alevo` *coulomb* | `balevom g+2` | `urazu` *charge* |
| radian | `udugu` *radian* | `budugum g+1` | `aruge` *cycle* |

**Related form:** general **`+1xROOT`** (*primary / singleton of a kind*) is a [numeric prefix](/numeric-derivation#scalar-digit-morphs), not an SI alias.

### *Until tomorrow*, *by tomorrow* {#pole-from-now}
<a id="until-tomorrow"></a>

**Needs:** [Time poles](/dependents#dependent-clauses) · [Signed count from now](/universality#dated-channel) · [Dated channel](/universality#dated-channel)

English *until tomorrow* (also *till tomorrow*), *by tomorrow*, and *before next week* count from today. A command or a plan has no channel to count from, so the time pole takes the count itself: a signed measure in its `/b/` runs from now.

> `yol vuhamum zawan harum bluzurem grabal.`
>
> y-command | v-confidentiality | z-listener | [h-until | [bl-day | g-one]]
>
> "Keep the secret until tomorrow."

Use **`harum`** for *until* / *till*, **`hevahum`** for *by*, **`habam`** for *before*, and **`halam`** for *after*. A negative count runs back from now.

### Date as books vs event-when
<a id="as-of-vs-clock"></a>

**Needs:** [Time](/digit-strings#time) · [*as-of*](/as-of#as-of)

Bare `h_#…` / `h_…` locates **when the event sits**. Whose “now” leftover, climate, plan, and forecast score against is [*as-of*](/as-of#as-of) (`henam` / `hunem` plus `/b/`). Put the date on that pole (`henam bl_#22,7`); do not also write spare `h_#22,7` for the same snapshot. Clock / *before* / *after* may sit beside *as-of*: books vs event-when are different jobs.

## Brainstorming {#brainstorming}

### Numbered alternatives (problem / solution / goal)
<a id="numbered-alternatives"></a>
<a id="ideation"></a>

**Needs:** [Ordinals](/numbers#ordinals)

When you brainstorm, number the candidates (*problem 1*, *solution 2*, *goal 3*) so the first frame does not look unique. Use ordinary lexicon nouns plus a free [ordinal](/numbers) (`g#N`), not a closed overlay. *Problem* and *solution* take the published **-m** senses; *goal* is already the **-l** literal. Prefer at least **`grobal`**, and often name a second candidate, so ranking itself marks that more than one frame is in play.

> `zuzozen dogewem grodol vezuyel.`
> z-Uzozen | [d-solution | g-2nd] | v-tell
>
> "Uzozen names solution 2."

| Agebon | Use | English | Same root as | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|--------------|-----|
| **`abevam`** | numbered-alternative problem | *problem* (something to fit / solve) | `abeval` *puzzle-piece* | 🧩: pieces must fit |
| **`ogewem`** | numbered-alternative solution | *solution* (what unlocks it) | `ogewel` *key* | 🔑: opens what was locked |
| **`oval`** | numbered-alternative goal | *goal* (net you aim at) | | 🥅: the net you shoot for |
| free **`g#N`** | rank in **this** numbered-alternative series | *candidate N* | | `#` = ordinal place |
| soft **`g~#N`** | tentative ranking | *maybe candidate N* | | **-m** leaves the order open |

**Recipe:** `zabevam grobal` / `zogewem grodol` / `zoval grobal`

Unnumbered `zabevam` is just *a problem*, not a candidate in a set, write **`g#N`** when brainstorming. `-l` on the first two roots is still the picture (`zabeval` *a puzzle piece*; `zogewel` *a key*; `vogewel` *to unlock*). Reframing the **same** situation is different wording of one frame, not a second digit.

**Compare with:** discourse *point N:* uses free **`x#N`** ([number as discourse](/number-markers#number-as-discourse-marker-by-marker)), a continue. Numbered alternatives are problem / solution / goal nouns plus **`g#N`**. Clause *so that* is [**`hovam`**](/dependents#so-that), not **`oval`**.

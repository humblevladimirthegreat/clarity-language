# Numeric derivation {#numeric-derivation-page}

To name a kind of thing by a number idea, join a [number](numbers.md) stem onto a content root, the way a [lexical compound](x-compounds.md#lexical-compounds) joins two roots: **-l** after the root's everyday sense, **-m** after its abstract sense. The number then describes the kind itself (its essence, its parts, its rank, its catalog code) instead of counting anything in the sentence. (cue: join then number **r**: **`lr`** / **`mr`**)

## Advanced {#advanced}

### Numeric derivation (`ROOT` × number stem) {#numeric-derivation}

English often names a *kind of thing* with a number idea baked in (*essence of love*, *a three-part dialogue*, *specimen #1*). In Agazan you write the content root, a [lexical-compound](x-compounds.md#lexical-compounds) join (**-l** / **-m**), then a number stem. That stem has no role letter and no ending of its own; the role letter and one [word ending](word-endings.md) belong to the **whole derived word**. You can then use that word in any role: the number describes the kind, not how many there are. (cue: same glue as *bedroom*, right half is a number)

> `zalavalrabal.`
>
> z-love-l-infinity
>
> "essence of love"

```text
PoS + ROOT + {l|m} + NUM + {-l|-m|-n|-r}
```

| Piece | Use | Cue |
|-------|-----|-----|
| **PoS** | Ordinary slot for the derived word | |
| **ROOT** | Any content root (`V(CV)+`) | |
| **join** | Same letter as a [lexical compound](x-compounds.md#lexical-compounds): **-l** the root’s everyday sense, **-m** its abstract sense | `ebedalahaza` *bedroom* uses **-l** |
| **NUM** | Number stem: same marker / digits / digitless-exp body as [numbers.md](numbers.md), without a role letter and without its own ending. Tables below use [shorthand](numbers.md#writing-preferred-shorthand) (`+e`, `_12`, `+1e`, …). Actual words are always spelled CV (`raba`, `roba`, `rawoba`, …): [writing style](numbers.md#writing-style-numeric-vs-spelled) | spoken stem starts with **`r`**, so you hear **`lr`** or **`mr`** |
| **Ending** | Word ending on the **whole word**, after the number: **-l** the kind itself, **-m** approximately that kind (*roughly a three-part dialogue*), **-n** a name, **-r** a resume. The join letter, not this ending, says whether the root is in its everyday or abstract sense. | |

The **Agazan** columns below write the shape in shorthand (`ROOTl+e`), but a real word is always spelled out (`zalavalrabal`): shorthand is for free numbers only. After the abstract sense, the join is **-m**: `zalavamrabal`.

A final **-m** makes the kind approximate:

> `zazawan dezebelrarem vahahal.`
>
> z-Azawan | d-discourse-l-three | v-see
>
> "Azawan sees a roughly three-part dialogue."

**Compare with:** a free [number](numbers.md) word counts, ranks, or labels something in the sentence. A derived word describes the kind.

A stem with digits has one digit only: **`+N` / `-N`** (how many parts) or **`#N`** (which rank). The marker gives the spoken vowel (**`ra`** / **`ru`** / **`re`**). A derived noun takes plural **-x** as usual ([plurality](plurality.md)).

### Limit / polarity / stage (digitless)

The digitless number words (plus or minus infinity, first or last place, *more than one*) also work as stems. After a root and the join, they say **what the kind is** at a limit: its essence, its smallest grain, its absence, its first or final form, many of it, or its undoing.

| Agazan | Use | English | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|-----|
| **`ROOTl+e`** | essence / absolute / pure type of ROOT | `zalavalrabal`: *essence of love* | **+e** = plus with no cap: nothing left to add |
| **`ROOTl+e-`** | grain / seed / infinitesimal of ROOT | `zalavalrabul`: *grain of love* | **+e-** = that same plus, at the tiny end: a speck |
| **`ROOTl-e`** | void of / absolute anti-ROOT | `zevehelrubal`: *void of fear* | **-e** = minus with no floor: none of the kind |
| **`ROOTl-e-`** | **quasi-ROOT**: the shape of ROOT without treating it as fully real (English **`quasi-`**); **`ROOTl-e-1`** / **`ROOTl-e-2`** = [quasi-N](#quasi-n) | `zanalobelrubul`: *quasi-friend*; `zedamrubudul`: *quasi-pair* | **-e-** = imaginary amount: the outline, not the ledger |
| **`ROOTl#e-`** | origin / prototype / onset-form of ROOT | `zalavalrebul`: *love in its beginning form* | **#e-** = start-place: the first rung |
| **`ROOTl#e`** | telos / culmination / final form of ROOT | `zozemrebal`: *story’s final form* | **#e** = last place: where the kind finishes |
| **`ROOTl+`** | poly- / multi-ROOT (unspecified >1) | `zehehamral`: *multi-faith* | **+** = more than one, how many unnamed |
| **`ROOTl-`** | de- / un- / deficit-ROOT | `vozemrul`: *to de-tale* | **-** = take that structure away |

### Quasi-ROOT (`-e-` / `-e-1` / `-e-2`)
<a id="quasi"></a>
<a id="quasi-root"></a>
<a id="quasi-n"></a>

English *quasi-* says something has the **shape or role** of a kind without counting as the real thing (*quasi-friend*, *quasi-official*). Spell that as **`ROOTl-e-`**: the kind’s outline, not a full claim that it is that kind. Change only the role letter to use it as a noun, verb, adjective, and so on.

> `zahaben ganalobelrubul.`
>
> z-Ahaben | g-friend-l-quasi
>
> "Ahaben is a quasi-friend."

| PoS | `…l-e-` | Examples |
|-----|---------|----------|
| **`/z/` `/d/` `/b/`** (noun) | a quasi-entity | `zanalobelrubul`: *a quasi-friend*; `debazemrubul`: *a quasi-melancholy* |
| **`/v/`** (verb) | to treat as quasi- / as-if that kind | `vanalobelrubul`: *to quasi-befriend* / treat as friend-shaped |
| **`/ɡ/`** (adjective) | *quasi-* (ascribed) | `gezevomrubul`: *quasi-official* |
| **`/h/`** (adverb) | under quasi- framing | `hezevomrubul`: *quasi-officially* |
| **`/w/`** (extra detail) | host graded as quasi- | `webazemrubul gebazem`: *quasi-melancholy* |
| **`/y/`** (interjection) | *Quasi-!* / *As if that kind!* | `yanalobelrubul` |

**Compare with:** with no root, **`grubul`** is the [just short](numbers.md#just-short) amount, and **`vrubul`** / **`thrubul`** / **`yrubul`** / **`xrubul`** are that number as a verb, stance, interjection, or discourse word. After a root, **`ROOTl-e-`** makes *quasi-* that kind.

#### Quasi-N (`-e-1` / `-e-2`)

For *quasi-* with a **number of parts** (*as if one*, *as if a pair*), write **`-e-N`**, where N is the digit that **`+N`** uses for that many parts. Spoken, the stem is **`ru`** + **`bu`** + the digit N: a bare order of magnitude, with no digit before the exponent. The word says the kind has that many parts only as if, not really.

**Compare with:** with no root, **`grubuwol`** / **`grubudul`** are real negative amounts in the order-of-magnitude bands −1 / −2.

| Agazan | Use | English | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|-----|
| **`ROOTl-e-1`** | quasi-unit / as-if singleton: unit-shaped without being a real one (twin **`ROOTl+1`**) | `zadehamrubuwol`: *a quasi-single voice* / as-if primary voice | **-e-** outline + **1** = as-if one |
| **`ROOTl-e-2`** | quasi-dual / imaginary duality: pair/mutual *as if*, not real reciprocity (twin **`ROOTl+2`**) | `gebewemrubudul`: *quasi-mutual commitment*; `zedamrubudul`: *quasi-pair / as-if partnership* | **-e-** outline + **2** = as-if a pair |

| Agazan | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **`ROOTl+2`** | real dual / mutual structure |
| **`ROOTl-2`** | privative: non-reciprocal / one-sided |
| **`ROOTl-e-2`** | quasi-dual: mutuality *as if* / imaginary duality |
| **`ROOTl-e-`** | general quasi (N unnamed) |
| **`g-e-2`** | free real negative amount, OoM −2 band |

For a zero-shaped quasi, use **`ROOTl-0`** (anti-null) or **`ROOTl-0e-`** (micro-residue). The role letters work as in the quasi table above.

### Scalar digit morphs (`+N` / `-N`)
<a id="scalar-digit-morphs"></a>

English *a pair of…*, *three-part…*, *primary…* can say **how many parts the kind has**, not how many things the sentence is about. Write a one-digit plus stem after the join: spoken, the marker then the digit (`ra`+`wo` for `+1`, spelled `…lrawol`). A minus stem **`-N`** (including **`-0`**, spoken `ru`+digit) is the **privative**: the kind lacks or undoes the `+N` structure (*one-sided* instead of *mutual*). The tables write the stems in shorthand (**`+N`** / **`-N`**).

> `zazawan debewemradul vahahal.`
>
> z-Azawan | d-pen-m-two | v-see
>
> "Azawan sees mutual commitment."

|--------|-----|---------|------|-------------------|-----|
| **`ROOTl+1`** | unit / primary / singleton | `zadehamrawol`: *a single / primary voice*; SI measure nouns use published unit metaphors instead ([measure phrases](numbers-applied.md#measure-phrases)) | `zadehamruwol` | *an indistinct / non-singular voice* | **1** = one piece |
| **`ROOTl+2`** | dual / mutual / reciprocal / pair | `gebewemradul`: *mutual commitment* | `gebewemrudul` | *one-sided commitment* | **2** = two sides |
| **`ROOTl+3`** | triad / triplet / 3-part / ternary | `zezebelrarel`: *a three-part dialogue* | `zagedumrurel` | *non-tripartite institution* | **3** = three parts |
| **`ROOTl+4`** | quad / 4-part / square / frame | `zahahumramol`: *a four-part / framed judgment* | `zemehamrumol` | *an unframed message* | **4** = four sides of a frame |
| **`ROOTl+5`** | hand / pentad / agency-set | `zulolraval`: *a handy / five-fold toolkit* | `zamamruval` | *a plan without practical grasp* | **5** = fingers of a hand |
| **`ROOTl+6`** | hex / 6-part / honeycomb / pack | `zavagelragul`: *a hexagonal / six-pack product* | `zahodomrugul` | *an unpacked team* | **6** = honeycomb / six-pack |
| **`ROOTl+7`** | heptad / week-cycle / complete series | `zezegumralel`: *a seven-fold / week-cycle ritual* | `zezegumrulel` | *a broken-cycle ritual* | **7** = days that close a week |
| **`ROOTl+8`** | octet / 8-part / doubled-quad | `zagezamrahal`: *an octet / eight-part blockage* | `zagezamruhal` | *a non-octet blockage* | **8** = two frames (4+4) |
| **`ROOTl+9`** | ennead / near-full / almost-ten | `zonodalranal`: *a nine-part / near-full choir* | `zonodalrunal` | *a far-from-full choir* | **9** = one short of ten |
| **`ROOTl+0`** | null / reset / emptied | `vodovemrazol`: *to zero-score / null the score* | `godovemruzol` | *a score that won’t null* / *stubborn residual score* (**anti-null**) | **0** = empty count |

**Not the same job as:** [ordinal digit morphs](#ordinal-digit-morphs) **`#N`** (*which order*). **`+N`** is *how many parts*.

#### Null / anti-null by PoS (`+0` / `-0`)
<a id="null-anti-null"></a>

**`+0`** treats ROOT as **resettable / emptied**; **`-0`** treats ROOT as **resisting emptying** (insistent residue). The role letter says *what kind of thing* is emptied or resists. **`ROOTl-e`** is *void of ROOT* (no such kind); **`ROOTl+0`** is a resettable instance of that kind.

| PoS | `…l+0` | `…l-0` | Examples |
|-----|--------|--------|----------|
| **`/z/` `/d/` `/b/`** (noun) | emptied / wiped instance | entity that won’t zero / stubborn residue | `zamomrazol`: *a wiped debt*; `zamomruzol`: *a debt that won’t zero*; `devomruzol`: *that memory that won’t blank*; `bodovemruzol`: non-null residue as argument |
| **`/v/`** (verb) | act of nulling / resetting | act that refuses null / keeps residual | `vodovemrazol`: *to null the score*; `vodovemruzol`: *to anti-null / refuse to zero the score*; `vozewemrazol`: *to zero effort*; `vozewemruzol`: *to leave effort unzeroable* |
| **`/ɡ/`** (adjective) | null / reset (ascribed) | non-emptyable / residual (ascribed) | `godovemrazol`: *null / reset*; `godovemruzol`: *won’t-null / residual*; `gevegemruzol`: *hope that won’t go to zero* (vs `gevegemrubal` *void of hope*) |
| **`/h/`** (adverb) | clause framed as reset / zeroed | clause framed as irreducible residue | `hozewemrazol`: *with effort zeroed*; `hozewemruzol`: *with irreducible effort* / *despite any zeroing* |
| **`/w/`** (extra detail) | host `/ɡ/` graded toward null | host `/ɡ/` graded toward anti-null | `wamomrazol gamom`: *debt (ascribed) zeroed*; `wamomruzol gamom`: *debt whose balance is anti-null* |
| **`/y/`** (interjection) | discourse *Zero it!* / *Null!* | discourse *Won’t zero!* / *Residue stands!* | `yodovemrazol`; `yodovemruzol` |

With a final **-m**, the wipe is only approximate: nearly emptied.

> `zazawan damomrazom vahahal.`
>
> z-Azawan | d-debt-m-zero | v-see
>
> "Azawan sees a nearly wiped debt."

**Related form:** a stronger wipe of the kind is [total null](#zero-exponent-derivation) **`ROOTl+0e`**. *Annihilate* with no root is the free number verb **`vrazobal`**.

### Zero × exponent (`+0e` / `±0e-1` / …)
<a id="zero-exponent-derivation"></a>
<a id="total-null"></a>
<a id="engineering-null-morph"></a>

The [zero × exponent](numbers.md#zero-exponent) stems from free numbers also work after a root. There they say the kind is **wiped out, or leaves a residue**, instead of naming an amount. Free **`vrazobal`** is *annihilate* with no kind attached; **`vodovemrazobal`** is *annihilate the score*.

| Agazan | Use | English | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|-----|
| **`ROOTl+0e`** | total null / absolute wipe of the kind | `vodovemrazobal`: *annihilate the score* (kind); `zamomrazobal`: *a totally wiped debt-kind* | **0** empty × **e** unbounded = wipe with no leftover scale |
| **`ROOTl+0e-`** | sterile / null grain of ROOT | `zevegemrazobul`: *sterile hope*; `zerehulrazobul`: *registration with no seed* | **0e-** = empty at the tiny end: no seed |
| **`ROOTl+0e-1`** | engineering null at order of magnitude −1 of ROOT: kind wiped at scale 10⁻¹ | `zehegomrabuwoyazol`: *deci-null problem*; `vodovemrabuwoyazol`: *null the score at deci scale* | **0e-1** = empty at the tenths band |
| **`ROOTl-0e`** | absolute residue of ROOT | `zamomruzobal`: *debt as absolute residue*; `vozewemruzobal`: *leave effort as absolute residue* | **-0e** = the minus of total wipe: residue that will not go |
| **`ROOTl-0e-`** | micro-residue of ROOT | `zevomruzobul`: *memory as micro-residue* | **-0e-** = residue at the tiny end |
| **`ROOTl-0e-1`** | engineering residue at order of magnitude −1 of ROOT | `zamomrubuwoyazol`: *debt as deci-scale residue* | **-0e-1** = residue at the tenths band |

The ending on the whole word marks soft (**-m**), named (**-n**), or resume (**-r**) as usual.

**Emptiness chain (kinds):** **`ROOTl-e`** (void of kind), **`ROOTl+0`** (resettable null), **`ROOTl+0e`** (total null), **`ROOTl+0e-`** (sterile grain), **`ROOTl+0e-1`** (engineering null at 10⁻¹). On free numbers, bare **`grabazol`** / **`Ne0`** instead [assert order of magnitude 0](numbers.md#bare-oom-bands).

On a free number, **`±0e-1`** is an amount. After a root, the same stems wipe the kind, or leave a residue of it, at that scale.

### Ordinal digit morphs (`#N`)
<a id="ordinal-digit-morphs"></a>

English *first draft*, *secondary adventure*, *third-order problem* can name **which rank the kind holds**, not which item is Nth in a list. Write a one-digit rank stem after the join: shorthand **`ROOTl#N`**, spoken **`re`** + digit, spelled `…lrewol`. A free **`g#N`** after a noun picks the Nth item in a series instead.

**`#N`** names a stage between the digitless ends **`#e-`** (first form) and **`#e`** (final form); **`#0e`** is [rank annihilated](#rank-annihilated-morph). For *lacks N parts*, use the scalar minus stem **`-N`**. For a code with several digits, use free `g#12` or the label marker **`_`**.

> `zalahen debeyomrewol varadal.`
>
> z-Alahen | d-pencil-m-1st | v-write
>
> "Alahen writes a first draft."

| Agazan | Use | English | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|-----|
| **`ROOTl#1`** | first-class / premiere / first-order | `debeyomrewol`: *a first-draft* (kind); `debeyom g#1` *the first draft (in this stack)* | **#1** = first place, not “one piece” |
| **`ROOTl#2`** | secondary / second-order / backup | `gamademredul`: *a secondary adventure* | **#2** = the next slot after first |
| **`ROOTl#3`** | tertiary / third-order | `zehegomrerel`: *a third-order problem* | **#3** = third rung |
| **`ROOTl#4`** … **`#9`** | Nth-order / Nth-place form | `zahavumreval`: *fifth-order harmony* (as a kind) | **#N** = Nth place on the kind |
| **`ROOTl#0`** | zeroth-order / pre-rank / unranked form | `zabugamrezol`: *a zeroth-order calculation* | **#0** = the rung before ranking starts |

**Not the same job as:** **`+1`** / **`+2`** (singleton / dual). **`#1`** is premiere-place; **`#2`** is secondary. **`#0`** is a zeroth rung; **`#0e`** is rank annihilated / disqualified.

### Label / code sense (`_`) {#label-code-sense}

When English makes a code part of what a thing is (*specimen #1*, *room 12* as a kind of room), the digits are a **catalog ID**, not a count or a rank. Put the digit-string marker **`_`** after the join; its digits are the code. With no digits, **`_`** describes the catalog itself ([infinite labels](#infinite-labels)).

> `zazawan debodelrowol vahahal.`
>
> z-Azawan | d-petri-dish-l-_1 | v-see
>
> "Azawan sees specimen #1."

| Agazan | Use | English | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|-----|
| **`ROOTl_`** | ROOT as something that **bears a code** (taggable unit; code unnamed) | `zebodelrol`: *a coded specimen* / *a specimen-as-ID* | **`_`** = catalog stamp, digits not yet filled |
| **`ROOTl_N`** | **specimen / item #N**: the code is part of the kind | `zebodelrowol`: *specimen #1*; `zahazalrowodul`: *room-12* as a coded kind; `bahazalrowodul`: *room-12* as `/b/` | the digits **are** the name, not a count |

When the number is only an argument of an ordinary noun, prefer ordinary syntax: `zahazal g_12` = *the room numbered 12*. Use **`ROOTl_N`** when the code is part of the **concept** (specimen ID, SKU, protocol code). To cite a numbered point in the conversation, use free **`x_…`**; to dial or enter a code, use free **`v_…`**.

Digitless **`+`/`-`/`#`** on ROOT = *what the kind is*; digitless **`_`** = *how the catalog names it*.

### Hyperbole sense (`+1e` / `#1e` / `_1e`)

To exaggerate a kind for comic effect (*a gazillion-mess*, *an umpteenth attempt*), put a mantissa before a digitless exponent, as in free [hyperbole](numbers.md#hyperbole-gazillion). Use **`1`** as the mantissa unless the joke needs another digit. Label hyperbole (`_1e` / `_1e-`) is catalog comedy: [infinite labels](#infinite-labels).

| Agazan | Use | English | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|-----|
| **`ROOTl+1e`** | *gazillion-ROOT* (comic huge) | `zazabomrawobal`: *a gazillion-mess* | **1e** = one × unbounded: comic huge |
| **`ROOTl+1e-`** | *gazillionth-ROOT* (comic tiny) | `zadazamrawobul`: *a gazillionth-of-a-chance* | **1e-** = one × unbounded tiny |
| **`ROOTl#1e`** | *umpteenth-ROOT* (comic late rank) | `zagayalrewobal`: *an umpteenth attempt* (as a kind) | **#1e** = unbounded place: “umpteenth” |
| **`ROOTl_1e`** | *gazillion-coded ROOT* (comic SKU sprawl) | `zavagelrowobal`: *a gazillion-SKU product* | **`_`** + **1e** = comic SKU sprawl |
| **`ROOTl_1e-`** | *gazillionth-of-a-code ROOT* (vanishing ID) | `zerehulrowobul`: *a vanishingly coded registration* | **`_`** + **1e-** = a vanishing ID |

Free **`hrewobal`** = *for the gazillionth time* (clause adverb). Derived **`zahamrewobal`** = *an umpteenth co-participation* (noun kind).

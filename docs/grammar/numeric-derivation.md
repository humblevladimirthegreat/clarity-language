# Numeric prefixes {#numeric-derivation-page}

To name a kind of thing by a number idea, put a [number](numbers.md) body in front of a content root, joined by **`x`**, the way English puts *quasi-*, *proto-*, *multi-*, or *tri-* in front of a noun. The number then describes the kind itself (its essence, its parts, its rank, its catalog code) instead of counting anything in the sentence. The root comes last, as in every **`x`** compound, so the word is a kind of the root. (cue: number, then **`x`**, then the root)

## Advanced {#advanced}

### Numeric prefixes (number + `x` + `ROOT`) {#numeric-derivation}

English often names a *kind of thing* with a number idea baked in (*essence of love*, *a three-part dialogue*, *a quasi-friend*, *specimen #1*). In Agebon you write the role letter, a number body, **`x`**, then the content root and its ending. The number body has no role letter and no ending of its own; the role letter and one [word ending](word-endings.md) belong to the **whole word**. You can then use that word in any role: the number describes the kind, not how many there are. (cue: the hyphen in *tri-dialogue*)

> `zrawaxolamal.`
>
> z-infinity-x-love
>
> "There is an essence of love."

```text
PoS + NUM + {x|mx} + ROOT + {-l|-m|-n|-r}
```

| Piece | Use | Cue |
|-------|-----|-----|
| **PoS** | Ordinary slot for the whole word | |
| **NUM** | Number body: the same marker vowel and digit or digitless exponent as a free [number](numbers.md), without the role letter and the ending (`grawal` → **`rawa`**). Tables below use [shorthand](number-markers.md#writing-preferred-shorthand) (`+e`, `_12`, `+1e`, …), but real words are always spelled out (`rawa`, `robado`, `rabawa`, …): [writing style](number-markers.md#writing-style-numeric-vs-spelled) | it starts with **`r`**, like every spoken number |
| **seam** | **`x`** the number is exact; **`mx`** approximately that number | the same **`m`** as `gragem` *about three* |
| **ROOT** | Any content root (`V(CV)+`), or an ordinary [**`x`** compound](compound-families.md#ordinary-compound-order), whose last root is the kind | |
| **Ending** | The root's sense, as on any **`x`** compound: **-l** its everyday sense, **-m** its abstract sense; or **-n** a name, **-r** a resume | `zrawaxolamam` is the essence of love in its abstract sense, *cherished* |

The **Agebon** columns below write the shape in shorthand (`+exROOT`), but a real word is always spelled out (`zrawaxolamal`): shorthand is for free numbers only.

An **`m`** before the **`x`** makes the number approximate:

> `zuzozen dragemxezuyel vawebol.`
>
> z-Uzozen | d-three.about-x-speech | v-see
>
> "Uzozen sees a roughly three-part dialogue."

**Compare with:** a free [number](numbers.md) word counts, ranks, or labels something in the sentence. A numeric prefix describes the kind.

A body with digits has one digit only: **`+N` / `-N`** (how many parts) or **`#N`** (which rank). The marker gives the spoken vowel (**`ra`** / **`ru`** / **`re`**). A numeric-prefix noun takes plural **-x** at the end as usual: `zragexezuyelx` ([plurality](plurality.md)). It is a content word, so it stresses its first syllable (*zra* in `zragexezuyel`), not the last digit as a free number does. In a hosted `bl-` slot the **`l`** is syllabic before **`r`**, as on a hosted number: `blragexezuyel`.

### Limit / polarity / stage (digitless) {#limit-polarity-stage}

The digitless number words (plus or minus infinity, first or last place, *more than one*) also work as prefixes. Before a root, they say **what the kind is** at a limit: its essence, its smallest grain, its absence, its first or final form, many of it, or its undoing.

| Agebon | Use | English | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|-----|
| **`+exROOT`** | essence / absolute / pure type of ROOT | `zrawaxolamal`: *essence of love* | **+e** = plus with no cap: nothing left to add |
| **`+e-xROOT`** | grain / seed / infinitesimal of ROOT | `zrawuxolamal`: *grain of love* | **+e-** = that same plus, at the tiny end: a speck |
| **`-exROOT`** | void of / absolute anti-ROOT | `zruwaxevemul`: *void of fear* | **-e** = minus with no floor: none of the kind |
| **`-e-xROOT`** | **quasi-ROOT**: the shape of ROOT without treating it as fully real (English **`quasi-`**); **`-e-1xROOT`** / **`-e-2xROOT`** = quasi-N | `zruwuxahuyayol`: *quasi-friend*; `zruwudoxehem`: *quasi-pair* | **-e-** = imaginary amount: the outline, not the ledger |
| **`#e-xROOT`** | origin / prototype / onset-form of ROOT | `zrewuxolamal`: *love in its beginning form* | **#e-** = start-place: the first rung |
| **`#exROOT`** | telos / culmination / final form of ROOT | `zrewaxonem`: *story’s final form* | **#e** = last place: where the kind finishes |
| **`+xROOT`** | poly- / multi-ROOT (unspecified >1) | `zraxehoham`: *multi-faith* | **+** = more than one, how many unnamed |
| **`-xROOT`** | de- / un- / deficit-ROOT | `vruxonem`: *to de-tale* | **-** = take that structure away |

### Quasi-ROOT (`-e-` / `-e-1` / `-e-2`)
<a id="quasi"></a>
<a id="quasi-root"></a>
<a id="quasi-n"></a>

English *quasi-* says something has the **shape or role** of a kind without counting as the real thing (*quasi-friend*, *quasi-official*). Spell that as **`-e-xROOT`**: the kind’s outline, not a full claim that it is that kind. Change only the role letter to use it as a noun, verb, adjective, and so on.

> `zahelan gruwuxahuyayol.`
>
> z-Ahelan | g-quasi-x-friend
>
> "Ahelan is a quasi-friend."

| PoS | `-e-x…` | Examples |
|-----|---------|----------|
| **`/z/` `/d/` `/b/`** (noun) | a quasi-entity | `zruwuxahuyayol`: *a quasi-friend*; `druwuxobemum`: *a quasi-melancholy* |
| **`/v/`** (verb) | to treat as quasi- / as-if that kind | `vruwuxahuyayol`: *to quasi-befriend* / treat as friend-shaped |
| **`/ɡ/`** (adjective) | *quasi-* (ascribed) | `gruwuxezazom`: *quasi-official* |
| **`/h/`** (adverb) | under quasi- framing | `hruwuxezazom`: *quasi-officially* |
| **`/w/`** (extra detail) | host graded as quasi- | `wruwuxobemum gobemum`: *quasi-melancholy* |
| **`/y/`** (interjection) | *Quasi-!* / *As if that kind!* | `yruwuxahuyayol` |

**Compare with:** with no root, **`gruwul`** is the [just short](digitless-exponents.md#just-short) amount, and **`vruwul`** / **`thruwul`** / **`yruwul`** / **`xruwul`** are that number as a verb, stance, interjection, or discourse word. Before a root, **`-e-xROOT`** makes *quasi-* that kind.

#### Quasi-N (`-e-1` / `-e-2`)

For *quasi-* with a **number of parts** (*as if one*, *as if a pair*), write **`-e-N`**, where N is the digit that **`+N`** uses for that many parts. Spoken, the body is **`ru`** + **`wu`** + the digit N: a bare order of magnitude, with no digit before the exponent. The word says the kind has that many parts only as if, not really.

**Compare with:** with no root, **`gruwubal`** / **`gruwudol`** are real negative amounts in the order-of-magnitude bands −1 / −2.

| Agebon | Use | English | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|-----|
| **`-e-1xROOT`** | quasi-unit / as-if singleton: unit-shaped without being a real one (twin **`+1xROOT`**) | `zruwubaxadowom`: *a quasi-single voice* / as-if primary voice | **-e-** outline + **1** = as-if one |
| **`-e-2xROOT`** | quasi-dual / imaginary duality: pair/mutual *as if*, not real reciprocity (twin **`+2xROOT`**) | `gruwudoxabewem`: *quasi-mutual commitment*; `zruwudoxehem`: *quasi-pair / as-if partnership* | **-e-** outline + **2** = as-if a pair |

| Agebon | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **`+2xROOT`** | real dual / mutual structure |
| **`-2xROOT`** | privative: non-reciprocal / one-sided |
| **`-e-2xROOT`** | quasi-dual: mutuality *as if* / imaginary duality |
| **`-e-xROOT`** | general quasi (N unnamed) |
| **`g-e-2`** | free real negative amount, OoM −2 band |

For a zero-shaped quasi, use **`-0xROOT`** (anti-null) or **`-0e-xROOT`** (micro-residue). The role letters work as in the quasi table above.

### Scalar digit morphs (`+N` / `-N`)
<a id="scalar-digit-morphs"></a>

English *a pair of…*, *three-part…*, *primary…* can say **how many parts the kind has**, not how many things the sentence is about. Write a one-digit plus body before the **`x`**: spoken, the marker then the digit (`ra`+`ba` for `+1`, spelled `zrabax…`). A minus body **`-N`** (including **`-0`**, spoken `ru`+digit) is the **privative**: the kind lacks or undoes the `+N` structure (*one-sided* instead of *mutual*). The tables write the bodies in shorthand (**`+N`** / **`-N`**).

> `zuzozen dradoxabewem vawebol.`
>
> z-Uzozen | d-two-x-commitment | v-see
>
> "Uzozen sees mutual commitment."

| Agebon | Use | English | `-N` | Privative English | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|------|-------------------|-----|
| **`+1xROOT`** | unit / primary / singleton | `zrabaxadowom`: *a single / primary voice*; SI measure nouns use published unit metaphors instead ([measure phrases](numbers-applied.md#measure-phrases)) | `zrubaxadowom` | *an indistinct / non-singular voice* | **1** = one piece |
| **`+2xROOT`** | dual / mutual / reciprocal / pair | `gradoxabewem`: *mutual commitment* | `grudoxabewem` | *one-sided commitment* | **2** = two sides |
| **`+3xROOT`** | triad / triplet / 3-part / ternary | `zragexezuyel`: *a three-part dialogue* | `zrugexagahem` | *non-tripartite institution* | **3** = three parts |
| **`+4xROOT`** | quad / 4-part / square / frame | `zrahuxuhuyem`: *a four-part / framed judgment* | `zruhuxemunam` | *an unframed message* | **4** = four sides of a frame |
| **`+5xROOT`** | hand / pentad / agency-set | `zrazaxuwal`: *a handy / five-fold toolkit* | `zruzaxagem` | *a plan without practical grasp* | **5** = fingers of a hand |
| **`+6xROOT`** | hex / 6-part / honeycomb / pack | `zravoxuvahal`: *a hexagonal / six-pack product* | `zruvoxahunam` | *an unpacked team* | **6** = honeycomb / six-pack |
| **`+7xROOT`** | heptad / week-cycle / complete series | `zrarexezudam`: *a seven-fold / week-cycle ritual* | `zrurexezudam` | *a broken-cycle ritual* | **7** = days that close a week |
| **`+8xROOT`** | octet / 8-part / doubled-quad | `zraluxegohum`: *an octet / eight-part blockage* | `zruluxegohum` | *a non-octet blockage* | **8** = two frames (4+4) |
| **`+9xROOT`** | ennead / near-full / almost-ten | `zranaxenurol`: *a nine-part / near-full choir* | `zrunaxenurol` | *a far-from-full choir* | **9** = one short of ten |
| **`+0xROOT`** | null / reset / emptied | `vramoxodoram`: *to zero-score / null the score* | `grumoxodoram` | *a score that won’t null* / *stubborn residual score* (**anti-null**) | **0** = empty count |

**Not the same job as:** ordinal digit morphs **`#N`** (*which order*). **`+N`** is *how many parts*.

#### Null / anti-null by PoS (`+0` / `-0`)
<a id="null-anti-null"></a>

**`+0`** treats ROOT as **resettable / emptied**; **`-0`** treats ROOT as **resisting emptying** (insistent residue). The role letter says *what kind of thing* is emptied or resists. **`-exROOT`** is *void of ROOT* (no such kind); **`+0xROOT`** is a resettable instance of that kind.

| PoS | `+0x…` | `-0x…` | Examples |
|-----|--------|--------|----------|
| **`/z/` `/d/` `/b/`** (noun) | emptied / wiped instance | entity that won’t zero / stubborn residue | `zramoxaram`: *a wiped debt*; `zrumoxaram`: *a debt that won’t zero*; `drumoxevum`: *that memory that won’t blank*; `brumoxodoram`: non-null residue as argument |
| **`/v/`** (verb) | act of nulling / resetting | act that refuses null / keeps residual | `vramoxodoram`: *to null the score*; `vrumoxodoram`: *to anti-null / refuse to zero the score*; `vramoxuzubam`: *to zero effort*; `vrumoxuzubam`: *to leave effort unzeroable* |
| **`/ɡ/`** (adjective) | null / reset (ascribed) | non-emptyable / residual (ascribed) | `gramoxodoram`: *null / reset*; `grumoxodoram`: *won’t-null / residual*; `grumoxoguyem`: *hope that won’t go to zero* (vs `gruwaxoguyem` *void of hope*) |
| **`/h/`** (adverb) | clause framed as reset / zeroed | clause framed as irreducible residue | `hramoxuzubam`: *with effort zeroed*; `hrumoxuzubam`: *with irreducible effort* / *despite any zeroing* |
| **`/w/`** (extra detail) | host `/ɡ/` graded toward null | host `/ɡ/` graded toward anti-null | `wramoxaram garam`: *debt (ascribed) zeroed*; `wrumoxaram garam`: *debt whose balance is anti-null* |
| **`/y/`** (interjection) | discourse *Zero it!* / *Null!* | discourse *Won’t zero!* / *Residue stands!* | `yramoxodoram`; `yrumoxodoram` |

With a final **-m**, the wipe is only approximate: nearly emptied.

> `zuzozen dramomxaram vawebol.`
>
> z-Uzozen | d-zero.about-x-debt | v-see
>
> "Uzozen sees a nearly wiped debt."

**Related form:** a stronger wipe of the kind is total null **`+0exROOT`**. *Annihilate* with no root is the free number verb **`vramowal`**.

### Zero × exponent (`+0e` / `±0e-1` / …)
<a id="zero-exponent-derivation"></a>
<a id="total-null"></a>
<a id="engineering-null-morph"></a>

The [zero × exponent](digitless-exponents.md#zero-exponent) bodies from free numbers also work before a root. There they say the kind is **wiped out, or leaves a residue**, instead of naming an amount. Free **`vramowal`** is *annihilate* with no kind attached; **`vramowaxodoram`** is *annihilate the score*.

| Agebon | Use | English | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|-----|
| **`+0exROOT`** | total null / absolute wipe of the kind | `vramowaxodoram`: *annihilate the score* (kind); `zramowaxaram`: *a totally wiped debt-kind* | **0** empty × **e** unbounded = wipe with no leftover scale |
| **`+0e-xROOT`** | sterile / null grain of ROOT | `zramowuxoguyem`: *sterile hope*; `zramowuxurunul`: *registration with no seed* | **0e-** = empty at the tiny end: no seed |
| **`+0e-1xROOT`** | engineering null at order of magnitude −1 of ROOT: kind wiped at scale 10⁻¹ | `zrawubayamoxabevam`: *deci-null problem*; `vrawubayamoxodoram`: *null the score at deci scale* | **0e-1** = empty at the tenths band |
| **`-0exROOT`** | absolute residue of ROOT | `zrumowaxaram`: *debt as absolute residue*; `vrumowaxuzubam`: *leave effort as absolute residue* | **-0e** = the minus of total wipe: residue that will not go |
| **`-0e-xROOT`** | micro-residue of ROOT | `zrumowuxevum`: *memory as micro-residue* | **-0e-** = residue at the tiny end |
| **`-0e-1xROOT`** | engineering residue at order of magnitude −1 of ROOT | `zruwubayamoxaram`: *debt as deci-scale residue* | **-0e-1** = residue at the tenths band |

The ending on the whole word marks soft (**-m**), named (**-n**), or resume (**-r**) as usual.

**Emptiness chain (kinds):** **`-exROOT`** (void of kind), **`+0xROOT`** (resettable null), **`+0exROOT`** (total null), **`+0e-xROOT`** (sterile grain), **`+0e-1xROOT`** (engineering null at 10⁻¹). On free numbers, bare **`grawamol`** / **`Ne0`** instead [assert order of magnitude 0](digitless-exponents.md#bare-oom-bands).

On a free number, **`±0e-1`** is an amount. Before a root, the same bodies wipe the kind, or leave a residue of it, at that scale.

**`-0e` / `-0e-` / `±0e-1`** take the role letters the same way (absolute residue / micro-residue / engineering null·residue at order of magnitude −1). With no root: **`vramowal`** *annihilate*; **`gramowal`** absolute-zero amount; **`g+0e-1`** / **`g-0e-1`** engineering null / residue: [numbers.md § Zero × exponent](digitless-exponents.md#zero-exponent).

<a id="rank-annihilated-morph"></a>

| Agebon | Use | English | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|-----|

### Ordinal digit morphs (`#N`)
<a id="ordinal-digit-morphs"></a>

English *first draft*, *secondary adventure*, *third-order problem* can name **which rank the kind holds**, not which item is Nth in a list. Write a one-digit rank body before the **`x`**: shorthand **`#NxROOT`**, spoken **`re`** + digit, spelled `drebax…`. A free **`g#N`** after a noun picks the Nth item in a series instead.

**`#N`** names a stage between the digitless ends **`#e-`** (first form) and **`#e`** (final form); **`#0e`** is [rank annihilated](#rank-annihilated-morph). For *lacks N parts*, use the scalar minus body **`-N`**. For a code with several digits, use free `g#12` or the label marker **`_`**.

> `zolazun drebaxebewem varayel.`
>
> z-Olazun | d-1st-x-draft | v-write
>
> "Olazun writes a first draft."

| Agebon | Use | English | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|-----|
| **`#1xROOT`** | first-class / premiere / first-order | `drebaxebewem`: *a first-draft* (kind); `debewem g#1` *the first draft (in this stack)* | **#1** = first place, not “one piece” |
| **`#2xROOT`** | secondary / second-order / backup | `gredoxamunem`: *a secondary adventure* | **#2** = the next slot after first |
| **`#3xROOT`** | tertiary / third-order | `zregexabevam`: *a third-order problem* | **#3** = third rung |
| **`#4xROOT`** … **`#9`** | Nth-order / Nth-place form | `zrezaxaharom`: *fifth-order harmony* (as a kind) | **#N** = Nth place on the kind |
| **`#0xROOT`** | zeroth-order / pre-rank / unranked form | `zremoxuborem`: *a zeroth-order calculation* | **#0** = the rung before ranking starts |

**Not the same job as:** **`+1`** / **`+2`** (singleton / dual). **`#1`** is premiere-place; **`#2`** is secondary. **`#0`** is a zeroth rung; **`#0e`** is rank annihilated / disqualified.

| **`/v/`** (verb) | treat as / assign that order (kind-act) | `vrebaxamahum`: *to first-class / premiere a challenge* (free `v#1` *take 1st place*) |
| **`/ɡ/`** (adjective) | *Nth-order* (ascribed) | `gredoxamunem`: *secondary* |
| **`/h/`** (adverb) | *at Nth-order framing* | `hremoxuborem`: *zeroth-order* |
| **`/w/`** (extra detail) | host `/ɡ/` graded as Nth-order | `wregexezuyel gezuyel`: *third-order (dialogue)* |
| **`/y/`** (interjection) | discourse call of that order-kind | `yrebaxebewem`: *First-draft!* (kind cheer; free `y#1` *First!* place cheer) |

**Related form:** **`_N`** is *which code*; free `g#N` is *which item in the current series*.

### Label / code sense (`_`) {#label-code-sense}

When English makes a code part of what a thing is (*specimen #1*, *room 12* as a kind of room), the digits are a **catalog ID**, not a count or a rank. Put the digit-string marker **`_`** before the **`x`**; its digits are the code.

> `zuzozen drobaxudewal vawebol.`
>
> z-Uzozen | d-_1-x-petri-dish | v-see
>
> "Uzozen sees specimen #1."

| Agebon | Use | English | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|-----|
| **`_xROOT`** | ROOT as something that **bears a code** (taggable unit; code unnamed) | `zroxudewal`: *a coded specimen* / *a specimen-as-ID* | **`_`** = catalog stamp, digits not yet filled |
| **`_NxROOT`** | **specimen / item #N**: the code is part of the kind | `zrobaxudewal`: *specimen #1*; `zrobadoxahehal`: *room-12* as a coded kind; `brobadoxahehal`: *room-12* as `/b/` | the digits **are** the name, not a count |

When the number is only an argument of an ordinary noun, prefer ordinary syntax: `zahehal g_12` = *the room numbered 12*. Use **`_NxROOT`** when the code is part of the **concept** (specimen ID, SKU, protocol code). To cite a numbered point in the conversation, use free **`x_…`**; to dial or enter a code, use free **`v_…`**.

Digitless **`+`/`-`/`#`** on ROOT = *what the kind is*; digitless **`_`** = *how the catalog names it*.

### Hyperbole sense (`+1e` / `#1e`) {#hyperbole-sense}

To exaggerate a kind for comic effect (*a gazillion-mess*, *an umpteenth attempt*), put a mantissa before a digitless exponent, as in free [hyperbole](digitless-exponents.md#hyperbole). Use **`1`** as the mantissa unless the joke needs another digit.

| Agebon | Use | English | Cue |
|--------|-----|---------|-----|
| **`+1exROOT`** | *gazillion-ROOT* (comic huge) | `zrabawaxezulam`: *a gazillion-mess* | **1e** = one × unbounded: comic huge |
| **`+1e-xROOT`** | *gazillionth-ROOT* (comic tiny) | `zrabawuxodevom`: *a gazillionth-of-a-chance* | **1e-** = one × unbounded tiny |
| **`#1exROOT`** | *umpteenth-ROOT* (comic late rank) | `zrebawaxuguvel`: *an umpteenth attempt* (as a kind) | **#1e** = unbounded place: “umpteenth” |

Free **`hrebawal`** = *for the gazillionth time* (clause adverb). The prefix **`zrebawaxayam`** = *an umpteenth co-participation* (noun kind).

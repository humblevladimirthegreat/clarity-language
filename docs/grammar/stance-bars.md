# Stance bars {#stance-bars-sitting}

### Every bar {#stance-bars}
<a id="worse-than"></a>
<a id="best-effort"></a>

Intermediate ranked against a plan, and [sake bars](prescription.md#sake-bars) rank against what a sake needs. Any stance that **sets a value** can be the [bar](comparative-arity.md#bars), and each keeps its own endings and meaning. A channel word (how you know something: you saw it, remember it, were told it, worked it out) sets **the level that source led you to expect**. So *than usual*, *than I remember*, and *than advertised* each put one channel word in the bar slot.

> `zuzozen thubem zel guzemol.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | th-PATTERN | z-rank/more | g-sleepy]
>
> "Uzozen is sleepier than usual."

> `zuzozen zel guzemol thubem.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | z-rank/more | g-sleepy] | th-PATTERN
>
> "Uzozen is the sleepiest, going by the pattern."

<!-- cheat-sheet: roles-comparing -->
| Bar | Ranks against | English |
|-----|---------------|---------|
| met sake `thudatham`, `thobatham`, … ([sake bars](prescription.md#sake-bars)) | what that sake needs | *enough* / *too much* / *not enough* |
| LIVE `thugom` | how it looks | *than it looks* |
| MEMORY `thevum` | how you remember it | *than I remember* / *than last time* |
| PATTERN `thubem` | the trail of cases | *than usual* / *normal* |
| CLUES `theyem` | what was worked out | *than calculated* / *than it should be* |
| REPORTED `thebam` | what was said or written | *than advertised* / *than they said* / *than the stories say* |
| INTUITION `thabom` | your gut expectation | *than I expected* |
| FORMER `helam` | how it used to be | *than it used to be* / *increasingly* |
| NOTIONAL `theyum` | the imagined case | *than imagined* / *than in theory* |
| PLAN `thagem` | the plan or the schedule | *than planned* / *than scheduled* |
| WANT `thalel` / `thalem` / `thaler` | the level wanted | *than wanted* / *than I wanted* |
| ABIL `hodaxal` | what can be done | *as … as possible* |
| a requirement `hwavel` / `hwavem` / `hwaver` | the rule, the demand, or custom | *up to code* / *than demanded* / *than customary* |
| a permission `hwaval` / `hwavam` / `hwavar` | the rule, the grant, or what is tolerated | *over the limit* / *more than they allow* / *than tolerated* |
| consent `hwadam` | the agreed terms | *more than agreed* |
| speaker attitude (`thoguyem`, `thewemum`) | the case you hoped for or feared | *than hoped* / *than feared* |

The bar's ending keeps its own job: on a channel it is [evidence strength](evidentiality.md#evidence-strength) (`thubel` *than it reliably is*, `thuber` *than the few cases so far*).

> `zuzozen thevum zel gelazom.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | th-MEMORY | z-rank/more | g-big]
>
> "Uzozen is bigger than I remember."

> `zanal thebam zuel gagebom.`
>
> [z-book | th-REPORTED | z-rank/less | g-clarity]
>
> "The book is less clear than advertised."

> `zuzozen thabom zel guzemol.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | th-INTUITION | z-rank/more | g-sleepy]
>
> "Uzozen is sleepier than I expected."

> `zuzozen helam zel guzemol.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | h-FORMER | z-rank/more | g-sleepy]
>
> "Uzozen is sleepier than they used to be."

> `zanal thalem zuel garolom.`
>
> [z-book | th-WANT-unstated | z-rank/less | g-heavy]
>
> "The book is lighter than I wanted."

The bar's `/b/` works as it does on the clause. On REPORTED it is the source, and on a [say word](feelings-permission.md#say-words) over the act (`hwavem`, `hwavam`) it is the person who demands or grants. WANT takes no `/b/`: for someone else's wants, put a [holder](evidentiality.md#holder) on the clause. On PATTERN it is **whose cases** set the pattern: the population you compare against. With no `/b/`, PATTERN is the ranked item's own usual level, or the usual case in the situation.

> `zanal thebam blolazun zuel gagebom.`
>
> [z-book | [th-REPORTED | bl-Olazun] | z-rank/less | g-clarity]
>
> "The book is less clear than Olazun said."

> `zuzozen thubem blwezeluyelx zel gezuyol.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | [th-PATTERN | bl-role-agent-learn-x] | z-rank/more | g-sing]
>
> "Uzozen sings well for a learner."

On a rank join, an event root on `/ɡ/` is the scale itself: how well the act goes (*sings well*), not an act [in progress](predication.md#in-progress).

An adjective after that `/b/` describes it, as after any [host](left-bound.md#complex-chaining), so it narrows the population.

> `zuzozen thubem blwezeluyelx guzemol zel gezuyol.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | [th-PATTERN | [bl-role-agent-learn-x | g-sleepy]] | z-rank/more | g-sing]
>
> "Uzozen sings well for a sleepy learner."

The ranked item can be a whole kind. Write the [universal fence](rank-joins.md#universals-domains-generics) first, then the bar: the whole closed fence counts as the one ranked item, the way an inner list sits inside an outer one ([fence nesting](rank-joins.md#fence-nesting)). Open **`zuam`** is *people of that kind, as a rule*.

> `zuam gwazodamul thubem zel hral vabezum.`
>
> [[z-everything.open | g-episode-agent-tired] | th-PATTERN | z-rank/more | h-how-often] | v-flaw
>
> "Tired people make mistakes more often than usual."

To rank one kind against another, keep one kind as the item and put the other in the PATTERN bar's `/b/`.

> `zuam gwazenuvom thubem bleyolx godamum zel hemebem vuzubal.`
>
> [[z-everything.open | g-episode-agent-rest] | [th-PATTERN | [bl-person-x | g-burnout]] | z-rank/more | h-achievement] | v-sweat
>
> "Rested people work more successfully than exhausted people."

A CLUES or PATTERN bar can also take [`blarl`](evidentiality.md#evidence-clause): the next sentence is the grounds you worked the bar out from.

**Ability.** The ability word [`oda`](ability.md#ability-fallback) as the bar is the limit of **what can be done**. A tie against **`hodaxal`** is *as … as possible*. The ability vowel says how fixed that limit is, so rank **`zel`** against **`hodaxel`** is *more than can be done for now*, and against **`hodaxul`** *more than could ever be done*.

> `zolazun hodaxal zoel hedurem vowurel.`
>
> [z-Olazun | h-ABIL-able | z-equal-rank | h-haste] | v-walk
>
> "Olazun walks as hastily as possible."

**Rules and permission.** A requirement as the bar is the demand; a tie meets it. A permission is the limit; rank **`zel`** goes over it.

> `zahehal hwavel zoel gobobom.`
>
> [z-house | h-episode-event-REQUIRE-rule | z-equal-rank | g-strength]
>
> "The house is as strong as the rules require."

> `zududal hwaval zel gral.`
>
> [z-tea | h-episode-event-ALLOW-rule | z-rank/more | g-amount]
>
> "There is more tea than the rules allow."

**Hopes and fears.** A speaker-attitude word as the bar is the case you hoped for or feared.

> `zanal thoguyem zel gagebom.`
>
> [z-book | th-hope | z-rank/more | g-clarity]
>
> "The book is clearer than I hoped."

> `zanal thewemum zuel gagebom.`
>
> [z-book | th-concern | z-rank/less | g-clarity]
>
> "The book is even less clear than I feared."

Only these stances set a value. A [cause or condition pole](causation.md), [MAY](knowing.md#may), [MIRATIVE](notional-standing.md#mirative), [DECISION](ability.md#decision), and [ATTEMPT](ability.md#try) do not, so none of them is a bar. A want sets the level wanted; a decision or an attempt commits to an act and sets no level, so *more than decided* is the [plan](intention.md#plan-predict) bar.

### Vague amounts (*many* / *few*) {#vague-amounts}

English *many*, *few*, *often*, and *late* rank against an unstated baseline, and *too much*, *too late*, and *as fast as possible* against an unstated need or limit. Agebon always names the bar: put it in the fence and rank on [amount](comparative-arity.md#amount-scale), the [frequency scale](comparative-arity.md#frequency-scale), or the [time scale](comparative-arity.md#time-scale).

> `zagozolx thubem zel gral.`
>
> [z-cat-x | th-PATTERN | z-rank/more | g-amount]
>
> "There are many cats." (more than usual)

> `zuzozen thubem zel hral vowurel.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | th-PATTERN | z-rank/more | h-how-often] | v-walk
>
> "Uzozen often walks." (more often than usual)

<!-- cheat-sheet: roles-comparing -->
| English | Bar and rank |
|---------|--------------|
| *many* / *few* | PATTERN **`thubem`**, `zel` / `zuel` with `gral` |
| *often* / *rarely* | PATTERN **`thubem`**, `zel` / `zuel` with `hral` |
| *late* / *early* | PATTERN **`thubem`**, `zel` / `zuel` with `bral` |
| *than usual* (the same person or thing) | PATTERN **`thubem`** with no `/b/` |
| *too much* / *enough* / *not enough* | a met sake bar (`thudatham`), `zel` / `zoel` / `zuel` |
| *too late* / *too soon* | a met sake bar (`thudatham`), `zel` / `zuel` with `bral` |
| *as … as possible* | ABIL **`hodaxal`**, `zoel` |
| *more and more* / *increasingly* | FORMER **`helam`**, `zel` |

The bar is never dropped. A single-item `zagozolx zel gral` already means *the most cats* (a [superlative](comparatives.md#superlatives)).

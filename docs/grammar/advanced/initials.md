# Initials {#initials-page}

### Initials {#initials}

English shortens a long title to its initials (*FBI*). Ageben does too: take each root of a long [hyphenated name](/proper-names#multipart-names), in order, and write its **first consonant** as a [letter code](/numbers-applied#letter-codes) with **`@`** (**-n**, an official designation). A root always starts with a vowel, so its first letter would leave only four choices; its first consonant is the letter you hear.

> `z@_nl vezuyol.`
>
> 🔊 `zrenulan vezuyol.`
>
> z-_nl.named | v-tell
>
> "The NL announces." (the office whose full title is *Book-Courage*)

| Ageben | Use | English |
|--------|-----|---------|
| `zanaxalazun` | long title (`ana` × `alazu`) | *Book-Courage* |
| `z@_nl` (🔊 `zrenulan`) | initials: `n` from `ana`, `l` from `alazu` | *the NL*, the same office |
| `z=_nl` (🔊 `zrenular`) | resume | *that office again* |

- **Any title works.** Each initial is its own syllable, so two roots with the same first consonant give a doubled letter (`zanaxanomun` → `z@_nn`, 🔊 `zrenunun`), and a title of any length gives one letter per root.
- **Initials can be shared,** as in English. Context sorts out which office is meant. When you need a name nobody else has, give the office a [proper name](/proper-names#proper-name--n) of its own instead: one published root + **-n**.
- **Say it mid-sentence.** The whole rule is *say each root's first consonant by its letter name*, so you can make the initials while you talk.

#### Pick one {#advanced-pick-one}

**1.** *The DH sees Azozon.* (the Train-Bridge office) `z@_dh dazozon vayebel.` or `z@_eo dazozon vayebel.`

::: details Show answer
`z@_dh dazozon vayebel.`

🔊 `zredehan dazozon vayebel.`

z-_dh.named | d-Azozon | v-see

Initials take each root's first **consonant** (*d* in *train*, *h* in *bridge*). Every root starts with a vowel, so first letters (`eo`) would tell almost nothing.
:::

#### Fix it {#advanced-fix-it}

**1.** *The VZ writes.* (the Family-School office) <!-- lint: error -->`z_vz varuyol.`

::: details Show answer
`z@_vz varuyol.`

🔊 `zrevezun varuyol.`

Initials are the office's official designation, so they take **-n** (`@`); without it, `_vz` is only a code.

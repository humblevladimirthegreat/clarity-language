# Universality {#universality-and-dated-channel}

### Mood on one adjective
<a id="mood-on-adjective"></a>

The `/th/` words MAY and the channels, [NOTIONAL](/notional-standing#notional), and the `/h/` words [RESIDUE and FORMER](/notional-standing#residue) cover the whole sentence. When one applies only to a single `/ɡ/` adjective, put the same word on `/w/` right before that adjective instead.

Only words about **how you know** or **how things stand** move to `/w/` this way: the ones above, [CAUSE](/iff-fault#cause), the [*already* / *still* words](/already-still-yet#phasal), and [*can* with `oda`](/can-and-cant#ability-fallback). A plan, decision, try, or want stays on its own letter (`/th/`, or `/h/` for a try): on `/w/` the same spelling is the ordinary word of its root (`wagem` *as planned*, `wogom` *decidedly*, `wowam` *tentatively*, `walem` *wishfully*).

> `zuzozen danobal wevum gerulel vawebol.`
>
> z-Uzozen | [d-hat | [w-MEMORY | g-red]] | v-see
>
> "Uzozen sees a hat that I remember as red."

> `zuzozen danobal wugom gerulel vawebol.`
>
> z-Uzozen | [d-hat | [w-LIVE | g-red]] | v-see
>
> "Uzozen sees a hat that I can see is red."

> `zuzozen waram godamul vowurel.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | [w-RESIDUE | g-tired]] | v-walk
>
> "Uzozen walks, still tired." (the tiredness from earlier has not worn off)

### Dated channel (signed offset) {#dated-channel}
<a id="ago"></a>
<a id="time-offset"></a>

English *three hours ago* and *in three hours* count from now. Agebon counts from the channel instead: put a [measure phrase](/numbers-applied#measure-phrases) in `/b/` immediately after the evidential. Any other noun there is the [source](/evidentiality#source); a time measure is the offset. The amount is **signed**: **`-`** is earlier than now, **`+`** is later than now. The offset always dates the **event**, not the moment you learned about it. There is no bare *ago* word, so a dated claim always says how you know it.

> `zuzozen thevum blagorum grugel vowurel.`
>
> z-Uzozen | [th-MEMORY | [bl-hour | g-minus-three]] | v-walk
>
> "I saw Uzozen walk three hours ago."

> `zolazun thebam blagorum gragel velurul.`
>
> z-Olazun | [th-REPORTED | [bl-hour | g-three]] | v-departure
>
> "I hear Olazun leaves in three hours."

Some channels only point one way:

| Channel | Sign | Why |
|---------|------|-----|
| **`thevum`** MEMORY | **`-`** only | you already observed it |
| **`thugom`** LIVE | no offset | the scene is in view now |
| **`thubem`** PATTERN | **`-`** or **`+`** | **`-`**: it happened then, if the pattern held; **`+`**: it will, if the pattern holds |
| **`theyem`** CLUES, **`thebam`** REPORTED, **`thabom`** INTUITION | **`-`** or **`+`** | the channel does not fix the time |
| [PLAN](/intention#plan-predict) **`thagem`** | **`+`** only | the intended act is later |

The wrong sign on a one-way word (**`thevum`** with **`+`**, **`thagem`** with **`-`**) is not a sentence.

A [carried](/evidentiality#carry-forward) channel keeps its offset. Date the first sentence of a story, and the bare sentences after it happen then too, so a past story needs no tense and no offset on every line. A new offset starts a new frame.

> `zuzozen thevum bluzurem grubal vowurel. zuzozen vuhohol.`
>
> z-Uzozen | [th-MEMORY | [bl-day | g-minus-one]] | v-walk . z-Uzozen | v-sit
>
> "I saw Uzozen walk yesterday. Uzozen sat down." (the sitting was yesterday too, as I remember)

A signed measure in the `/b/` of a [time pole](/dependents#time-poles) (**`harum`** *until*, **`hevahum`** *by*, **`habam`** *before*, **`halam`** *after*, **`herem`** *during*) counts from now, the same way. The pole itself needs no channel, but its clause must already be a command, a request, or a plan, or carry a channel of its own (a carried frame does not count). So a command or a plan can name a day without saying how anyone knows, while a plain statement cannot use a pole to slip in a past or future with no warrant. An absolute date in the pole (`harum bl_#22,7`) needs none of this.

> `yel vuhamum zawan harum bluzurem grabal.`
>
> y-command | v-confidentiality | z-listener | [h-until | [bl-day | g-one]]
>
> "Keep the secret until tomorrow."

For *just* and *about to*, use the [just-short](special-values.md#just-short) amount with no unit: **`blruwul`** is *a hair before now*, **`blrawul`** is *a hair after now*.

> `zuzozen thevum blruwul vowurel.`
>
> z-Uzozen | [th-MEMORY | bl---e-] | v-walk
>
> "I just saw Uzozen walk."

> `theyem blrawul verulal.`
>
> [th-CLUES | bl-+-e-] | v-rain
>
> "It looks like it's about to rain."

For *soon*, soften that hair with open **-m**: **`blrawum`** is *a short while after now*. It is later than *about to* (`blrawul`) but still close, unlike open-ended *later* (`blral`).

> `theyem blrawum verulal.`
>
> [th-CLUES | bl-+-e-.about] | v-rain
>
> "It looks like it will rain soon."

The mirror, **`blruwum`**, is *a short while before now*: *recently*.

> `zuzozen thevum blruwum vowurel.`
>
> z-Uzozen | [th-MEMORY | bl---e-.about] | v-walk
>
> "I saw Uzozen walk recently."

> `zolazun thubem blagorum grugel vezuvol.`
>
> z-Olazun | [th-PATTERN | [bl-hour | g-minus-three]] | v-sleep
>
> "Going by the pattern, Olazun slept three hours ago."

A channel used as a comparison [bar](/comparative-arity#bars) takes the same offset, which dates the bar: MEMORY with a day before is *than yesterday*.

> `zuzozen thevum bluzurem grubal zel guzemol.`
>
> [z-Uzozen | [th-MEMORY | [bl-day | g-minus-one]] | z-rank/more | g-sleepy]
>
> "Uzozen is sleepier than yesterday."

With an [as-of](as-of.md#as-of) pair, the offset counts from the as-of moment instead of from the moment you speak.

**Compare with:** an absolute clock or date (`h_#22,7`) dates the event without a channel. [RESIDUE](/notional-standing#residue) and [FORMER](/notional-standing#former-climate) do not locate time, so they take no offset.

#### Now and today {#now}

Agebon has no short *now* word. Say *now* with an offset of **zero**: the event sits no time away from the moment you speak. The channel stays, so *now* still says how you know it.

> `zolazun theyem blagorum gramol vezuvol.`
>
> z-Olazun | [th-CLUES | [bl-hour | g-zero]] | v-sleep
>
> "Olazun must be asleep right now."

The unit sets how wide *now* is. Zero hours is *right now*; zero [days](/numbers-applied#stock-units) is *today*.

> `zuzozen thebam bluzurem gramol vezuyol.`
>
> z-Uzozen | [th-REPORTED | [bl-day | g-zero]] | v-sing
>
> "I hear Uzozen sings today."

Use zero on a channel that takes either sign. **LIVE** `thugom` already means *now in view*, so it needs no offset. **MEMORY** takes **`-`** only and PLAN takes **`+`** only, so zero is not a sentence on them.

### Channels on a generalization {#universality}
<a id="universality-mood"></a>
<a id="generalization"></a>

English *always*, *usually*, *every*, and *never* make a general claim. The [universal fences](/universals-domains#universals-domains-generics) (`zual` / `zuam`) and the [restrictors](/restrictors) (`hual` / `huam` / `hal`) already say **who** or **when** the claim covers, and their ending says how many exceptions it allows: closed **-l** allows none, and open **-m** leaves them open, as on any list (*usually*, *as a rule*). No other word is needed for the claim itself.

> `zuzozen huam vowurel.`
>
> z-Uzozen | h-always.open | v-walk
>
> "Uzozen usually walks."

To say **how you know** the general claim, add a [channel](/evidentiality#evidentiality). On a clause with a universal fence or restrictor, the channel is your warrant for the whole generalization, not for one case, and its [ending](/evidentiality#evidence-strength) says how much backs it.

> `zuzozen hual vowurel thuber.`
>
> z-Uzozen | h-always | v-walk | th-PATTERN.weak
>
> "Uzozen always walks, from the few cases I have seen."

> `zual gwezuduyel vuduyel thanal.`
>
> [z-everything | g-role-agent-teach] | v-teach | th-DEDUCED.strong
>
> "It follows that every teacher teaches." (every step could be shown)

> `zolazun hal vanuvul thebal blazunal.`
>
> z-Olazun | h-never | v-punch | [th-REPORTED.strong | bl-soccer]
>
> "Olazun never punches, per the soccer rules."

The fence says how far the claim reaches, and the channel says what it rests on. Keep the two apart: *always, as far as I have checked* is closed **`hual`** with a weak channel, because you claim no exceptions on thin evidence. Open **`huam`** does not claim that: it leaves the exceptions open.

| English | Fence or restrictor | Channel | Example |
|---------|---------------------|---------|---------|
| *usually*, *as a rule* | `huam` / `zuam` | any, or none | `zuzozen huam vowurel.` |
| *always, as far as I have checked* | `hual` / `zual` | a weak channel | `zuzozen hual vowurel thuber.` |
| *it follows that every …* | `zual` | DEDUCED.strong `thanal` | `zual gwezuduyel vuduyel thanal.` |
| *by definition* | `zual` + `thehem blarl` | none | `zual geyol gwezuduyel thehem blarl zeyor vuduyel.` |
| *under the rules of …* | `hal` / `hual` | REPORTED.strong with the rules in `/b/` | `zolazun hal vanuvul thebal blazunal.` |
| *cats are sleepy* (in general) | `zuam` + kind | any, or none | `zuam gagozol guzemol thubem.` |

A [cause or condition](/causation) on a general claim says how things work. It holds for **each** member or occasion, the same way the verb does. Inside the sentence after **`blarl`**, resume the kind with **-r** (`zagozor`) to mean *that same one*: each cat, in turn.

> `zual gagozol vezuvol thewem thohom blarl zagozor guzemol.`
>
> [z-everything | g-cat] | v-sleep | th-CAUSE | [th-if | bl-that-clause] | [z-←cat | g-sleepy]
>
> "Every cat sleeps when it is sleepy; that is how it works."

A **definition** works both ways: whatever has the property is that kind, and every member of the kind has it. Write it on a closed fence with **`thehem`** (*if and only if*, from [causation](/iff-fault#only-because)). The grounds sentence is the defining property. A definition needs no channel, because it is a decision about a word, not a report about the world.

> `zual geyol gwezuduyel thehem blarl zeyor vuduyel.`
>
> [z-everything | g-person] | g-role-agent-teach | [th-iff | bl-that-clause] | z-←person | v-teach
>
> "By definition, a person is a teacher if and only if they teach."

The channel stays on the main sentence and covers the whole generalization. Put it before the pole, as with the [evidence clause](/evidentiality#evidence-clause): `zual gagozol vezuvol thubel thohom blarl …` is *going by a well-established pattern, every cat sleeps when …*.

**Compare with:** a general claim you are **not** giving as today's report is [FORMER](/notional-standing#former-climate) (`helam`). *Every cat must sleep* as a rule someone sets is a [requirement](/feelings-permission#say-words) (`hwavem`), not a law of how things work. *Maybe every cat sleeps* is [MAY](/knowing#may) on the same clause.

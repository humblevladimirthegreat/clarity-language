# Dated channel {#dated-channel-sitting}

### Dated channel (signed offset) {#dated-channel}
<a id="ago"></a>
<a id="time-offset"></a>

English *three hours ago* and *in three hours* count from now. Ageben counts from the channel instead: put a [measure phrase](/numbers-applied#measure-phrases) in `/b/` immediately after the evidential. Any other noun there is the [source](/evidentiality#source); a time measure is the offset. The amount is **signed**: **`-`** is earlier than now, **`+`** is later than now. The offset always dates the **event**, not the moment you learned about it. There is no bare *ago* word, so a dated claim always says how you know it.

> `zazozon thevum blagorum grugel vowurol.`
>
> z-Azozon | [th-MEMORY | [bl-hour | g-minus-three]] | v-walk
>
> "I saw Azozon walk three hours ago."

> `zalazun thebam blagorum gragel vudurul.`
>
> z-Alazun | [th-REPORTED | [bl-hour | g-three]] | v-departure
>
> "I hear Alazun leaves in three hours."

Some channels only point one way:

| Channel | Sign | Why |
|---------|------|-----|
| **`thevum`** MEMORY | **`-`** only | you already observed it |
| **`thugem`** LIVE | no offset | the scene is in view now |
| **`thubom`** PATTERN | **`-`** or **`+`** | **`-`**: it happened then, if the pattern held; **`+`**: it will, if the pattern holds |
| **`theyom`** CLUES, **`thebam`** REPORTED, **`thabem`** INTUITION | **`-`** or **`+`** | the channel does not fix the time |
| [PLAN](/intention#plan-predict) **`thagom`** | **`+`** only | the intended act is later |

The wrong sign on a one-way word (**`thevum`** with **`+`**, **`thagom`** with **`-`**) is not a sentence.

A [carried](/evidentiality#carry-forward) channel keeps its offset. Date the first sentence of a story, and the bare sentences after it happen then too, so a past story needs no tense and no offset on every line. A new offset starts a new frame.

> `zazozon thevum blazurom grubal vowurol. zazozon vehohel.`
>
> z-Azozon | [th-MEMORY | [bl-day | g-minus-one]] | v-walk . z-Azozon | v-sit
>
> "I saw Azozon walk yesterday. Azozon sat down." (the sitting was yesterday too, as I remember)

A signed measure in the `/b/` of a [time pole](/dependents#time-poles) (**`harum`** *until*, **`huvahum`** *by*, **`habam`** *before*, **`halam`** *after*, **`herom`** *during*) counts from now, the same way. The pole itself needs no channel, but its clause must already be a command, a request, or a plan, or carry a channel of its own (a carried frame does not count). So a command or a plan can name a day without saying how anyone knows, while a plain statement cannot use a pole to slip in a past or future with no warrant. An absolute date in the pole (`harum bl_#22,7`) needs none of this.

> `yol vuhamum zawan harum blazurom grabal.`
>
> y-command | v-confidentiality | z-listener | [h-until | [bl-day | g-one]]
>
> "Keep the secret until tomorrow."

For *just* and *about to*, use the [just-short](special-values.md#just-short) amount with no unit: **`blruwul`** is *a hair before now*, **`blrawul`** is *a hair after now*.

> `zazozon thevum blruwul vowurol.`
>
> z-Azozon | [th-MEMORY | bl---e-] | v-walk
>
> "I just saw Azozon walk."

> `theyom blrawul verulal.`
>
> [th-CLUES | bl-+-e-] | v-rain
>
> "It looks like it's about to rain."

For *soon*, soften that hair with open **-m**: **`blrawum`** is *a short while after now*. It is later than *about to* (`blrawul`) but still close, unlike open-ended *later* (`blral`).

> `theyom blrawum verulal.`
>
> [th-CLUES | bl-+-e-.about] | v-rain
>
> "It looks like it will rain soon."

The mirror, **`blruwum`**, is *a short while before now*: *recently*.

> `zazozon thevum blruwum vowurol.`
>
> z-Azozon | [th-MEMORY | bl---e-.about] | v-walk
>
> "I saw Azozon walk recently."

> `zalazun thubom blagorum grugel vezuvel.`
>
> z-Alazun | [th-PATTERN | [bl-hour | g-minus-three]] | v-sleep
>
> "Going by the pattern, Alazun slept three hours ago."

A channel used as a comparison [bar](/comparative-arity#bars) takes the same offset, which dates the bar: MEMORY with a day before is *than yesterday*.

> `zazozon thevum blazurom grubal zol guzemel.`
>
> [z-Azozon | [th-MEMORY | [bl-day | g-minus-one]] | z-rank/more | g-sleepy]
>
> "Azozon is sleepier than yesterday."

With an [as-of](as-of.md#as-of) pair, the offset counts from the as-of moment instead of from the moment you speak.

**Compare with:** an absolute clock or date (`h_#22,7`) dates the event without a channel. [RESIDUE](/notional-standing#residue) and [FORMER](/notional-standing#former-climate) do not locate time, so they take no offset.

#### Now and today {#now}

Ageben has no short *now* word. Say *now* with an offset of **zero**: the event sits no time away from the moment you speak. The channel stays, so *now* still says how you know it.

> `zalazun theyom blagorum gramol vezuvel.`
>
> z-Alazun | [th-CLUES | [bl-hour | g-zero]] | v-sleep
>
> "Alazun must be asleep right now."

The unit sets how wide *now* is. Zero hours is *right now*; zero [days](/numbers-applied#stock-units) is *today*.

> `zazozon thebam blazurom gramol vozuyel.`
>
> z-Azozon | [th-REPORTED | [bl-day | g-zero]] | v-sing
>
> "I hear Azozon sings today."

Use zero on a channel that takes either sign. **LIVE** `thugem` already means *now in view*, so it needs no offset. **MEMORY** takes **`-`** only and PLAN takes **`+`** only, so zero is not a sentence on them.

**Rule:** [Lexicalized stand-ins in other roles](stand-in-roles.md#stand-in-roles)

**2.** *Alazun and Ahelan cook something other than a tomato and a watermelon.*

::: details Show answer
`zalazun zahelan zal dodedel dowodel dur vuguyol.`

[z-Alazun | z-Ahelan | z-and] | [d-tomato | d-watermelon | d-something-else] | v-cook

#### Pick one {#advanced-pick-one}

**1.** *Alazun cooks “Tomato and Watermelon.”* (the familiar dish) `zalazun dodedel dowodel dan vuguyol.` or `zalazun dodedel dowodel dal vuguyol.`

::: details Show answer
`zalazun dodedel dowodel dan vuguyol.`

z-Alazun | NAME[d-tomato | d-watermelon | d-and] | v-cook

**-n** on the join names the list as one set phrase; **`dal`** is just *a tomato and a watermelon*.

**Rule:** [Named phrase](named-phrases.md#named-list)
:::

**2.** *Alazun is sleepier than Ahelan said.* `zalazun thebam blahelan zol guzemel.` or `zalazun zol guzemel thebam blahelan.`

::: details Show answer
`zalazun thebam blahelan zol guzemel.`

[z-Alazun | [th-REPORTED | bl-Ahelan] | z-rank/more | g-sleepy]

Before the rank join, REPORTED is the bar: what Ahelan said sets the level. After the fence it is a stance on the whole superlative: *Alazun is the sleepiest, Ahelan says*.

**Rule:** [Every bar](stance-bars.md#stance-bars)
:::

**3.** *Azozon sees a huge but finite number of baguettes.* `zazozon debodolx grawam vayebel.` or `zazozon debodolx grawal vayebel.`

::: details Show answer
`zazozon debodolx grawam vayebel.`

z-Azozon | [d-baguette-x | g-plus-infinity.about] | v-see

Open **-m** keeps the amount finite, just very large; **`grawal`** is infinitely many.

**Rule:** [Special values](special-values.md#special-values)
:::

**4.** *Azozon, whom I remember as sleepy, is sleeping.* `zazozon wevum guzemel vezuvel.` or `zazozon guzemel vezuvel thevum.`

::: details Show answer
`zazozon wevum guzemel vezuvel.`

[z-Azozon | [w-MEMORY | g-sleepy]] | v-sleep

On `/w/` right before the adjective, MEMORY covers only *sleepy*; **`thevum`** on `/th/` covers the whole sentence: *I remember sleepy Azozon sleeping*.

**Rule:** [Mood on one adjective](universality.md#mood-on-adjective)
:::

**5.** *Azozon commands that Alazun cook.* `zazozon vorn zalazun vuguyol.` or `zazozon varn zalazun vuguyol.`

::: details Show answer
`zazozon vorn zalazun vuguyol.`

z-Azozon | v-command | z-Alazun | v-cook

**`vorn`** takes the next sentence as an instruction; **`varn`** would only state that Alazun cooks.

**Rule:** [Lexicalized stand-ins in other roles](stand-in-roles.md#stand-in-roles)
:::

**6.** *There is a second-to-last baguette.* `zebodol gruodol.` or `zebodol grodol.`

::: details Show answer
`zebodol gruodol.`

z-baguette | g-2nd-from-end

**`rue`** counts from the end; **`re`** would be the second baguette from the start.

**Rule:** [From the end](stacked-markers.md#from-the-end)
:::

**7.** *Azozon usually sits.* `zazozon huam vehohel.` or `zazozon hual vehohel.`

::: details Show answer
`zazozon huam vehohel.`

z-Azozon | h-always.open | v-sit

Open **-m** leaves exceptions; closed **-l** allows none.

**Rule:** [Channels on a generalization](usually-vs-must.md#generalization)
:::

**8.** *Ahelan sees at most five baguettes.* `zahelan debodolx gruwal uel grazal vayebel.` or `zahelan debodolx gruwal oel grazal vayebel.`

::: details Show answer
`zahelan debodolx gruwal oel grazal vayebel.`

z-Ahelan | [d-baguette-x | g-minus-infinity] | through | g-five | v-see

**`oel`** keeps the five in (*at most five*); **`uel`** would leave it out (*fewer than five*).

**Rule:** [Rays](open-ranges.md#rays)
:::

#### What changes {#advanced-what-changes}

**1.** `zazozon dawahol vowurolul.` / `zazozon dawahol vowurolum.`

::: details Show answer
z-Azozon | d-station | v-leave

z-Azozon | d-station | v-recede

With **`ul`** fused, Azozon leaves the station. With **`um`**, Azozon only recedes from it, moving away without leaving it behind.

**Rule:** [Cite plus a hook](hook-compounds.md#hook-compounds)
:::

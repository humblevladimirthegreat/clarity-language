# Stance bars {#stance-bars-sitting}

### Every bar {#stance-bars}
<a id="worse-than"></a>
<a id="best-effort"></a>

Intermediate ranked against a plan, and [sake bars](/prescription#sake-bars) rank against what a sake needs. Any stance that **sets a value** can be the [bar](/comparative-arity#bars), and each keeps its own endings and meaning. A channel word (how you know something: you saw it, remember it, were told it, worked it out) sets **the level that source led you to expect**. So *than usual*, *than I remember*, and *than advertised* each put one channel word in the bar slot.

> `zazozon thubom zol guzemel.`
>
> [z-Azozon | th-PATTERN | z-rank/more | g-sleepy]
>
> "Azozon is sleepier than usual."

> `zazozon zol guzemel thubom.`
>
> [z-Azozon | z-rank/more | g-sleepy] | th-PATTERN
>
> "Azozon is the sleepiest, going by the pattern."

<!-- cheat-sheet: roles-comparing -->
| Bar | Ranks against | English |
|-----|---------------|---------|
| met sake `thudatham`, `thobatham`, … ([sake bars](/prescription#sake-bars)) | what that sake needs | *enough* / *too much* / *not enough* |
| LIVE `thugem` | how it looks | *than it looks* |
| MEMORY `thevum` | how you remember it | *than I remember* / *than last time* |
| PATTERN `thubom` | the trail of cases | *than usual* / *normal* |
| CLUES `theyom` | what was worked out | *than calculated* / *than it should be* |
| REPORTED `thebam` | what was said or written | *than advertised* / *than they said* / *than the stories say* |
| INTUITION `thabem` | your gut expectation | *than I expected* |
| FORMER `helam` | how it used to be | *than it used to be* / *increasingly* |
| NOTIONAL `theyum` | the imagined case | *than imagined* / *than in theory* |
| PLAN `thagom` | the plan or the schedule | *than planned* / *than scheduled* |
| WANT `thalol` / `thalom` / `thalor` | the level wanted | *than wanted* / *than I wanted* |
| ABIL `hodaxal` | what can be done | *as … as possible* |
| a requirement `hwuvol` / `hwuvom` / `hwuvor` | the rule, the demand, or custom | *up to code* / *than demanded* / *than customary* |
| a permission `hwuval` / `hwuvam` / `hwuvar` | the rule, the grant, or what is tolerated | *over the limit* / *more than they allow* / *than tolerated* |
| consent `hwudam` | the agreed terms | *more than agreed* |
| speaker attitude (`thuwuyom`, `thewemum`) | the case you hoped for or feared | *than hoped* / *than feared* |

The bar's ending keeps its own job: on a channel it is [evidence strength](/evidentiality#evidence-strength) (`thubol` *than it reliably is*, `thubor` *than the few cases so far*).

> `zazozon thevum zol gelazem.`
>
> [z-Azozon | th-MEMORY | z-rank/more | g-big]
>
> "Azozon is bigger than I remember."

> `zanal thebam zuol gagebem.`
>
> [z-book | th-REPORTED | z-rank/less | g-clarity]
>
> "The book is less clear than advertised."

> `zazozon thabem zol guzemel.`
>
> [z-Azozon | th-INTUITION | z-rank/more | g-sleepy]
>
> "Azozon is sleepier than I expected."

> `zazozon helam zol guzemel.`
>
> [z-Azozon | h-FORMER | z-rank/more | g-sleepy]
>
> "Azozon is sleepier than they used to be."

> `zanal thalom zuol garolem.`
>
> [z-book | th-WANT-unstated | z-rank/less | g-heavy]
>
> "The book is lighter than I wanted."

The bar's `/b/` works as it does on the clause. On REPORTED it is the source, and on a [say word](/feelings-permission#say-words) over the act (`hwuvom`, `hwuvam`) it is the person who demands or grants. WANT takes no `/b/`: for someone else's wants, put a [holder](/holder#holder) on the clause. On PATTERN it is **whose cases** set the pattern: the population you compare against. With no `/b/`, PATTERN is the ranked item's own usual level, or the usual case in the situation.

> `zanal thebam blalazun zuol gagebem.`
>
> [z-book | [th-REPORTED | bl-Alazun] | z-rank/less | g-clarity]
>
> "The book is less clear than Alazun said."

> `zazozon thubom blwozeluyolx zol gozuyel.`
>
> [z-Azozon | [th-PATTERN | bl-role-agent-learn-x] | z-rank/more | g-sing]
>
> "Azozon sings well for a learner."

On a rank join, an event root on `/ɡ/` is the scale itself: how well the act goes (*sings well*), not an act [in progress](/predication#in-progress).

An adjective after that `/b/` describes it, as after any [host](/left-bound#complex-chaining), so it narrows the population.

> `zazozon thubom blwozeluyolx guzemel zol gozuyel.`
>
> [z-Azozon | [th-PATTERN | [bl-role-agent-learn-x | g-sleepy]] | z-rank/more | g-sing]
>
> "Azozon sings well for a sleepy learner."

The ranked item can be a whole kind. Write the [universal fence](/universals-domains#universals-domains-generics) first, then the bar: the whole closed fence counts as the one ranked item, the way an inner list sits inside an outer one ([fence nesting](/rank-joins#fence-nesting)). Open **`zuam`** is *people of that kind, as a rule*.

> `zuam gwuzadamul thubom zol hral vabezum.`
>
> [[z-everything.open | g-episode-agent-tired] | th-PATTERN | z-rank/more | h-how-often] | v-flaw
>
> "Tired people make mistakes more often than usual."

To rank one kind against another, keep one kind as the item and put the other in the PATTERN bar's `/b/`.

> `zuam gwuzunuvem thubom bleyelx gadamum zol hemebom vuzubal.`
>
> [[z-everything.open | g-episode-agent-rest] | [th-PATTERN | [bl-person-x | g-burnout]] | z-rank/more | h-achievement] | v-sweat
>
> "Rested people work more successfully than exhausted people."

A CLUES or PATTERN bar can also take [`blarl`](/evidentiality#evidence-clause): the next sentence is the grounds you worked the bar out from.

**Ability.** The ability word [`oda`](/can-and-cant#ability-fallback) as the bar is the limit of **what can be done**. A tie against **`hodaxal`** is *as … as possible*. The ability vowel says how fixed that limit is, so rank **`zol`** against **`hodaxol`** is *more than can be done for now*, and against **`hodaxul`** *more than could ever be done*.

> `zalazun hodaxal zoel hadurom vowurol.`
>
> [z-Alazun | h-ABIL-able | z-equal-rank | h-haste] | v-walk
>
> "Alazun walks as hastily as possible."

**Rules and permission.** A requirement as the bar is the demand; a tie meets it. A permission is the limit; rank **`zol`** goes over it.

> `zohehal hwuvol zoel gabobem.`
>
> [z-house | h-episode-event-REQUIRE-rule | z-equal-rank | g-strength]
>
> "The house is as strong as the rules require."

> `zedudal hwuval zol gral.`
>
> [z-tea | h-episode-event-ALLOW-rule | z-rank/more | g-amount]
>
> "There is more tea than the rules allow."

**Hopes and fears.** A speaker-attitude word as the bar is the case you hoped for or feared.

> `zanal thuwuyom zol gagebem.`
>
> [z-book | th-hope | z-rank/more | g-clarity]
>
> "The book is clearer than I hoped."

> `zanal thewemum zuol gagebem.`
>
> [z-book | th-concern | z-rank/less | g-clarity]
>
> "The book is even less clear than I feared."

Only these stances set a value. A [cause or condition pole](/causation), [MAY](/knowing#may), [MIRATIVE](/notional-standing#mirative), [DECISION](/ability#decision), and [ATTEMPT](/ability#try) do not, so none of them is a bar. A want sets the level wanted; a decision or an attempt commits to an act and sets no level, so *more than decided* is the [plan](/intention#plan-predict) bar.

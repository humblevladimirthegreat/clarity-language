# Stand-in words {#stand-in-words}

### Lexicalized stand-ins in other roles {#stand-in-roles}

English has verbs that name a speech act as the event: *state*, *confirm*, *deny*, *agree*, as in *Azozon confirms it* or *I deny that …*. Ageben builds them from the stand-ins. The vowel still says what kind of content follows, and the first letter says which role that content fills. With the ending `-rn` (or `-n` after two vowels, as in `vaen`), the stand-in becomes a fixed word for that kind of content: **`darn`** *a statement*, **`dern`** *a question*, **`dorn`** *a command*, **`durn`** *a prohibition*.

On `/v/`, the same `-rn` / `-n` endings make verbs that take the following sentence as their content, so you can make that speech act or report it. Use the same vowels with another role letter when the following sentence fills that role. Each content type has one fixed form; there is no locked / open pair here.

<!-- cheat-sheet: linking-clauses -->
| Ageben | Dependent content | English |
|--------|-------------------|---------|
| **`varn`** | statement-like (`a`) | *state…* |
| **`vern`** | question-like (`e`) | *question…* |
| **`vorn`** | instruction-like (`o`) | *command…* |
| **`vurn`** | avoidance-like (`u`) | *prohibit…* |
| **`vaon`** | accepts a statement | *confirm that…* |
| **`vuon`** | rejects a statement | *deny that…* |
| **`vaen`** | accepts a proposed action | *agree to…* |
| **`vuen`** | rejects a proposed action | *decline to…* |
| **`vuan`** | rejects the premise behind a question or claim | *dispute the premise that…* |

For example, the `/v/` word takes the following sentence as its content:

> `zazozon vaon zalazun vehohel.`
>
> z-Azozon | v-confirm | z-Alazun | v-sit
>
> "Azozon confirms that Alazun sits."

> `zazozon vuen zazozon vehohel.`
>
> z-Azozon | v-decline-to | z-Azozon | v-sit
>
> "Azozon declines to sit."

**Compare with:** a speaking verb + [`darl`](/dependents#dependent-clauses) reports the act of speaking, and the content is its object, what was said. Pick it when the speaking is the event, as in *Azozon tells Alazun that …*; pick the fixed word when the act (confirming, denying) is the event.

> `zazozon vezuyol balazun darl zalazun vehohel.`
>
> z-Azozon | v-tell | b-Alazun | d-that-clause | z-Alazun | v-sit
>
> "Azozon tells Alazun that Alazun sits."

A `/z/` stand-in puts the whole following sentence in the subject role. With a `/ɡ/` word describing that sentence, that is English *it is … that* / *it is … to*.

> `gamahum zarl zazozon vowurol.`
>
> g-challenge | z-that-clause | z-Azozon | v-walk
>
> "It is hard for Azozon to walk."

The sentence after one of these verbs has its own subject when the chosen content type requires one, just as it does after `darl` / `derl`. Changing the role letter lets the same kind of content serve as an object, a described property, or another role the clause allows.

### Practice {#advanced-practice}

Short drills for Advanced. Try each item before opening **Show answer**.

**Setting:** a complaint desk

**New words:**

| English | Ageben | Cue |
|---------|--------|-----|
| *command* | `vorn` | **o** ≈ rank: the next sentence is an instruction |
| *state* | `varn` | **a** ≈ add: the next sentence is a claim |
| *prohibit* | `vurn` | **u** ≈ undo: the next sentence is something to avoid |
| *statement* | `darn` | **a** ≈ add: a statement as object |

**Review:**

| English | Ageben |
|---------|--------|
| *Azozon* | `azozon` |
| *Alazun* | `alazun` |
| *Ahelan* | `ahelan` |
| *sit* | `vehohel` |
| *see* | `vayebel` |
| *ticket* | `odenol` |
| *pay* | `varal` |
| *luggage* | `elobal` |
| *challenge* | `amahum` |
| *lift* | `velunel` |

#### English → Ageben {#advanced-english-to-ageben}

**1.** *Azozon commands that Alazun sit.*

::: details Show answer
`zazozon vorn zalazun vehohel.`

z-Azozon | v-command | z-Alazun | v-sit
:::

**2.** *Azozon sees the statement that Ahelan sits.*

::: details Show answer
`zazozon vayebel darn zahelan vehohel.`

z-Azozon | v-see | d-statement | z-Ahelan | v-sit
:::

**3.** *Alazun confirms that Azozon sees the ticket.*

::: details Show answer
`zalazun vaon zazozon vayebel dodenol.`

z-Alazun | v-confirm | z-Azozon | v-see | d-ticket
:::

**4.** *Ahelan declines to pay.*

::: details Show answer
`zahelan vuen zahelan varal.`

z-Ahelan | v-decline-to | z-Ahelan | v-pay
:::

#### Ageben → English {#advanced-ageben-to-english}

**1.** `zazozon varn zalazun vehohel.`

::: details Show answer
z-Azozon | v-state | z-Alazun | v-sit

*Azozon states that Alazun sits.*
:::

**2.** `zazozon vurn zalazun vehohel.`

::: details Show answer
z-Azozon | v-prohibit | z-Alazun | v-sit

*Azozon prohibits Alazun from sitting.*
:::

**3.** `zahelan vuon zazozon varal.`

::: details Show answer
z-Ahelan | v-deny | z-Azozon | v-pay

*Ahelan denies that Azozon pays.*
:::

**4.** `gamahum zarl zazozon velunel delobal.`

::: details Show answer
g-challenge | z-that-clause | z-Azozon | v-lift | d-luggage

*It is hard for Azozon to lift the luggage.*
:::

#### Pick one {#advanced-pick-one}

**1.** *Azozon commands that Alazun sit.* `zazozon vorn zalazun vehohel.` or `zazozon varn zalazun vehohel.`

::: details Show answer
`zazozon vorn zalazun vehohel.`

z-Azozon | v-command | z-Alazun | v-sit

**`vorn`** takes the next sentence as an instruction; **`varn`** only states that Alazun sits.
:::

#### What changes {#advanced-what-changes}

**1.** `zalazun vaen zalazun varal.` / `zalazun vuen zalazun varal.`

::: details Show answer
z-Alazun | v-agree | z-Alazun | v-pay

z-Alazun | v-decline-to | z-Alazun | v-pay

The first: Alazun agrees to pay. The second: Alazun declines to pay.
:::

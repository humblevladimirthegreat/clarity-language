# Stand-in words {#stand-in-words}

### Lexicalized stand-ins in other roles {#stand-in-roles}

A stand-in's vowel says what kind of content follows, and its first letter says which role that content fills. With **-rn** (or **-n** after two vowels, as in **`vaen`**), the stand-in becomes a fixed word for that kind of content: **`darn`** *a statement*, **`dern`** *a question*, **`dorn`** *a command*, **`durn`** *a prohibition*. Use the same vowels with another role letter when the following sentence fills that role. On `/v/`, the same **-rn** / **-n** endings make verbs that take the following sentence as their content.

Each content type has one lexicalized form; there is no closed / open pair here.

<!-- cheat-sheet: linking-clauses -->
| Ageben | Dependent content | English |
|--------|-------------------|---------|
| **`varn`** | statement-like (`a`) | *state…* |
| **`vern`** | question-like (`e`) | *question…* |
| **`vorn`** | instruction-like (`o`) | *command…* |
| **`vurn`** | avoidance-like (`u`) | *prohibit…* |
| **`vaon`** | accepts a statement | *confirm that…* |
| **`vuon`** | rejects a statement | *deny that…* |
| **`vaen`** | accepts a proposed action | *agree to…* |
| **`vuen`** | rejects a proposed action | *decline to…* |
| **`vuan`** | rejects the premise behind a question or claim | *dispute the premise that…* |

For example, the `/v/` word takes the following sentence as its content:

> `zazozon vaon zalazun vehohel.`
>
> z-Azozon | v-confirm | z-Alazun | v-sit
>
> "Azozon confirms that Alazun sits."

> `zazozon vuen zazozon vehohel.`
>
> z-Azozon | v-decline-to | z-Azozon | v-sit
>
> "Azozon declines to sit."

A `/z/` stand-in puts the whole following sentence in the subject slot. With a `/ɡ/` word describing that sentence, that is English *it is … that* / *it is … to*.

> `gamahum zarl zazozon vowurol.`
>
> g-challenge | z-that-clause | z-Azozon | v-walk
>
> "It is hard for Azozon to walk."

The sentence after one of these verbs has its own subject when the chosen content type requires one, just as it does after **`darl`** / **`derl`**. Changing the role letter lets the same kind of content serve as an object, a described property, or another role the clause allows.

### Practice {#advanced-practice}

Short drills for Advanced. Try each item before opening **Show answer**.

**Setting:** a complaint desk

**New words:**

| English | Ageben | Cue |
|---------|--------|-----|
| *command* | `vorn` | **o** ≈ rank: the next sentence is an instruction |
| *state* | `varn` | **a** ≈ add: the next sentence is a claim |
| *prohibit* | `vurn` | **u** ≈ undo: the next sentence is something to avoid |
| *statement* | `darn` | **a** ≈ add: a statement as object |

**Review:**

| English | Ageben |
|---------|--------|
| *Azozon* | `azozon` |
| *Alazun* | `alazun` |
| *Ahelan* | `ahelan` |
| *sit* | `vehohel` |
| *see* | `vayebel` |

#### English → Ageben {#advanced-english-to-ageben}

**1.** *Azozon commands that Alazun sit.*

::: details Show answer
`zazozon vorn zalazun vehohel.`

z-Azozon | v-command | z-Alazun | v-sit
:::

**2.** *Azozon sees the statement that Ahelan sits.*

::: details Show answer
`zazozon vayebel darn zahelan vehohel.`

z-Azozon | v-see | d-statement | z-Ahelan | v-sit
:::

#### Ageben → English {#advanced-ageben-to-english}

**1.** `zazozon varn zalazun vehohel.`

::: details Show answer
z-Azozon | v-state | z-Alazun | v-sit

*Azozon states that Alazun sits.*
:::

**2.** `zazozon vurn zalazun vehohel.`

::: details Show answer
z-Azozon | v-prohibit | z-Alazun | v-sit

*Azozon prohibits Alazun from sitting.*
:::

#### Pick one {#advanced-pick-one}

**1.** *Azozon commands that Alazun sit.* `zazozon vorn zalazun vehohel.` or `zazozon varn zalazun vehohel.`

::: details Show answer
`zazozon vorn zalazun vehohel.`

z-Azozon | v-command | z-Alazun | v-sit

**`vorn`** takes the next sentence as an instruction; **`varn`** only states that Alazun sits.
:::

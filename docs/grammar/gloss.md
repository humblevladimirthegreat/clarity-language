# Gloss overlay

Paste Agelan and click or highlight a word for a floating gloss card with part of speech, reference ending, lexicon or overlay gloss, and morph chips. Failed words stay in the stream with an error card. Highlight a join, span fence, or `^` to inspect the construction.

**Speak Agelan** plays Agelan in the browser (lazy eSpeak-NG). **Show IPA** sits next to it and transcribes the same spoken forms (phonology letter table, with syllable dots). Writing shorthand and span brackets expand before speech and IPA; foreign `<>` interiors and compact loan words are still skipped (not Agelan phonology). Speech is Agelan, not the English gloss.
